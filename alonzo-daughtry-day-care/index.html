Alonzo A. Daughtry Memorial Day Care Center

Child care center · Boerum Hill, Brooklyn

Alonzo A. Daughtry Memorial Day Care Center is a child care center at 565 Baltic Street in Boerum Hill.

In its own words: “Dr. Karen S. Daughtry, the Educational Director of the Alonzo A. Daughtry Memorial Day Care Center has been at the helm of the center’s successful operation since its inception in late 1969 when the center was founded by The House of the Lord Church as a result of a community outreach program in which residents of the community expressed a need for quality child care services.”
