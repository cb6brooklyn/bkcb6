*** MINUTES ***

The meeting commenced at 6:31pm. A motion was made by Pauline Blake and seconded by Tom Miskel to approve the minutes of the previous meeting held on October 23, 2017.

Motion Approved: Unanimously

Presentation and review of a new on-premises liquor license application submitted to the State Liquor Authority on behalf of Grindhaus at 275 Van Brunt Street (between Pioneer Street/Visitation Place).

Presented by Erin Norris, the owner. She is applying to change from a beer/wine license to a full liquor license due to impending closing of the nearby bar Bait and Tackle.

Ms. Norris lives upstairs from the establishment and intends to close at reasonable hours.  She agrees to modify hours of operation to 2pm until 2am – 7 days a week.  Agrees to outdoor hours of 4pm to 11pm – 7 days a week.  Agrees to keep back door closed and will not play music outdoors.

Glenn Kelly made a motion to approve the application, seconded by Pauline Blake.

Vote: 10…..Yeas 0…..Nays 0…..Abstentions

Motion approved: Unanimously

Presentation and review of a new on-premises liquor license application submitted to the State Liquor Authority on behalf of 421 Bond Inc. at 421 Bond Street (between 3rd/4th Streets).

Alex Donskoi is the owner. No show by presenter. Josh Skaller moved to table until next month, seconded by Karyn Broughton.

Motion approved: Unanimously

239 – 5th Avenue – Not on the agenda Due to incorrect community notification.

There being no further business to come before the Committee. The meeting was adjourned. Minutes were submitted by Glenn Kelly.
