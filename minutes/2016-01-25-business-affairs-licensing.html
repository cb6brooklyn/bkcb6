** M I N U T E S ***

Presentation and review of a new on-premises liquor license application submitted to the State Liquor Authority on behalf of A & E Supply Corp. at 548 4th Avenue (between 14th/15th Streets).

Two principals appeared from the proposed restaurant/coffee bar/butcher/cheese larder. They will have 9 tables with 23 seats and 8 seats at the bar. The proposed hours are 7am to midnight daily.

Mark Shames moved to approve as presented with hours adjusted to allow 2am closing subject to owner’s discretion and community response. Deborah Buscarello seconded.

VOTE: 7…..YEAS 0…..NAYS 0…..ABSTENTIONS

MOTION PASSED

Presentation and review of a new on-premises liquor license application submitted to the State Liquor Authority on behalf of Union Investor LLC at 645-651 Union Street (between 3rd/4th Avenues).

A new hotel across from Dinosaur BBQ of 5 stories – Gowanus Inn plus yard. Outdoor areas include courtyard, rooftop and terrace. All public areas accommodate fewer than 74 people.

Mark Shames moved to approve the application with outdoor hours as follows: 1am closing from Sunday to Wednesday and 2am closing from Thursday to Saturday. The motion was seconded by Sayar Lonial.

VOTE: 8…..YEAS 0…..NAYS 0…..ABSTENTIONS

MOTION PASSED

Presentation and review of a new on-premises liquor license application submitted to the State Liquor Authority on behalf of 421 Bond Inc. at 421 Bond Street (between 3rd/4th Streets).

Casual food restaurant in a very large space. The first floor will have 49 tables and 282 seats. The rooftop will have 70 tables and 216 seats.

Sayar Lonial moved to approve as presented with stipulation that premises will not become a cabaret space. The motion was seconded by Jane Li.

VOTE: 8…..YEAS 0…..NAYS 0…..ABSTENTIONS

MOTION PASSED

There being no further business to come before the committee, the meeting was adjourned.

The minutes were submitted by Glenn Kelly.
