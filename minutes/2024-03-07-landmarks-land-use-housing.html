1. 404A Henry Street - Cobble Hill Historic District

Certificate of Appropriateness application to allow for removal of existing fire escape and

installation of a new balcony. Questions raised by committee about dimension and design

3 against.

Motion passes and will be presented at full board meeting.

2. 214, 216, 218, 222, 228, and 230 Court Street - Cobble Hill Historic District

Certificate of Appropriateness application to replace deteriorated facades with new brick.

The DOB Safety Unit has determined the facades present a life safety hazard and require

immediate repair. The work includes removal of stucco at all but one of the buildings and

replacing the face brick. A sidewalk shed is in place.

When asked why certain addresses were not included in the repair work that was presented,

Applicant provided the following information: 212 is not unsafe, 220 does not require

replacing the brick, 224 is already under construction, and 226 has been non-responsive.

In response to committee questions about building dates, Applicant provided the following

information: 214-220 were built at same time, 222-226 were built later, and 228-230 were

built together even later. Each group of buildings appears to represent similar, but slightly

different styles from the other two groups.

Jerry - Motion to conditionally approve if the exposed header courses proposed by

applicant are removed and the mortar joint width matches the original condition.

Maddie and Peter - seconded

Dave - After withdrawing a first friendly amendment, proposed a second friendly

amendment that stipulated additional investigative work take place when removing the

stucco to determine the original face brick pattern at each building. Based on the results,

the new face brick pattern at each building will match the original pattern. The friendly

amendment was accepted.

11 in favor.

1 abstention.

Motion passes and will be presented at full board meeting.

Bob - motion to recommend new cornice installation at 216.

Jerry and Peter - seconded
