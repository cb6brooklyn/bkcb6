Land Use

1. 150 Mill Street ULURP application - proposed change in zoning district

from M1-1 to M1-5 to facilitate the development of a ten-story mixed-use

building for retail, health care, and office space.

Presentation made by application team. Drawings show a supermarket on the

ground floor, light manufacturing uses and community space on the second

floor, ambulatory diagnostic uses on the fourth floor, and offices on the

remaining floors.

Applicant highlighted the following benefits:

• Supermarket - The proposed supermarket will provide access to fresh and

affordable groceries, meeting essential needs of local residents.

• Community Space for Local Nonprofits - The proposed community space will

offer meeting rooms for local nonprofits to collaborate and serve the

community.

• Light Industrial Space - The light industrial space will encourage

economic development and local jobs by providing space for small-scale

manufacturing and production activities.

• Maker Space - The maker space will empower individuals to engage in

creative and entrepreneurial endeavors and promote innovation and

craftsmanship within the community.

• Job Training - The community classroom space will be utilized to provide

job training programs, which will nequip local community members with

skills and certifications for employment in various light manufacturing

industries.

Several questions from the committee about notification of neighbors,

community benefits agreement (CBA), ability for property owner to lease

spaces, who would run the job training classes, parking requirements,

delivery access for grocery store, proximity to highway, and target market

for grocery store.

Several attending community members expressed strong support for the

project.

Jerry - Motion to conditionally approve with the following stipulations: a)

The applicant and local community-based organizations (CBOs) will prepare

either a letter or MOU of what the CBA will include and share it with the

community board prior to its April 10 board meeting, and b) the applicant

will continue its dialogue with local CBOs throughout the ULURP process.

Kathleen - seconded

Mark - friendly amendment to include local councilmember in negotiation of

CBA. Amendment was not accepted.

3 in favor.

1 abstention.

1 against.

Motion passes and will be presented at full board meeting.

End of meeting.
