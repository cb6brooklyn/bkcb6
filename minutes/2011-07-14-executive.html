The meeting was called to order at 6:56pm.

Board member attendance review The attendance of the following board members was reviewed by the committee: Joseph Igneri, Harriet Hughes, Marguerite Scott and Terry Alexander.

Harriet Hughes – Harriet appeared and spoke in her defense. Any action was tabled and will be reviewed at a later date, if necessary.

Terry Alexander – wants to check his calendar. Any action was tabled until dates are checked.

Marguerite Scott – seven absences – conflicts with NYC Housing Authority meetings. Recommendation: to put off any action until October Executive Committee meeting.

Joseph Igneri – recommendation to remove from the Board. A motion was made by Peter Fleming and seconded by Bob Levine.
