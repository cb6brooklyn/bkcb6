******MINUTES******

Project LETS gave a presentation on its work building peer support collectives, leading political education, developing new knowledge and language around mental distress, organizing and advocating for the liberation of community members globally, and creating innovative, peer-led alternatives to the current mental health system. The committee discussed this and various policy measures that could be implemented. Reema’s work in making this presentation happen is greatly appreciated.
