CHAIRPERSON’S REPORT…………………………………………...PETER FLEMING, BOARD CHAIR

Chairperson Peter Fleming opened with brief updates regarding a new supermarket, Food Garden Market, which will occupy the entire eight thousand sq. ft. ground floor of the new building on 4th Avenue between Warren and St. Marks.

The Chair then reported to the Board that the District Office staff has yet to receive a raise in nearly three years. As such, the Chair asked for and received a motion from the floor to immediately give District Manager Mike Racioppo, Assistant District Manager Ty Beatty, and Office Manager Liza Santiago-Sierra equivalent salary increases.

Motion: Made by Board Member Alec Schierenbeck, seconded by Board Member Jerry Armer, To increase the salaries of District Manager Mike Racioppo, Assistant District Manager Ty Beatty, and Office Manager Liza Santiago-Sierra an equivalent percentage point of roughly 5% per annum.

VOTE: 29 YEAS, 0 NAYS, 0 ABSTENTIONS

MOTION PASSED UNANIMOUSLY
