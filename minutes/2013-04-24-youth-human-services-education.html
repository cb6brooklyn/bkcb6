***MINUTES***

Discussion with representatives for our area’s public elementary schools on school-based needs.

PUBLIC SCHOOL 15 – RED HOOK

 Severe Hurricane Sandy damage (25 feet of water).  Department of Education facilities did a great job.  Unsure if FEMA or DOE will handle basement restoration (out/closed for 2 weeks).  Lost all basement storage area.  Needed capital funding for air conditioning for several rooms (auditorium, etc.).  Hazard for students with asthma.  City Council Member, Sara Gonzalez and State Assembly Member, Felix Ortiz are working with them to secure funding.  Applied for funding this year but waiting for word.  Population of 390 students with large population of students with special needs.  Dental, health clinic, mental health services makes a huge impact for students.  Issues with transportation – landlocked but have met with many groups (MTA, Parks Department) to look at various transportation options.  Core Curriculum – challenge to determine resources for schools to utilize (due to DOE working out specifics with new stndards).  Applied for Magnet Grant to increase enrollment – room for more students.  Concern about Charter Schools moving into areas – What is the process for moving in?  PS 15 top 3 or 4 schools in the district.  Heating system damaged – lost furniture and books during the storm.

Ask - is to get air conditioning installed into the 3 main spaces. Felix Ortiz’s office working on it but still not sure of the process.

NYSERDA – might check in to see if there is a green solution for the cooling system.

Ask – Record room was destroyed and need shelving to store records.

Ask – Transportation options for students in Red Hook (can’t do city bus to subway) MTA rejected request to get coding for city bus use.

LEE CHURCH – PS282, Jessica Collins – Community Coordinator, Kathleen Larkins – Parent Coordinator.  E-MAIL sent from Magalie Alexis to CB6 with concerns.  Pre-K to 8th – 1100 students in District 13.  Take trips – theater camp, camping trips often times funded through principal’s own pocket.  School is close to capacity.  Core curriculum – students have the biggest adjustment especially preparing for a big exam. We test them a lot.  No longer a Title 1 school at this moment.  ASK – within Park Slope but doesn’t fall in District 15 – represent well but there needs to be more of a relationship with community – want to feel like they belong to Park Slope.  Invite to CB^ members to come to staff/teacher basketball game this Friday at 6:00pm.  Kitchen issue – no hot water unless boiler and heat come on (not good during the summer).  Lee Church or other representatives will come to CB6 meetings.  Students aren’t neighborhood kids and the neighborhood regards them as such.  Most students are from Bed-Stuy, Crown Heights.
