CHAIRPERSON’S REPORT…………………………………………...PETER FLEMING, BOARD CHAIR

Board Chair Peter Fleming opened the meeting with very brief remarks, commenting on the presumed timeline for and likely digital nature of the proposed Gowanus Rezoning, while stating that the Board’s obvious wish is to hold more traditional, in-person meetings sometime in the 2021 calendar year.

DISTRICT MANAGER’S REPORT……………………MICHAEL RACIOPPO, DISTRICT MANAGER

District Manager Michael Racioppo also offered brief remarks, offering his perspective on interactions with the Department of City Planning specific to the proposed Gowanus Rezoning. Mr. Racioppo received some questions and comments from a few Board Members relative to this process as it is occurring, with he and the Chair offering responses. 1
