LANDMARKS/LAND USE COMMITTEE- DAVID BRIGGS, LANDMARKS CHAIR

ALEC SCHIERENBECK, LAND USE CHAIR

• Land Use Committee Chair Alec Schierenbeck reported Motion to disapprove an application for a ULURP application for 9th Street between 2ndAvenue/3rd Avenue to the Department of City Planning for a proposed zoning map amendment from M2-1 to M1-4/R7A, a special permit pursuant to ZR 74-533 to waive required residential off-street parking, and a zoning text amendment to designate a Mandatory Inclusionary Housing Area and Mixed-Use District in order to facilitate a nine-story mixed-use development with 48 dwelling units and ground floor commercials space. Motion: To disapprove an application for a ULURP application for 9th Street between 2ndAvenue/3rd Avenue to the Department of City Planning for a proposed zoning map amendment from M2-1 to M1-4/R7A.

VOTE: 34 YEAS, 6 NAYS, 1 ABSTENTION

MOTION FAILS

TIME: 7:35 P.M.

• After the committee’s motion failed a motion to approve the application was made. David Briggs offered an amendment to designate portions of the development for artists which was not accepted. Conditions were added to the amendment to allow for lower incomes to qualify for the affordable housing component as well as asking to DCP to commit to rezoning the IBZ. Motion: To approve an application for a ULURP application for 9th Street between 2ndAvenue/3rd Avenue to the Department of City Planning for a proposed zoning map amendment from M2-1 to M1-4/R7A, a special permit pursuant to ZR 74-533 to waive required residential off-street parking, and a zoning text amendment to designate a Mandatory Inclusionary Housing Area and Mixed-Use District in order to facilitate a nine-story mixed-use development with 48 dwelling units and ground floor commercials space.

VOTE: 31 YEAS, 6 NAYS, 1 ABSTENTION

MOTION PASSES

TIME: 7:35 P.M.

• Landmarks Committee Chair David Briggs reported that the committee reviewed an application Certificate of Appropriateness for a Motion to approve an application to the Landmarks Preservation Commission for a Certificate of Appropriateness to allow for the installation of visible railings at the roof and replacing existing leaded glass windows on the front facade at 121- 1236th Avenue, Park Slope Historic District.
