VI. EDC Presentation on Brooklyn Marine Terminal

- 122 acres, roughly the size of Battery Park City - Generational opportunity for land use change - Today is essentially off-limits to the public, that will change - Maritime business and public use - EDC expertise in maritime business/industry and neighborhood scope planning is why they are spearheading - Site is a key part of maritime transportation - Piers 11 and 12 already invested in, 7 and 10 have capital funding committed by City, 9a+b are both nonfunctional - Mixed use neighborhood envisioned - “Representative and robust” community engagement - Offered for any tenant/block association leaders to ask for EDC to come speak to them and they’ll do it

- Q&A:

- Jerry said to check IKEA rezoning agreement, he recalls a mandatory inquiry into maritime transport

- Jerry also noted this is not a ULURP but a GPP (allowed thru the state). - A: this is more efficient for a project of this scale (multiple geographies) would otherwise require a series of ULURPs - Goes through CPC - David refers to Gowanus Rezoning (ULURP) length of engagement as the expectation, and points to the complexity of Gowanus. How much of the land will be publicly owned? - A: once GPP is approved, City will be sole owner of the property - David: mitigate resilience/public park, transportation (F train reliance) - Leroy asked about who makes advisory council decisions? - Member asked about timeline–answer was “some time”, winter 2025 floated - Hildegaard: “Go EDC!” - AM Simon asked about the GPP process at it pertains to changes and community outreach in that.
