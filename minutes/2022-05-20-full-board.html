Landmarks Chair David Briggs reported that the committee considered an application to the Landmarks Preservation Commission at 171 Baltic Street.

Motion: To approve a Certificate of Appropriateness application to the Landmarks Preservation Commission to install a new rooftop bulkhead and solar canopy at 171 Baltic Street in the Cobble Hill Historic District.

VOTE: 15 YEAS, 7 NAYS, 4 ABSTENTIONS

MOTION PASSED

TIME: 7:00 P.M.

Landmarks Chair David Briggs reported that the committee considered an application to the Landmarks Preservation Commission at 193-195 Congress Street.

Motion: To approve a Certificate of Appropriateness application to the Landmarks Preservation Commission to restore railings and cornice at 195 Congress Street and for fully new construction of a two-story carriage house at 193 Congress Street in the Cobble Hill Historic District.

VOTE: 23 YEAS, 2 NAYS, 1 ABSTENTION

MOTION PASSED

TIME: 7:10 P.M.

Landmarks Chair David Briggs reported that the committee considered an application to the Landmarks Preservation Commission at 337 Clinton Street.

Motion: To approve a Certificate of Appropriateness application to the Landmarks Preservation Commission to alter the existing rear fire escape structure to facilitate multiple rear-yard decks at 337 Clinton Street in the Cobble Hill Historic District.

VOTE: 26 YEAS, 0 NAYS, 0 ABSTENTIONS

MOTION PASSED UNANIMOUSLY

TIME: 7:20 P.M.

Landmarks Chair David Briggs reported that the committee considered an application to the Landmarks Preservation Commission at 31 Strong Place.

Motion: To approve a Certificate of Appropriateness application to the Landmarks Preservation Commission for a new rooftop penthouse and a new enlarged 2-story rear yard extension in the Cobble Hill Historic District.

VOTE: 19 YEAS, 5 NAYS, 1 ABSTENTIONS

MOTION PASSED

TIME: 7:30 P.M.
