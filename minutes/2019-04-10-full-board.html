CHAIRPERSON’S REPORT…………………………………………...PETER FLEMING, BOARD CHAIR

Board Chair Peter Fleming opened his brief remarks by bringing forward an item from the previous Monday evening’s executive committee meeting: a request to co-name Columbia Street between Commerce Street and Delavan Street to “Daniel Defonte Way” after Mr. Daniel Defonte, the founder of Defonte’s Sandwich Shop. The Chair took a motion from the floor

Motion: By Board Member Jerry Armer; seconded by Board Member Karyn Broughton To support the request to co-name Columbia Street between Commerce Street and Delavan Street to “Daniel Defonte Way.”

VOTE: 28 YEAS, 0 NAYS, 2 ABSTENTIONS

MOTION PASSED

TIME: 6:47 P.M.

DISTRICT MANAGER’S REPORT……………………..MICHAEL RACIOPPO, DISTRICT MANAGER

Mr. Racioppo mentioned that FDNY has provided both replacement smoke detector batteries and coloring books, which were available at the table where the public signed in, at the entry way.

PARKS, RECREATION & ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION………………..GLENN KELLY, CHAIR

Parks Chair Glenn Kelly reported that the committee heard a presentation by the Play Fair campaign to increase maintenance funding by $100,000,000 in fiscal year 2020 and to add Brooklyn Community Board Six to the list of supporting members of the Play Fair Coalition.

Motion: To support the Play Fair campaign to increase NYC Parks maintenance funding by $100,000,000 in fiscal year 2020, but to withhold the Board’s name from a list of supporters.

VOTE: 31 YEAS, 3 NAYS, 3 ABSTENSTIONS

MOTION PASSED

TIME: 7:30 P.M.
