CB6 Transportation Committee Agenda

Feb 25, 2026

●​ Committee updates, recap (5 min):

○​ Last meeting recap

■​ Doug: 5th Ave BID presentation

■​ Tony: Presentation on safety and traffic issues on Bergen St. and the

potential impacts of the Flatbush bus redesign

○​ Most recent actions by the committee (Tony will summarize)

■​ Letter to DOT in support of adding traffic signals and daylighting in the

Columbia St. Waterfront area

■​ Resolution in support of the Ride Safe Right Right bill, Intro 1380,

which did not pass last session, restricting sales of e-bikes that go

faster than 20mph. We also supported a consistent citywide speed limit

of 20mph for bikes and micromobility vehicles.

●​ Possible resolution to consider: CB6 Slow Zone (5-10 min, Tony/Kevin)

○​ Kevin LaCherra of Make McGuinness Safe suggests we consider a resolution to

ask DOT to make CB6 a Slow Zone under Sammy's Law, with a speed limit of 20

MPH on all applicable streets.

○​ Brooklyn CB1 and Queens CB2 have passed resolutions. Kevin will present

these slides for us to consider

○​ Discussion, public comment, and vote on resolution language if committee

wishes to proceed

○​ Resolution passed unanimously with 1 abstention for cause.

■​ Recognizing CB6’s commitment to safer streets and support for Sammy’s

Law, we recommend that NYC DOT institute a district-wide 20 MPH slow

zone on all applicable streets, and adjust signal timing to reflect the new

limit, with camera enforcement.​

We also call for adoption of a citywide speed limit of 20mph on all

applicable streets.
