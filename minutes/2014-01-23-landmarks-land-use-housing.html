***MINUTES***

Presentation and review of a Certificate of Appropriateness application submitted to the Landmarks Preservation Commission for the enlargement of an existing areaway in the front yard of 777 Carroll Street (between Smith/Hoyt Streets), Carroll Gardens Historic District.

The owner is requesting approval of an expansion of a basement hatch to allow better access than current ladder.

Questions were asked about the purpose of the expansion. Ans. Better access and more convenient for meter reading/deliveries/storage.

A motion was made by Glenn Kelly: If Landmarks Preservation Committee has no objection to the enlargement of the basement doors, we have no objection to bluestone removal as long as it is re-used in order to maximize bluestone in the front courtyard. The motion was seconded by Noel Berk-Rauch.

VOTE: 5…..YEAS 1…..NAY

Motion to hold over LPC record until CB6 vote at Board Meeting.

MOTION APPROVED: UNANIMOUSLY

There being no further business to come before the committee, a motion was made to adjourn. The

motion was approved unanimously.

The minutes were submitted by Glenn Kelly.
