*** MEETING MINUTES***

The meeting was called to order at 6:39pm. There were 4 of 9 members present.

Presentation and review of a new on-premises liquor license application submitted to the State Liquor Authority on behalf of Brooklyn Boy Catering at 388 5th Avenue between 6th/7th Streets).

This is for a new on premises license. Mike Frazier from Brooklyn Boy presented. Stephen Samaniego resident of 322 6th Avenue expressed concerns regarding backyard space and amplified noise.

Vote to approve conditioned on amending outdoor hours and no speakers/music. Hours: Sunday to Wednesday – 10pm and Thursday – Saturday – 11pm.

A motion to approve was made by Glenn Kelly.

VOTE: 4…..YEAS 0…..NAYS 0…..ABSTENTIONS

MOTION APPROVED: UNANIMOUSLY

Presentation and review of a new on-premises liquor license application submitted to the State Liquor Authority on behalf of Hometown Pan Fried Chicken at 329 Van Brunt Street (between Sullivan/King Streets).

Billy Durney and Michael Rosen presented.

There is no outdoor space and they will amend application. Motion was made by Pauline Blake to approve.

VOTE: 4…..YEAS 0…..NAYS 0…..ABSENTIONS

MOTION APPROVED: UNANIMOUSLY

Presentation and review of a new on-premises liquor license application submitted to the State Liquor Authority on behalf of Nargis Bar & Grill at 155 5th Avenue (between Lincoln/Saint Johns Place).

Niel Triveti and Boris Bangiyer presented. Application to be amended to closing hours for Thursday – Saturday at 12am.

Motion to approve was made by Glenn Kelly.

VOTE: 4…..YEAS 0…..NAYS 0…..ABSENTIONS

MOTION APPROVED: UNANIMOUSLY

Presentation and review of a new on-premises liquor license application submitted to the State Liquor Authority on behalf of Brooklyn Burger at 259 5th Avenue (between Garfield Place/1st Street).

Colia Tennyson and Frank Palillo presented.

A motion to approve was made by Josh Skaller.

VOTE: 4…..YEAS 0…..NAYS 0…..ABSENTIONS

MOTION APPROVED: UNANIMOUSLY

There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned.
