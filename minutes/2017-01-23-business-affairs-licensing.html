*** MEETING MINUTES***

Presentation and review of a new on-premises liquor license application submitted to the State Liquor Authority on behalf of Big Chief Brooklyn, LLC at 491 3rd Avenue (between 10th/11th Streets).

Backyard will be enclosed and applicants agreed to the closing hours of 12am on Friday and Saturday for the backyard space.

They will have some background DJ and some simple small band – live jazz.

A motion was made to approve by Rachel and seconded by Glenn Kelly.

VOTE: 3…..YEAS 0…..NAYS 0…..ABSTENTIONS

MOTION APPROVED: UNANIMOUSLY

Presentation and review of a new on-premises liquor license application submitted to the State Liquor Authority on behalf of Popina NYC, LLC at 127 Columbia Street (between Kane/Degraw Streets).

This is a neighborhood Italian restaurant. The original space was previously occupied by Pok Pok Pad Thai restaurant.

No Department of Buildings permits required and they agree to the backyard hours of 11-12pm on Friday and Saturday.

Recommendation to approve was made by Rachel Freeman and seconded by Josh Skaller.

VOTE: 4…..YEAS 0…..NAYS 0…..ABSENTIONS

MOTION APPROVED: UNANIMOUSLY

Presentation and review of an alteration to a current on-premises liquor license application submitted to the State Liquor Authority on behalf of Freeks Mill Inc. at 285 Nevins Street (between Union/Sackett Streets).

Presentation and review of an unenclosed sidewalk café permit application submitted to the Department of Consumer Affairs on behalf of Freeks Mill Inc. at 285 Nevins Street (between Union/Sackett Streets) to permit 10 tables and 22 seats.

Applicant is requesting a change to their SLA license to allow a sidewalk café consisting of 10 tables with 22 seats. They will agree to CB6’s outdoor hours for closing of 11-12pm on Friday and Saturday.

Recommendation to approve was made by Glenn Kelly and seconded by Rachel Freeman.

VOTE: 4…..YEAS 0…..NAYS 0…..ABSENTIONS

MOTION APPROVED: UNANIMOUSLY

A motion to approve the minutes of the previous meeting held on January 9, 2017 was made by Glenn Kelly and seconded by Josh Skaller.

VOTE: 4…..YEAS 0…..NAYS 0…..ABSENTIONS

MOTION APPROVED: UNANIMOUSLY

There being no further business, the meeting was adjourned.
