DISTRICT MANAGER’S REPORT………………………………………….…….CRAIG HAMMERMAN

District Manager Craig Hammerman began his report by advising the board that the Carroll Street Bridge over the Gowanus Canal will close to all traffic for at least four months starting April 1st. The bridge, one of five along the Gowanus Canal, will be shuttered to cars, bicyclists, and pedestrians while the city's Department of Transportation repairs it. DOT will revamp the bridge, which was first built in 1889, by replacing its deteriorated timber deck, structural steel, and making other upgrades. DOT is directing motorists and cyclists to use the nearby Union Street Bridge as an alternative. The work will take place from 7 am to 4 pm on weekdays and is expected to be finished in August.

District Manager Hammerman informed the Board that after a two-year rehabilitation project, Smith-9th Street Station will reopen on April 26th, restoring and service to the Red Hook, Gowanus and Carroll Gardens communities. The 79-year-old facility—the world's highest subway station at nearly 88 feet—underwent a ground up renewal over the course of 22 months. The rehabilitation began in May 2011 in conjunction with the rebuilding of the Culver Viaduct.

District Manager Hammerman reported to the Board that following extensive damage caused by Hurricane Sandy, the Red Hook Branch Library re-opened on Tuesday, April 2nd. Joining the re-opening ceremony were Brooklyn Public Library President & CEO Linda E. Johnson, Red Hook community members, Brooklyn Public Library staff and leadership, Borough President Marty Markowitz, Representative Nydia M. Velázquez and Assembly Member Félix Ortiz. When Hurricane Sandy hit New York, the Red Hook Library was covered by 18 inches of water, causing the destruction of roughly 2,500 books and other materials, as well as damaged floors, mechanical equipment, furniture, shelving, and supplies. In total, the library suffered $750,000 worth of damages. Brooklyn Public Library President & CEO Linda E. Johnson also announced that the Red Hook Library will undergo a complete renovation in 2014. Together with the non-profit Spaceworks, they will create

2

two new Spaceworks studios in the library, which will be affordable workspaces available for local performance artists to rent, and collaborate with artists to provide new arts education programming in the branch for patrons.

District Manager Hammerman advised the board that the Department of Transportation along with Brooklyn Borough President Marty Markowitz and the 4th Avenue Task Force will be hosting a Park Slope Open House on April 9th at St Thomas Aquinas Church. At the Open House one would be able to view new design concepts for 4th Avenue between Pacific Street and 15th Street and provide feedback. This open house follows a public workshop in February where community members discussed the future of 4th Avenue. The goals of the project are to reduce speeding, create simpler turns, create safer pedestrian crossings and improve safety for all users of 4th Avenue. DOT will update our Transportation Committee at their May 16th meeting.

District Manager Hammerman informed the board of a new initiative by the 76th Police Precinct, entitled "Spot It To Secure It," in which a team of four police officers will patrol a selected area of the 76th Precinct and look for parked cars where iPhones, iPads, laptops, or wrapped packages are left on the seats. The officers will snap a picture and mail it to the car's owner with a form letter. Precinct Commanding Officer Schiff also said the new task force will also be checking apartment building doors to see if they are locked. If one isn't, the landlord will be getting a letter from the NYPD encouraging him to make sure the door is locked at all time.

District Manager Hammerman reported to the Board that the district office would be hosting an informational meeting on April 30th in an effort to better understand the rules and regulations governing restaurants and bars in our district. Representatives from the State Liquor Authority, City agencies and local precincts will be on hand to answer any questions in respect to noise, smoking, backyard usage, garbage and other related issues.

District Manager Hammerman ended his report by advising the Board that the Governors Island Meeting has been moved from LICH to the Cobble Hill Health center. Ever since the closing of the hospital has been announced we are no longer able to have meetings in the hospital. He also asked if anyone knew of any new locations to have our committee or board meetings please contact the district office.
