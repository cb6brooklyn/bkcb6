AGENDA

Adoption of March Minutes Motion by Alec, seconded by Katie. Unanimously approved (minus Daniel Kummer, who abstained) II. Chair Report Council Member Hanif addressed the community, emphasizing the importance of collective action in response to harmful agendas. She discussed recent events, including a town hall and a drag queen story hour, and announced upcoming training sessions to support community members facing harassment. Hanif also introduced legislation to protect transgender rights and simplify access to IDNYC.

III. DM Report Discussed upcoming including a presentation from Open Plans New York and a discussion with Womankind NYC about gender-based violence. Additionally, Speaker 5 mentioned the delay of the Carroll Gardens Library project and efforts to utilize a vacant city building for interim library services. The speaker emphasized the importance of addressing housing needs in the community. IV. Committee Meetings
