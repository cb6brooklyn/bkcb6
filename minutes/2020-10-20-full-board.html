CHAIRPERSON’S REPORT…………………………………………...PETER FLEMING, BOARD CHAIR

Board Chair Peter Fleming welcomed attendees and board members to the first of an indefinite number of virtual full board meetings. Mr. Fleming highlighted the work that the Board has done in the time since the COVID-19 pandemic hit the city, noting that none of it required a vote to date until the items on tonight’s agenda – a range of items from public schools to the Gowanus Rezoning were all addressed in various publicly distributed letters and in various committee meetings.

1

Mr. Fleming announced to the Board that communications between the district office and the Department of City Planning outlined an expected certification for the proposed Gowanus Rezoning in early January. The Chair outlined a number of meetings coming up at which DCP would present more on the timeframe of the rezoning.

The Chair finished his comments by welcoming the new Board members to the Board and acknowledging the passing of several members of the CB6 community and community at-large in the time since the Board last met. He then introduced the Board’s new elections chair, Jason Reischel, who outlined the pending Board elections.

DISTRICT MANAGER’S REPORT……………………..MICHAEL RACIOPPO, DISTRICT MANAGER

Mr. Racioppo made brief comments, also welcoming the Board back from the hiatus. The District Manager then outlined some changes to various committees and committee chairpersons. Mr. Racioppo also gave brief comments relative to a truck traffic study for Red Hook and offered information on the upcoming elections.
