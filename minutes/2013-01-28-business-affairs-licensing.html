***MINUTES***

Presentation and discussion with representatives from the Department of

Environmental Protection on the agency’s response to Hurricane Sandy.

Julie Stein from DEP made the presentation on the impact of Hurricane Sandy. She provided a handout giving post-Hurricane Sandy updates. • $95 million dollars of damage • Some sewage discharged into the canal • Testing regularly since the storm • Pumping station off line for 33 hours on October 30 to October 31 due to loss of power and flooding damage. • Construction is 61% complete for the pumping station and flushing tunnel • Hurricane Sandy resulted in an additional 3-month delay. • Anticipated project completion is Spring 2014.

Q&A:

Mark Shames asked if 2014 is realistic? A: Yes

Glenn Kelly – would there have been CSO discharge? A: Yes

Glenn Kelly was there damage to CFC equipment – loss of utility power. A: No

Bette Stoltz – does rendering show the dykes and dams? A: No

Bette asked how high was flood? A: 18 inches.

Matt Silverman – Cost of putting oxygen transfer station online? A: Still evaluating

The committee chair, Gary Reilly concluded the presentation.
