LANDMARKS/LAND USE COMMITTEE MEETING

AUGUST 03, 2017

Attendance:

J. Armer P B. Fuller-Goggins A M. Racioppo P P. Basile A V. Hagman A A. Reeves A P. Blake E G. Kelly E D. Scotto P D. Briggs E Y. Lee E M. Shames A G. Faeth A R. Levine P R. Sloane P P. Fleming E T. Miskel E R. Freeman E M. Murphy P

P = present, E = excused, A = absent

Guests: K. Hedstrom P. Kwalwasser

Meeting was called to order by Robert Levine.

Presentation and review of a Certificate of Appropriateness application submitted to the Landmarks Preservation Commission for the installation of a rooftop addition at 536 1st Street (between 8th Avenue/Prospect Park West), Park Slope Historic District.

Motion was made by Jerry Armer and seconded by Madelaine Murphy

MOTION: To table this Certificate of Appropriateness application for 536 1st Street until the August 24th meeting of this committee so the applicant can properly notify the neighbors as outlined on the Brooklyn Community Board 6 website under. “Resources,” Landmarks Applicants’.”

Also, either supply the Committee with drawings or photos of mock-ups of the heights of the neighbors chimneys that will need to be raised because of the rooftop addition

VOTE: Yea: 6 Nay: 0 Abst: 0

Presentation and review of an application submitted to the Landmarks Preservation Commission for the legalization of a stoop and window combination at 314 Clinton Street (between Kane/Baltic Streets), Cobble Hill Historic District.

Motion was made by Jerry Armer and seconded by Debra Scotto

Motion: To approve the legalization of a stoop and window combination at 314 Clinton Street as Mixed Fenestration.

VOTE: Yea: 6 Nay: 0 Abst: 0

The minutes were submitted by Madelaine Murphy.
