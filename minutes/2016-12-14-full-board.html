CHAIRPERSON’S REPORT………………………………………….………………….....SAYAR LONIAL

Chairperson Sayar Lonial opened his report by thanking the Executive Committee for continuing to provide stalwart leadership for the Board. The Chair then thanked the entire board for ratifying the re-election of the existing officers and for all they have done in the past calendar year and challenged them to continue to accept their responsibilities in the coming year.

Mr. Lonial thanked Board Member Glenn Kelly for attending the Borough President’s meeting with the Parks Department on December 13th, a meeting that focused on Parks Department Capital projects across the borough. The Chair also thanked the Landmarks/Land Use committee for holding an informational meeting regarding the Thor Equities project, commonly referred to as “Red Hoek Point”, on December 1st. The meeting brought up Thor’s interest in a parking requirement waiver, which will be discussed at the December 22nd joint public hearing held by the Landmarks/Land Use and Transportation committees.

Chairperson Lonial also announced that the Transportation/Public Safety committee will tomorrow evening be hearing a presentation by the Department of Transportation regarding a proposal for a new bicycle lane on 7th Avenue between Flatbush and 15th Street, as well as some enhancements for the intersection of Court, Amity, and Dean Streets in Cobble Hill.

The Chair closed his remarks by reminding attendees that the Friends of Brooklyn CB6 Holiday Party will be held Monday, December 19th at Loki Lounge on 2nd Street off 5th Avenue in Park Slope.

DISTRICT MANAGER’S REPORT………………………………………….…….CRAIG HAMMERMAN

District Manager Craig Hammerman opened his report by stating that he and Assistant District Manager Ty Beatty recently met with the new community relations liaison for NYU Langone Cobble Hill Medical Center to discuss some of the community’s concerns with on-going construction and the need for pro-active maintenance on site. They’ve been invited to come back to the Youth/Human Services/Education committee to offer an official update to their progress for the board.

Mr. Hammerman announced that the Department of Sanitation’s BK6 garage has put Sector 61 (Red Hook and the southern part of Carroll Gardens) on night collection for recycling. Night-time collections happen each year November through April so that there is manpower available around the clock should they be needed for emergency snow removal purposes. The District Manager reminded board members and meeting attendees that this is a procedural formality for the garage and that the impacted sector is rotated every year so that no neighborhood has to deal with night collection, which can be noisy, for multiple years consecutively.

2

The District Manager closed his remarks by announcing that the New York State Department of Environmental Conservation has notified us of an application by ConEdison for a Brownfield Clean-up Program for their 3rd Avenue yard site. The application has been submitted and is available online. The public can review the application and submit comment until January 17th.
