***MINUTES***

The meeting was called to order at 6:35pm.commenced at 6:40 pm.

Presentation and discussion with representatives for Council Member Brad Lander on “Next Bus Please: Improving the B61 Bus,” a report that assesses the level and quality of bus service along the B61 line.

The Co-chair, Steve Burzio introduced Councilman Lander who explained the study process and introduced the team from his office who conducted the study – Michael Freedman-Schnapp and Matt Green. Data was collected by volunteers on over 700 bus runs.

Four bus lines serving Community Board 6 have been eliminated since 2005. Only two bus lines remain with only one serving Red Hook.

Findings:  7% of all B61 buses arrive outside of acceptable headway times.  B61 buses arrive late and experience “bunching” in the A.M. and P.M. hours.  B61 buses carry many more passengers than the loading guidelines prescribed by the

MTA.

 B61 buses bypass stops when over-crowded. 42% of buses bypassed stops between 8 A.M. and 9 A.M. 23% bypassed stops between 5 P.M. and 6 P.M.  Closure of Smith/9th Street subway station has caused hardship for passengers.

Recommendations:  Increase service during peak ridership hours  B61 should be included in MTA “Bus Time” programs as soon as possible.  Stop improvements at Smith/9th Street stop to include a new bus shelter, countdown clock, lighting, street trees, planters and bike share.  Route improvements - Extend B57 line into Red Hook - B61/B103 transfer - Improve B61 route across the BQE

 Operational improvements: - Measure impact of the operation of the 9th Street lift bridge - Alter route of Park Slope bound B61 to provide a more direct route across the BQE - Eliminate stops where excessively close together.

Committee comments:  More enforcement by Police to keep traffic moving on Van Brunt and Columbia Streets.  Has off-board fare collection been considered?  Lack of loading zones contributes to delays.  Lack of investment in bus transit is primary issue.  School bus garages in Red Hook significantly contribute to congestion, resulting in delays.  State funding level is a primary issue.

Actions by Council Member Lander’s office:  Lander’s office will meet with the MTA  Presented report at MTA Board meeting.  Met with bus drivers union.  Publicizing issue.

A motion by Eric McClure to endorse the recommendations presented in the report and to urge any agencies involved to implement the recommendations was seconded by Marilyn Pettit.

MOTION PASSED: UNANIMOUSLY

A motion to approve to approve the minutes of the previous meeting held on November 17, 2011 was made by Andrea McKnight and seconded by Roger Rigolli.

A motion to adjourn was made by Gary Reilly and seconded by Eric McClure.

MOTION PASSED: UNANIMOUSLY
