CHAIRPERSON’S REPORT…………………………………………......SAYAR LONIAL, BOARD CHAIR

Chairperson Lonial opened his remarks by thanking committee chairs and those who took minutes for getting them into the district office on time. This allowed time for Assistant District Manager Ty Beatty to more thoroughly review motions with committee chairs, which led to a more complete and accurate general board agenda.

Mr. Lonial again thanked WNET and Council Member Lander for their work in allowing the meeting to be live- streamed.

The Chair reminded attendees of the Board’s Holiday Get Together to be held on December 18th at Royal Palsm Shuffle Board Club in Gowanus. There is no ticketing process and attendance is free. He asked that anyone interested in attending contact the district office to RSVP.

Chairperson Lonial closed his report by informing the board of a planned Community Advisory Board that will be convened by BKCB-6. The CAB will work with city agencies, local elected, and the Cobble Hill Association to help remediate any issues relevant to the various individual projects being developed by Fortis on the former LICH site.

YOUTH, HUMAN SERVICES & EDUCATION COMMITTEE…………SUZANNE TURET, CO-CHAIR

Education Committee Chair Suzanne Turet reported that the committee reviewed a proposal by the School Construction Authority for site selection and acquisition of 21-31 & 35 Delevan Street (Block 523, Lots 1 & 13R) for a new, approximately 650-seat intermediate school.

Motion: By Board Member Jerry Armer; seconded by Board Member Peter Fleming Conditionally approve the site selected by the School Construction Authority at 21-31 & 35 Delevan Street (Block 523, Lots 1 & 13R) for a new, approximately 650-seat intermediate school.

Conversation ensued relative to the safety of the area of the school, specifically that neither the Department of Transportation nor the NYPD can guarantee safe passage as this proposed school is to be a middle school and there is no bussing.

Some board members stated their opposition to the premise on these terms and wished for a stronger position on the matter.

VOTE: 22 YEAS, 4 NAYS, 1 ABSTENTION

MOTION PASSED

TIME: 7:56 P.M.

Chairperson Sayar Lonial recognized Board Member David Briggs, who along with Mr. Lonial and other Board Members had been communicating with Council Member Stephen T. Levin on the matter of the out-standing NYC Department of Environmental Protection’s Uniform Land Use Review application C180065 PCK, site selection and acquisition of properties located at 242 Nevins Street, 234 Butler Street, and 270 Nevins Street for a combined sewer overflow control facility at the northern edge of the Gowanus Canal, commonly referred to as the Gowanus CSO Head House.

Mr. Briggs outlined the issue at hand, which is an extension of the Board’s previous conditional approval of the ULURP, and made a motion that the Board request Council Member Levin’s approval of the ULURP be contingent upon a commitment from DEP to a collaborative design process that would include input from the Community Board and require the Board’s review and approval.

Motion: By Board Member David Briggs; seconded by Board Member Jerry Armer A request to Council Member Stephen T. Levin that any approval from him relevant to NYC Department of Environmental Protection’s Uniform Land Use Review application C180065 PCK by contingent upon a commitment from NYC DEP to a collaborative design process that would include input from the Community Board and require the Board’s approval.

VOTE: 22 YEAS, 1 NAY, 2 ABSTENTIONS

MOTION PASSED
