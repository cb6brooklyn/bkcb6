❖❖❖COMMITTEE REPORTS ❖❖❖

• Permits and Licenses Committee Committee chair Rebecca Kobert presented 3 motions for new on premises liquor licenses. All three were approved. The locations and applicants names are as follows. • Motion to approve an application for an on-premises liquor license for Roy Saubai Inc (DBA Thai Town New York) at 204 Smith Street, Brooklyn, NY 11231. • Motion to approve an application for an on-premises liquor license for Alex & Ari Inc (DBA Stellas's) at 321 9th Street, Brooklyn, NY 11215

• Landmarks Committee Committee chair David Briggs presented 2 motions for certificate appropriateness applications. Both motions were approved- one motion was to approve an application and the other was to disapprove. • Motion to approve an application to the Landmarks Preservation Commission to obtain a Certificate of Appropriateness to allow for a rear yard and roof extension at 30 Tompkins Place, Brooklyn, NY 11201 - Cobble Hill Historic District. Condition are as follows: Applicant reduces area of fenestration at roof top enlargement • Motion to disapprove an application to the Landmarks Preservation Commission to obtain a Certificate of Appropriateness to allow for mechanical equipment to be added to the roof along with a roof terrace and a rear door at the garden facade at 17 Prospect Park West, Brooklyn, NY 11215- Park Slope Historic District.
