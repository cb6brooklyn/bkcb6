-Motion unanimously approved

Environmental Protection Committee: Representatives from NYC Department of Environmental Protection and S.W.I.M. Coalition presented to the committee regarding NYC Green Infrastructure Plan. NYC DEP will hold a public meeting at Pace University on February 2, 2011 at 6:30pm.

Margo Walker, of NYC DEP presented an overview of the NYC Green Infrastructure plan. She stated the goal of the plan was to address the following.  Manage storm water on 10% of impervious (solid) surface  Create standard specifications for all new construction –buildings and roadways  Encourage greater communication between city and state agencies  Establishment of a Green Infrastructure Task Force Program will support the stated goals by supplementing capital development funds of the partnering agencies

M. Walker stated DEP is very interested in supporting existing infrastructure, like community gardens, that support achieving the goal of storm water management. If you have further questions, please contact Margo by email. margotw@dep.nyc.gov

Kate Zidar, of S.W.I.M.- Stormwater Infrastructure Matters introduced the coalition to the subcommittee. For further information on the organization please go to www.swimmablenyc.info She presented further information on the citywide plan to address stormwater retention issues for New York City.  S.W.I.M. request for support of the following issues:  A plan that meets established Federal EPA guideline outlined in the Clean Water Act  Addresses existing and new construction buildings  Focus within the plan on creation of “green jobs”  Promotion of better interagency collaboration  Equitable distribution of resources
