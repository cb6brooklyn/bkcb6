CHAIRPERSON’S REPORT………………………………………….………………….....SAYAR LONIAL

Chairperson Sayar Lonial opened his report by wishing attendees a Happy New Year. Mr. Lonial then reported that he and District Manager recently met with Department of Transportation Brooklyn Borough Commissioner Keith Bray regarding a number of issues relevant to holistic planning for the streetscape in the district. The Chair stated that he found thought the meeting provided some hope in the realm of bettering communications between the Board and DOT. Mr. Lonial also reported that he was sure to communicate the board’s displeasure with the way DOT had responded to our invitation to participate at our CitiBike hearing.

Mr. Lonial closed his brief remarks by congratulating Board Member Paige Bellenbaum on the impending opening of The Motherhood Center, a new service provider that will assist new and expecting mothers in numerous areas relative to their health and that of their children. The Chair pointed out that this is a significant career achievement for Ms. Bellenbaum, and on behalf the board wished she and her colleagues the best of luck in the endeavor.

DISTRICT MANAGER’S REPORT………………………………………….…….CRAIG HAMMERMAN

District Manager Craig Hammerman offered a short update about the Hugh L. Carey Tunnel (formerly Brooklyn-Battery Tunnel) implementation of a new toll-less entry process, similar to the Henry Hudson Bridge. The tollbooths are scheduled for removal on January 11th and 12th. The District Manager reminded the assemblage that this Community Board fought for the restoration of the two-way toll on the Verrazano Bridge to reverse the regional effect of the morning rush hour on the district and that a toll-less Verrazano creates a new opportunity for the matter to be revisited.
