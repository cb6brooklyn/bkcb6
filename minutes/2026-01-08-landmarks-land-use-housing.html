*Land Use*

46 Nelson Street Rezoning II

Presented by Richard Lobel and architect. New mixed-use district with MIH proposed in lieu of

existing manufacturing district. Total of 96 units with 10 Studios, 56 1-BRs, 18 2-BRs, and 12 3-BRs.

Several questions asked:

- Were neighbors notified? Yes

- How was unit mix determined? Market analysis of highest return

- Will building appearance that is presented be adhered to? That is the intent

- Can there be a setback on the upper floors to mitigate the tall facade? Yes, there is flexibility within

the proposed zoning

- How long has it taken them from project kick-off to reach this point in ULURP? Three years

- What is the mix of affordable units? Same as market rate units.

Jerry: Motion to approve with the following conditions:

1. Applicant agrees that building will be constructed as currently designed.

2. Reduce the number of studios and add more 2 and 3-BRs.

Peter: Seconded.

Nine votes for.

One abstention for cause.

Motion passes.

End of meeting.
