CHAIRPERSON’S REPORT…………………………………………...PETER FLEMING, BOARD CHAIR

Board Chair Peter Fleming opened the meeting with brief remarks, informing attendees and board members of the effects of the covid-19 booster shot he received, thanked Board Member Ariel Krasnow and District Manager Mike Racioppo for attending a press conference with Council Members Lander and Levin regarding the Gowanus Rezoning, and briefly outlined the committee formation that will be taking place over the next few months to monitor the Rezoning.

The Chair then recognized District Manager Mike Racioppo, who outlined the Board’s officer election process in lieu of Election Chair Jason Reischel, who was unable to attend the zoom. Mr. Racioppo stated that the only nominations received were for the existing slate of officers. The Board Chair then opened the floor for nominations and none were received. As such, the existing slate of officers remains in place.
