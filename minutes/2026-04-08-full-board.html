B. The Transportation, Parks & Public Infrastructure Resolution to Shift Traffic Enforcement Responsibilities to NYCDOT Tony Melone presented the following resolution: BKCB6 supports returning parking enforcement to the NYC Department of Transportation where it was before 1996, so that it can get the attention it needs and allow NYPD to focus on crime reduction. BKCB6 asks our elected officials to support this work through legislation that would make it a possibility. The motion was proposed by Jerry Armer and passed unanimously at the most recent committee meeting, with quorum. It has also been recommended as part of the board’s District Needs assessment. Laura Osorio asked whether NYCDOT has a position on this transfer of responsibility, and Jerry reiterated that a decision like this lies with elected officials and not with the agencies responsible for implementation. Tony explained that one advantage of this change would be that NYCDOT has additional tools to resolve parking issues not at NYPD’s disposal, such as installing granite blocks in no parking zones. Reema noted that Manhattan Community Board 6 has passed a similar resolution inspired by ours. The motion passed unanimously, with Pauline Blake abstaining and Leroy Branch abstaining for cause.

V.New/Other Business Special Presentation from MTA and NYCDOT on Flatbush Av Bus Priority Project (State Street to Grand Army Plaza)

Luke Sabados (DOT) and Deeka Michael (MTA) explained several upgrades coming to Flatbush Avenue this year as part of a center-running bus lane installation. Other project features will include curb regulation changes, painted pedestrian spaces, bike parking, shortened crossing distances, bus boarding islands, a slight reroute of the B63 bus, and changes along several of the immediate side streets off of Flatbush Avenue (including a direction reversal on Pacific Street). This project is a continuation of work completed last fall along Flatbush Avenue from Livingston Street to State Street.

In response to a question about speed reducers and dollar van use of the bus lane, Luke explained that speed reducers are not possible on this segment because it is a designated truck route. Regarding the rerouting of the B63 bus onto Bergen and Dean Streets, Tony asked whether DOT has plans to limit trucks on Bergen Street, which is currently mapped as a truck route. MTA confirmed that although Dean and Bergen are narrow streets, the bike lanes there help keep traffic moving and because they already serve one bus route, adding a second bus should have limited impacts. Eric suggested that the board request that the B63 be designated as an ACE (automated camera enforcement) route to help with double parking enforcement on these streets. Katie Bishop asked whether Flatbush Avenue would be resurfaced as part of this work, and Luke explained that it has recently been resurfaced, so only impacted areas would be redone.
