CHAIRPERSON’S REPORT…………………………………………...PETER FLEMING, BOARD CHAIR

Board Chair Peter Fleming offered only brief remarks, including an outline of the upcoming Board election process. The Chair also announced that the upcoming Permits & Licenses committee meeting would begin at 6pm rather than the typical 6:30pm due a large number of applicants to consider. The Chair closed his remarks by announcing that he would not be in attendance at the November general board meeting and that 1st Vice Chair Eric McClure would serve as chair for that meeting.

DISTRICT MANAGER’S REPORT……………………..MICHAEL RACIOPPO, DISTRICT MANAGER

Mr. Racioppo made brief comments, including an invitation for anyone in attendance who might wish to receive his daily emails and information about voter registration as we head toward Election Day.

 COMMITTEE REPORTS 

ECONOMICS/WATERFRONT/COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT/HOUSING COMMITTEE

…………………...MARK SHAMES, LAND USE CHAIR

Board Member Mark Shames presented on behalf of the EWCDH committee’s review of a consideration relevant to portions of Bond Street and 4th Street as they are represented in the NYC Department of City Planning’s draft rezoning of Gowanus.

Motion: To support longtime Gowanus businesses in attempts to remain viable in the neighborhood after the pending rezoning, Brooklyn Community Board 6 requests that the Department of City Planning make the following changes to the Draft Rezoning on the condition that the properties listed remain in their current use group: (1) To not de-map the end of Bond Street where it meets the canal to maintain full vehicle traffic; (2) To exempt 98 4th Street & 413 Bond Street from waterfront access requirements; (3) to exempt 413 Bond Street from the required visual corridor between 3rd Street and the Gowanus Canal.

VOTE: 30 YEAS, 0 NAYS, 1 ABSTENTION

MOTION PASSED

TIME: 6:48 P.M.

LANDMARKS & LAND USE COMMITTEE………….……………………...DAVID BRIGGS, CO-CHAIR

…………………………….MARK SHAMES, CO-CHAIR

2

Land Use Chair Mark Shames reported that the committee considered a request by the Old American Can Factory to re-evaluate the zoning envelope of their properties as they are represented in the NYC Department of City Planning’s draft rezoning of Gowanus.

Motion: To support the Old American Can Factory’s requested that the zoning envelope for the properties be re-evaluated as a part of the Gowanus rezoning and within the DEIS without increasing the proposed FAR in light of the imminent landmark designation the properties and in order to maintain affordable arts spaces within the Gowanus Mix.

VOTE: 32 YEAS, 0 NAYS, 0 ABSTENTIONS

MOTION PASSED UNANIMOUSLY

TIME: 6:58 P.M.

PARKS/RECREATION/ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION COMMITTEE....GLENN KELLY, CHAIR

Park committee chair Glenn Kelly reported that the committee had previously considered the proposed design of Henry Park III in Cobble Hill.

Motion: To support the proposed design of Henry Park III in Cobble Hill.

VOTE: 32 YEAS, 0 NAYS, 0 ABSTENTIONS

MOTION PASSED UNANIMOUSLY

TIME: 7:05 P.M.

TRANSPORTATION/PUBLIC SAFETY COMMITTEE…….....……………….ERIC MCCLURE, CHAIR

Transportation committee chair Eric McClure reported that the committee considered a proposal to consider FAR bonus incentives for transportation-related improved in the Gowanus corridor in conjunction with the Gowanus Neighborhood Rezoning.

Conversation ensued relative to the on-going process of the Gowanus Rezoning, including a debate over the value of declaring such support prior to actually formally reviewing the proposed rezoning. As such, a motion to table was accepted and a vote to table to motion was taken.

VOTE: 35 YEAS, 0 NAYS, 1 ABSTENTION

MOTION TABLED

TIME: 7:26 P.M.
