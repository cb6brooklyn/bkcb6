Land Use

● City of Yes

○ Emily Bachman from DCP joined by Katherine and Maria (transpo

planner)

○ Third and final (Carbon neutrality, economic opportunity,

housing), citywide text amendment to change zoning to enable a

little more housing in every neighborhood

○ Committee needs to vote on whether we support the proposal

○ Severe housing shortage (1.27% vacancy rate in BK rentals), over

52% are rent burdened

○ Existing zoning tools: voluntary inclusionary housing (4th ave and

365 Bond), MIH in Gowanus rezone

○ Majority of residential zoning in CD6 is medium density

○ TOD proposal: 5000sqft lot, located on short end of block or wide

street, within 0.5mile of subway, impacts most of CD6

○ ADUs: basement ADUs not allowed in coastal floodplain (shown in

CD6 covering much of Red Hook and Gowanus around the canal)

○ UAP :allows buildings to add 20% more housing if it is permanently

affordable or supportive housing

■ Affordability requirement of 60% AMI

■ Income averaging: replace VIH for deeper levels of

affordability, no band exceeding 100% AMI

■ MIH in place would remain: Gowanus Neighborhood Plan

■ Historic district regulations and LPC procedures remain in

place

■ Limited height district in Cobble Hill historic district:

proposal would … (get clarification)

■ MIH Option 3 (Deeply Affordable) would be standalone option

without needing to opt into 1 and 2 (currently the rule)

■ Streamline rules for 100% affordable projects

○ Remove parking mandates: parking optional for new developments

throughout the city

■ Parking spaces take up space and is very expensive, obstacle

to affordable housing due to price and FAR limitations

● Does not change on street parking regulations at all,

not impose new parking maximums, wouldn’t

automatically remove any existing parking

● Underbuilding has been an issue due to automatic unit

triggering that is proposing to be removed

○ Conversions:

■ Adaptive reuse regulations expanded, vacant office building

to be converted to housing, also vacant schools/religious

buildings

■ Move eligibility requirement from current 1970s construction

date to 1992 to qualify for adaptive reuse

○ Landmark TDR program
