Full Board Meeting 5/14/25

14 May 2025/ 6:30 PM / Van Alen + Zoom

AGENDA

Adoption of Minutes The minutes of the April meeting were adopted. II. Chair Report Chairperson Eric McClure welcomed newly appointed members of Brooklyn Community Board 6. He then nominated David Briggs to fill the vacancy left by Angelica Ramdhari. III. DM Report District manager Mike Racioppo provided updates on district matters. He congratulated CB6 Land Use Coordinator Rebecca Kobert on completing her thesis and thanked her for the opportunity to reference it in a recent op-ed he authored in City Limits. He also gave a run down of upcoming meetings and events relevant to the board and community. IV. Committee Meetings Business Affairs & Housing Committee Committee chair Mandi Spishak Thamos presented the committee recommendations to approve the following on-premise liquor license applications: A French restaurant at 226 7th Avenue A Jewish American Tavern at 524 Court Street A Mexican seafood restaurant at 332 5th Avenue All Three applications were approved unanimously by the Full board. V. Adjournment There being no further Business the meeting was adjourned.
