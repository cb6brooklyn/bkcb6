*** MINUTES ***

Presentation and review of a Certificate of Appropriateness application submitted to the Landmarks Preservation Commission for the installation of banners and interpretative signage at Congregation Beth Elohim, 274 Garfield Place (southeast corner 8th Avenue/Garfield Place), Park Slope Historic District.

The application was approved as presented as long as Congregation Beth Elohim agrees to repair any damage that may be caused when the signage is removed.
