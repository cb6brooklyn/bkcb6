***MINUTES***

Presentation and review of a proposal by the Gowanus Canal Conservancy and Alloy to install temporary planted dumpsters with a seating component at 6 locations in the Gowanus Canal corridor from July through November, under the Department of Transportation’s Street Seats program.

Members of the staff of Alloy Real Estate Development gave the Committee a brief history of their company and how they try to fit in and work with the community that they are coming into.

Alloy Real Estate Development, the Gowanus Canal Conservancy and the Department of Transportation Street Seats program then proceeded to explain their idea for a summer program to enhance the Gowanus Canal corridor this summer.

Motion was made by Jerry Armer and seconded by Madelaine Murphy: To accept as presented the proposal by the Alloy R. E. Development and the Gowanus Canal Conservancy to install and maintain temporary planted dumpsters with a seating component at 6 locations in the Gowanus Canal corridor from July through November, under the Department of Transportation’s Street Seats program.

VOTE: Unanimous in favor of the proposal as presented.

Motion was made by Jerry Armer and seconded by Madelaine Murphy: To accept the Minutes for last month’s meeting held on May 19, 2016.

VOTE: Unanimous to Accept the Minutes as presented.
