TRANSPORTATION & PUBLIC SAFETY………….………………………ERIC MCCLURE, CO-CHAIR

……………………………….TOM MISKEL, CO-CHAIR

Transportation co-chair Eric McClure reported that the committee reviewed an application for installation of a NYC DOT Street Seat at 258 7th Avenue.

Some discussion ensued between Board Members who believe one- or two-fewer parking spots is a significant loss to the community and others who believe that a seated area would do well to serve the community, particularly those who work at New York Presbyterian Brooklyn Methodist Hospital.

Ultimately, the debate resulted in a roll call vote.

Motion: Approval of installation of a NYC DOT Street Seat at 250 7th Avenue.

VOTE: 16 YEAS, 13 NAYS, 0 ABSTENTIONS

MOTION PASSED

TIME: 7:16 P.M.

…………………………...JASON REICHEL, CO-CHAIR

…………………………..SUZANNE TURET, CO-CHAIR

Youth, Human Services, and Education co-chair Suzanne Turet reported that the committee considered a motion to urge the NYC Department of Education to hire a new head of the Office of School Nutrition Services.

Motion: To urge the NYC Department of Education to hire a new head of the Office of School Nutrition Services and that the person who they hire has deep knowledge and interest in changing the way in which school children are fed.
