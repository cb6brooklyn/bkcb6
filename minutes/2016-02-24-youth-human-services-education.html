***MINUTES***

Presentation and discussion with representatives for the Veterans Access to Benefits Project, a project of non-profit firm of New York Legal Assistance Group, which educates and assists veterans about the benefits that are available to them.

Rachel Levine of New York Legal Assistance Group made the presentation. The group is non-profit and is involved with the LGBT community, families, housing and veterans.

They assist veterans who are homeless, mentally ill and the jobless. Veterans are not aware of these services. Applying is difficult educationally and the process of assisting veterans to apply.

Veteran Administration benefits:  If a veteran is wounded in the line of service, an attorney can intervene after the veteran applies.  Info sessions  Operates in 5 boroughs  Discharges upgrades  PTSD narrative change  Civil law
