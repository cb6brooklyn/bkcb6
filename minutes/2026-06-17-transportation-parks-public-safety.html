BROOKLYN COMMUNITY BOARD 6

TRANSPORTATION, PARKS & PUBLIC INFRASTRUCTURE COMMITTEE

June 17, 2026 @ Principles Coffee House & Zoom

6:30 p.m.

MINUTES

Council Member Shahana Hanif

- The committee updated CM Hanif on CB6 priorities: a Low Traffic Neighborhood working group for Gowanus, safety improvements for 8th Avenue, completing the protected bike lane network, Bergen Street (trucks, rerouted buses, safety, noise and pollution) and Columbia Waterfront street safety.

- CM Hanif expressed support for low traffic neighborhoods and completing the protected bike network, and emphasized universal daylighting and more public plazas.

- Gowanus LTN: the working group's walk, with focus on pedestrianizing the Carroll Street Bridge and diagonal diverters on streets like Hoyt Street.

- 8th Avenue: recommendations on signal timing, painted curb extensions, additional daylighting, and speed and red light camera enforcement, with support from the St. John's Community Association.

DOT: 5th Avenue Curb Buildouts

- DOT presented plans for the 5th Avenue corridor (1.87 miles, 40 intersections, two council districts): curb extensions with bike corrals at nearly every intersection, daylighting, and parking regulation changes to increase commercial loading turnover.

- Resolution passed: CB6 supports plans to add daylighting, curb extensions, loading zones, traffic calming, expanding bike parking, ebike battery exchange stations and other safety improvements. We also urge DOT to add red light cameras in this area, and implement improved signaling for Barnes Dance locations. We ask DOT to present a more detailed plan when the board is back in session in the fall, including more intersections and information on parking, in a larger forum so that the community is informed.
