CHAIRPERSON’S REPORT………………………………………….………………….....SAYAR LONIAL

Chairperson Lonial opened his report by offering an update on the status of the Finance, Personnel, and Law committee’s consideration of the District Manager. Mr. Lonial reported that the committee was considering what, if any, action should be taken while working to ensure that the day to day operations of the Board continue. Mr. Lonial reiterated that any action taken by the FPL committee would require a full board vote. The Chair expressed his appreciation for the District Manager’s request for a leave of absence. Mr. Lonial further acknowledged his confidence in the board office, and thanked Assistant District Manager Ty Beatty, Special Project Manager Sarah Phillips, and Office Manager Liza Santiago-Sierra for their efforts. The Chair commented that board members would need to help the district office by tightening up committee motions, letters, resolutions, and recommendations – something he has been stressing for several months now.

The Chair made a request that board members make a greater effort to attend committee meetings, noting that the work of the board is done at the committee level, and attendance of these meetings was critical not just for those committees to properly function, but for the board to function as a whole.

Mr. Lonial closed his comments by offering his congratulations to anyone who has a friend, coworker, or loved one graduating in the coming weeks, noting that graduation season is a beautiful time of year for reflecting on the work people do. He further wished an early Happy Mother’s Day to everyone, noting that without mothers, none of us would be here or would have achieved what we have.

Vice Chairperson Mike Racioppo recognized representatives of elected officials who were in attendance.
