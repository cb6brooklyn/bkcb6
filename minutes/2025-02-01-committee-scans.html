27 Strong Place (Cobble Hill Historic District) - Review of a Certificate of Appropriateness application to replace front door and non-historic stoop railing, construction of a roof top addition, and reconstruction of a rear yard extension

Presentation made by Cas Stachelberg of Higgins Quasebarth,

Questions/Comments from committee:

1. Where are photos of mock-up?

2. Does the project confonn to the requirements of the Limited Height District?

3. Disposition of fire escapes.

4. How far is the roof top addition set back?

5. How far is the referenced roof top addition at 31 Strong Place setback?

6. Could HVAC unit on roof of roof top addition be smaller or screened? Applicant will consider.

Questions/comments from the community:

1. Construction at 31 Strong Place was highly disruptive to the neighbors. The committee requested that the architect, owner, and contractor meet with neighbors prior to construction. They agreed.

2. Questions regarding roof top addition setback and if it actually matches 31 Strong Place. Applicant confirmed that it was set further back.

3. Neighbor at 25 Strong Place has solar panels that will be partially cast in shadow by roof top addition. Owner has offered to pay for relocation. Neighbor claims this will help, but productivity of panels will still be reduced.

4. Same neighbor stated that construction will reduce daylight into skylight and Dining Room.

5. Neighbor at 23 Strong Place pointed out that rear yard extension is deeper than other extensions and asked if it could be shallower.

6. Other neighbors asked if roof top addition could be lowered in height.

Jerry - motion to recommend conditional approval of project if rear yard extension matches depth of original extension and if HVAC units could be smaller or screened.

Peter - seconded.

Six votes for approval.

None against.

One abstention.

Motion passes.

Note: Number of residential units reduced from four to one.
