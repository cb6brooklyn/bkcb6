4.​ Consider a resolution in support of Intro 1380 prohibiting the sale of Class 3 e-bikes

with a top speed of over 20 mph.

a.​ Proposed resolution text:​

CB6 supports Intro 1380, the Ride Safe, Ride Right bill sponsored by

Council Members Crystal Hudson, Alexa Avilés, and others. This bill

prohibits the sale or rental of e-bikes with a top speed of over 20 mph. We

also encourage the Mayor's office to return to a speed limit of 20 mph for

e-bikes in NYC, to establish one clear standard for the legal purchase and

operation of micromobility vehicles in NYC. We reiterate our support for

Sammy’s Law.

b.​ Discussion:

i.​ Is there a way to switch the language from e-bike to “throttle-powered”?

1.​ This would be supporting the law, so it would need to match

whatever language the city council used.

ii.​ Community boards are advisory, it’s positive for the council and

councilmembers who support this law to have community support around

this.

iii.​ Can make a motion to append onto this if we feel we need to make a

change.

iv.​ There are already laws that exist that prohibit certain speed limits for

e-bikes, but there is no enforcement as it is.

1.​ There are fines built into the law re: enforcement.

v.​ How quickly can e-bikes get to 20 mph?

vi.​ Does the law define what an e-bike is?

1.​ The law says it would prohibit class 3 bicycles…

vii.​ Very confusing without a standard that matches the bike speed that you

purchased, which is why 20 mph is an important marker.

viii.​Speed and size of vehicles contribute to injuries, citywide people have

made peace with losing hundreds of people a year by cars, but we can do

better than this. The data proves that hundreds of people in New York

City are injured or killed every year. Speed limits writ large should be

lowered, especially for cars.

ix.​ Add an addendum to the resolution that reminds the council that CB6

supports Sammy’s Law, limiting the speed of cars in addition to e-bikes.

x.​ ?? Motion. Dillon/Madelaine second. Leroy abstained for cause. Motion

carries unanimously.
