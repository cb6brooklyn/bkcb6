CHAIRPERSON’S REPORT………………………………………….………………….....SAYAR LONIAL

Chairperson Sayar Lonial opened his report by commenting on the results from the previous night’s general election, congratulating a number of elected officials who won the right to represent the constituents within Community Board 6. Mr. Lonial specifically congratulated United States Senator Chuck Schumer; Congress Members Nydia Velazquez, Yvette Clarke, and Jerrold Nadler; State Senators Jesse Hamilton, Kevin Parker, Velmanette Montgomery, and Daniel Squadron; Assembly Members Felix Ortiz and Jo Anne Simon; and newly elected Assembly Member Bobby Carroll, who is taking over the seat held by Assembly Member Jim Brennen. Mr. Lonial closed his remarks about the election by thanking Assembly Member Brennen for his years of service to the district, the city, and the country, and thanked him further for the generous parting donation he made to the Friends of CB6 non-profit.

Mr. Lonial reminded attendees that the Community Board 6 Holiday Party would be held Monday, December 19th beginning at 6:30 pm at the Loki Lounge on 2nd Street off 5th Avenue in Park Slope. The chair also reminded attendees that sponsorships and raffle prize donations are still being accepted, and he directed anyone interested in contributing to contact Special Projects Manager Sarah Phillips at the board office.

The Chairperson thanked Board Members Eric McClure, Matt Silverman, Tom Miskel, and Jerry Armer for leading the October 20th CitiBike hearing. Mr. Lonial noted that he was proud of the board’s initiative to hold the meeting, proud of the meeting’s execution, and felt more clarity than ever about the district’s true feelings regarding the program.

Mr. Lonial thanked Special Projects Manager Sarah Phillips for setting up the SIMS recycling center tour in October and stated his pleasure for the turnout of this tour. He also thanked Permits and Licenses Chair Mike Racioppo for meeting with Assistant District Manager Ty Beatty to discuss changes to the questionnaire and public notification process for the Permits & Licenses committee. The Chair also thanked District Manager Craig Hammerman for attending a roundtable held by City Comptroller Scott Stringer.

Chairperson Lonial closed his remarks by announcing that a ceremony to honor veterans would be held at Carroll Park at 11 am on Friday, November 11th in honor of Veterans Day.

Chairperson Lonial recognized Councilmember Brad Lander, who offered a few updates to the attendees.

Councilmember Lander offered brief updates to attendees. He first offered his comments on the previous night’s general election, and remarked that as long as Community Boards like Brooklyn CB6 continue to do the work that they do the federal elections will have a limited impact on the shaping on communities.

The Councilmember then remarked on two meetings. First, he thanked CB6 for organizing and facilitating the CitiBike feedback meeting, specifically recognizing the effort and performance of Transportation Committee Chair Eric McClure in his role as chair of the meeting. Mr. Lander then offered a brief overview of the first

Mr. Lander closed his remarks by stating that he would be attending the Veterans Day Service at Carroll Park and was looked forward to seeing many in attendance.

DISTRICT MANAGER’S REPORT………………………………………….…….CRAIG HAMMERMAN

District Manager Craig Hammerman opened his report by announcing that the Department of Transportation has approved a new city bench for 370 4th Avenue. The aluminum bench will be installed sometime this upcoming winter.

Mr. Hammerman announced that he attended a ribbon cutting for the Sponge Park opening, along with Assembly Member Jo Anne Simon and Congresswoman Nydia Velazquez. Located at the street end of 2nd Street between Bond Street and the Gowanus Canal, Sponge Park was review and approved several years ago by CB6. The goal of Sponge Park is to use native plantings to help manage storm water runoff naturally.

The District Manager announced that he was recently briefed on a planned Formula E race proposed to take place in Red Hook in the next year. Mr. Hammerman explained that Formula E is an electric car racing circuit similar to Formula 1. Thus far there is no scheduled date or time, but Mr. Hammerman stated the he and the 76th Precinct do find their logistical ideas amenable as they have been presented to date.

Mr. Hammerman closed his remarks by stating that he recently attended an early planning meeting for the now funded PS 32 expansion. The District Manager stated that the meeting was between Councilmember Lander, CEC 15, and the School Construction Authority to discuss logistics and timing for the expansion. Mr. Hammerman noted that all four entities involved would be holding public hearings on the matter in the coming months, and that he would provide information about those meetings when it is available.

ELECTION COMMITTEE……………………………………….PETER FLEMING, ELECTION CHAIR

Election Committee Chair Peter Fleming reported that the existing officers of the board would all be seeking re- election, as they are all eligible. Mr. Fleming then opened the floor for other nominations. No other nominations were made from the floor. As such, the election process was deemed closed and the existing board officers had been re-elected by way of being the only nominees for their respective positions. Therefore, no election would be held at the December general board meeting.

Latoya Benjamin, representing the Kings County District Attorney’s office, offered updates on the office since the passing of the late DA.
