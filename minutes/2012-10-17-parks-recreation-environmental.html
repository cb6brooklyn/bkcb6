*** MINUTES ***

Presentation and discussion with representative for Prospect Park on Prospect Park- related planned and contemplated projects, programs and policies of interest to park users and supporters.

Eric Landau – Vice President for Government and External Affairs and Special Assistant to the President of the Prospect Park Alliance was the representative.

Committee Co-Chair Nica Lalli greeted special guests – the Girl Scouts of Troop 2295.

She also reported on the vote to extend Cobble Hill Park to 10:00pm.

Glenn Kelly, Co-Chair reported on conversation with the 76th Precinct who told him that they were obligated to enforce.

Eric Landau introduced himself and described his duties.  Lakeside Center – described new rinks and facilities.

- 1 open rink - 1 covered rink - Hockey - Roller skating - Giant fountain

 Celebrating the restoration of the lake and restoration of the music island. Dedication ceremony on Friday morning. Open to the public on Saturday and Sunday. Fall 2013 – the center will be complete and open.  Abraham Lincoln Monument will remain where it is for now.  Parking on Breeze Hill  Long Meadow ball fields – multi-year project/multi $M. Phase 1 – field mostly focused on drainage. Starting next year will be closed for one year. Phased to permit continued use of fields.  Prospect Park will receive Participatory Budget funds – Money to rehabilitate Pedestrian paths, drainage and add garbage cans. To start 2013.  Building at Well House Drive being converted to restrooms – in future toilets at the Tennis House.  New restroom in the Parade Ground in construction will be done by Spring/Summer 2013.  Parade Grounds – drainage/fence heights improvements - summer 2013/1 year duration. Fields are in good shape at present.

Q: Can historical markers plaques be included? Q: Limited access from park side – more gates? Part of gate work. A: Could be studied.

Future Projects:  Grand Army Plaza Memorial Arch – restoration of the arch. Raising money – design as started.  Restore/alter Tennis House for administrative use. Raising money.  Picnic House: add air conditioning. Design is ready – trying to raise money. (All changes require Landmarks approval as well as other agencies/boards)  Bowling Green working with Gallop NYC to provide classrooms. They are raising money to build a riding ring – private endeavor  Received grant for master plan to study: - NE perimeter (Vale of Kashmir) - Restore rose garden - Restore ponds - New restroom - New entry - Restore Flatbush Avenue sidewalk/fence (next major capital project) - Fix Oriental Pavilion and upper concrete grove. Q: Three monuments in Vale of Kashmir? A: Will be restored. Q: Lakeside: Empire Music?

A: No music planned. RFP for operator being created.

Q: Gates to Parade Ground are often locked – have to walk? A: Can check

Q: Safety of Vale of Kashmir? A: Activate the area to bring users in.

Prospect Park Alliance pays approximately 2/3 of park operations.

Q: Will top of arch ever be opened? A: Maybe if restoration.

Q: Is Prospect Park Alliance responsible for the Villa? A: Shares with borough offices and other organizations.

Park Drive: facelift and changes. Joint effort of many groups to plan changes to the drive.  Drive was re-marked to clarify the lanes.  Crosswalks: cars: timed system, without cars: lights are green except they can push button for red.  Has volunteers helping pedestrians and bikers understand new system.  Formal bike races must be done by 8:30.

Q: Stats on bike-pedestrian accidents, can we have? A: Yes, but old numbers are not accurate.

 Working with race marshals to improve race management.

Q: PPW sidewalk: weeds and poor condition? A: Not spraying anymore/short staffed, can’t really address it.

Bartel Pritchard Square parking lot – regarding complaints about events/noise.  Will lower decibel limit to 75 @ 350 feet after 10:00pm  No event permits with band or DJ.

Q: Use Bartel Pritchard parking in the evening? Q: Redesign Bartel Pritchard? Trash - extra crews – early and late. Q: Any consideration of “blue light” system in park or PPW? A: Hasn’t been considered.

Comment – keep dirt path clear along the drive. Keep drainage clear at arches.

There being no further business to come before the committee, the meeting was adjourned.
