*** M I N U T E S ***

Presentation and review of a new on-premises liquor license application submitted to the State Liquor Authority on behalf of Brooklyn Union LLC at 126 Union Street (between Columbia/Hicks Streets).

Brooklyn Union LLC  Extremely small.  Tiny bar (more service than a bar with seats)  Food oriented – liquor is an extra to go along with food.  Applicant to provide hours of operation.

Recommendation to approve applicant with the stipulation that the kitchen and outdoor space hours reflect the operations hours. i.e. 11:30am to 10:00pm on Sunday thru Friday and 11:30am to 11pm on Saturday.

VOTE: 4…..YEAS 1…..NAY

MOTION PASSED

Presentation and review of a new on-premises liquor license application submitted to the State Liquor Authority on behalf of CDT 272 Inc. at 494 4th Avenue (between 11th/12th Streets).

CDT 272 Inc. taking over cantina:  Use of side yard  NYS beers on tap  No live music  Dinner volume music is the goal  No speakers outside

Recommendation is to approve the request as presented.

MOTION PASSED UNANIMOUSLY
