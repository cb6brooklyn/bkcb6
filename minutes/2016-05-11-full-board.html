CHAIRPERSON’S REPORT………………………………………………………..............SAYAR LONIAL

Chairperson Sayar Lonial opened his report by expressing his excitement for the on-going internal review of the Community Board committee mission statement review process, noting that he was encouraged by the feedback he has received thus far.

The Chair thanked Board Member Eric McClure, District Manager Craig Hammerman, and Assistant District Manager Ty Beatty for attending a briefing with EDC and DOT regarding the proposed BQX light rail project. The briefing was a very early stage conversation that mapped out the proposed process for public review and unveiled some potential technical details. There will eventually be a ULURP for BQX that will come before the Community Board.

Mr. Lonial next thanked Board Members Ariel Krasnow and Mike Racioppo as well as District Manager Hammerman for attending a meeting with NYCHA regarding their NextGen plan, which includes a proposal to construct new, market rate housing on portions of the Wyckoff Gardens campus in our district. NextGen will also be subject to ULURP in the future, and NYCHA is currently engaged in a series of intensive listening sessions with Wyckoff Gardens tenants.

The Chairperson also thanked Board Members Jerry Armer, Victoria Hagman, and Mark Shames for attending the kick-off meeting of the board’s ad hoc Resiliency and Sustainability Committee. Mr. Lonial noted that the committee will initially serve as a clearinghouse for information related to numerous projects, programs, services, and policies related to resiliency and sustainability efforts affecting the district.

Chairperson Lonial closed his remarks by announcing that this year’s Youth Acknowledgment Awards nomination process has been extended through the fall with a target of announcing commendations at the December full board meeting.

Chairperson Lonial recognized Councilmember Brad Lander, who was in attendance.

Councilmember Lander opened his brief remarks by commenting on the passing of a five cent tax on single use paper and plastic bags. Mr. Lander noted that others cities around the world which have implemented such a tax saw a relatively rapid decrease in the use of single use bags.

Mr. Lander announced that this coming Saturday, May 14th, is the rescheduled Brooklyn PTA 5k Fun Run at Prospect Park, and he invited all meeting attendees and board members to come out to the event.

Councilmember Lander closed his remarks by announcing several winners of the Participatory Budget process, including a Lake Mess Monster, a machine that cleans the lake, for Prospect Park, and year round freeze-

resistant drinking fountains in the Park. Mr. Lander directed attendees to his website for a full list of the Participatory Budget process’ winning ideas.

DISTRICT MANAGER’S REPORT………………………………………….…….CRAIG HAMMERMAN

District Manager Craig Hammerman reported that the affordable housing lottery was about to open for 461 Dean Street at Pacific Park, part of the Atlantic Yards Project. Mr. Hammerman stated that applications are now available online at nyc.gov/housingconnect. The deadline to apply is June 27, 2016.

Mr. Hammerman closed his remarks by updating the Board on DOT’s pending presentation regarding CitiBike implementation. The District Manager stated that DOT would come back to the Transportation Committee on May 19th at PS 58 in Carroll Gardens, beginning at 6:30pm.
