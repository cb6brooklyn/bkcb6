LANDMARKS

17 Montgomery Place – Application for a Certificate of Appropriateness for a 2-story Rear Yard

Addition in the Park Slope Historic District

Presented by Anat Soudry, the project architect. A new projecting bay addition and balcony are being

added to the rear of the townhouse. Questions were asked about the scope of work. Community

members who live at 19 and 25 Montgomery Place expressed support for the project.

Jerry: Motion to approve as presented.

Maddie: Second.

Motion passes unanimously.

9 Cheever Place - Application for a Certificate of Appropriateness for a 2-story structure in the

Cobble Hill Historic District

Presented by Ingui Architects. A new two-story building is proposed for the rear yard of 136 Kane

Street. The bottom floor will be a garage, and the upper floor will be an apartment. Extensive

discussion with the committee and community members who expressed concern about the

accuracy of measurements on the drawings and loss of daylight on the entire block. The architect

confirmed that the presentation shows a distance of approximately 7 1/2 feet from the face of 12

Cheever Street to the face of the garage. During the discussion, the 12 Cheever neighbor went

outside and measured the distance to be approximately 11’. Alternate solutions discussed that

would allow the garage to be set back. Other neighbors requested that no structure be built or that

only a one-story building be allowed. The architect offered to reschedule LPC hearing and return to

the committee with a revised design.

Jerry: 1st motion to table discussion until the architect returns with a new design.

Maddie: Seconded.

Motion passes unanimously.

Jerry: 2nd motion to disapprove the application as presented if LPC hearing is not rescheduled.

Maddie: Seconded.

Motion passes unanimously.
