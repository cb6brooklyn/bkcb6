CHAIRPERSON’S REPORT………………………………………ERIC MCCLURE, FIRST VICE CHAIR

First Vice Chair Eric McClure, filling in for Board Chair Peter Fleming, kept his opening comments brief, welcoming meeting attendees and explaining that he would be filling in for the Chair.

The First Vice Chair then announced, with help from the District Manager, that only the existing slate of Board Officers had been nominated for their existing positions, and that each had accepted their nomination. Mr. McClure then opened the floor to final nominations, and there were none. As such, the existing Board Officers will remain in place through the next year.

Mr. McClure closed his remarks by congratulating District Manager Michael Racioppo on his recent engagement to his fiancé.

DISTRICT MANAGER’S REPORT……………………..MICHAEL RACIOPPO, DISTRICT MANAGER

Mr. Racioppo made brief comments, thank Assistant District Manager Ty Beatty for attending a meeting relative to a Department of Design and Construction sewer main project which has caused some issues in the north part of Park Slope and announcing the upcoming meeting schedule.
