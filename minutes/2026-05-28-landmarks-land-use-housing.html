May 28, 2026 on Zoom

Attendees

Dave Kathleen Tong Laura Maddie Lena The World Cup is coming to town, the Knicks are rolling, and the LULMH committee quorum streak reignites. Landmarks 184 Lincoln Place - Application for a Certificate of Appropriateness for a rooftop addition in the Park Slope Historic District (Extension). Diego Celaya from Architecture + Construction presented the project. Committee had several questions and concerns about the appropriateness of the new brick parapets on the party walls, access to the terrace, the gate in the middle of the railing above the rear elevation, the stucco color on the bulkhead, the screen on the north side of the pergola, visibility from Berkeley Street, and the lack of historical character in the design. Community members also shared concerns, objecting to the size of the proposed rooftop extension and visibility. Committee members shared suggestions on how to improve the proposal. Maddie - Motion to disapprove due to several factors: size, palette of materials and colors, and lack of historical appropriateness. Laura - seconded motion

Motion unanimously approved.

End of meeting.
