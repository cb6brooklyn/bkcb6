CHAIRPERSON’S REPORT…………………………………………...PETER FLEMING, BOARD CHAIR

Board Chair Peter Fleming offered brief remarks after allowing Assembly Member Carroll and District 15 Superintendent Anita Skop to open the meeting with their own brief updates relative to the state of schools in relation to the on-going pandemic. Mr. Fleming announced that the Board would be facilitating a meeting via Zoom on November 19th for the Department of City Planning where DCP will present their plans relative to the Gowanus Rezoning and the Public Place site along the Gowanus Canal.

1

Mr. Fleming ceded the remainder of his report to Board Member Jason Reischel, who is directing the Board’s annual elections. Mr. Reischel reported that the only nominations for Board leadership were of the existing slate of officers. With no nominations coming from the floor, the existing slate of Board offers was re-elected.

PERMITS & LICENSES COMMITTEE……………………………………RACHEL FREEMAN, CHAIR

Permits & Licenses Committee Chair Rachel Freeman reported that the committee reviewed a new on-premises liquor license for 280 5th Avenue.

Motion: To approve a new on-premises liquor license application submitted to the State Liquor Authority on behalf of Terrace Restaurant and Bakery Corp. at 280 5th Avenue.

VOTE: 40 YEAS, 0 NAYS, 0 ABSTENTIONS

MOTION PASSED UNANIMOUSLY

TIME: 7:33 P.M.

CAPITAL & EXPENSE BUDGET WORKING GROUP………….…………..RICK LUFTGLASS, CHAIR

Board Member Rick Luftglass, with the help of Assistant District Manager Ty Beatty, presented the proposed Fiscal Year 2022 Capital & Budget recommendations as prepared for reporting to the Department of City Planning, along with an outline of the new Working Group’s processes for compiling the lists and how they plan to operate moving forward.

Motion: To approve the proposed Fiscal Year 2022 Capital & Expense Budget recommendations as prepared by the Capital & Expense Budget Working Group for reporting to the Department of City Planning.

VOTE: 40 YEAS, 0 NAYS, 0 ABSTENTIONS

MOTION PASSED UNANIMOUSLY

TIME: 7:45 P.M.

LANDMARKS/LAND USE COMMITTEE………….…………...DAVID BRIGGS, LANDMARKS CHAIR

…………………………...MARK SHAMES, LAND USE CHAIR

Landmarks Committee Chair Robert Levine reported that the committee reviewed a Certificate of Appropriateness for 519 2nd Street.

Motion: Approval of a Certificate of Appropriateness application submitted to the Landmarks Preservation Commission to allow for barrier-free handicap access to the under-stoop basement entry by replacing existing concrete-surfaced areaway with historically appropriate bluestone pavers pitched to marry the sidewalk grade with the basement floor height at 519 2 Street Park Slope nd

Historic District.

VOTE: 40 YEAS, 0 NAYS, 0 ABSTENTIONS

MOTION PASSED UNANIMOUSLY

TRANSPORTATION/PUBLIC SAFETY………….ERIC McCLURE, CHAIR, TRANSPORTATION
