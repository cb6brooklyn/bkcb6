BROOKLYN COMMUNITY BOARD 6

HUMAN SERVICES & ENVIRONMENTAL SUSTAINABILITY COMMITTEE

January 16, 2025 @ Cobble Hill Community Room, 250 Baltic Street & Zoom

6:30 p.m.

Committee Chair: Mandi Spishak-Thomas

MINUTES

Percent for Art: Red Hook Library

- Overview of the Percent for Art program, the artwork selection process for the Red Hook Library project, and opportunities for community participation.

City Sponge: Local Flooding

- City Sponge presented on local flooding, including community members' experiences, and the Flood Solutions Fair on Saturday, January 25, 1 to 4 PM, co-sponsored by Gowanus Canal Conservancy, FOFA, the Park Slope Civic Council, the Boerum Hill Association and CB6.

- Discussion of flood mitigation, the responsibilities of landlords and renters, outreach to small businesses and tradespeople, street tree planting codes and the city's block-by-block planting program.

Committee Business

- The committee agreed to be more proactive in bringing community issues to meetings, using awareness days and months as themes, and to organize future agendas in a shared document.
