*** MINUTES ***

Orientation: Rules, regulation and procedures governing committee participation.

Mike Racioppo introduced focus and procedures of the committee.

Presentation and review of a new on-premises liquor license application submitted to the state Liquor Authority on behalf of Just Delicious LLC at 257 Columbia Street (between President/Carroll Streets)

Neil Visoky presented. Amended application hours to Sunday – Thursday closing 11pm and Friday and Saturday closing 12pm.

Chris Curran, a community member in favor of the application.

Glenn Kelly made a motion to approve the application and Pauline Blake seconded.

Motion approved: Unanimously

Note: instructions regarding public notification were not properly transmitted. Process needs to be addressed by

CB6.

Presentation and review of a new on-premises liquor license application submitted to the State Liquor Authority on behalf of Park Slope Ale House at 356 6th Avenue (between 4th/5th Streets).

Park Slope Ale House is changing hands. Music will be off or doors closed by 10pm every night. Outdoor hours will be 11pm weeknights and 12pm Friday and Saturdays

Motion Approved: Unanimously

There being no further business to come before the Committee. The meeting was adjourned.

Minutes were submitted by Josh Skaller.
