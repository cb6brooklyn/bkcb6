Transportation, Parks & Public Infrastructure Resolution Supporting Intro 1380 Tony Melone presented the following resolution: CB6 supports Intro 1380, the Ride Safe, Ride Right bill sponsored by Council Members Crystal Hudson, Alexa Avilés, and others. This bill prohibits the sale or rental of e-bikes with a top speed of over 20 mph. We also encourage the Mayor's office to return to a speed limit of 20 mph for e-bikes in NYC, to establish one clear standard for the legal purchase and operation of micromobility vehicles in NYC. We reiterate our support for Sammy’s Law. The Transportation, Parks & Public Infrastructure Committee approved the resolution (with quorum), with quorum. Tony explained that the bill would prohibit the sale/rental of e-bikes capable of speeds over 20 MPH and that the purpose of the bill is to align rules for the sale and operation of these vehicles, consistent with Sammy’s Law, which allows New York City to lower its speed limit to 20 MPH on individual streets. Tony explained that the bill only applies to Class I and II throttle/pedal e-bikes and that it penalizes vendors, not riders. Leroy Branch abstained for cause; all others approved the measure.
