CHAIRPERSON’S REPORT………………………………………….………………….....SAYAR LONIAL

Chairperson Sayar Lonial opened his report by directing board members to the Executive Committee’s proposed resolution calling on the President of the United States to repeal the existing immigration ban, as the board would be voting on the motion to adopt the resolution once business session begins.

Mr. Lonial updated the board on the progress of the Red Hook Community Microgrid, which was presented to the Executive Committee on the previous Monday evening. The Chair stated that there are significant barriers limiting the Microgrid project from entering its next phase, including Con Edison’s reluctance or inability to share equipment, and an estimated cost of nearly $15,000,000 to bury all the associated wiring.

The Chair offered a brief update on the working groups for the proposed Gowanus Rezoning, noted that the district office would distribute meeting information upon its finalization, and requested that Board Members consider participating in the process.

Mr. Lonial closed his report by reminding the board that nominations are open for Youth Awards, which the board has awarded since 1988 at the May General Meeting.

DISTRICT MANAGER’S REPORT………………………………………….…….CRAIG HAMMERMAN

District Manager Craig Hammerman reported that the Board Office was in the process of finishing a regular periodic audit by the Comptroller’s Office. Expectation is that no violations will be found.

Mr. Hammerman noted that the Department of Homeless Services has assigned a new liaison to the district, and a meeting was held between the new liaison, Mr. Hammerman, and Assistant District Manager Ty Beatty. Mr. Hammerman and Mr. Beatty were told that a new set of city policy initiatives relative to homelessness would be announced by the Mayor in the next few weeks.

Mr. Hammerman closed his report by further expanding on Mr. Lonial’s previous remarks regarding the Red Hook Microgrid, and the Solarize program in general.
