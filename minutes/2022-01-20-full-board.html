EWCDH COMMITTEE…………………………………………………..SAMARA TRILLING, CO-CHAIR

Board Member Samara Trilling, a co-chair of the Economics, Waterfront, Community Development, and Housing committee, reported on a letter the committee wrote to local elected officials asking that they study further economic and environmental impacts of the recent proliferation of last mile warehouses in the city, specifically in Red Hook.

Motion: To approve the committee’s letter to local elected officials.

VOTE: 36 YEAS, 0 NAYS, 0 ABSTENTIONS

MOTION PASSED UNANIMOUSLY

TIME: 7:10 P.M.

LANDMARKS & LAND USE COMMITTEE……………………DAVID BRIGGS, LANDMARKS CHAIR

…………..ALEC SCHIERENBECK, LAND USE CHAIR

Land Use Chair Alec Schierenbeck reported that the committee considered a series of BSA applications at 12 Coles Street, 13 Luquer Street, and 375 Columbia Street

Motion: To approve applications to the Board of Standards & Appeals pursuant to Section 72-21 of the Zoning Resolution of the City of New York seeking a variance of Section 42-00 to permit the development of (1) a four-story, 4290 sq. ft. residential use building in an M1-1 district at 12 Coles Street; (2) a four-story, 4482 sq. ft. residential use building in an M1-1 district at 13 Luquer Street; (3) a four-story, 6157 sq. ft. residential use building in an M1-1 district at 375 Columbia Street.

VOTE: 36 YEAS, 0 NAYS, 0 ABSTENTIONS

MOTION PASSED UNANIMOUSLY

TIME: 7:30 P.M.
