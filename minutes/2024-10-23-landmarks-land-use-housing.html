Landmarks

136 Kane Street (Cobble Hill Historic District) - Application for Certificate of

Appropriateness to allow for 2-story rear yard extension and roof top addition

Presentation made by an architect.

Questions/Comments from committee and community:

1. A mock-up was not built for the rooftop addition.

2. Building height limit? Confirmation of 50’. Only chimneys extend above it.

3. Too many windows on the west-facing elevation of rooftop addition.

4. Will the CMU wall be modified? It will be shortened to allow for two-story rear yard

extension and coated with cementitious stucco to match brownstone.

5. Long and engaging discussion with neighbors about history and appropriateness of

design. Committee encouraged the architect to advise the client to meet with the

neighbor as soon as possible. Architect will follow up with the client and confirm that

the neighbor will be invited to the construction kickoff meeting.

Jerry - Following motion is contingent on construction of mock-up for rooftop addition

and including photos in the presentation prior to the next board meeting.

Motion to conditionally approve. Reduce the amount of fenestration on west-facing

elevation of roof top addition so it is similar to 435 Henry Street.

Maddie - second

6 in favor.

1 abstentions.

0 against.

Project will be presented at a full board meeting for formal vote.

End of meeting.
