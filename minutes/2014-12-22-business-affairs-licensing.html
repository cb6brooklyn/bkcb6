*** M I N U T E S ***

The meeting was called to order at 6:41pm by Mark Shames of order from the agenda

Presentation and discussion with representatives for the Department of Environmental Protection on the upcoming planned construction of the first phase of Bioswales and Stormwater Greenstreets in the Gowanus Canal drainage area at various locations in Boerum Hill, Gowanus and Park Slope in CD6.

Presentation and review of a new on-premises liquor license application submitted to the State Liquor Authority on behalf of Red Hook Lobster Pound LLC at 284 Van Brunt Street (between Verona Street/Visitation Place).

At 7:35pm a quorum was attained with 11 members present.

The Red Hook Lobster Pound has been in operation for six years. Glenn Kelly moved to approve the application and Tom Miskel seconded.

MOTION PASSED: UNANIMOUSLY

The minutes of the previous meeting held on November 24, 2014 were approved unanimously.

There being no further business to come before the committee, the meeting was adjourned.

The minutes were submitted by Trudy Reeves.
