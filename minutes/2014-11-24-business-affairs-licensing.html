*** M I N U T E S ***

The meeting was called to order at 6:45 pm with a quorum present, which remained throughout the meeting. The items were taken out of order from the agenda

Presentation and review of a new on-premises liquor license application submitted to the State Liquor Authority on behalf of Y.B.S. LLC at 326 Douglass Street (between 3rd/4th Avenues).

The applicant is opening a Korean BBQ restaurant (unnamed as yet). There were several questions. A motion was made by Tom Miskel to approve the application as presented. The

motion was seconded by Mike Racioppo.

VOTE: 10…..YEAS 0…..NAYS 0…..ABSTENTIONS

MOTION APPROVED: UNANIMOUSLY

(Continued) Applicant Presentation and review of a new on-premises liquor license application submitted to the State Liquor Authority on behalf of Ring Ring Ring LLC at 359 Sackett Street (between Smith/Hoyt Streets).

The applicant would like to open a coffee to cocktail European style café with 12-14 seats.

Presented by Regina Myers.

Community members: For – 7, Against – 11. Block residents presented a list of requests/demands.

Applicant agrees to close windows and doors at 10pm on 7 days. Last call for drinks at 11:30pm Sunday thru Wednesday and 12:30pm Thursday thru Saturday.

Eric Anderson made a motion to approve with stipulations, seconded by Mike Racioppo. There was a discussion by the Committee.

VOTE: 8…..YEAS 0…..NAYS 2…..ABSTENTIONS (1 FOR CAUSE)

MOTION PASSED

(Continued) Presentation and review of a new on-premises liquor license application submitted to the State Liquor Authority on behalf of 228 Van Brunt Street (between Commerce/Delevan Streets).

The applicant was a no show. Please send a letter to the State Liquor Authority in case application is moving forward.

(Continued) Presentation and review of a new on-premises liquor license application submitted to the State Liquor Authority on behalf of 240 Flatbush Avenue (between Bergen Street/6th Avenue).

This is a new application for Taps Sports Bar. A community member asked a question about neighbor notification. A motion was made by Sayar Lonial to approve the application subject to doors and windows remaining closed. The motion was seconded by Tom Miskel.

VOTE: 9…..YEAS 1…..NAY 0…..ABSTENTIONS

MOTION APPROVED

Presentation and review of a new on-premises liquor license application submitted to the State Liquor Authority on behalf of BAM Hospitality LLC at 450 Dean Street (between 5th Avenue/Flatbush Avenue).

Patsy’s Pizzeria proposes to operate a restaurant with bar. Backyard will have a canopy structure. They are also licensed as Patsy’s of Harlem.

Applicant offers to change his closing hours to 1am and will close the backyard by 11pm – 7 days.

Eric Anderson moved to approve with the change in hours and Sayar Lonial seconded.

VOTE: 8…..YEAS 2…..NAYS 0…..ABSTENTIONS

MOTION APPROVED

Presentation and review of a new on-premises liquor license application submitted to the State Liquor Authority on behalf of Henri on Fifth at 279 5th Avenue (between 1st/2nd Streets).

The applicant was represented by Michael Kelly, attorney for owners Binh Douglas.

Bar/Restaurant has existing sidewalk café and will transfer license. Tom Miskel moved to approve the application, seconded by Pauline Blake.

VOTE: 10…..YEAS 0….NAYS 0…..ABSTENTIONS

MOTION APPROVED: UNANIMOUSLY

There being no further business to come before the committee the meeting was adjourned.

Minutes submitted by Glenn Kelly.
