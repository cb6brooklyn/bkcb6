 MINUTES 

Continued review of a renewal revocable consent for an unenclosed sidewalk café application submitted to the Department of Consumer Affairs on behalf of 283 Smith Street Food Corp. dba Zaytoons at 283 Smith Street (northeast corner Smith/Sackett Streets), to permit 7 tables and 14 seats.

Applicant requested to renew existing sidewalk café application. A motion was made to approve the application subject to the tables being moved to accommodate the eight foot rule from the light post.

VOTE: 10…..YEAS 0….NAES

MOTION PASSED: UNANIMOUSLY

Presentation and review of a new unenclosed sidewalk café permit application

submitted to the Department of Consumer Affairs on behalf of Act 718, LLC dba Sheep Station at 149 4th Avenue (northeast corner 4th Avenue/Douglass Street), to permit 8 tables and 18 seats.

A motion was made to approve the request subject to operating outside no later than 10pm on weekdays and 11pm on weekends and the owner is to put up signs about minimizing noise and smoking.

VOTE 10…..YEAS 0…..NAES

MOTION PASSED: UNANIMOUSLY

Presentation and review of a new unenclosed sidewalk café permit application

submitted to the Department o f Consumer Affairs on behalf of Clay Farm, LLC

dba The Bell House at 149 7th Street (between 2nd/3rd Avenues), to permit 5 tables and 20 seats.

A motion was made to approve without our typical request to reduce the operating hours because of the strictly commercial and completely non-residential location.

VOTE: 8…..YEAS 0….NAES 2…..ABSTENTIONS WITH CAUSE

MOTION PASSED

Presentation and review of a new unenclosed sidewalk café permit application

submitted to the Department of Consumer Affairs on behalf of Ataka Taka Taka, Inc. at 252 7th Avenue (northeast corner 7th Avenue/5th Street), to permit 15 tables and 30 seats.

K. Negri Stathopoulous presented a request for a new application for 15 tables and 30 seats

Motion was made to approve subject to an agreement to reduce the request to 10 tables and 20 seats. (Northern most 3-2 tops and southern most 2-2 tops will be removed).

VOTE: 10…..YEAS 0…..NAES

MOTION PASSED: UNANIMOUSLY

There being no further business to come before the committee, the meeting was

adjourned.
