CB6 Liquor License Meeting Minutes - January 8

Board member attendees:

● Aray Rivera - Minute taker

● Pauline Blake

● Leroy Branch

● Ed Sawyer

● Mandi Spishak-Thomas - Chair

Meeting start time: 6:33 pm

Meeting end time: 6:54 pm

Applicant: 80 4th Street - between Bond & Hoyt St. (PKLYN One LLC)

● Applicant (David Friedman) is opening an indoor pickleball court and is applying for a

liquor license for a full bar.

● Entertainment includes live music, recorded music, and D.J.’s for special events

● The amenities include five pickleball courts, one ping pong table, restrooms, showers,

bike storage, small kitchen for light bites, and a full bar

● This is a second location. The first location is in Columbus, Ohio.

Community comments:

● Two community members, who live across the location, joined the meeting to express

their concerns. They’re worried about loud noise, drunk patrons, limited parking, and

trash on the streets.

Board member comments:

● Pauline and Leroy were also concerned about the loud noise and want to make sure that

the owner/manager is in contact with residents in the area to ensure they have each

other's contact numbers and can reach out if anything.

● The applicant will ensure to instal sound panels and to adhere to noise regulations.

● The applicant also noted that this is not a bar, but an entertainment facility with a focus

on pickleball and not drinking.

● Pauline motioned to approve with the conditions that the owner must adhere to the noise

regulations, no loud music, install sound panels to limit noise, and exchange contact

information with the residents in the area and community board 6. Leroy seconded the

motion.

● All Board Members were in favor.
