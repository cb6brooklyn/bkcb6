CHAIRPERSON’S REPORT…………………………………………...SAYAR LONIAL, BOARD CHAIR

Chairperson Lonial opened his report by offering an outline of the board processes for the summer, including the executive committee’s capacity to act and the up-coming committee assignment process. He then allowed the six new board members in attendance – Leroy Branch, Karen Broughton, Giona Fabbri, Jasmeet Krause-Vilmar, Angelica Ramdhari, and Neal Zephyrin – to briefly introduce themselves to the rest of the board.

Mr. Lonial then offered a brief rundown of the up-coming summer meetings, as the board needed to review a number of land use applications at various meetings. The June 19th Economic, Waterfront, Community Development, and Housing Committee meeting would hear an update regarding the Department of City Planning’s Gowanus Places project as well as DCP’s pending Citywide Self-Storage Text Amendment. The June 21st Parks, Recreation, and Environmental Protection Committee would hear the Department of City Planning’s Flood Text Amendment. The June 22nd Landmarks and Land Use Committee would hear an application to alter the Urban Renewal Plan height limitation relative to the upcoming project at the Key Food’s site. And the June 28th Youth, Human Services, and Education Committee would hear a presentation from the Department of Social Services regarding the Mayor’s new homelessness plan. The Chair reminded board members that the Permits and Licenses Committee and the Transportation Committee would both meet as usual in June, and he urged board members to attend these meetings.

Mr. Lonial closed his remarks by stating the a Landmarks Preservation Commission proceeding required executive action on his part to provide feedback on the Landmarks application at 160 St. John’s Place, but that tonight’s vote would still be important for our records. He also wished all the fathers in attendance a happy early Father’s Day.

DISTRICT MANAGER’S REPORT………………...TY BEATTY, ASSISTANT DISTRICT MANAGER

Assistant District Manager Ty Beatty offered a report from the district office, first thanking board members Frances Brown and Jerry Armer for attending the launch of the NYC Ferry’s South Brooklyn Route at Red Hook’s Atlantic Basin.

Mr. Beatty announced the up-coming Friends of CB6 sponsored RETI Center Day of Action taking place at various Red Hook locations, and closed his remarks by offering a brief update on the launch of the 76 th Precinct’s Neighborhood Policing Patrol Plan.
