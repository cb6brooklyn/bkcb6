CHAIRPERSON’S REPORT…………………………………………...PETER FLEMING, BOARD CHAIR

Board Chair Peter Fleming offered only brief remarks by thanking the Board for its work over the past year and reminding everyone that the Board would not meet again until September. Mr. Fleming outlined that the summer months almost always include meetings of the Landmarks & Land Use as well as the Permits & Licenses committees, and he asked that those members on those committees keep an eye on the calendar and stay in touch with the district office. The Chair explained that the executive committee would meet to carry out any necessary Board business in the summer.

DISTRICT MANAGER’S REPORT……………………..MICHAEL RACIOPPO, DISTRICT MANAGER

Mr. Racioppo made brief comments updating the Board and all attendees on the up-coming meeting schedule and highlighting that over the last few months each committee has had active and productive meetings.
