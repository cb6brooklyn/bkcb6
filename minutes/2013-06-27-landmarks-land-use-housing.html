***MINUTES***

Continued review of a Certificate of Appropriateness application submitted to the Landmarks Preservation Commission for the construction of a new roof deck and legalization of alterations to the parapet wall at 218-220 Baltic Street (between Court/Clinton Streets), Cobble Hill Historic District.

Motion: Recommend alterations not be legalized, further addition brick work on parapet be removed, if any fencing be required that it be mounted behind the wall.

Motion was made by Jerry Armer, seconded by Madeline Murphy

VOTE: 13…..YEAS 0…..NAYS 0…..ABSTENTIONS

MOTION PASSED
