 MINUTES 

Presentation and update from representatives for the Governors Island Corporation on the further development of the island, including a briefing on recent land use action that will establish a Special Governors Island District to facilitate the reuse and retenanting of the existing historic structures and the construction of a new building at the northern end of the island.

Lezlie Simon of the Governors Island Corporation made the presentation.  7 years at Governors Island  Geographically the island is part of Manhattan  Psychologically it is part of Brooklyn  4th summer – rides from Brooklyn  Presentation to be posted on their website  Will offer tours to the board  Memorial Day weekend opening  Diverse programming this summer. A pivotal year for the island.

2007 – beginning of the design process. Phase 1 will open later this year – in the Fall. A Landmark building, terrace, hammock grove and play lawn to open in the fall. The high rises were demolished except for one remaining for demo in June.

Phase 2: Infrastructure  Been running massive capital campaign – seawall, docks, etc.  Create public spaces and to get ready for year round  Urban Assembly (the harbor) 3rd year; 432 students; college prep  Lower Manhattan Cultural Council  New tenants in historic buildings  Need to align the zoning with purpose  40% from Brooklyn

Zoning Process  R32 – low density residential (creating own zoning – consider all commercial uses – but over $75K to respond to CB1) best ideas to come out of RFP process  Doesn’t increase density – C1 overlay – every proposal to State Historical Preservation office and Landmarks Preservation Environmental Impact report  May 8th – May 20th. May 8th meeting at City Planning Commission.  Middle of process-right now-CB 1 unanimous  Hotel/Office/Restaurant/Retail all considered for commercial use – only for North Island area – historical area – rezoning only for North Island.

Questions  Surveying for visitors to aid in lobbying our Community/Borough for support  8-10,000 per day on weekends  Praesidio – Island in San Francisco – rental income and Federal subsidies but Governors Island had more focus on public  Little League baseball (2) Soccer (1). Southern end will become “pickup games”  33 acres “new construction” but no plan yet  Designed to anticipate 10 year flood  Only lost 8-10 out of 1700 trees.  Kayak dock – welcome to come dock

Follow-up – The presentation will be sent to the District Manager and will also talk to him about hard hat tours for Board.
