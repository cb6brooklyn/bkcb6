***MINUTES***

Presentation and review of an on-premises liquor license application submitted to the State Liquor Authority on behalf of Brooklyn Crab LLC at 24 Reed Street (between Van Brunt/Conover Streets)

This is to be a new structure on a large lot. The applicant for this liquor license states that it will be a seafood restaurant with hours of operation being from 5 to 10:30 week days and 5 to 11:30 weekends and holidays.

There will be two open air spaces, to wit: a deck and a covered roof area. Given the hours of operation the applicant had no objection to stopping service outside at 10 on week days and 11 on weekends and holidays with the outside closed by 11 on weekdays and 12 on weekends and holidays.

Applicant stated notice was distributed.

There was concern expressed about the large undeveloped area at the site. Applicant states that there is no intention to expand the eating and drinking establishment beyond the confines of the structure.

Applicant is considering a miniature golf course on balance of site. No determination had been made concerning parking.

Presentation and review of an on-premises liquor license application submitted to the State Liquor Authority on behalf of Kumiko Terao at 550 Court Street (between W. 9th/Garnet Streets).

The applicant Ms. Kumiko Terao is the owner of the building. She is seeking a liquor license for what she describes as a French metro café with a minimalist menu of open sandwiches. There is a 900 square foot garden space, She stated that she gave notice but Steve who lives in area says he did not see a notice. Intend to open in the fall. Approximately 30 seats inside, and 25 seats outside. Applicant agreed that hours of operation of backyard space will be stopping of service at 10 on weekdays and 11 on weekends and holidays and closing at 11 and 12 respectively.

Tom Miskel moved and seconded by Gary Reilly to approve subject to the stipulation with respect to hours.

MOTION PASSED: UNANIMOUSLY.

The committee then took up the issue of whether or not to adopt a waiver policy for certain liquor license applications.

After considerable discussion Matt Silverman made a motion seconded by Lou Sones to approve the following procedure for a one-time waiver of the 30-day waiting period with respect to renewal applications only:

In the absence of a history of complaints (which under existing policy would trigger a review) the applicant must provide community notice consistent with the sample form and guidelines for giving notice to residents and businesses in the area surrounding the applicant’s location. The notice form will include the CB6 District Office’s telephone number and e-mail address and invite recipients to communicate their comments on a date certainly no later than the 5th date after the notice is posted and absent any complaints being received by the district office by the close of business on said date such waiver shall be issued by the district office.
