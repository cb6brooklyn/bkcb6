CHAIRPERSON’S REPORT…………………………………………...PETER FLEMING, BOARD CHAIR

Board Chair Peter Fleming offered only brief remarks, including some general remarks on the prior day’s Primary Elections and the nature of their featuring the first ranked choice selection process in the history of city politics. Mr. Fleming also commented on the status of the expected-to-be-closed Key Food Grocery on 5th Avenue, pointing out that a new grocery is expected to open in roughly 2024 with completion of the site development.

The Chair closed his remarks by acknowledging that tonight’s agenda includes the Board’s final vote on the proposed Gowanus Rezoning, further acknowledging the work put it by many members of the Board and the community at large during this review process, and calling for continued civility during the meeting’s deliberation.

DISTRICT MANAGER’S REPORT……………………..MICHAEL RACIOPPO, DISTRICT MANAGER

District Manager Michael Racioppo briefly outlined that the motion before the Full Board tonight regarding the Gowanus Rezoning is fifteen pages long and was prepared and passed by the Board’s Landmarks & Land Use Committee the previous week.
