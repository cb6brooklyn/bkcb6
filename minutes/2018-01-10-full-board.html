 COMMITTEE REPORTS 

LANDMARKS/LAND USE COMMITTEE……………….ROBERT LEVINE, LANDMARKS CO-CHAIR

…………………...JERRY ARMER, LANDMARKS CO-CHAIR

…………………...……..PETER FLEMING, LAND USE CHAIR

Landmarks Committee co-chair Robert Levine reported that the committee reviewed a certificate of appropriateness application for enlarged storefront windows, new doors and other facade changes, new parapet wall and crown, and rooftop mechanical equipment screening at 192 Prospect Park West.

Motion: By Board Member Eric McClure; seconded by Board Member Daniel Kummer Conditionally approve a Certificate of Appropriateness application submitted to the Landmarks Preservation Commission for enlarged storefront windows, new doors and other façade changes, new parapet wall and crown, and rooftop mechanical equipment screening at 192 Prospect Park West (between 14th Street/15th Street), Park Slope Historic District.

Brief conversation ensued regarding the potential visibility of the rooftop mechanical equipment. A friendly amendment to the motion was accepted, making the motion conditional upon guarantee that the rooftop mechanicals not be visible from any public thruway, excluding Prospect Park.

VOTE: 16 YEAS, 9 NAYS, 3 ABSTENTIONS

MOTION PASSED

TIME: 7:03 P.M.

Landmarks Committee co-chair Jerry Armer reported that the committee reviewed a certificate of appropriateness application for alterations of the areaway/front yard to allow access for barrier free entry at 452 9th Street.

Motion: By Board Member Mark Shames; seconded by Board Member Peter Fleming Approval a Certificate of Appropriateness application submitted to the Landmarks Preservation Commission for alterations of the areaway/front yard to allow access for barrier free entry at 452 9th Street (between 7th/8th Avenues), Park Slope Historic District.

VOTE: 32 YEAS, 0 NAYS, 1 ABSTENTION

TRANSPORTION & PUBLIC SAFETY COMMITTEE……………………ERIC MCCLURE, CO-CHAIR

Transportation Committee Co-Chair Eric McClure reported that the committee reviewed a proposal by the NYC Department of Transportation for a redesign of 4th Avenue.

Motion: Approval of the installation of the NYC Department of Transportation’s proposed redesign of 4th Avenue while strongly encouraging NYC DOT to prioritize extending the project northward during Phase A and encouraging NYC DOT to use temporary materials to extend the protected bike lanes to Bergen Street and Dean Street, pending Phase B capital project.

VOTE: 30 YEAS, 0 NAYS, 2 ABSTENTIONS

MOTION PASSED
