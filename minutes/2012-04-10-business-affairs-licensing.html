***MINUTES***

Meeting called to order at 6:41pm by CB6 Permits & Licenses Chair Gary Reilly with Ruth Saunders, chair of Brooklyn CB2’s Health, Environment and Social Services Committee.

Gary introduces CB2 Chairperson John Dew and District Manager Robert Perris, and Inspector Terry Riley from NYPD’s OMAP office who is in attendance as a resource for security-related matters.

Gary goes over the rules and protocols: Meeting will only concern the liquor license application. CB6 and CB2 will both have general meetings tomorrow that will take up any resolutions passed here.

Invites applicants to present:

Applicant: Robert Skene representing Levy Premium food with co-applicant from Barclays Senior Vice President Mike Lutino

Co-Counsel Barbara Kwan represents Brooklyn Event Center w/ David Anderson and Director of Security of Barclays.

Robert: We are here tonight for community input. Notifications were made on March 27nd Submitted handouts containing responses to CB2 and 6 questionnaires. List of licenses. Sample menus but final menus not formulated. Bio of manager and photos.

Overview of Levy: Concessionaire at USTA, National company that holds 85 licenses in 24 states and Canada. Specialize in sporting venues. National Operator. Manager came from Staples Center and Nokia Arena in LA. Value of national company. Started out as family-owned company. Understand community needs.

Actual method of operation: 57 points of sale of alcoholic beverages, 27 for beer only, the rest for beer, wine and spirits. 5 full bars on the ground level, 2 are portable sale point. Third level 24 points of sale: hot dogs, pizza, and Brooklyn-theme outlets. 13 will be beer only. Fourth level: Lower Suite level 8 points of sale, 4 beer only 65 suites on this level. Upper suite level: 2 full bars 30 suites. Level 6 upper concourse: 18 points of sale pizza, hotdogs, nachos, and Brooklyn-themed eateries 10 beer only

Arena has 18,000 seat capacity containing 30 liquor bars and 27 beer only bars.

Client intends to follow NBA policy (i.e.; no service in fourth quarter of the game). Other events cut-off time before end of event decided on and event basis.

Plan to feature food more than other arenas of this size: all-inclusive ticket where food and drink included in the ticket.

Julie Margolan will be director of operations, lives half a mile from arena, assistant manager lives a half mile away as well.

Security: All Levy employees are server trained, National Restaurant Associate Safe Server training. Front and back of the house. 2 alcoholic beverages will be sold per ID. Anyone appearing under 30 will be carded. All employees will be carding people appear under the age of 21. Building security will be notified if someone is underage. All trained if someone is overly intoxicated.

Constant communication between Levy staff and guest services and building management. For any concert or special event there will be pre-planning meetings.

Barbara Kwon: Represents Brooklyn Events Center LLC David Anderson Robert Scena: Director of Security

Under Joe’s suggestion we proceed on two procedural motions, both accepted:

Motion: to table voting on the application

Vote: 10 yea votes, 4 nay votes

Motion: that the district office be asked to contact the lawyer of the applicant to set up a community task force. The chair of CB6 is asked to select committee members as well as members of the community for the task force.

Vote: 12 yea, 1 nay, 1 abstention

CB2 approved a motion to conditionally approve stipulating under the following conditions: They submit a transportation plan and a security plan; The liquor license is for the shortest time possible; That we revisit in a year; That they develop a clean up program for immediately after events; Stipulate a two drink per ID maximum per person per purchase; Stipulate that they have ID readers in no less than half the stations at the arena; Stipulate no alcohol outside of the arena; A community advisory group be created immediately to note potential problems and solutions; Patrons not be allowed to take cups with alcohol out of the arena; A recycling program be developed.
