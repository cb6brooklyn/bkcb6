CHAIRPERSON’S REPORT………………………………………………………....….DANIEL KUMMER

Chairperson Daniel Kummer began his report by congratulating all of the Perfect Attendance Award recipients.

Chairperson Kummer thanked Parks Committee Co-Chair Nica Lalli and our District Manager for attending a recent meeting at the Prospect Park Alliance in response to the impact that events at the Bartel Pritchard Square entrance to the park have had on the adjacent residential community. At this meeting, Prospect Park Administrator Emily Lloyd committed to several meaningful steps that will reduce the impact of events beginning with next year’s season of events as permits have already been granted to this year’s event organizers. Probably the most significant concession is that they will no longer allow bands and DJ’s to entertain from this location. Meanwhile, Ms. Lloyd also offered to meet with residents on site during the early morning weekend hours when these events are taking place so they could all assess the impact in real time.

Chairperson Kummer announced that the Borough President has appointed 7 new members to our Community Board who were invited to join us at tonight’s meeting. Our new members include: Eric Andersen (BP), a resident of the northern section of 4th Avenue; Paul Basile (BP), one of our Gowanus industrial business owners and current member of our Transportation Committee; Eladia Causil-Rodriguez (BP), a resident of north Park Slope; Marco Chirico (BP), a resident of Carroll Gardens; Joni Kletter (39), a resident of south Park Slope and member of our Parks Committee; Lance Pinn (BP), the owner of Brooklyn Boulders across from the Thomas Greene Playground in Gowanus, and; Matthew Silverman (BP), resident of north Park Slope and long- standing member of our EWCDH and PSEPPL committees. Our current Board Member roster totals 48 members.

Chairperson Kummer reported to the Board that Rick Luftglass has graciously taken on the position of Housing Chair in addition to his many other duties as Chair of our Budget and Economic Development Committees.

2

Chairperson Kummer reminded all Board Members that the district office has sent out Committee Membership Request Forms for our upcoming 2012-13 session which begins on September 1st. He appealed to all board members to take the few minutes to complete and return the form. The district office also uses these forms to update any personal contact information that may have changed – phone number, address, email address, etc. – so that their records are up-to-date and accurate. Committee assignment notifications will be sent out in August.

Chairperson Kummer welcomed back Assistant District Manager Leroy Branch who was unable to attend last month’s meeting because of some elective surgery.

Chairperson Kummer informed the Board that our State legislature has drawn up new lines for our State and Federal representatives which go into effect January 1st. Park Slope will experience a fairly substantial change in local elected leadership as most of the area that is currently being represented by State Senator Eric Adams will be transferred over to the district currently represented by State Senator Kevin Parker. Senator Parker has recently met with District Manager Craig Hammerman and me to introduce himself and get to know more about what our issues are. I’d like to publicly thank him for demonstrating initiative and reaching out to us.

Chairperson Kummer advised the Board that the EWCDH committee will be holding our second of two Public Housing Town Hall meetings, this time for residents of Red Hook East and West Houses. I’d like to thank our committee Co-Chairs Richard Bashner and Rick Luftglass, and our District Manager for taking time out of their weekend to join me on a site visit so we would have a better idea of what some of the issues are. I’d also like to especially thank our former Board Member Judith Dailey and Red Hook activist Wally Bazemore, for meeting with us, taking us on a tour, and running through a list of issues that really helped bring us up-to-speed. After our meeting Monday night we will begin the process of compiling our notes and preparing a report which we hope to present and discuss with NYCHA Chairperson John Rhea.

Chairperson Kummer reported to the Board that the second State Liquor Authority hearing for the Barclays Center liquor license is scheduled for 7:00pm on Wednesday, June 20 at the SLA’s office in Harlem. While we not pleased with the surprise news that the applicant intends to serve alcohol one-hour past event times in the limited, exclusive portions of the facility, we are standing by our resolution and in general are very pleased that the applicant has agreed to our proposed stipulation that all alcohol service be cut-off by 2am. We are also pleased to note that the Empire State Development Corporation and Barclays Center have scheduled a kick-off interagency meeting to begin actively focusing on operational concerns so the security plans, sanitation plans, transportation plans, etc. all have a proper venue for continued dialogue moving forward.

Chairperson Daniel Kummer ended his report by extending condolences on behalf of the Community Board to Congresswomen Nydia Velázquez and her family on the recent passing of her mother.

DISTRICT MANAGER’S REPORT………………………………………….…….CRAIG HAMMERMAN

District Manager Craig Hammerman began his report by informing the board that the Department of Transportation has determined that a speed hump on Prospect Place between Flatbush Avenue and 6th Avenue is unwarranted at this time.

District Manager Hammerman reported to the Board that the MTA has advised the district office that the B103 bus route has been revised. The B103 will return to its former path via 3 rd Avenue when traveling from the Prospect Expressway to Flatbush Avenue. There will be no bus stops along the 3rd Avenue route; however, one westbound bus stop on 3rd Avenue and State Street will be restored. They plan to implement this revision in early June 2012.

3

District Manager Hammerman also reported to that he and Assistant District Manager Leroy Branch participated in a site visit with DEP and DOT to discuss our many concerns regarding flooding on 9 th Street as well as 2nd Avenue, i.e. missing sidewalks and inadequate drainage. We asked them to start working up a scope with some estimates on how much money it would cost to rectify the problems. Once we receive those estimates we will lobby our elected officials to allocate funds by way of a capital project to fix the problems.

District Manager Hammerman ended his report by informing the board that he also participated in a site visit with DEP at the Shaft 22B site which is located at 4th Avenue and Sackett Street, which will become a community garden once all work has been completed. The project has falling behind and DEP has offered some interim space for the gardeners. They are in the process of signing an agreement for not less than 8 years under a City Green Thumb-like program. If all things go well the community gardeners will be in the space sometime in the fall.

Chairperson Kummer recognized Manhattan Borough President Scott Stringer

Manhattan Borough President Scott Stringer began by thanking the Board Members for their tireless efforts in representing the residents of the district. He stated that we need to begin to look at the future of Community Boards in a different way. This is the 20th anniversary in which Community boards have not received an increase in their budgets because they are not valued at City Hall. We need to create a citywide planning agenda which is neighborhood based and the community boards are the best vehicle to accomplish this. We have to have a charter revision commission to take a look at how neighborhoods can play a better role in figuring out what gets built in their communities. We need to make sure there are more resources at the local level where by each district office has a professional urban planner on staff because local communities do not have the expertise in land use, zoning and planning to be able to negotiate with large developers. Community Board 6 should be proud that they have an Urban Planning Graduate that has been working with District Manager Hammerman over the last few years. Because of his diligence he was able to bring that fellow program to Brooklyn. He ended by stating that he looks forward to working with Community Boards in the next year to get the resources that would make the above-mentioned items come to fruition.
