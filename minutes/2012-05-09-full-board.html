CHAIRPERSON’S REPORT………………………………………………………....….DANIEL KUMMER

Chairperson Daniel Kummer began his report by extending condolences on behalf of the Community Board to Assistant District Manager Leroy Branch and his family on the recent passing of his sister, Tammy Stokes. Our District Manager has contact information for the family if anyone would like to send them a personal message, note or mass card. Also, the Chairperson announced that our Assistant District Manager will be out of the office for the next few weeks on a personal matter and that you can contact Craig directly for any service delivery matter in his absence.

Chairperson Kummer thanked Parks Committee Co-Chair Nica Lalli and our District Manager for attending a recent meeting at the Prospect Park Alliance in response to the impact that events at the Bartel Pritchard Square entrance to the park have had on the adjacent residential community. At this meeting, Prospect Park Administrator Emily Lloyd committed to several meaningful steps that will reduce the impact of events beginning with next year’s season of events as permits have already been granted to this year’s event organizers. Probably the most significant concession is that they will no longer allow bands and DJ’s to entertain from this location. Meanwhile, Ms. Lloyd also offered to meet with residents on site during the early morning weekend hours when these events are taking place so they could all assess the impact in real time and react to it together.

Chairperson Kummer went on to thank both Parks Committee Co-Chairs Nica Lalli and Glenn Kelly for attending a meeting last week at Brooklyn Borough Hall where our Red Hook elected officials were briefed by representatives for the Parks Department and Xavier High School on the upcoming plans to rehabilitate Red Hook Field #3. The plans were well received by the officials and there was a good assortment of user groups represented in the room as well who are all looking forward to the improvements, especially the extended hours that the new lighting will bring to the field. Brooklyn CB6 voted to endorse these plans at our March general meeting.

The Chairperson also thanked Environmental Protection Committee Chair Gary Reilly who attended a tour of the Gowanus Canal Pump Station and Flushing Tunnel along with our district office staff. The tour, given by the Department of Environmental Protection spotlighted their ongoing Gowanus Canal Facilities Upgrade project which, when completed, will substantially reduce the amount of Combined Sewer Overflow events at the Gowanus Canal and improve the reliability of the pump station and flushing tunnel mechanisms.

For any interested members of the board, at 11:00am this coming Friday, May 11th, the Parks Department is holding a ribbon-cutting ceremony at the J.J. Byrne Playground at Washington Park. Anyone wanting to attend can just show up to The Old Stone House which is located inside the park along 3 rd Street between 4th and 5th Avenues. 2

As I reported at a previous general meeting, our Housing Committee will be hosting a series of two Town Hall meetings for our residents in public housing to solicit their feedback on some of the problems they have living in public housing, and get their ideas for how to improve the public housing experience. We are also visiting our public housing developments in the district and meeting with the tenant association leadership to capture their input and suggestions as well. I’d like to thank Rick Luftglass, Richard Bashner and our Assistant District Manager Leroy Branch for joining me last weekend on our visit to Gowanus, Wyckoff Gardens, and 572 Warren Street Houses, and a special thanks to Marguerite Scott, Charlene Nimmons and Harriet Hughes, the respective TA Presidents, for taking the time to meet with us. We took pictures and are keeping a running list of issues and expect to have a detailed report put together by the summer. Meanwhile, the first Town Hall meeting will be held at 6:30pm on Monday, May 21st at the Cuyler-Warren Church, 450 Warren Street, and the second Town Hall meeting will be 6:30pm on Monday, June 18th in Red Hook.

Progress on the Barclays Center arena is moving steadily toward completion for the anticipated September opening. The Empire State Development Corporation and the 3 affected Community Boards (Nos. 2, 6 & 8) are co-sponsoring a forum on the soon-to-be released Transportation Demand Management (TDM) study being developed by Sam Schwartz Engineering, Forest City Ratner’s traffic consultant. The TDM aims to reduce vehicular traffic to the arena and minimize the effects of the cars that do come. Recently, at a meeting attended by our district manager, ESD President Ken Adams announced that the size of the parking lot was being reduced by half – from 1,100 to roughly 550 spaces – in accordance with local residents who have been concerned that providing parking is an enticement to patrons to drive to the arena. ESD has found that sufficient private, off-street capacity exists within a reasonable radius around the arena that will allow them to reduce the capacity of their lot. Anyone interested in the TDM forum should know that it’s taking place at 6:00pm on Tuesday, May 22nd at Brooklyn Borough Hall.

Lastly, this is a reminder that in early June the district office will be sending out Committee Membership Request Forms for our 2012-13 session that begins September 1st. The Chair explained that he makes a concerted effort to give board members their top choices – a process that’s made a lot easier when our members return their forms in a timely manner. Consider this an urgent appeal to all members to take the few minutes to complete and return the form. The district office also uses these forms to update any personal contact information that may have changed – phone numbers, addresses, email addresses, etc. – so that their records are as up-to-date and accurate as possible. Notifications of committee assignments will be sent out in early August.

DISTRICT MANAGER’S REPORT………………………………………….…….CRAIG HAMMERMAN

District Manager Craig Hammerman began his report by announcing to the board that the City’s Bike Share program, which is scheduled to begin this summer, will not be coming to our district immediately. Instead, the Department of Transportation has decided to roll out the program in two phases – and the Brooklyn CB6 district should receive it by spring 2013. In the meantime, the department has committed to returning to us, probably sometime in the fall, to review and finalize the locations for the docking stations which is a vital component of the program. Bike Share will make approximately 10,000 bicycles available at over 600 docking stations where people can effectively rent a bike and return it to any other docking station to encourage cycling by making bicycles available on a broad basis. The program will initially be rolled out in Manhattan and Brooklyn CB2 in the first phase.

Within the next few weeks we should expect to see 2 new Green Streets installations in our district at Union Street and 3rd Avenue, and at Hamilton Avenue/Henry Street/Luquer Street. These locations were built as part of the Downtown Brooklyn Traffic Calming project, and space has been left for plantings that will be installed and maintained by the Parks Department.

3

We are in the process of setting up a special second committee meeting in June for our Environmental Protection Committee who will be briefed on the Department of Environmental Protection’s High-Level Storm Sewer project, a Capital project that is being designed to help alleviate some of the chronic flooding conditions that effect the 3rd and 4th Avenue corridors around Carroll Street. The department will present their plans to the community who will have a firsthand opportunity to learn about the nature and impact of the impending work.
