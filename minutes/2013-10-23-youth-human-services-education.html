MS88 would be good but need to look at what it entails (funding, commitment, etc.). May have to start as afterschool program but not in CB6. MS447 is another good contender – STEM school (District 15) - Jamika Mathieson – Superintendent of High Schools. - Millennium Brooklyn at John Jay (Lisa Gioe) - PS282, 266, 133 – all schools within CB6 (District 13)  Have had discussions about programs. Working with NYU-Poly and work with Brooklyn Tech.

 Less structure of programs in District 13.  PS266 is small middle school – funding issues.  Room for programs to come in, less intensive than NYU Poly.  PS133 has tech infrastructure but not robotics.  PS29 has new principal, older building so may lack tech.  PS261 good match on Pacific/Smith streets – newer building.  PS15 Red Hook would be a great school – Principal is great.
