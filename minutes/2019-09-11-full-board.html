CHAIRPERSON’S REPORT…………………………………………...PETER FLEMING, BOARD CHAIR

Board Chair Peter Fleming offered only brief remarks, welcoming Board Members back from the summer hiatus and remarking on the death of a member of the public who had a long-standing relationship with the Board, several Board members, and the broader political community within the footprint of the district. Mr. Fleming then called the names of the newest Board Members and allowed them to offer a brief introduction. The Chair closed his remarks by announcing his plans to travel to Florida immediately after the meeting for the purpose of meeting his grand-niece, who had just been born.

DISTRICT MANAGER’S REPORT……………………..MICHAEL RACIOPPO, DISTRICT MANAGER

Mr. Racioppo made brief comments also welcoming the Board back from its summer hiatus and outlining a few of the upcoming committee meetings. Mr. Racioppo also announced that the Board’s October General Meeting, which is typically on the second Wednesday of every month, would take place on Thursday, October 10 due to Wednesday October 9 being Yom Kippur.
