Presentation and review of a Certificate of Appropriateness application submitted to the Landmarks Preservation Commission for the installation of a rooftop addition at 536 1st Street (between 8th Avenue/Prospect Park West), Park Slope Historic District.

Question about chimney five pipe mock-up. No mock-up was done. Presentation was made by the Design Team. Bob Levine requested a mock-up be done and the neighbors notified.

Peter Fleming made a motion to approve and Debbie Scotto seconded the motion.

VOTE: 5…..YEAS 2…..NAYS 2…..ABSTENTIONS

MOTION PASSED

Presentation and review of a Certificate of Appropriateness application submitted to the Landmarks Preservation Commission for the installation of an elevator shaft at 188 Prospect Park West (between 14th/15th Streets), Park Slope Historic District.

Presentation was made by the Architect.

Mark Shames made a motion to approve as presented and Roy Sloane seconded the motion.

VOTE: 9…..YEAS 0…..NAYS

MOTION PASSED: UNANIMOUSLY

Presentation and review of a Certificate of Appropriateness application submitted to the Landmarks Preservation Commission for the installation of a rear extension at 69 7th Avenue (between Lincoln/Berkeley Places), Park Slope Historic District.

Presentation was made by the Architect.

Peter Fleming made a motion to approve the structure with the condition that all mechanicals are located on main roof of existing house and that the door at second floor be made a window with no access. Allison Reeves seconded the motion.

VOTE: 8…..YEAS 1…..NAY

MOTION PASSED

There being no further business to come before the committee, the meeting was adjourned.
