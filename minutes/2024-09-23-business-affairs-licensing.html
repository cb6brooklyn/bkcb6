Business A@airs: Spring Bank

• Reviewed application for a bank (Spring Bank) to open up in Red Hook and be part of

the Banking Development District program. Members of the company came and

spoke to the committee updating attendees their outreach and what they plan to do

for the community, given there is no bank in the area.

• Leroy proposed the motion

• Ed second the motion

• Unanimous acceptance; all Board Members were in favor

Licensing Applicant: 517 Court Street – Loafers Cocktail Bar

• Brendan Donohoe is the owner

• First queer bar in the area and plans to show lots of queer history throughout the bar

• No outdoor seating

• Aray proposed the motion

• Ed second the motion

• Unanimous acceptance; all Board Members were in favor

Licensing Applicant: 2-8 Prospect Pl.

• Refael Hasid is the owner

• Has been open for over 20 years and has a great relationship with community

• They’re renivotating after so many years

• Community member has concern about the noise level, and Refael mention that

they close at 10 and they won’t have outdoor seating so they noise level should not

be a problem

• Ed proposed the motion

• Ana second the motion

• Unanimous acceptance; all Board Members were in favor
