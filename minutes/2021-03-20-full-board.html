CHAIRPERSON’S REPORT…………………………………………...PETER FLEMING, BOARD CHAIR

Board Chair Peter Fleming opened the meeting with very brief remarks, commenting on the presumed timeline for and likely digital nature of the proposed Gowanus Rezoning, while stating that the Board’s obvious wish is to hold more traditional, in-person meetings sometime in the 2021 calendar year.

DISTRICT MANAGER’S REPORT……………………..MICHAEL RACIOPPO, DISTRICT MANAGER

District Manager Michael Racioppo also offered brief remarks, offering his perspective on interactions with the Department of City Planning specific to the proposed Gowanus Rezoning. Mr. Racioppo closed his remarks by offering attendees ways to sign up for his newsletter.

EWCDH COMMITTEE……………………………………………………...ARIEL KRASNOW, CO-CHAIR

………….RICK LUFTGLASS, CO-CHAIR

…….MARK SHAMES, CO-CHAIR

Economics, Waterfront, Community Development & Housing Committee co-chair Ariel Krasnow reported that the committee discussed and voted to endorse H.R. 235, the Public Housing Emergency Response Act, a congressional bill introduced by Congresswoman Nydia Velázquez authorizing emergency funding for critical, long over-due capital repairs to public housing.

Motion: To support H.R 235, the Public Housing Emergency Response Act.

VOTE: 38 YEAS, 0 NAYS, 0 ABSTENTIONS

MOTION PASSED UNANIMOUSLY

TIME: 7:00 P.M.

LANDMARKS/LAND USE COMMITTEE………….…………...DAVID BRIGGS, LANDMARKS CHAIR

…………………………...MARK SHAMES, LAND USE CHAIR

Land Use Committee Chair Mark Shames reported that the committee reviewed and disapproved an application to the Board of Standards and Appeals under section 71-21 of the Zoning Resolution of the City of New York to permit the building of a 14-story mixed use development in an M1-1 zoning district contrary to Zoning Resolution section 42-10 at 145 Wolcott Street.

Motion: To disapprove the application to the Board of Standards and Appeals under section 71-21 of the Zoning Resolution of the City of New York to permit the building of a 14-story mixed use development in an M1-1 zoning district contrary to Zoning Resolution section 42-10 at 145 Wolcott Street.

VOTE: 37 YEAS, 0 NAYS, 1 ABSTENTION

MOTION PASSED

TIME: 7:35 P.M.
