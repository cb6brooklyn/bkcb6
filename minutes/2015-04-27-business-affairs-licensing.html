*** M I N U T E S ***

The meeting was called to order at 6:43pm.

Presentation and review of a new on-premises liquor license application submitted to the State Liquor Authority on behalf of YNWA Corp. at 428 Van Brunt Street (between 4th/5th Avenues).

Damian Selczak of YNWA Corp. was present.  Previously known as Black Sheep Pub  Will not permit people to use patio  Will maintain sound levels – will purchase a sound meter  Will close by 11:00pm  Also owner of the Bar 718 in CB7. Have been there for 5 years.  Printed letter from local police re. Bar 718.

Comments from the committee: - Previously the bar was Black Sheep Pub, keep it clean even if it is not open yet - Glenn Kelly asked about outside space – responded that they will not be serving on the patio. - No live music even though they have checked it on the application, - _________ on sidewalk - Glenn Kelly suggested that they remove the outdoor space. Applicant agreed to remove outdoor specification from the application. - Applicant will remove live music. Committee input – no amplified music can be used if windows are open. Will not exceed 45 decibels.

A motion was made to approve the application with the following stipulations:

1. No alcohol should be consumed outdoors 2. No live music 3. Outdoor space to be removed from diagram.

MOTION PASSED UNANIMOUSLY
