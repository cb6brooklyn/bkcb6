CHAIRPERSON’S REPORT……………………………...……..………….....…...RICHARD S. BASHNER

Chairperson Richard S. Bashner began his report by reporting to the Board that Board Member Vanessa Twyford has resigned citing competing personal and professional demands on her time. He thanked her for her service to our community. He also extended condolences to her on behalf of the board for the loss of her grandmother, Connie Gibbons, who was one of the founding members of the First Place and Summit Street Tri- Block Association, which became the Carroll Gardens Neighborhood Association, one of our foremost active civic groups in our district. Our roster is now at 48 members officially.

Chairperson Bashner informed that Board that Claire Hudson from Assemblywoman Joan Millman's office has moved to the west coast and will no longer be representing the Assemblywoman when Tamar Smith is unable to attend. In addition Michael Curtin, our representative from Council Member Brad Lander’s Office, has taken a new position and will be leaving the Councilman’s Office by the end of the month. Jonah Blumstein from the Councilman’s Office will be taking over Michael’s slot. He thanked Claire and Michael for all the help they have given us and our board office, and offered his best wishes to Jonah in stepping up to this new position.

Chairperson Bashner reported that Thursday, December 16th, commemorates the 50th anniversary of the plane crash in Park Slope. On the morning of December 16, 1960, 134 people lost their lives when United Airlines Flight 826 and TWA Flight 266 collided over the skies of Staten Island. The United aircraft crashed in Park Slope, at the intersection of Sterling Place and Seventh Avenue. The Green-Wood Cemetery, along with the Park Slope Civic Council and New York Methodist Hospital, are holding a memorial service and will be unveiling a new 8-foot tall granite monument near the grave where the unidentified remains of victims have rested for the last half century. The service will take place at 9:45am on Thursday, December 16, at the Green- Wood Cemetery at 25th Street and 5th Avenue.

Chairperson Bashner also reported to the Board that on November 18th, our incoming Chairperson Daniel Kummer, District Manager Craig Hammerman and he went to meet with City Council Member James Vacca who chairs the City Council’s Transportation Committee to talk about our experiences working with the Department of Transportation in planning bicycle lanes. Many people have been reaching out to Council Member Vacca from our community, and we thought it was important for him to get the history of our involvement in the Prospect Park West bike reconfiguration, along with our general work with the department in planning bike lanes, from us directly. The Council Member was also in the process of planning a hearing on bicycling in New York City, which has been scheduled for 10:00am tomorrow, December 9 th at 250 Broadway, 16th floor. The Chair has asked District Manager Hammerman to present testimony at that hearing to share with the Council some of the experiences over the years. The week after our meeting the Council Member also visited our district and got to walk Prospect Park West with our district manager.

Chairperson Bashner also informed the Board that the results of the Prospect Park West Reconfiguration community survey that was conducted by Council Members Lander and Levin, with help from our district office is ready for review. Council Member Lander’s Office will be formally presenting the results at our next Transportation Committee meeting. District Manager Hammerman sent out an email yesterday with the link to the survey results when it was released so that anyone wishing to can review the document in advance of our meeting.

Chairperson Bashner ended his report by reminding everyone that the CB6 holiday party is next Tuesday, December 14th from 6-9pm at The DRAM Shop, 339 9th Street (between 5th/6th Avenues) in Park Slope. The cost is $30 per person for food and soda, with a cash bar. Any proceeds from the event above our costs will go to Friends of Brooklyn Community Board 6, Inc., and our non-profit organization. Since your check will be a 2

tax-deductible donation to our organization to the extent the law allows, we are asking folks to consider rounding up their contributions above the costs of the party – to $50 or even $100 if that’s at all possible.

__________________________________________________________________________________________ DISTRICT MANAGER’S REPORT……………..................................................…CRAIG HAMMERMAN

District Manager Craig Hammerman began his report by informing the board that Board Member Nica Lalli and he had attended an on-site scoping meeting for the DiMatina Dog Run. Both Borough President Marty Markowitz and Councilman Brad Lander each set aside $225,000 to redo the run, giving the project a total budget of $450K. The Parks Department will come to a future committee meeting to present and discuss the new design.

District Manager Hammerman informed the Board that Assistant District Manager Leroy Branch and he met with the Department of Buildings Borough Commissioner Thomas Fariello to hear from him about a new system he is putting in place for the borough. They will be seeking input from the district office regarding request of contractors who would like to work outside of the normal working hours. Their normal hours are 7am – 6pm, Mon. – Fri. and 10am – 6pm on Sat. He would like to develop a draft of a policy giving the district office guidelines on how to participate in this new procedure. He will be sending a draft to the Permits & Licenses Committee for their recommendations.

District Manager Hammerman advised the board that he attended an on-site meeting at 4th Avenue and Sackett Street, which is the DEP Shaft 22B site where the Third Water Tunnel passes under our district. That site was originally envisioned to be a community garden when DEP finishes their work. In the interim, since it will be another 8 years before the community will get the site, DEP is open to the idea to give the community a portion of the site to use before they finish their work.. If everything works out the community could have partial use of the site by this spring.

District Manager Hammerman ended his report by informing the Board that he would be on vacation the week of December 20th. He also asked volunteers to help at the holiday party on December 14th.

First Vice Chairperson Raymond Lohier and incoming Chairperson Daniel Kummer expressed warm wishes and kind words to Chairperson Bashner, thanking him for his service to the district as the Community Board’s Chairperson for the last 4 years.

 COMMUNITY SESSION 

Jordan Zinovich, resident in the Cobble Hill Towers spoke to the Board in reference to the replacement of the windows in the Towers. He stated that about 150 families will be directly affected by this action in that they will be charged for the replacement of the windows. The residents were not notified by the developer to attend any of the meetings related to these changes. He was able to go to LPC and present a statement on behalf of the residents and would like to give that prepared statement to the Board. Also, they believe that the report 7

submitted to LPC on the scope of the work by the architect firm of the developer is inadequate in view of the residents involved because some want to buy their apartments and some want to remain tenants. They have commissioned their own engineers report about the condition of the buildings, which the submitted at the LPC hearing. Their report addresses the fact that the developer is only replacing the aluminum frame of the windows and not the wooden frame, which are badly, deteriorate. This item was approved by LPC and their staff will monitor the work very carefully. He also submitted to the Board the resident’s engineers report.
