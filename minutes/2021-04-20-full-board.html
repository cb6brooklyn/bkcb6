CHAIRPERSON’S REPORT…………………………………………...PETER FLEMING, BOARD CHAIR

Board Chair Peter Fleming opened the meeting with brief remarks, announcing two new committee chair appointments: Board Member Samara Trilling was appointed as the chair of Community Development in the EWCDH committee and Board Member Alec Shierenbeck was appointed Land Use chair in the LMLU committee. Mr. Fleming noted that Mr. Shierenbeck was replacing Board Member Mark Shames, who asked to step down as chair. Mr. Fleming then recognized Mr. Shames, who offered his thoughts on his stepping down as chair. Mr. Fleming thanked Mr. Shames for his service as chair.

Mr. Fleming closed his remarks by updating the Board and attendees on the on-going developments relative to the anticipated Gowanus Rezoning review and hearing, including a potential outdoor meeting, possibly in J.J. Byrne Park, to be facilitated by the Department of City Planning along side a live streaming. 1

DISTRICT MANAGER’S REPORT……………………MICHAEL RACIOPPO, DISTRICT MANAGER

District Manager Michael Racioppo also offered brief remarks, including commenting on the new committee chair appointments. Mr. Racioppo offered voter registration and absentee ballot deadlines.
