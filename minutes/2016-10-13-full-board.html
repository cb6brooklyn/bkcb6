With Chairperson Sayar Lonial delayed due to subway delays, 1st Vice Chair Mike Racioppo proceeded with the meeting, first recognizing State Assembly Member Jo Anne Simon.

Assembly Member Simon opened her remarks by outlining the importance of voting, as she reminded attendees that Election Day falls before the next general board meeting. Ms. Simon stated that she recently attended a voting reform summit at Mayor de Blasio’s office and outlined her support for early voting, which many other states have been doing successfully and without issue for quite some time. She also stated that she supports various versions of automatic registration measures, as well as same-day registration and no-excuse absentee voting.

The Assembly Member closed her remarks by announcing her office’s annual free flu shot days on October 26th and 27th from 10am to 2pm at 341 Smith Street. Ms. Simon directed anyone interested to contact her community representative Jim Harvey, who was in attendance at the meeting.

ELECTION COMMITTEE………………………………………….………………CRAIG HAMMERMAN

District Manager Hammerman, giving the report on behalf of Election Committee Chair Peter Fleming who could not attend the meeting, offered the parameters of the election process. The District Manager directed board members to an email sent out by the Election Committee which solicited written nominations for the board’s five officer positions.

Mr. Hammerman noted that all of the current officers were nominated and that all are eligible to run for another one-year term. No additional written nominations were received.

Mr. Hammerman outlined the remaining process for nomination and election. He stated that the floor will be opened for nominations at the November general board meeting. The full slate of candidates will be named by the end of the Election Committee report at the November general board meeting. An election, if necessary, will be held at the December general board meeting.

The District Manager closed the election committee report by offering a reminder that all board officer positions are one-year terms with a maximum of four consecutive terms served in any one office.

CHAIRPERSON’S REPORT………………………………………….………………….....SAYAR LONIAL

Chairperson Sayar Lonial apologized for his tardiness and thanked the 1st Vice Chair and the District Manager for starting the meeting in his absence.

The Chair opened his report by thanking District Manager Hammerman and Board Members Mike Racioppo and Eric McClure for attending a meeting organized by numerous elected officials to consider the MTA’s proposal to cut F train service in the district. The proposal, which would convert some F trains to express during rush hour, would effectively half the district’s F train service during rush hour, as every other F train would bypass every station within CB6, except for 7th Avenue. CB6 has invited the MTA to brief the board on their plans, but a date has not yet been set. Mr. Lonial thanked Council Member Lander, Senator Squadron, and Assembly Member Simon for attending the meeting, as well.

3

Mr. Lonial then highlighted a few October calendar events. First, the Economic/Waterfront/Community Development & Housing Committee will be hosting the Carroll Gardens Association, headed by Board Member Vilma Heramia. CGA will be reporting on a new organizing effort they’ve undertaken called the Southwest Brooklyn Tenants Union. Second, the Parks/Recreation & Environmental Protection Committee will be receiving an update from the Department of Environmental Protection on how the City intends to proceed with the construction of the Gowanus Canal outfall retention tanks now that the city and Federal Government have entered into a consent decree on their construction. Third, the upcoming Youth/Human Services/Education Committee meeting will hear from a new service provider who intends to take over the remaining space at the Parole Center on 2nd Avenue and 6th Street and offer specific services for treatment for substance abuse, mental health, and medical care to substance abusers. Lastly, the Transportation/Public Safety Committee is hosting a Public Hearing regarding CitiBike station deployment within the district. The goal of the hearing is to gather feedback on how the board can help improve the program; DOT has declined to attend to hear direct feedback or field questions.

Mr. Lonial offered a moment of silence in remembrance of the departed Kings County District Attorney Ken Thompson, who passed away from cancer suddenly this week.

The Chair closed his report by stating that he would now enter into the Budget Committee report on behalf of Budget Chair Rick Luftglass, who could not attend this evening’s meeting.

BUDGET COMMITTEE………………………………………………..SAYAR LONIAL, BOARD CHAIR

Board Chair Sayar Lonial reported that at the last Executive/Budget Committee meeting, Fiscal Year 2018 Capital and Expense budget priorities were reviewed and prioritized relative to some prior priorities being funded in the previous Fiscal Year.

Mr. Lonial reported on some changes from last year’s Capital Budget priorities, highlighting that last year’s number one request, “Area Wide Repairs to Gowanus Watershed Flooding Problems”, was funded and included in a budget for the City’s Fiscal Year 2017 budget. The new proposed number one request is “Recommission FDNY Engine Company 204”, due to the accelerated buildout of downtown Brooklyn, anticipated population growth resulting from the Fortis Development Group’s redevelopment of LICH properties, and the rapid expansion of population density within our district. After a few brief comments, the chair called the question on the Capital Budget Priorities.

Motion: To adopt the Fiscal Year 2018 Capital Budget Priorities.

VOTE: 34 YEAS, 0 NAYS, 0 ABSTENTIONS

MOTION PASSED UNANIMOUSLY

TIME: 7:25 P.M.

Board Chair Sayar Lonial then reported on the Fiscal Year 2018 Expense Budget Priorities, remarking that this year’s item number seven, “Creation of a Quality of Life Detail for the NYPD’s 78th Precinct”, is new. Mr. Lonial remarked that this request is relevant to the rapid increase in density of bars in the North Park Slope area, particularly relative to the area immediately south of the Barclays Center near Flatbush Avenue.

After fielding a few questions relevant to the importance of ranking items, the Chair called the question on the Expense Budget Priorities.

Motion: To adopt the Fiscal Year 2018 Expense Budget Priorities.

Chairperson Sayar Lonial recognized Council Member Brad Lander.

Council Member Lander opened his remarks by echoing Mr. Lonial’s thoughts on the late Kings County District Attorney Ken Thompson and praising the work Mr. Thompson did while in office.

TRANSPORTATION/PUBLIC SAFETY……………TOM MISKEL, CO-CHAIR, TRANSPORTATION

……….ERIC McCLURE, CO-CHAIR, TRANSPORTATION

………MATTHEW SILVERMAN, CHAIR, PUBLIC SAFETY

Transportation Committee Co-Chair Eric McClure reported on a proposal by the Department of Transportation for a new bike corral at 341 5th Avenue. Mr. McClure outlined the committee’s recommendation to approve the proposal on the condition that letters of support from local businesses be provided to the district office in advance of the October General Board Meeting.

A motion was made by Board Member Joanna Oltman Smith to accept the committee’s recommendation as presented and to request that DOT come back in a year to report on the use of the bike corral. Board Member Tom Miskel seconded the motion. Board Chair Sayar Lonial opened the floor for discussion on the motion.

During the process of discussion of the previous motion, the fact that no letters have yet been provided to the board prompted Board Member Jerry Armer to make a motion to table this vote at this time. Mr. Armer’s motion was seconded by Board Member Mark Shames. This being a procedural motion, it took precedent over the earlier motion, and it does not allow for discussion or abstentions.

Motion: By Board Member Jerry Armer; seconded by Board Member Mark Shames. To table the Department of Transportation’s proposal for a new bike corral at 341 5th Avenue due to their not adhering to the committee’s request for letters of approval from surrounding businesses.

VOTE: 29 YEAS, 3 NAYS, 0 ABSTENTIONS

MOTION TO TABLE RESOLUTION PASSES
