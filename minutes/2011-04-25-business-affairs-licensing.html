 MINUTES 

Meeting called to order at 6:40 pm. Quorum in attendance.

Presentation and review of a new unenclosed sidewalk café permit application

submitted to the Department of Consumer Affairs on behalf of Capodimonte LLC dba Apertivo at 279 5th Avenue (northwest corner 5th Avenue/1st Street, to permit 11 tables and 29 seats.

A motion was made by Tom Miskel to approve the request and it was seconded by Gary Reilly.

VOTE: 8…..YEAS 0…..NAES

MOTION APPROVED UNANIMOUSLY

Presentation and continued review of an on-premises liquor license application

submitted to the State Liquor Authority on behalf of BBBrooklyn LLC dba Beauty Bar at 249 5th Avenue (between Carroll St/Garfield Pl).

The Beauty Bar features manicures while you drink. They are open Sunday through Thursday from 4pm to 2am and Friday and Saturday from 4pm to 4am. A motion was made by Tom Miskel to approve the application and seconded by Matt Silverman.

VOTE: 8…..YEAS 0…..NAES

MOTION APPROVED UNANIMOUSLY

Presentation and continued review of an on-premises liquor license application

submitted to the State Liquor Authority on behalf of 604 Pacific LLC dba Players at 604 Pacific Street (between Flatbush/4th Avenues).

There was a question of when the liquor license posting was done for Players Sports Bar & Grill. Many community residents are opposing.

Presentation and review of Assembly Bill 4667-A, an act to amend the alcohol

beverage control law in relation to rooftop and rear yard bars.

A motion to support the bill was made by Gary Reilly and seconded by Tom Miskel.

VOTE: 6…..YEAS 1…..NAY 1…..ABSTENTION

MOTION APPROVED

At this time, a motion was made by Tom Miskel and seconded by Terry Alexander to accept the minutes of the previous meeting held on March 28, 2011.

MOTION APPROVED

Presentation and review of a renewal unenclosed sidewalk café permit application submitted to the Department of Consumer Affairs on behalf of Paradis 5, Inc. dba Moutarde at 239 5th Avenue (northeast corner 5th Avenue/Carroll Street), to permit 10 tables and 20 seats.

A motion was made by Pauline Blake to hold over this application request since the principals failed to show up. The motion was seconded by Terry Alexander.

MOTION APPROVED

motion was seconded by Pauline Blake.
