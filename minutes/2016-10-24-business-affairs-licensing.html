*** MEETING MINUTES***

The meeting was called to order at 6:33pm. Minutes being taken by Glenn Kelly. There were 5 of 9 members present.

Presentation and review of a new on-premises liquor license application submitted to the State Liquor Authority on behalf of Nighthawk Cinema at 188 Prospect Park West (between 14 th/15th Streets).

This is the old Pavilion Theatre. The attorney, Anthony Caraballo and the owner, Matthew Viragh were

present to request a full liquor license.

They have been operating in Williamsburg since 2011. They will have full food service in and outside of the theatre. Over 18 years of age only except if accompanied by an adult chaperone. There will be servers in the theatre at all times. They plan to open in September of 2017 after substantial renovation.

Glenn Kelly made a motion to approve as submitted, seconded by Tom Miskel.

VOTE: 6…..YEAS 0…..NAYS 0…..ABSTENTIONS

MOTION APPROVED: UNANIMOUSLY

Presentation and review of a new on-premises liquor license application submitted to the State Liquor Authority on behalf of La Royale Beer Burger House at 492 5th Avenue (between 11th/12th Streets).

The attorney, Anthony Caraballo and the owners, Anna and Saliha Reckovic were present. The business was called Mazzini licensed since 2011.

A full liquor license was requested. They already operate several establishments with 25 years of experience and no SLA violations. They agree to close all doors and windows at 10pm. They have no outdoor space and no plan for a sidewalk café. They will only have background music.

Tom Miskel made a motion to approve as presented, seconded by Pauline Blake with door and window closure at 10pm.

VOTE: 7…..YEAS 0…..NAYS 0…..ABSENTIONS

MOTION APPROVED: UNANIMOUSLY

Motion was made to approve the minutes of the previous meeting held on September 26, 2016.

MOTION APPROVED: UNANIMOUSLY

The meeting was adjourned at 6:54pm.
