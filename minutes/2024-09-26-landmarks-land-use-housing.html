Landmarks

309 Carroll Street - Application for Certificate of Appropriateness for the rear facade, yard deck,

window replacement, and hardscape upgrades.

Presentation made by architect.

Questions

1. Height of wall. It will increase by 6”.

2. Door design on 2nd level. Not detailed, but applicant provided a verbal description.

3. Location of property line since applicant is proposing to extend step. Line is at east edge of

planting bed.

4. Thickness of bluestone cap at top of wall and amount of overhang. It is proposed to be 6” with

1/2” overhang.

Jerry - Motion to conditionally approve. Reduce thickness of stone cap to 2-3” so it is more

consistent with details at other nearby properties. Increase overhang to at least 1” to allow for

drip detail on the underside.

Maddie - second

7 in favor.

0 abstentions.

0 against.

Motion passes and will be presented at full board meeting.

Land Use

334 Douglass Street - BSA application to allow for a 7-unit residential unit building in a C4

district.

Presentation made by attorney.

Questions

1. Mechanical bulkhead height and visibility. Clarified on drawings.

2. Term of current ownership. 10 years.

3. Number and types of residential units. Seven units, all market rate.

Jerry - Motion to not approve for the following reasons:

- Does not support City of Yes and CB6’s previously stated positions on MIH, CofY, and

Gowanus Rezoning.

- Does not meet all five points for granting BSA variance. The applicant purchased the property

prior to the Gowanus rezoning and can benefit from the increased as-of-right development

opportunity. Applicant appears to be attempting to maximize ROI by claiming a hardship that

simply doesn’t exist.

Maddie - second

Dave - friendly amendment to point out that proposal does not include any non-market rate

housing. Jerry accepts.

7 in favor.

0 abstentions.

0 against.

Motion passes and will be presented at full board meeting.

End of meeting.
