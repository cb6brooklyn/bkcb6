*** MINUTES ***

Presentation and discussion with representatives for the Governors Island Preservation and Education Corporation on the park and public space plan for Governors Island, and other topics of interest.

Betty Chen works with West 8 Architects. Leslie Koch said there are 4 key strategies.

1. Expanded public access 2. Stabilization and infrastructure 3. Mixed use

There were 275,000 visitors in 2009. They are aiming for more – bikes, picnics, arts.

Brooklyn – Governors Island connection. Now we have a ferry from pier 6 every 10 minutes. Goal is long term connectivity to park space.

Brooklyn Partners: • Mexisle Ice Cream • Water Taxi Boats • Added Value • Etsy

Boats run every 10 minutes but be patient. Fewer bikes allowed from Brooklyn – sometimes it takes a few boats to get there.

Rules for getting to Governors Island will be on line soon.

Free bike Friday – free for you for first hour - $15.00 for 1st hour, $20.00 for 2 hours.

MIY of Profit and NFP uses as per original plan.

RFP’s 1. Entertainment 2. Artists Studio No further RFP’s at this point.

87 acres of public space remains the same from inception of GIPEC Plan in 2006.

Betty Chen talked about the phases toward making the Island a world-class park destination. 4 phases -1, 2A, 2B and 3.

$200 million construction budget. Realistic cost and phasing has been confirmed. Fall of 2012 start phase 1 but the Island will remain open during phases.

www.govislandpark.com www.govisland.com (state website)

150 acres being transferred from State to City. Earliest date for transfer would be July 14th.

Appointees – one each from Squadron, Silver, CB1 and Governor. Mayor has 9 appointees.

Operating budget $12.3 million per year. Transfer releases funding for capital construction. Phase 1 – $30 to 40 million. No funding commitments beyond phase 1.

Motion was made by Judi Francis. Thanks to Leslie and GIPEC team for advocating for the ferry/Pier 6. We urge GIPEC to look closely at visitor data and see how many people come from Brooklyn, to accommodate the Brooklyn population and make adjustments to ensure further Brooklyn visitors.

The motion was seconded by Glenn Kelly.

VOTE: 8…..YEAS, 1…..ABSTENTION

MOTION APPROVED

Glenn Kelly made a motion to send a letter to Mayor/all parties to request greater representation in the GIPEC Board at least proportionate with the percentage of the population that uses the island (in 2010) from Brooklyn, as evidenced by growing numbers of visitors from Brooklyn. The motion was seconded by Judi Francis.

VOTE: 8…..YEAS, 1…..ABSTENTION

MOTION APPROVED
