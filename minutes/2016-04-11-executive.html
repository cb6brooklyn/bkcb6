Annual review of committee structure.

There are some active committees and other committees are not as active. Committees can combine and recombine.

Discussion of ideas for committees:  Meeting dates  Transportation & Public Safety Committee – each committee does not have any item every month.  Economic/Waterfront/Community Development and Housing – does not have a clear mandate. Makes it hard for explicit items.  Environmental Protection/Permits & Licenses – Environmental has real issues but does not have immediate issues.  Landmarks/Land Use – A suggestion was made by Matt Silverman to see Landmarks and Land Use split.

The Sustainability & Resiliency Sub-Committee should be within the Environmental Protection Committee but the chairs are not members of the EPPL Committee. It should be a clearinghouse for all the information on sustainability and resiliency – Red Hook, Gowanus, etc.

Sayar Lonial said we should not rely on what are required but should create discussions with solutions – we can hold hearings even if there are not mandated issues.

The Sustainability & Resiliency Sub-Committee can learn from other parts of the city. CB6 can help clarify what is not there. Resiliency is spearheaded by Victoria Hagman because of her work in Red Hook.

Jerry Armer: Where it sits can be in a number of different committees.

CB6: There is a sense that CB6 could be more plugged in to these issues.

Glenn Kelly: Host neighborhood meetings to find out what communities want and need.

Robert Levine: CB6 used to do that.

Sayar Lonial: Can bring resiliency into Land Use as a set of goals. Permits and Licenses does not fit. Mentioned that the following need reconsideration:  Community Development  Housing  Public Safety  Human Services  Environmental Protection  Land Use – Sustainability & Resiliency Sub-Committee

Topics should make sense – together. Problem to bundle.

Economic/Waterfront/Community Development & Housing – how to motivate. Rewrite mission statement.

Youth/Human Services/Education Committee – Has no mandate but does take on initiatives – no formal role.

Landmarks/Land Use Committee – Make sense together. Landmarks alone would have low turn-out. Put together to get quorum for Landmarks.

Quorums are hard to get. Meat of a community – We need to discuss what is going on in the community.

What is going on in the community – criteria?

Mission statement for each committee should be written. The Chair, working with each chair, should write a mission statement for the committee.

Synergy between Youth/Human Services/Education comes out of what they want to do. Big issues.

Suzanne Turet – Bring back the Town Hall meetings.

Using the same format every year led to meeting fatigue and skewed results. Eric McClure – maybe we need an Outreach Committee.

Mike Racioppo – Maybe a focused, thematic Town Hall meeting.

Craig Hammerman – Yes, we have received the lists from Council Member Lander’s office of prior Participatory Budgeting projects that did not receive funding. Some of the projects have migrated over into the Community Board’s budget priorities. Other Council Members have not made their lists available yet.

Matt Silverman – Do one meeting each community – each year. - What needs to be done in the community? - CB6 has done that but it is over and not there anymore.

Sayar Lonial asked for volunteers to work with him on a plan. Suzanne Turet, Jerry Armer, Eric McClure, Mike Racioppo, Glenn Kelly and Mark Shames all volunteered. They will help to review committee realignments.
