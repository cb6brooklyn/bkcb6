*Landmarks*

26 Verandah Place

Application for a Certificate of Appropriateness to allow for a

rear yard extension. Presented by Studio Schalk. Committee

asked questions about visibility from public ways, chimney and

skylight modifications, and finishes. Applicant confirmed that

there will be no visibility from the surrounding streets,

sidewalks, or the park across the street.

Given the scope of work and lack of visibility, Committee noted

that the project could have been reviewed at LPC staff level.

Jerry: Motion to approve as presented.

Maddie: Seconded.

Unanimously approved

*Community Session*

Committee discussed district needs to be considered and possibly submitted

as part of our Fiscal 2027 budget request.

End of meeting.
