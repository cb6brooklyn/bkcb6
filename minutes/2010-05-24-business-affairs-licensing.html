Presentation and review of a Liquor, Wine and Beer license application submitted to the State Liquor Authority on behalf of Hello Brooklyn, Inc. at 18 Commerce Street (between Columbia/Richards Street).

Craig Fyffe is the proprietor. Chair Daniel Kummer said some time ago tonight gave applicant’s attorney a petition we received from residents of Columbia Street against the application dated April 18, 2010 to the State Liquor Authority.

District Manager’s e-mail to attorney regarding receipt of application and request for questionnaire and applicant notification. We just received the questionnaire responses at meeting. License of prior owner expired. The attorney is Guy Oksenhendler, Esq. Failed to respond to District Manager’s request for information.

Synopsis of complaints to 311 regarding Hello Brooklyn. 40 complaints, some forwarded by Police. There had been no complaints at this address prior to this. Average of 2.2 complaints per month.

Hello Brooklyn is an event facility with temporary SLA approval prior to application. Outdoor area will not be used for any purpose of the club. Fyffe said a neighbor is his main critic.

 Didn’t have the money to apply for a permanent license so relied for 2 years on the temporary licenses.  There was one arrest for serving alcohol without a license but that was dismissed because

presented the temporary license.

 Petition from neighbors

Motion was made by Steven Miller to approve and seconded by Tom Miskel subject to receiving evidence of all temporary licenses previously issued to the applicant and certificates of disposition on arrests and noise summonses and letters of support from neighbors.

VOTE: 4…..YEAS 4…..NAYS 1…..ABSTENTION

NO QUORUM, MOTION FAILED TO PASS
