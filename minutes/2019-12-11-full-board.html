CHAIRPERSON’S REPORT…………………………………………...PETER FLEMING, BOARD CHAIR

 COMMITTEE REPORTS 

PARKS, RECREATION & ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION COMMITTEE

………….……………………...GLENN KELLY, CO-CHAIR

……………………….HILDEGAARD LINK, CO-CHAIR

Parks, Recreation & Environmental Protection co-Chair Hildegaard Link reported that the committee considered a proposal by the Gowanus CAG to have water quality requirements changed.

Motion: To send a letter to the NYS Department of Enviromental Conservation indicating support for the Gowanus CAG’s efforts to have the water quality requirements changed (Class-SD to Class-I) so that it’s more consistent with existing and future uses of the canal.

VOTE: 29 YEAS, 0 NAYS, 0 ABSTENTIONS

MOTION PASSED UNANIMOUSLY

TIME: 7:04 P.M.
