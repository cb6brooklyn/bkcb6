BROOKLYN COMMUNITY BOARD 6

Business Affairs and Licenses Committee

October 26, 2026 @ 250 Baltic Street

Attendance: Jules Block, Erica Cohen, Mike Vacheresse, Leroy Branch, Mandi Spishak-Thomas

MINUTES

Cabra Hospitality LLC, 445 Court Street (between 3rd and 4th Places)

Presentation and review of a new on-premises liquor license application submitted to the State Liquor Authority on behalf of Cabra Hospitality LLC at 445 Court Street (between 3rd and 4th Places).

One neighbor was in attendance and was a little concerned about the possibility of back yard noise since the establishment would be utilizing their rear yard. The applicant assured him that would not be the case and they exchanged contact information.

Motion made by Mike Vacheresse

Seconded by Jules Block

To approve the application with modification to the closing hours to 1am

Vote: 6 Yay, 0 Nay

Park Slope Hospitality LLC, 522 6th Avenue (between 13th and 14th Streets)

Presentation and review of a new on-premises liquor license application submitted to the State Liquor Authority on behalf of Park Slope Hospitality LLC at 522 6th Avenue (between 13th and 14th Streets).

This application is a corporate entity change, same owners same menu.

No community members were present to comment on this location.

Motion made by Erica Cohen

Seconded by Jules Block

To approve the application with modification to the closing hours to 1am

Vote: 6 Yay, 0 Nay

The Red Hook Winery LLC (Lundy’s), 44-46 Beard Street (between Dwight and Otsego Streets)

Presentation and review of a new on-premises liquor license application submitted to the State Liquor Authority on behalf of The Red Hook Winery LLC (Lundy’s) at 44-46 Beard Street (between Dwight and Otsego Streets).

Motion made by Leroy Branch Jr.

Seconded by Mike Vacheresse

To approve the application with modification to the closing hours to 1am

Vote: 6 Yay, 0 Nay

There being no further business to come before the committee, the meeting was adjourned.

The minutes were respectfully submitted by Leroy Branch Jr.
