Jessica Ramos

State Senator · State Senate District 13

In brief

State Senator Jessica Ramos represents State Senate District 13. It sits in Queens.

The district overlaps community boards Queens 3 and Queens 4.

It is policed by the 110th and 115th Precincts.

It overlaps council districts 21, 22 and 25 and Assembly districts 30, 34, 35 and 39.

Members of the State Senate serve two year terms.
