Ariva

🤝 Community based organization · Bronx

Mission

Ariva’s mission is to empower low-wealth New Yorkers with effective tools and resources so they can make informed financial decisions and achieve economic stability.

Volunteering

Ariva, founded in 2003, hopes to give the financial services and access needs of low-income individuals in the South Bronx. We have since expanded to other boroughs, providing free on-site financial counseling and tax preparation services to under served populations in NYC
