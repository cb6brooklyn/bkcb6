Sallie and William Stephens Group, Inc.

🤝 Community based organization · Brooklyn

Mission

SALLIE AND WILLIAM STEPHENS GROUP INC. is a non-profit organization that aims to support young disenfranchised gay men between the ages of 13 to 22 years old. We are here to provide support, encouragement, and resources to help them live a happier, more fulfilled life.

Volunteering

We work tirelessly to end homelessness among gay male disenfranchised youth between 13 to 22 years old. We strive to promote a community where all young people can be their true selves without fear of rejection, discrimination, or judgment.
