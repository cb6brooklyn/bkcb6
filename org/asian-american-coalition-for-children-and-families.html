Asian American Coalition for Children and Families

🤝 Community based organization · Manhattan

Mission

CACF advocates for equity and opportunity for marginalized Asian Pacific American (APA) children and families.

CACF builds a community too powerful to ignore by:

Using and sharing information that counters the model minority myth and sheds light on community needs, Activating APA youth and community members as “social justice” leaders, and Mobilizing a coalition of partners to fight for systems and policy change.

Our vision is for all children and families, including Asian Pacific Americans, in New York City to be safe, healthy, and able to reach their full potential in life.

Volunteering

CACF is the nation's only pan-Asian children and families' advocacy organization that advocates for equity and opportunity for marginalized Asian Pacific American children and families.
