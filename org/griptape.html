GripTape

🤝 Community based organization · Manhattan

Mission

Education is a beautiful part of life, and GripTape works to put youth in the driver’s seat of learning so they can explore as they please and light up their hearts and minds. Everyone says young people are the future, but GripTape actually places them in the position to become the future now.

Volunteering

Young people feel like they are in the back seat of their education and learning. And with a single conversation, dreams were already forming as we imagined a world where all youth are seizing and driving their learning, and have the passion, support, and resources to do it.
