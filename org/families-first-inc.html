Families First, Inc.

🤝 Community based organization

Mission

Families First is dedicated to providing an affordable community space and classes for the entire community.

Volunteering

Non-Profit Early Childhood center in Cobble Hill, Brooklyn, with drop-in music, story time, and art classes; a play space, snack room and lending library; and Practically Preschool classes. We also offer parenting workshops and events that are open to the entire community.
