CMMB - Healthier Lives Worldwide

🤝 Community based organization · Manhattan

Mission

Inspired by the example of Jesus, CMMB works in partnership globally to deliver locally sustainable, quality health solutions to women, children, and their communities.

Volunteering

CMMB is an international NGO providing long-term, co-operative medical and development aid to communities affected by poverty and unequal access to healthcare. It strengthens and support communities through healthcare programs and initiatives through the placement of volunteers.
