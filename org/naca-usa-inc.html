NACA (USA) INC

🤝 Community based organization

Mission

Promoting mutual understanding and respect among the members and society.

  Projecting a positive image of ourselves and the association.

  Be good patriots to both our "home" and "adopted" countries

  Serve as a mentor and role model to our children and others in the community.

  Serves as a channel of communication between our community and the government.

  Gather and disseminate information which is pertinent to and or beneficial to our members.

Volunteering

The Nigerian-American Community Assoc. (USA), Inc., also known as NACA (USA), INC, is a community-based, not-for-profit, non-religious, non-ethnic, and non-partisan association originally founded to address the needs of the New York City’s expanding Nigerian and African immigrant community.
