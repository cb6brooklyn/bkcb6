1894: the question that made Brooklyn a borough

On November 6, 1894, voters in New York, Kings, Queens and Richmond counties and in four Westchester places were asked whether to consolidate into one city. Brooklyn was then its own city, in Kings County. Greater New York took effect on January 1, 1898.
