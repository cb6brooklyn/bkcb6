    The places on the 1894 ballot

    New York, Kings, Queens and Richmond counties, and four Westchester places (the Town of Westchester, East Chester, Pelham and Mount Vernon), voted in 1894 on whether to join one city. Here is how those places have grown and how their economies compare. To put your own city together from them, use Build your own city.

    The real city and everything on the 1894 ballot
