Liquor licenses in Prospect Park

Every active State Liquor Authority license inside the Prospect Park joint interest area, which includes the Brooklyn Museum and is shared by Community Boards 6, 7, 8, 9 and 14.

All Brooklyn districts: /liquor/brooklyn/. Citywide: /liquor/. CB6 map: CB6 liquor map. Source: NYS Liquor Authority, Current Liquor Authority Active Licenses, retrieved September 1, 2026. District assignment by point-in-polygon against the NYC Department of City Planning community district boundary; addresses on or near any district boundary were checked against the NYC Geoclient community district. Temporary permits are marked. "Additional bar" is a second bar under a parent license at the same premises. Where the SLA has no trade name on file, the licensee's legal name is shown. Websites and logos have not yet been added for this district; each business shows an initial. Check a license at sla.ny.gov.
