Liquor licenses in Brooklyn Community District 12

Every active State Liquor Authority license located in Brooklyn Community District 12, listed by the name the business actually uses.

All Brooklyn districts: /liquor/brooklyn/. Citywide: /liquor/. CB6 map: CB6 liquor map. Source: NYS Liquor Authority, Current Liquor Authority Active Licenses, retrieved September 1, 2026. District assignment by point-in-polygon against the NYC Department of City Planning community district boundary; addresses on or near any district boundary were checked against the NYC Geoclient community district. Temporary permits are marked. "Additional bar" is a second bar under a parent license at the same premises. Where the SLA has no trade name on file, the licensee's legal name is shown. Websites come from OpenStreetMap and logos from each business's own site; businesses without one show an initial. Check a license at sla.ny.gov.
