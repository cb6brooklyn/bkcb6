CrashCount

Traffic crash data · citywide, online

In its own words: “CrashCount.nyc tracks the relentless toll of traffic violence in New York City. We break the data down by neighborhood and political district—555 regions in total—so that advocates, political representatives, journalists, and everyday New Yorkers can speak plainly and fight smart.”

CrashCount draws on the NYPD’s crash reports, political records, local reporting and open geographic data. It is not part of Brooklyn Community Board 6 or bkcb6.app.

What it has

Crash Finder looks up any street, school, address or intersection to see how safe the streets around it are.

Each district page has a crash counter (crashes, injuries, serious injuries and deaths, year to date and by year), a crash map and the latest crashes from police reports, with a version to print or present.

Every council, Assembly, Senate and community board profile on bkcb6.app carries that district's CrashCount crash map and counter.
