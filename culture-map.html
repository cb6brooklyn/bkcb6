Places drawn from the lot records carry zoning, ownership, land use and year built. Places on piers, islands, or inside Central Park and Flushing Meadows fall outside any community district; those are assigned to the nearest one and flagged. Owner of record is the tax roll owner, which is often not the operator. Confirm hours and admission directly. Mike Racioppo, Brooklyn Community Board 6.
