Precinct and community district lines are drawn by different agencies for different purposes and do not line up. Read each row twice. The first figure is how much of QNCB2 that precinct covers. Why these are precinct figures

Parking tickets are published without coordinates, so they exist only at the precinct level and cannot be cut to a community district. The numbers above are the combined totals for 108th Precinct, which together cover 99.2% of QNCB2. They therefore include ticketing outside the district. Camera violations such as school zone speed and bus lane carry no precinct at all and are excluded.
