Why these are precinct figures

Parking tickets are published without coordinates, so they exist only at the precinct level. These are the combined totals for the 17 precincts in Queens. A precinct straddling the boundary carries all of its tickets into this figure. Camera violations such as school zone speed and bus lane carry no precinct at all and are excluded. The other four datasets on this page are cut to the boundary itself.
