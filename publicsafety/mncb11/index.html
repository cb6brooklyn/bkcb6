Why these are precinct figures

Parking tickets are published without coordinates, so they exist only at the precinct level and cannot be cut to a community district. The numbers above are the combined totals for 25th Precinct and 23rd Precinct and 32nd Precinct, which together cover 99.4% of MNCB11. They therefore include ticketing outside the district. Camera violations such as school zone speed and bus lane carry no precinct at all and are excluded.
