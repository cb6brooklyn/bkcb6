Precinct and community district lines are drawn by different agencies for different purposes and do not line up. Read each row twice. The first figure is how much of MNCB1 that precinct covers. The second is how much of that precinct sits inside MNCB1. A precinct can cover most of a district while most of the precinct lies elsewhere. Together these precincts account for 99.2% of MNCB1. How these are counted

Every reported crime and every moving violation shown here is placed by the coordinates on its own record, inside this district’s boundary. Nothing is approximated from precinct totals, because precincts cross district lines and a precinct sum counts activity that happened somewhere else. Parking tickets remain the exception for now: the City publishes them without coordinates, so the parking figures below the precinct heading are precinct figures, labelled as exactly that, not district figures.

Counts are placed by each record's own coordinates inside the MNCB1 boundary. A complaint is a report, not a conviction. Counts are raw and not adjusted for population. Built by Mike Racioppo, District Manager, Brooklyn Community Board 6. Open MNCB1 in the explorer →   Public safety hub →
