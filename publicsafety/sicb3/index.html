Precinct and community district lines are drawn by different agencies for different purposes and do not line up. Read each row twice. The first figure is how much of SICB3 that precinct covers. The second is how much of that precinct sits inside SICB3. A precinct can cover most of a district while most of the precinct lies elsewhere. Together these precincts account for 99.7% of SICB3. Crime, arrest, shooting and moving violation counts on this page are cut to the district boundary itself, not to precinct lines. Patrol sectors here: 121D, 122A, 123A, 123B, 123C.

Why these are precinct figures

Parking tickets are published without coordinates, so they exist only at the precinct level and cannot be cut to a community district. The numbers above are the combined totals for 123rd Precinct and 122nd Precinct and 121st Precinct, which together cover 99.7% of SICB3. They therefore include ticketing outside the district. Camera violations such as school zone speed and bus lane carry no precinct at all and are excluded.
