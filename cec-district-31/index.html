Community Education Council District 31

Elected parent council · School District 31

The Community Education Council for District 31 (CEC31) is the elected parent council for the public elementary and middle schools of School District 31, which reaches into SICB3, SICB2, SICB1.

Where it is

715 Ocean Terrace, Building A the council's office and School District 31

What a Community Education Council does

Each Community Education Council has 12 voting members: 10 parents of children in pre-K through 8th grade in the district's schools, elected by parents, and 2 members appointed by the Borough President, with 2 non-voting high school seniors appointed by the Community Superintendent. The council approves school zoning lines and advises on education policy.
