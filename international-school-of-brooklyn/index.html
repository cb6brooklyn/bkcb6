International School of Brooklyn

Independent school · Carroll Gardens, Brooklyn

International School of Brooklyn is an independent school at 477 Court Street in Carroll Gardens.

In its own words: “International School of Brooklyn offers a multilingual educational experience for Pre-K 3 through 8th Grade that prepares students for open-minded communication, critical thinking, and compassionate action. The International Baccalaureate provides the framework for an engaging, relevant, and challenging education.”
