Shares are land area, computed by intersecting the two boundary sets projected to NY State Plane Long Island (EPSG:2263). Slivers under 0.1% of a community district are dropped. School district polygons do not tile the whole city, so a few community districts total slightly under 100%: Bronx CD 10 is the largest gap at 96.0%, with the remainder falling outside any school district boundary.

Data: NYC Department of City Planning Facilities Database (FacDB), dataset ji82-xba5, published on NYC Open Data. Sites are placed in community districts using the district code published with each record; records missing that code are placed by point-in-polygon against DCP community district boundaries, or by geocoding the published address through NYC Planning GeoSearch. Every source record is accounted for on the hub page.

School counts include public, charter, private and parochial, and special education sites. Childcare counts include day care, DOE pre-K and 3-K, and preschools for students with disabilities. FacDB reports a capacity figure for some school records and none for childcare records, so no seat totals are published here; where a capacity figure exists it is shown on that site's marker and card as reported capacity.
