Park Slope Schoolhouse

Child care center · South Slope, Brooklyn

Park Slope Schoolhouse is a child care center at 556 5th Avenue in South Slope.

In its own words: “As a non-profit for 40 years, Park Slope Schoolhouse nurtures a close-knit community and inspires children to become joyful, curious learners for life.”

The program

Licensed by the NYC Health Department as a preschool program for ages 2 to 5, with room for 47 children (permit DC19652).

Licensed by the NYC Health Department as a infant and toddler program for ages birth to 2, with room for 10 children (permit DC21250).

Hours, per the program: full-day (7:30am – 6:30pm), full-year (Sep – Aug).
