Community School District 8

NYC Public Schools · Bronx

Community School District 8 is one of the 32 community school districts of NYC Public Schools, for the public elementary and middle schools in its area. It reaches into BXCB10 (45%), BXCB9 (31%), BXCB2 (21%), BXCB3 (2%).
