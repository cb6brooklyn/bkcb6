Park Slope Food Coop

Member-owned food cooperative · Park Slope, Brooklyn

The Park Slope Food Coop has been at 782 Union Street since 1973. Members own the store and run it: every member works a shift, only members shop, and the labor is what keeps the prices down. The monthly General Meeting, open to every member with a vote for everyone who shows up, has been the decision-making body from the start.

The Coop is one of the oldest and largest member-run food cooperatives in the country, and it is a Park Slope institution in the plainest sense: a two storey building on a residential block that thousands of households pass through every week.

How the Coop works

The Coop is a member-owned and operated food store. Membership is open to all, only members may shop, and members contribute their labor on recurring work shifts, sharing the responsibilities and the benefits equally. The store buys as an agent for its members, with an emphasis on organic, minimally processed food and local, earth-friendly producers.

The General Meeting is held on the last Tuesday of each month at 7 PM. The agenda, with the location and how to attend, is posted inside the Coop and online about three weeks ahead. The Board of Directors is elected at the Annual Meeting in June.

The building at 782 Union Street is a two storey store built in 1920 on a 7,980 square foot lot, owned by the Coop itself. The lot is zoned R6A.
