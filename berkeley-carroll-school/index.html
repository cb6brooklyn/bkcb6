Berkeley Carroll School

Independent school · Park Slope, Brooklyn

Berkeley Carroll School is an independent Pre-K through Grade 12 school in Park Slope, founded in 1886. It runs four buildings in CB6: the Upper and Middle School at 181 Lincoln Place, the Lower School at 701 Carroll Street, the Athletic Center at 762 President Street and the Performance Space at 152 Sterling Place.

In its own words: “A passion for learning is at the heart of Berkeley Carroll. We are an intellectual, creative, and equitable community where dedicated teachers challenge and support talented students.”
