The Maurice Sendak Community School, PS 118

Public elementary school · Park Slope, Brooklyn

The Maurice Sendak Community School, PS 118 is a public elementary school at 211 8th Street in Park Slope.

In its own words: “The Maurice Sendak Community School is a small, collaborative learning community dedicated to fostering intellectual curiosity and a life-long love of learning. Using an interdisciplinary, inquiry-based approach, we will prepare our students to be problem solvers and creative thinkers as they also develop a strong sense of social responsibility to our global community.”
