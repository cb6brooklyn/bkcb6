Kirsten Gillibrand

United States Senator for New York

Kirsten Gillibrand is the United States Senator, elected statewide.

One of New York’s two members of the United States Senate.

Appointed to the Senate in January 2009 and first elected in 2010. Senators serve six year terms, with no term limit.
