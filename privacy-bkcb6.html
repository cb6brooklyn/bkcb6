Privacy Policy

BKCB6, the Brooklyn Community Board 6 app

Data collection

BKCB6 does not collect, store, transmit or share any information that identifies you. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no trackers. Nothing you do in the app is sent to the app developer.

What the app loads

When you open the app it loads two public documents so that its contents stay current: the Brooklyn Community Board 6 meeting calendar published at brooklyncb6.cityofnewyork.us, and the board's public roster document. These are the same requests a web browser makes when it opens those pages. No user identifier is attached, and the app keeps a copy of both on your device so it works without a connection.

Calendar

If you tap "Add to my calendar" the app asks iOS for permission to add events. It uses write-only access: the app can add the meeting you chose and cannot read anything already in your calendar. You can deny or revoke this permission at any time in Settings.

Reminders

If you tap "Remind me one hour before" the app asks iOS for permission to show notifications and schedules a reminder on your device. Reminders are created and delivered entirely on the device.

Map

The District map is drawn with Apple Maps, which is governed by Apple's privacy policy. The app does not request or use your location.

External links

The app links to official sites including brooklyncb6.cityofnewyork.us, bkcb6.app, nyc.gov, the New York City Council, the New York State Senate and Assembly, and the offices of the elected officials who represent the district. Once you follow a link you are subject to that site's privacy practices.

Children

BKCB6 is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Contact

Questions about this policy: Brooklyn Community Board 6, 250 Baltic Street, Brooklyn, NY 11201, info@bkcb6.org.
