Jumaane Williams

Public Advocate for the City of New York

Jumaane Williams is the Public Advocate, elected citywide.

Ombudsman for New Yorkers. Investigates complaints against city agencies and is first in the line of mayoral succession.

First elected in a special election in February 2019 and sworn in March 2019, then elected that November to finish the term. Citywide offices carry four year terms.

Next on the ballot in November 2029, when the seat will be open: Public Advocate Williams is term limited and cannot run again. For the election in front of us, see where you vote in November 2026 or what is coming up on the calendar.
