Chickpeas

Preschool · South Slope, Brooklyn

Chickpeas is a preschool at 451 7th Avenue in South Slope.

In its own words: “Established in 1981, Chickpeas began in the living rooms and apartments of families across Park Slope. It has transformed over the last 40 years from a private, grown-up run cooperative to a public, worker-self directed non-profit. At its core, Chickpeas remains a preschool that aims to teach the whole child through curiosity, exploration, and discovery.”
