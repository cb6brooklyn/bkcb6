P.S. 375 Jackie Robinson School

Public elementary school · Crown Heights South

P.S. 375 Jackie Robinson School is a public elementary school in Crown Heights South, Brooklyn, in Community School District 17.

It is run by the Department of Education.

In its own words: “Jackie Robinson Public School 375 is located in Crown Heights, Brooklyn. At our core, we know that the relationship between our teachers and students is at the heart of every excellent academic institution. Jackie Robinson school cultivates this relationship by providing a dynamic learning environment that facilitates positive growth among all members of the school community. Our school develops students who are as keen to realize their potential as our teachers are to inspire it.”
