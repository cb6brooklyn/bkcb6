P.S. 221 Toussaint L'Ouverture

Public elementary school · Crown Heights

P.S. 221 Toussaint L'Ouverture is a public elementary school in Crown Heights, Brooklyn, in Community School District 17, run by NYC Public Schools.

In its own words: “P.S. 221 Toussaint L'Ouverture (DBN: 17K221) is a vibrant Brooklyn elementary school located at 791 Empire Boulevard, Brooklyn, NY 11213. At P.S. 221 we foster a safe, respectful, and inclusive learning environment where students are encouraged to be proud, ambitious, happy, and kind. Our dedicated teachers work with families and the community to deliver high-quality instruction and social-emotional support across all grade levels. Our mission. We teach academic excellence and character: respect for self and others, dedication to learning, and readiness for the future. Students at P.S.”
