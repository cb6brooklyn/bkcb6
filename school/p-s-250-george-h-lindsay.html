P.S. 250 George H. Lindsay

Public elementary school · East Williamsburg

P.S. 250 George H. Lindsay is a public elementary school in East Williamsburg, Brooklyn, in Community School District 14.

It is run by the Department of Education.

In its own words: “Please see flyer that will be backpacked home and information below on ordering uniforms for the upcoming school year. Please shop between the end of July and the first week of August to ensure that every student is in uniform by opening day. Processing school orders during the back-to-school season can take up to 4 weeks to deliver. Normally, it takes less than 10 days to receive throughout the year.”
