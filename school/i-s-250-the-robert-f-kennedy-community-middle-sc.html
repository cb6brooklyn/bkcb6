I.S. 250 The Robert F. Kennedy Community Middle Sc

Public middle school · Hillcrest

I.S. 250 The Robert F. Kennedy Community Middle Sc is a public middle school in Hillcrest, Queens, in Community School District 25, run by NYC Public Schools.

In its own words: “In 2016, RFK Principal, Tara Mrwik, secured a grant for a multi-million dollar; complete renovation of the school's playground/school yard. The grant helped to provide the school and it's community with a beautiful state-of-the-art space to enjoy many activities. With 3 new basketball hoops, a 75 x 80 turf field, handball court, running track, tennis/volleyball court along with other fun things to do. In addition to all the interactive aspects of the park, the playground is Eco-friendly. There is a living gazebo, numerous trees and two gardens. The playground also is designed to drain rainwater directly into the cities' water table helping to replenish the cities' natural water resources.”
