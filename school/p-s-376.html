P.S. 376

Public elementary school · Bayside

P.S. 376 is a public elementary school in Bayside, Queens, in Community School District 26, run by NYC Public Schools.

In its own words: “PS 376Q - Bayside Hills School of Excellence The P.S. 376Q community will help all students realize their individual potential as global leaders who are empowered, compassionate, and resilient when solving problems. Our caring students will collaborate to navigate through complex tasks and work through authentic learning experiences, building upon their leadership skills and enabling them to become impactful global citizens.”
