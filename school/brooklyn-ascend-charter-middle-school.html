Brooklyn Ascend Charter Middle School

Charter school · Brownsville

Brooklyn Ascend Charter Middle School is a charter school in Brownsville, Brooklyn, in Community School District 17.

In its own words: “For more than a decade, Ascend has stood at the forefront of educational excellence—one of Brooklyn’s largest and highest-performing K–12 charter networks. Today, Ascend serves more than 5,000 students across 17 schools in Brooklyn , supported by a dedicated team of over 800 educators and staff. Ascend has proven what is possible when rigorous academics meet joyful, equitable classrooms.”
