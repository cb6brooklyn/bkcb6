PROGRESS High School for Professional Careers

Public high school · Bushwick South

PROGRESS High School for Professional Careers is a public high school in Bushwick South, Brooklyn, in Community School District 14.

It is run by the Department of Education.

In its own words: “Is focused on culturally relevant education and rigorous instruction to equip students with the necessary skills and human compassion to be successful in a global society.”
