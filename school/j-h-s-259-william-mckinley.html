J.H.S. 259 William McKinley

Public middle school · Dyker Heights

J.H.S. 259 William McKinley is a public middle school in Dyker Heights, Brooklyn, in Community School District 20, run by NYC Public Schools.

In its own words: “William McKinley I.S. 259 is a large Title 1 middle school, located in the southwestern section of Brooklyn, New York, and is part of Community School District 20. The school serves approximately 1,600 students in Grades 6–8.”
