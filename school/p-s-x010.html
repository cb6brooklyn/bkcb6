P.S. X010

Public K-12 school for special education · Throggs Neck

P.S. X010 is a public k-12 school for special education in Throggs Neck, Bronx, in Community School District 8, run by NYC Public Schools.

In its own words: “It is P10X’s belief that all children can learn to the best of their ability by identifying and removing systemic barriers that impede success with active participation from staff, families, and our community. We recognize and value the unique cultural experiences and respect the individuality of all students and staff including race, ethnicity, gender, culture, economic status, native language, religion, and disability. We strive to promote student independence, communication, and self- advocacy by learning through culturally responsive teaching that takes into account everyone’s individual experiences.”
