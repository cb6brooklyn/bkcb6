Bronx Charter School for Better Learning II

Charter elementary school · Pelham Gardens

Bronx Charter School for Better Learning II is a charter elementary school in Pelham Gardens, Bronx, in Community School District 11.

In its own words: “At Bronx Better Learning Charter Schools (BBL 1 & BBL 2) , every child is more than a student; they’re part of our family. Our small, close-knit K–5 community allows us to know each child by name, providing the personalized attention and care that helps them grow with confidence and joy.”
