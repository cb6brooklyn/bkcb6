High School of American Studies at Lehman College

Public high school · Kingsbridge Heights

High School of American Studies at Lehman College is a public high school in Kingsbridge Heights, Bronx, in Community School District 10, run by NYC Public Schools.

In its own words: “We are one of eight specialized schools in New York City. Our school uniquely emphasizes the study of American history and offers students an academically rigorous program that is varied and challenging. Our goal is to prepare our students for admission to highly competitive colleges and for a wide range of careers. Our affiliation with Lehman College provides a unique setting in which students enjoy the intimacy of a small school, along with the resources of a large college. Many of our students participate in a wide variety of extra- curricular activities and PSAL sports .”
