P.S./M.S. 004 Crotona Park West

Public K-8 school · Claremont

P.S./M.S. 004 Crotona Park West is a public k-8 school in Claremont, Bronx, in Community School District 9, run by NYC Public Schools.

In its own words: “Our mission is to cultivate relationships by collaborating with scholars, families, staff, and partners within and beyond our school community. As we promote a supportive environment, all community members will engage in teamwork, innovative practices, and celebrations to nurture cultural diversity as well as elevate individual voices through leadership opportunities as we guide all stakeholders towards their highest potential.”
