The Active Learning Elementary School

Public early childhood · Downtown Flushing

The Active Learning Elementary School is a public early childhood in Downtown Flushing, Queens, in Community School District 25, run by NYC Public Schools.

In its own words: “TALES is a joyful and inclusive early childhood school committed to nurturing the whole child. We provide a warm, safe, and welcoming environment where students feel supported, families feel connected, and learning comes to life.”
