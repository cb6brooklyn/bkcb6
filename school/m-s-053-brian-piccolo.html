Public middle school · Far Rockaway

M.S. 053 Brian Piccolo is a public middle school in Far Rockaway, Queens, in Community School District 27, run by NYC Public Schools.

In its own words: “M.S. 053 Middle School, located at 10-45 Nameoke St, Far Rockaway, NY 11691 (Phone: 718-471-6900), is dedicated to advancing equity and providing every student with a high-quality education. As a strong community of learners, MS 53 prepares students for 21st-century college and career opportunities through rigorous academics, leadership development, and a supportive learning environment. With a commitment to empowering students as future community leaders, MS 53 fosters excellence, equity, and lifelong success.”
