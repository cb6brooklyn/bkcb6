P.S. 256 Benjamin Banneker

Public elementary school · Bedford-Stuyvesant

P.S. 256 Benjamin Banneker is a public elementary school in Bedford-Stuyvesant, Brooklyn, in Community School District 13, run by NYC Public Schools.

In its own words: “PS 256 is an innovative 3-K to 5th Grade public school in the vibrant neighborhood of Bedford-Styuvesant, Brooklyn. Our focus on STEAM (Science, Technology, Engineering, Art/Architecture and Math) was inspired by our namesake, Benjamin Banneker, who was a Black astronomer, naturalist, surveyor, and almanac author.”
