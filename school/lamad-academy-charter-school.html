Lamad Academy Charter School

Charter school · Brooklyn

Lamad Academy Charter School is a charter school in Brooklyn, in Community School District 18.

It is a charter school, publicly funded and independently run.

In its own words: “Every day, Lamad Academy Charter School proves what is possible when scholars are supported, encouraged, and challenged to reach their fullest potential. Our school was built to ensure that children from our community have access to high-quality education, strong relationships, and enriching experiences that prepare them for success in school and in life.”
