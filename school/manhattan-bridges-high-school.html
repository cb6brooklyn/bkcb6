Manhattan Bridges High School

Public high school · Hell's Kitchen (Clinton)

Manhattan Bridges High School is a public high school in Hell's Kitchen (Clinton), Manhattan, in Community School District 2, run by NYC Public Schools.

In its own words: “At Manhattan Bridges High School we provide access to academically challenging college preparatory coursework in Science, Technology, Engineering, Math (STEM), and Humanities, to all of our Latino English Language Learners (ELLs). We aim for all our students to meet the demands of the fast-moving world of technology and economics. At MBHS we focus on developing students' skills in bilingual academic language, communication, and career readiness. We empower students to use their native cultures and language as personal and professional assets to achieve their professional goals.”
