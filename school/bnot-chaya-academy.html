Bnot Chaya Academy

Private high school · Midwood

Bnot Chaya Academy is a private high school in Midwood, Brooklyn.

It is an independent school, overseen by the New York State Education Department.

In its own words: “In the summer of 2008, Rabbi Zechariah Wallerstein Z”L was aware that too many girls had no school to attend for the following year. His characteristic passion for helping the community’s youth propelled him to open a school. A place where stability, growth and learning would empower struggling young women.”
