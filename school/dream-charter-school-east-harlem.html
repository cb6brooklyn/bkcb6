DREAM Charter School East Harlem

Charter K-12 school · East Harlem South

DREAM Charter School East Harlem is a charter k-12 school in East Harlem South, Manhattan, in Community School District 4.

It is a charter school, publicly funded and independently run.

In its own words: “A school where your child is known, seen, and loved. A community where your family becomes our family. Welcome to DREAM. Welcome home.”
