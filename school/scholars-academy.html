Scholars' Academy

Public secondary school · Rockaway Beach

Scholars' Academy is a public secondary school in Rockaway Beach, Queens, in Community School District 27, run by NYC Public Schools.

In its own words: “Scholars’ Academy is one of the most diverse schools in New York City , bringing students of many races, ethnicities, and religions from surrounding neighborhoods to engage together in learning.”
