The Mott Hall School

Public middle school · Washington Heights

The Mott Hall School is a public middle school in Washington Heights, Manhattan, in Community School District 6, run by NYC Public Schools.

In its own words: “The Mott Hall School was one of 427 schools across the nation to receive the United States Department of Education Blue Ribbon Award for excellence in 2003 and 2004.”
