P.S. 109 Sedgwick

Public elementary school · University Heights-Morris Heights

P.S. 109 Sedgwick is a public elementary school in University Heights-Morris Heights, Bronx, in Community School District 9.

It is run by the Department of Education.

In its own words: “PS 109 sets out to meet and exceed the learning needs of the students. We are dedicated to working together with the community to ensure that each student reaches their potential. Our goal is to enrich the curriculum through technology, language development and the arts.”
