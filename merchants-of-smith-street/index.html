Merchants of Smith Street (MOSS)

Merchant association · Smith Street, Brooklyn

MOSS, the Merchants of Smith Street, is a merchant association created by local shop owners on Smith Street. Its site describes it as a collective of local businesses supporting community, retail and events on the street.

Smith Street connects Cobble Hill, Boerum Hill and Carroll Gardens. MOSS says its aim is to keep the street vibrant, welcoming and resourceful, with shop owners, restaurateurs and service providers working together, and to preserve the street’s historic character.

What MOSS does

MOSS represents merchants in local decisions, runs shared marketing and events, and provides supplemental street cleaning, holiday and seasonal decorations, safety initiatives, workshops and networking.

Its events include Smith Street Community Clean Up Days, the Neighbors & Makers Fair, quarterly Retailer Socials and the Smith Street Holiday Event, listed for December 5, 2026, 4pm to 6pm, at the corner of Bergen and Smith Streets.

Member dues are listed at $150 a year. Members are promoted on the MOSS Instagram and get a monthly newsletter. MOSS is funded by member contributions, sponsorships and donations; its membership page says it is a member of the South Brooklyn Local Development Corporation, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, and donations appear on statements under that name.
