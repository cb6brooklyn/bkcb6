Gowanus Green

Affordable housing, park and school site on the Gowanus Canal

What it is

Gowanus Green is a mixed-use development on a former manufactured gas plant site on the Gowanus Canal, a publicly owned site in the Gowanus Neighborhood Rezoning. The plan has affordable housing in six residential buildings, retail and community space, a public school site and a 1.5-acre park, designed to Passive House standards.

Developed by The Hudson Companies with the City (HPD); designed by Marvel.
