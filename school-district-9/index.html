Community School District 9

NYC Public Schools · Bronx

Community School District 9 is one of the 32 community school districts of NYC Public Schools, for the public elementary and middle schools in its area. It reaches into BXCB4 (52%), BXCB3 (23%), BXCB5 (21%), BXCB6 (4%).
