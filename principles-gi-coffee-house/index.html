Principles GI Coffee House

Community cafe and event space · Gowanus, Brooklyn

Principles GI Coffee House is inspired by the egalitarian, antiwar coffeehouses of the Vietnam War era. Principles is a community and event space that is also a cafe, run by owner Katie Bishop at 139 9th Street.

Principles pays workers a living wage, runs gratuity free, and offers pay what you want items.

What Principles GI Coffee House does

Principles has become a hub for cyclists. The Grown-Up Bike Bus leaves from the cafe, and the space hosts bike repair workshops and community meetings, including CB6 committee meetings.
