Community School District 3

NYC Public Schools · Manhattan

Community School District 3 is one of the 32 community school districts of NYC Public Schools, for the public elementary and middle schools in its area. It reaches into MNCB7 (47%), MNCB10 (10%), MNCB9 (10%).
