Directory

Every business, property and new building in the proposed district. Search by name or address, pick a category, or tap a listing to find it on the map.

Boundary: the Gowanus BID Formation Steering Committee's draft boundary, traced from the committee's own map: Wyckoff Street and St. Marks Place down to 3rd, 6th and 9th Streets, from Smith, Hoyt and Bond Streets to 4th Avenue. Businesses: the bkcb6.app business directory, which draws on New York State food store and liquor license records and OpenStreetMap. Properties: every tax lot inside the boundary, from the Department of City Planning's PLUTO file. New housing: the new buildings filed with the Department of Buildings in the Gowanus rezoning area, from the bkcb6.app Gowanus buildings pages.
