Bay Ridge Parking Tickets, Mapped

Every parking ticket written inside the Bay Ridge boundary this fiscal year, located to its intersection and clipped to the neighborhood polygon. Filter by violation type and month to see where and when each kind of ticket is written.

The Bay Ridge boundary used here runs from roughly 65th Street in the north down to 101st Street and the Fort Hamilton area in the south, and from the waterfront along Shore Road and the Belt Parkway in the west inland to about 7th Avenue, Fort Hamilton Parkway, and the Gowanus Expressway in the east. Only tickets that fall inside that shape are counted. I originally found an open sourced neighborhood map on Reddit that was popular and had a good mix of vibes and borders.

How this was built: I pulled all parking and camera violations issued during FY2026, filtered them to the 68th Precinct, and then matched each ticket's street codes to the NYC LION street centerline file to place violations at real-world locations.

From there, I tested each location against the Bay Ridge neighborhood boundary and only included tickets that fell within the neighborhood. A small number of tickets couldn't be placed with confidence because they were issued on streets that cross the boundary but lacked enough location information, such as a cross street, to determine exactly where they occurred. Rather than guess, I left them out.

As always, a parking ticket is an enforcement action, not a complaint. This shows where tickets were written, not where residents reported issues.
