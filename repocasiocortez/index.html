Representative · New York’s 14th Congressional District

In brief

Representative Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez represents New York’s 14th Congressional District in the United States House of Representatives. It sits in Bronx and Queens.

The district overlaps community boards Bronx 10, Queens 1, Bronx 9, Queens 3, Queens 7, Bronx 2, Bronx 11, Bronx 1, Queens 4, Bronx 12 and Bronx 3.

It is policed by the 40th, 41st, 42nd, 43rd, 45th, 47th, 49th, 109th, 110th, 114th and 115th Precincts.

It overlaps council districts 8, 13, 17, 18, 19, 21, 22 and 25, Assembly districts 27, 34, 35, 36, 39, 80, 82, 84, 85 and 87 and Senate districts 11, 12, 13, 29, 32, 33, 34, 36 and 59.

Members of the House serve two year terms, with no term limit.
