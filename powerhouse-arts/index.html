Powerhouse Arts

Nonprofit creative production center · Gowanus, Brooklyn

Powerhouse Arts (PHA) is a not for profit creative production center in Gowanus, in the restored powerhouse at 322 3rd Avenue on the canal.

PHA brings artists, fabricators, educators and neighbors together and connects them to the tools, spaces and support they need. Every program, rental and partnership helps fund the creative mission.
