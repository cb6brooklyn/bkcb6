Ladybug Daycare and Preschool, Union Street

Infant, toddler and preschool program · Columbia Street Waterfront District, Brooklyn

Ladybug Daycare and Preschool, Union Street is an infant, toddler and preschool program at 138 Union Street in Columbia Street Waterfront District.

In its own words: “At Ladybug, we believe that every child is a unique individual with their own pace, curiosity, and way of experiencing the world. Founded over 19 years ago in the heart of Brooklyn, we started with one simple mission: to create a place where children feel safe, loved, and excited to learn every single day.”
