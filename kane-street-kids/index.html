Kane Street Kids

Preschool of Kane Street Synagogue · Cobble Hill, Brooklyn

Kane Street Kids is a preschool of Kane Street Synagogue at 236 Kane Street in Cobble Hill.

In its own words: “Kane Street Kids is the preschool of Kane Street Synagogue, serving children ages 2–5 in the heart of Cobble Hill. Since 2005, we have welcomed families from a wide range of backgrounds, creating a warm, inclusive community where every child is known, valued, and encouraged to grow.”
