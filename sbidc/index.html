SBIDC

Southwest Brooklyn Industrial Development Corporation · Sunset Park, Red Hook and Gowanus

SBIDC, the Southwest Brooklyn Industrial Development Corporation, has been creating opportunity in Southwest Brooklyn since 1978. In the words of its own mission, SBIDC is a resource that creates equal opportunity for the people, businesses and community of the Southwest Brooklyn industrial waterfront.

SBIDC serves the waterfront neighborhoods of Sunset Park, Red Hook and Gowanus, two of them in CB6.

Executive Director Jesse Tinen Solomon leads the organisation.

What SBIDC does

For businesses, SBIDC offers business support, business training, green economy programs and help with employee ownership.

For workers, SBIDC runs workforce services, job placement and skill development, through the Sunset Park Economic Mobility Network.

SBIDC also provides advocacy support, representing the industrial business community.
