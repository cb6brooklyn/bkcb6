The same two agencies, counted where the money landed. This section adds up those awards for HUD and the Pentagon in New York State, New York City, each borough, every county and every congressional district.

Manhattan's HUD figure carries money for the whole city: USAspending files an award under the county of the recipient's address, so the New York City Housing Authority's $4.59 billion (FY2025) and the City's Section 8 program sit in New York County even though the apartments are in all five boroughs. USAspending lists $789.8 million of FY2025 HUD money in the city under the City's registration named "New York City Department of Transportation"; $779.6 million of it is Section 8 Housing Choice Vouchers, the program the City's Department of Housing Preservation and Development administers (HPD expected $649.6 million in federal HCV funds in City FY2025 plus $59.7 million in pandemic supplements, per the State Comptroller). The FY2021 and FY2022 Pentagon totals for New York State include the pandemic pharmaceutical purchases DoD contracted on the government's behalf: Pfizer Inc. $23.1 billion and Regeneron $5.6 billion in FY2021, Pfizer $18.0 billion in FY2022.

Checked against the agencies' own numbers

Pentagon, New York, FY2023. DoD's Office of Local Defense Community Cooperation reports $15.2 billion spent in New York State in FY2023: $11.7 billion in contracts, $372.4 million in grants and $3.1 billion in personnel pay for 58,080 personnel; 2.5% of total U.S. defense spending, 0.7% of state GDP, $777 per resident. Top contractors: Lockheed Martin $2.3B, Pfizer $1.9B, L3Harris $1.8B. USAspending's FY2023 award total for New York is $13.35 billion; the OLDCC figure adds military and civilian pay, which is not an award and never appears in USAspending. OLDCC, Defense Spending by State FY2023, New York (PDF)

HUD formula grants to the City, 2025. The City's 2025 Annual Action Plan reports $296,567,922.74 in HUD entitlement funds for the 2025 program year: CDBG $170,159,554; HOME $66,494,506.74; HOPWA $44,857,248; ESG $15,056,614. USAspending shows the same CDBG figure, $170,159,554, awarded to the City's Office of Management and Budget in FY2025. NYC 2025 Annual Action Plan, pp. 2 and 23 (PDF)

NYCHA, 2025. NYCHA told the City Council in March 2025 it expects about $1.29 billion in federal operating subsidy, about $2.29 billion for Section 8 vouchers and administrative fees, and approximately $731 million in 2025 federal capital funding; federal funding is two-thirds of its operating revenue. USAspending records $4.59 billion in HUD awards to NYCHA in FY2025. NYCHA preliminary budget testimony, March 10, 2025 (PDF)

Section 8 in the city. The State Comptroller: more than 100,000 households get vouchers through NYCHA and more than 36,000 through HPD (November 2024); NYCHA received $2 billion for Section 8 in 2024, and HPD plans $708 million of federal Section 8 spending in City FY2025. NYS Comptroller, Federal Funding and New York: Section 8 Housing Choice Vouchers

The pandemic contracts. DoD and HHS announced the Pfizer vaccine purchase agreements in 2020; the awards were made through DoD and carry Pfizer's New York place of performance in USAspending. DoD release, July 22, 2020

How the New York numbers were pulled

Source: USAspending.gov, the Treasury Department's federal spending database, through its public API, pulled . Every figure in this section is the sum of award transactions, by action date within each fiscal year (October 1 to September 30), awarded by the Department of Defense (toptier code 097) or the Department of Housing and Urban Development (086), as reported to USAspending by the agencies. Loans are counted at their subsidy cost, as USAspending counts them. The "US total" each share is measured against is the sum of the same query across all states, DC and the territories; awards performed abroad or with no recorded state are outside it. Award data in the API starts with FY2008. FY2026 is complete through September 30, 2026 as reported by the pull date; agencies have 30 days to report, so late entries can still raise it.

Place of performance is where the award says the work happens; recipient location is the awardee's registered address. HUD's recipient-location data is missing for most awards before FY2010, so that view starts low. Counties and districts are as recorded on each award. "Pentagon" here is every award by the Department of Defense and its components, including the Army Corps of Engineers' civil works, which OMB's tables above count outside the DoD military line.
