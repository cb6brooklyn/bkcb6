Carroll Gardens Association

🤝 Community based organization · Brooklyn Community Board 6

Mission

Carroll Gardens Association is an affordable housing and economic development grassroots organization that has been working in Southwest Brooklyn since 1971. CGA organizes with tenants and domestic workers to advocate for permanent affordable housing, cooperative economics, and immigrant worker rights.

What it does

CGA came out of the 1970s, when Carroll Gardens, Red Hook and the Columbia Waterfront were being written off. Affordable housing was scarce, government support was thin, and the Puerto Rican families who made up much of the neighborhood were being pushed to the margins. A small group of neighbors organized, and the association has been here ever since.

It works across Red Hook, the Columbia Waterfront and Carroll Gardens, with Gowanus and Sunset Park as secondary service areas, and it holds a portfolio of roughly twenty properties, several on Columbia Street and about half in Red Hook.

Its organizing centers leadership development, political education and mutual aid, on the premise that the residents and workers living a problem are the experts on it. That covers tenant organizing, domestic worker rights and cooperative economics.
