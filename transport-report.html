Sources: NYC DOT posted speed limits qtik-bcvk, DOT bike routes and truck routes, MTA bus routes 8vgb-zm6e and bus stops, MTA subway stations 39hk-dx4f, and the Citi Bike station feed. Every line and point was clipped to Department of City Planning community district boundaries before it was measured, so a segment counts in the district it actually sits in rather than the district an agency field claims. Miles are centerline miles along the segments in each dataset, not lane miles. Retired bike segments are measured separately and left out of the totals. Bus route counts include every route that touches a district, so a route crossing four districts counts in all four; district counts therefore do not sum to the borough count.
