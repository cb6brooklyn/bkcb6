| Mayor's Office of Worker Power | Created by executive order on September 7, 2026, the first office of its kind in the country. Uses City Hall to help workers get informed, connected and organized. Brings together rank-and-file workers, unions, worker centers, and immigrant and gig workers, holds public hearings, and refers workers to groups that can help. Led by Tony Perlstein. Answers to the Deputy Mayor for Economic Justice. | You and your coworkers want to organize, or you have no benefits, low pay and no say on the job. |
