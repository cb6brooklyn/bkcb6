Waterfront Museum

Floating museum and showboat · Red Hook, Brooklyn

The Waterfront Museum, founded in 1985, is housed aboard the 1914 Lehigh Valley Barge No. 79, a wooden railroad lighter listed on the National Register of Historic Places, docked at 290 Conover Street in Red Hook.

David Sharps found the barge in the Edgewater, NJ tideflats in 1985 and restored the barge into the museum.

What Waterfront Museum does

The museum provides free and low cost education, exhibitions and performance, promotes maritime heritage, and advocates for public waterfront access.

The barge is open for free tours on most Saturdays 1 to 5 PM and Thursdays 4 to 8 PM when docked in Red Hook, and hosts shows and art exhibits.
