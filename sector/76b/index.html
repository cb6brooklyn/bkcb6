The precinct behind it

The 76th Precinct serves south Brooklyn, and includes the neighborhoods of Carroll Gardens, Red Hook, Cobble Hill, parts of Gowanus, and the Columbia Street Waterfront District.

How these are counted

Every reported crime and moving violation here is placed by the coordinates on its own record inside this sector’s boundary. Sector boundaries are the NYPD’s published neighborhood policing sectors. A complaint is a report, not a conviction; counts are raw and not adjusted for population.
