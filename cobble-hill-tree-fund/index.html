Cobble Hill Tree Fund

Volunteer street tree group · Cobble Hill, Brooklyn

The Cobble Hill Tree Fund is an all-volunteer charitable trust, a 501(c)(3), set up by the Cobble Hill Association in 1981 to plant and care for street trees in Cobble Hill and the neighborhoods around it. Its history counts more than 200 trees planted.

What Cobble Hill Tree Fund does

Request a tree: the Fund helps residents get a street tree planted in front of their building and normally covers half the cost. It puts a tree at $1,200 to $2,100, plus stump removal or a bigger tree bed if needed. New trees are normally in by mid-December.

Each spring it holds a plant sale in Cobble Hill Park to pay for plantings and tree care. Each January it sponsors holiday tree chipping in Cobble Hill Park with NYC Parks’ Mulchfest.

Many of its members are Citizen Pruners, licensed city volunteers who care for street and park trees, and the Fund holds Street Tree Talks on local tree care.
