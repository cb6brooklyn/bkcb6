PS 282 Park Slope

Public elementary school · Park Slope, Brooklyn

PS 282 Park Slope is a public elementary school at 180 6th Avenue in Park Slope.

In its own words: “PS 282 is an NYC DOE District 13 International Baccalaureate school* serving 3-K to 5th Grades in Park Slope, Brooklyn. PS 282 provides a holistic learning experience, where students engage in constructing their understanding of the world through exploring content that not only provides them with the strategies and skills to communicate, collaborate, and reflect upon their relationships with themselves, their families, and the world around them.”
