How “what it takes” is worked out

To rent: the income needed is 40 times the monthly rent, the usual landlord rule in New York City, which is the same as rent taking 30% of income.

To buy: the median asking price with 20% down and a 30-year fixed mortgage at that month's average rate (Freddie Mac). The income needed is the one where principal and interest take 30% of it. Property taxes, insurance and maintenance or common charges are not included, so the real figure is higher.

Local income: median household income from the Census Bureau's American Community Survey (1-year estimates) for the community district, the borough or the city. A neighborhood uses its community district. A council district uses its borough. Each month uses the latest year published up to that month; there are no standard 2020 estimates, so 2020 uses 2019, and 2025 and 2026 use 2024 until the Census Bureau publishes more. Community district income starts in 2012, the first year the Census labels its areas by district.
