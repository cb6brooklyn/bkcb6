Community School District 7

NYC Public Schools · Bronx

Community School District 7 is one of the 32 community school districts of NYC Public Schools, for the public elementary and middle schools in its area. It reaches into BXCB1 (64%), QNCB1 (19%), BXCB4 (13%), BXCB3 (1%), BXCB2 (1%).
