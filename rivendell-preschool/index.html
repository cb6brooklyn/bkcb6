Rivendell Preschool

Montessori preschool · Gowanus, Brooklyn

Rivendell Preschool is a Montessori preschool at 277 3rd Avenue in Gowanus.

In its own words: “Rivendell Preschool, a pre-primary Montessori school, opened in September 1977. Originally called The Children’s House of Park Slope, in 1998 it was incorporated into Rivendell School as its model early childhood inclusion setting.”
