Arts Gowanus

Community based arts organisation · Gowanus, Brooklyn

Arts Gowanus has worked for the artists of Gowanus since 1996. In the words of its own mission, Arts Gowanus nurtures a diverse, inclusive creative community that welcomes all artists in the neighborhood, creating new spaces for art to bring people together, build a more vibrant community, and uplift the greater Gowanus area.

Arts Gowanus runs Gowanus Open Studios, which it calls Brooklyn’s largest celebration of local art, with more than 400 artists, businesses and venues opening their doors from Pacific Street to 19th Street and from Smith Street to Fifth Avenue. The 30th annual Open Studios is October 17 and 18, 2026.

Executive Director Johnny Thornton leads the organisation.

What Arts Gowanus does

Arts Gowanus runs art programming and partnerships that raise the visibility of Gowanus artists across the city, the Arts Gowanus Fellowship, which covers a selected artist’s studio rent, and curatorial services.

On the Gowanus rezoning, Arts Gowanus negotiated a Community Benefits Agreement that secures roughly 120 subsidized artist studios across the development sites, starting at $20 a square foot a year, with one studio in each building reserved for a low income artist. The agreement also secures a 2,000 square foot community arts center with three residency studios, affordable in perpetuity.

Arts Gowanus works alongside the Gowanus Neighborhood Coalition for Justice, the Gowanus Oversight Task Force, GRACE and the Gowanus Improvement District. The office at 543 Union Street is zoned M1-4/R7-2 inside the Gowanus special district.
