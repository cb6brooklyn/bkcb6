Charles D. Fall

Assembly Member · State Assembly District 61

Assembly Member Charles D. Fall represents State Assembly District 61. It reaches into Manhattan and Staten Island.

The district overlaps community boards Manhattan 1 and Staten Island 1.

It is policed by the 1st, 120th and 121st Precincts.

It overlaps council districts 1, 49 and 50 and Senate districts 23, 24, 26 and 27.

Members of the State Assembly serve two year terms.

Committees and legislation

Charles D. Fall chairs the Subcommittee on Consumer Fraud Protection.

Charles D. Fall also sits on the Committee on Aging, Committee on Cities, Committee on Consumer Affairs and Protection, Committee on Corporations, Authorities and Commissions, Committee on Governmental Employees, Committee on Tourism, Parks, Arts and Sports Development, Black Task Force, Black, Puerto Rican, Hispanic & Asian Legislative Caucus, Puerto Rican/Hispanic Task Force.

Recent bills sponsored include A02091, enacts the "tenant repair reconciliation act"; A02093, enacts the "consumer protection and automotive transparency act"; A02099, exempts Staten Island residents from Verrazzano-Narrows bridge tolls; A02102, prohibits the sale of vape products that are made to be attractive to or target persons under the age of twenty-one.
