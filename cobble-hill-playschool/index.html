Cobble Hill Playschool

Cooperative preschool · Carroll Gardens, Brooklyn

Cobble Hill Playschool is a cooperative preschool at 653 Hicks Street in Carroll Gardens.

In its own words: “For more than 50 years, Cobble Hill Playschool has offered a warm, cooperative preschool experience rooted in play, creativity, and connection.”
