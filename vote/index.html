Hours. Polls are open Tuesday, November 3, from 6 AM to 9 PM. If you are in line by 9 PM, you can vote.

Where. On Election Day you can only vote at your own poll site, the one shown above. It can be different from your early voting site.

Told you cannot vote? Ask for an affidavit ballot; they have to give you one.

Questions. 1-866-VOTE-NYC (1-866-868-3692), or the Brooklyn Board of Elections, 345 Adams Street, 4th Floor, 1-718-797-8800. Registration and mail ballots at vote.nyc.
