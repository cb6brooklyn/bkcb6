Brooklyn Sandbox Early Learning Center

Preschool · South Slope, Brooklyn

Brooklyn Sandbox Early Learning Center is a preschool at 548 6th Avenue in South Slope.

In its own words: “Brooklyn Sandbox Early Learning Center is a progressive preschool that provides developmentally appropriate, child-centered, play-based learning experiences for families of children ages 2 to 4.”
