Atlantic Avenue BID

Business improvement district · Atlantic Avenue

What it is

The Atlantic Avenue Business Improvement District runs along Atlantic Avenue from Fourth Avenue to the Brooklyn-Queens Expressway, with the side streets one block north and south. The north side between Court and Smith Streets is in the Court-Livingston-Schermerhorn BID instead.

It was established in 2011 and spans Community Boards 2 and 6.
