North Flatbush BID

Business improvement district · Flatbush Avenue

What it is

The North Flatbush Business Improvement District runs along Flatbush Avenue between Atlantic Avenue and Grand Army Plaza, in Community Boards 2, 6 and 8. It was established in 1986.
