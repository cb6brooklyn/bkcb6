In brief

The Atlantic Avenue BID is a business improvement district: a defined stretch of commercial street where property owners pay a mandatory assessment, on top of their property taxes, that funds extra sanitation, streetscape work, marketing and small business services inside the district.

Created by local law in 2011.

It runs through community boards Brooklyn 2 and Brooklyn 6.

It is policed by the 76th and 84th Precincts.

It sits inside council districts 33 and 39, Assembly district 52 and Senate district 26.

A BID is created, expanded or changed only by the City Council, after the community board weighs in. How a BID gets created.

Services

A BID contracts for work the city does not do at that intensity: daily sidewalk sweeping and trash removal, graffiti removal, holiday lighting and planting, banners and wayfinding, public safety supplements, marketing and events, and help for small businesses with permits, storefront grants and vacancies.

What any one district actually buys is set by its district plan and its annual budget, both of which are public. For this district, ask the BID directly.
