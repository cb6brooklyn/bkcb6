Community School District 2

NYC Public Schools · Manhattan

Community School District 2 is one of the 32 community school districts of NYC Public Schools, for the public elementary and middle schools in its area. It reaches into MNCB8 (20%), MNCB4 (17%), MNCB5 (16%), MNCB1 (15%), MNCB6 (14%), MNCB2 (13%), MNCB3 (4%).
