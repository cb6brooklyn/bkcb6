What the numbers mean

Median asking price is what sellers list, not what buyers pay. It comes from StreetEasy listings, so it reflects what is on the market now rather than what everyone in the area is paying. A neighborhood with few listings in a given month can swing sharply.

The number in parentheses beside each price is how many homes of that type were listed. A $7,000 single-family home drawn from 11 listings is a thinner figure than a $3,900 condos drawn from 400.

Colours are relative to the areas shown, not a fixed dollar scale, so the same colour means something different when you switch lens. The exception is the timeline, which uses one fixed scale across all years so you can see prices genuinely rise.

Council district figures begin in January 2023, when the current boundaries took effect.
