About This District

Manhattan Community District 7

Manhattan Community District 7 encompasses Upper West Side, Manhattan Valley, and Lincoln Square. Covering 1.9 square miles and home to more than 210,000 residents, it covers the Upper West Side between Central Park and Riverside Park.

The district includes apartment buildings, brownstones, schools, cultural institutions, parks, and major commercial corridors between Central Park and Riverside Park. It is dense, organized, and institution-rich.
