NYC Department of City Planning

City agency · land use and zoning

The Department of City Planning (DCP) plans for the city’s land use and zoning, and supports the City Planning Commission in reviewing land use applications through ULURP.

DCP’s Brooklyn borough office works with community boards on neighborhood plans and rezonings, including the 2021 Gowanus Neighborhood Plan.
