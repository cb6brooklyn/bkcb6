Brooklyn Creative Reuse

Creative reuse center · Industry City, Sunset Park, Brooklyn

Brooklyn Creative Reuse is a creative reuse center that helps divert discarded arts materials from the landfill so they are reimagined and repurposed. In its own words, Brooklyn Creative Reuse is dedicated to a more creative and sustainable Brooklyn.

Stephanie O’Brien launched Brooklyn Creative Reuse in December 2024. After a first year of pop-ups, the store opened at Industry City in spring 2026. Brooklyn Creative Reuse is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit, run largely by volunteers.

What Brooklyn Creative Reuse does

The store. Donated art and craft supplies, resold at affordable prices. Open Wednesday and Thursday, 2 PM to 7 PM, and Friday through Sunday, 11 AM to 6 PM.

Donations. Brooklyn Creative Reuse accepts adhesives, art books, ceramics and sculpture supplies, drawing and illustration supplies, fabrics and fibers, hardware, jewelry supplies, office supplies, paint and brushes, paper and canvas, and scrapbook items. Brooklyn Creative Reuse does not accept bedding, pillows, clothes, outdoor lighting or decorations, luggage, unlabeled liquids, furniture or household items.

Classes, mending circles and swaps. Workshops taught by teaching artists, a free mending circle series, and craft supply swaps and pop-ups with libraries and community organizations across Brooklyn.
