Gowanus Oversight Task Force

Volunteer, community-based group · Gowanus, Brooklyn

The Gowanus Oversight Task Force works proactively and collaboratively with New York City agencies to advance timely and equitable implementation of the commitments detailed in the 56 Points of Agreement (POA) from the Gowanus rezoning, as well as community goals outlined in the Gowanus Neighborhood Plan.

Formed as part of Commitment 1, the Task Force is a volunteer, community-based group that keeps an online repository tracking each commitment’s status and holds quarterly public meetings to promote accountability, transparency and continued engagement with the Gowanus community.

What the Task Force does

At the quarterly public meetings the public hears directly from city agencies on the status of the City’s commitments, submits questions about the POA and exchanges information about advancements in Gowanus; past meeting materials and registration for upcoming ones are on its site.

Its resources include the Gowanus Commitment Tracker, the Points of Agreement and their summary, the POA map, the Gowanus Racial Equity Report and fact sheets on the DEP and EPA CSO tank site and on the state Brownfield and federal Superfund programs. The Task Force recruits new committee members each fall.
