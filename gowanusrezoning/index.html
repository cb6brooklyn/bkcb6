The Gowanus rezoning

Every lot, its zoning before and after the 2021 vote, and what has happened since.

The City Council approved the Gowanus Neighborhood Plan on November 23, 2021 (application 210177ZMK). It changed the zoning on 724 of the 744 tax lots inside its boundary. This map shows every one of those lots: what it was zoned before, what it is zoned now, the homes added and lost on it, the new buildings, its sales and its full zoning history. Tap any lot for its record.
