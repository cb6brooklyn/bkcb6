Camp Friendship

Food pantry and youth programs · Park Slope, Brooklyn

Camp Friendship began in 1973 as a committee of concerned neighbors raising funds to send children in need to summer camp, and has grown into a nonprofit providing a food pantry and youth programs at 339 8th Street.

Since 1976, Camp Friendship has run programs for young people including baseball, basketball and handicrafts.

What Camp Friendship does

The Camp Friendship Food Pantry is open Saturdays 9 AM to 12 PM and serves over 500 households a week, including deliveries to homebound seniors. Food donations are accepted Fridays 6 to 7 PM and Saturdays 8 AM to 12:30 PM.

Volunteers run pantry distribution, deliveries, classes and youth sports.
