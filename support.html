CB6 & Beyond — help, contact, and frequently asked questions

About the App

CB6 & Beyond is the civic dashboard for Brooklyn Community Board 6 and every community district in New York City. It collects no personal data and serves no ads. All information comes from public city, state, and federal sources.

Contact

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the app track me or collect my data?
No. The app uses no analytics, no advertising trackers, and no third-party tracking tools. See the full privacy policy.

Where does the data come from?
Publicly available sources including NYC Open Data, the NYC Geoclient API, the Department of City Planning, the Board of Elections, the Department of Buildings, and individual agency feeds. Data is refreshed daily where possible.

Why are some maps slow to load on cellular?
Some maps render thousands of lots, election districts, or 311 complaints. Loading over Wi-Fi the first time helps. Once loaded, most pages work offline.

How do I find my community board?
Tap "Find My Community Board" on the home screen. The app will use your device location (with permission) or you can type any NYC address.

I found a bug or have a feature request.
Email Mike@bkcb6.org with a short description and, if possible, a screenshot. Suggestions are welcome.

Is this an official government product?
No. CB6 & Beyond is an independent civic project built by Mike Racioppo, who serves as District Manager of Brooklyn Community Board 6. The app is not endorsed by, operated by, or affiliated with the City of New York, and references to official sources are clearly labeled.
