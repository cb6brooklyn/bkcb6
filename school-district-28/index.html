Community School District 28

NYC Public Schools · Queens

Community School District 28 is one of the 32 community school districts of NYC Public Schools, for the public elementary and middle schools in its area. It reaches into QNCB12 (36%), QNCB6 (33%), QNCB9 (13%), QNCB8 (12%), QNCB10 (4%).
