Each count includes active and inactive voters, as the state reports enrollment. Boroughs are the state's county totals. Each community district adds up the election districts the Board of Elections ED map puts in it (the November 2022 map for 2022, the November 2025 map for 2025 and February 2026; eight EDs created after November 2025 are placed by the March 2026 map). A few EDs sit in one borough's county and another borough's board, such as Marble Hill (New York County, Bronx board 8), so board totals within a borough do not add up exactly to the county total; those EDs are listed under their board. Joint interest areas (parks, airports and Gateway) have their own small counts. In November 2022, 3 voters were in EDs the map does not place. EDs are redrawn and renumbered between maps, so an ED number in one year is not always the same blocks in another; the other-years line on each ED compares only where the same ED number exists.
