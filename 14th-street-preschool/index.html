14th Street Preschool

Preschool, Family Health Centers at NYU Langone · South Slope, Brooklyn

14th Street Preschool at 199 14th Street in the South Slope is one of the full-day, year-round early childhood centers run by Family Health Centers at NYU Langone.

In its own words: “Our Early Childhood Services, part of Community-Based Programs at Family Health Centers at NYU Langone, help prepare children for school and support parents and caregivers in building stable and caring relationships with their children.”
