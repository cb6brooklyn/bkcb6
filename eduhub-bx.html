ProgramsWhat sits behind these categories

Every site on this page belongs to one of these city programs. The logos are the agencies' own.

Public and charter schoolsRun or authorized by the Department of Education, which also draws the school zones and the 32 community school districts. Private and parochial schools are counted here too but are not DOE schools.
Day careLicensed child care programs, including school-based preschool child care.
Pre-K and 3-KThe DOE early childhood programs. The city's facilities data files both under one early education code, so this hub counts them together rather than guessing a split.

Data: NYC Department of City Planning Facilities Database (FacDB), dataset ji82-xba5, published on NYC Open Data. Sites are placed in community districts using the district code published with each record; records missing that code are placed by point-in-polygon against DCP community district boundaries, or by geocoding the published address through NYC Planning GeoSearch. Every source record is accounted for on the hub page.

School counts include public, charter, private and parochial, and special education sites. Grade level comes from the facility type published with each record; 331 school records carry no grade level and are shown as Level not published. Childcare counts include day care, DOE pre-K and 3-K, and preschools for students with disabilities. Public libraries are not on this hub. Basemap © OpenStreetMap contributors, © CARTO.
