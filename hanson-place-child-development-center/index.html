Hanson Place Child Development Center

Child care center · Fort Greene, Brooklyn

Hanson Place Child Development Center is a child care center at 55 Hanson Place in Fort Greene.

In its own words: “The Hanson Place Child Development Center is a non-profit state of the art early child center. We provide year-round child care services for the children of New York State employees and the community-at-large.”
