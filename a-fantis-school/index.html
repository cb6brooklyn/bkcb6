A. Fantis School

Greek Orthodox parochial school · Boerum Hill, Brooklyn

A. Fantis School is a Greek Orthodox parochial school at 195 State Street in Boerum Hill.

In its own words: “For over six decades, A. Fantis School has united Orthodox faith, Greek heritage, and academic excellence in the heart of Brooklyn Heights. Our National Blue Ribbon recognition (2024) and NAEYC-accredited preschool reflect our commitment to nurturing the whole child—mind, body, and spirit.”
