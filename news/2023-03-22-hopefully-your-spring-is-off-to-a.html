By Mike Racioppo, District Manager Wednesday, March 22, 2023

Hopefully, your spring is off to a good start. To all those who celebrate Ramadan, may you have a peaceful and blessed month.

I want to draw your attention to some spring meetings. 

Tomorrow, Thursday, March 23rds Landmarks/Land Use meeting will mark the first CB6 meeting of Spring 2023. Three applications in the Park Slope Historic District are on the agenda. It’ll be via Zoom, starting at 6:30, and if you want to attend, register at bit.ly/cb6mar23. 

Monday, March 27th’s Permits & Licenses meeting will be the second CB6 meeting of Spring 2023. The meeting will be via Zoom and will start at 6:30 PM. Register at bit.ly/327cb6. 

You’ll find more meetings and information about meetings by reading the rest of this email. So reading it could be useful, but no matter what you do, enjoy the rest of your Wednesday. 

Best,
Mike Racioppo, District Manager
