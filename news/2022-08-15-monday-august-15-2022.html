Mike Racioppo Newsletter

Monday, August 15, 2022

We’ve had two days of early voting for the State Senate and U.S House. In those two days, just over 17 thousand people voted across the city.

By Mike Racioppo, District Manager Monday, August 15, 2022

We’ve had two days of early voting for the State Senate and U.S House. In those two days, just over 17 thousand people voted across the city. These totals are slightly lower than after two days in the Gubernatorial and Assembly primaries in June and far below the Mayoral and Councilmatic primaries of 2021. 

If reading about abysmal turnout makes you want to run to the polls and have a say in who will represent you in Albany and D.C, but aren’t sure where and when to do so, please take note of the following. Early voting runs through Sunday, August 21st, and primary election day is a week from tomorrow, Tuesday, August 23rd. To find your polling site and hours, go to findmypollsite.vote.nyc. 

I’ll stop here and remind you that voting isn’t just the only thing going on. We’ve got movies, mapping, an ongoing pandemic, and an unwelcome return. If you scroll further down, you’ll find even more items. However, enjoy the rest of your day whether you scroll or stop here.

Best,
Mike Racioppo, District Manager
