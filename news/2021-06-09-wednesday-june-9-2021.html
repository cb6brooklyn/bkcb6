District Manager’s Notes

Wednesday, June 9, 2021

I hope this message finds you well overly sunny 9th day of June. In this heat, considering the sun and hear, I’ll be especially brief.

By Mike Racioppo, District Manager Wednesday, June 9, 2021

I hope this message finds you well overly sunny 9th day of June. In this heat, considering the sun and hear, I’ll be especially brief.

First, get vaccinated against COVID-19 asap. For yourself, others, and a normal that may seem a lot better than it was. 

Second, if you’re interested in the potential D15 school rezoning, check out tonight’s CEC meeting, or if you’re unsure about Ranked Choice Voting, check out tonight’s Southwest Brooklyn Tenant’s Union RCV workshop. You can start ranking candidates as soon as this Saturday, June 12th, so it’s good to know what RCV is, if you don’t already, ASAP. 

Our community is always busy, so these aren’t the only things happening in the near future. For example, tomorrow, there’s an Open Street Book Day in Red Hook and plenty more you can read about below my signature. 

Whether you check out the happening above or below, stay safe, get vaccinated, and enjoy the rest of your day! 

Best,
Mike Racioppo, District Manager
