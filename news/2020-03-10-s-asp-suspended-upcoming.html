By Mike Racioppo, District Manager Tuesday, March 10, 2020

Chaos seems to be a theme these days, but having as much accurate information on hand,asap, helps. With that in mind, our plan regarding the coronavirus is to send out what we know when we know it. We are and will continue to be, in contact with other government agencies and elected officials offices.

Wednesday’s board meeting and lots of things across our community are still going ahead as scheduled and you’ll find them listed below along with other and resources. 

One excellent resource of information is text updates from the City Government available by texting COVID to 692692. 

Regarding tomorrow’s board meeting , IF YOU ARE A BOARD MEMBER , please let me know if you will NOT be attending. 

Have a good Tuesday, and feel free to share this information however you’d like.  

Best,
Mike Racioppo, District Manager
