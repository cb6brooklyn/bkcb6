Repeat, Pause, Stay Home

If we had the right to vote on a day to be trapped in, a la Groundhogs Day , I doubt there would be a single vote cast for our necessary, “ pause ”.

By Mike Racioppo, District Manager Wednesday, March 25, 2020

If we had the right to vote on a day to be trapped in, a la Groundhogs Day , I doubt there would be a single vote cast for our necessary, “ pause ”. 

Nonetheless, I hope this message finds you well. Also, big thanks to everyone who signed up for CB6 text updates and for the feedback it’s generated that’s helped make the updates more informative. 

As of this email, there are 30,811 confirmed COVID-19 cases in New York State , 17,856 in New York City, and 4,656 in Brooklyn. We mourn the 285 people who have died in the state, including 199 of our fellow city residents. Available statistics show people between the age of 18 and 44 accounts for 46% of all infections while 33% of those infected are between 45 and 64 years old. The remaining 21 percent of infections are split between 11% of people from the age of 65 to 74, 8% are over 75 years of age and 2 percent of cases are found in those under 17 years of age. 

These numbers reflect an increase of thousands from yesterday alone, and it’s likely to increase for a while. If your labor is necessary and you can’t stay home, THANK YOU for serving others. But if you can stay home , STAY HOME! 

Not staying home will result in a greater time period during which people say (or shout) “ STAY HOME ” by hitting “Caps Lock” in newsletters from governmental agencies. 

Our continuing theme, if you can afford to, please support local businesses. For example you can find listings/suggestions below such as the North Flatbush BID’s listing of establishments taking pickup and delivery orders . 

There are more listings and other bits of information, such as the New York State tax deadline being moved to July 15 th , below. Enjoy the rest of this rainy Wednesday at a safe distance and feel free to share this information however you’d like. 

Best,
Mike Racioppo, District Manager
