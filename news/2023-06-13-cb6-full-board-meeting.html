IV.  COMMITTEE REPORTS:

A.  TRANSPORTATION/PUBLIC SAFETY- Doug Gordon, Committee Chair

The CB6 Transportation/ Public Safety committee unanimously approved the following motion during their May 17 th meeting. Approve the Department of Transportation's (DOT) plan for the 9 th  Street bike lane as presented with additional conditions:

Add Leading Pedestrian Intervals (LPIs) on 2 nd  Ave crossing 9 th  Street, and extend LPIs beyond the current 7 seconds to the maximum amount allowable.

Add LPI signage notifying cyclists that they may proceed on the pedestrian signal, add green hash marks at driveways where the bike lane protection disappears, add calming traffic measures as needed that would legally allow a 15 mph speed, and implement these changes this summer.

B. LANDMARKS - David Briggs , Committee Chair

The CB6 Landmarks Committee approved the following three applications during their May 25th meeting.

Motion to approve 169 Congress Street (Btw Clinton & Henry)- application for a Certificate of Appropriateness to allow for window replacement and a new roof terrace

Motion to approve 287 Carroll Street (BTW Smith & Hoyt) - application for a Certificate of Appropriateness to allow for alterations to the front facade, windows & awnings, and a rear yard extension . '

Motion to approve 304-306 Carroll Street (Btw Smith & Hoyt)- application for a Certificate of Appropriateness to allow for the restoration of primary and secondary facades, new windows and doors, excavation front and side areaway restoration, and non-visible rear yard extension.

Application materials are available at bit.ly/lmlumaterials52523
