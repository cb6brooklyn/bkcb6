By Mike Racioppo, District Manager Tuesday, February 25, 2020

Last night, as previewed in previous emails, our Permits and Licenses Committee met , had a quorum, and supported a motion to approve a sidewalk cafe for Medusa . The next stop for this motion will be a vote at our March 11th General Board meeting.

Before that, there are lots of things happening, such as tonight’s CAG Meeting , an open newsroom event at the Red Hook Library , CEC15 at PS 124 , and tomorrow’s CB6 Youth, Human Service, & Education committee meeting .

Remarkably there is even more happening, such as Alternate Side Parking being suspended tomorrow , so keep reading.

Have a good night, and feel free to share this information however you’d like.

Best,
Mike Racioppo, District Manager
