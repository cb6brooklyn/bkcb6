Wednesday, January 8, 2020

By Mike Racioppo, District Manager Wednesday, January 8, 2020

1-8-20

I hope this email finds you well as we approach tonight's January Board Meeting that stars at 6:30 PM in the PS 58 Auditorium (Entrance on Carroll between Smith & Court).

Our Agenda is relatively short this evening as we seek a motion to ratify a recommendation from our Permits and Licenses committee to approve an application for a sidewalk café at 72 5th Avenue on behalf of Pizza Secret . We’ll also have community session and reports from our elected official’s offices.

Speaking of elected officials, a big congrats to Assemblymember Jo Anne Simon on becoming the Chair of the New York State Assembly’s Ethics Committee !

If you choose to keep scrolling, you can find out more happenings throughout this week and next. It’s always possible you’ve seen something I missed in our active community, so if there is a community-centric item you’d like me to include,  please reach out.

Have a great evening, whether you are at the board meeting or not, and feel free to share  this information.

P.S. Please forgive any typos or brain freezes in the text as I get these updates out in haste at the end of the day.

Michael Racioppo, District Manager
