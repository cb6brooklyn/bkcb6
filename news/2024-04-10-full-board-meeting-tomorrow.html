II. CHAIRPERSON’S REPORT

A.     PERMITS & LICENSES

Recommendations to approve applications  for on-premises liquor licenses at the following locations:

274 3rd Avenue between President & Carroll Street

177 Dwight Street at the corner of Van Dyke

392 5th Avenue between 6th & 7th Street

Recommendation to conditionally approve a ULURP application for 150 Mill Street , which would allow for a change in its zoning from the current M1﻿-1 to an M1-5 to facilitate the development of a ten-story mixed-use building for retail, health and care, and office space.

C. Parks & Recreation

Motion to renew support for  The Play Fair for Parks  campaign which calls for the city’s FY 2025 budget to fully dedicate 1% of its total to New York City Parks.
