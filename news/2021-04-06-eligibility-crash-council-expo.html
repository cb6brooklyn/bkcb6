By Mike Racioppo, District Manager Tuesday, April 6, 2021

With today viewed as a tale of hope, the sun stands for the shining prospect that, in New York, EVERYONE who is at least 16 years old is now ELIGIBLE to receive a COVID-19 VACCINE. You can find appointments here or, if you’re at least 75 years of age or tag along with such a senior, you can walk into the Brooklyn Army Terminal and get a shot without making an appointment. You can even get one on one of the 20 buses administering vaccines. No matter how you chose to get it, get it ASAP. 

On a less optimistic note, the crash that occurred this morning at 4th Avenue & 3rd Street led to 5 injuries hospitalizations. Luckily there are no fatalities, but it certainly reinforces our consistent support and commitment to increasing street safety and traffic calming. As more information is received, I’ll share it.

﻿

We’ve got lots happening the rest of this week, and next week, you can read about below my signature. On that note, stay safe, get vaccinated and enjoy the rest of your Tuesday.

Best,
Mike Racioppo, District Manager
