Upcoming, Alternate, Reality

I hope this email finds you well on this Tuesday that might feel like a Monday but is just one big blur. Anyway, I hope you took some time to reflect and then enjoyed Memorial Day, as well as the previous 2 days that made up the weekend.

By Mike Racioppo, District Manager Tuesday, May 26, 2020

I hope this email finds you well on this Tuesday that might feel like a Monday but is just one big blur. Anyway, I hope you took some time to reflect and then enjoyed Memorial Day, as well as the previous 2 days that made up the weekend.

The COVID-19 crisis continues with regions closer to us and even land masses to which we are attached, such as Long Island, beginning to open up . Also, when things do “open,” keeping our distance and covering our faces will still be a key to staying open until a vaccine is found. 

When a vaccine is found, I’m sure you’ll hear about it before my newsletter makes mention of it, but I doubt that source will give you updates on the Alternate Side Parking regulations. Speaking of Alternate Side regulations, they are suspended until June 7th , while parking meters remain in effect.

Best,
