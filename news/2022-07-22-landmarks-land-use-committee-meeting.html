Landmarks/Land Use Committee Meeting

By Brooklyn Community Board 6 Friday, July 22, 2022

6:30PM on Thursday, July 28, 2022 on Zoom

Agenda

Presentation on behalf of 420 Carroll Street outlining their waterfront plan.

Presentation on behalf of  300 Nevins Street  outlining their waterfront plan.

356 Hoyt Street , Carroll Gardens Historic District : Certificate of Appropriateness application to the Landmarks Preservation Commission to replace front facade brick infill and windows with new windows and wood paneling; replace north/south facade with cement stucco; install new parapet walls and stair bulkhead on the roof.

159 Congress Street , Cobble Hill Historic District : Certificate of Appropriateness application to the Landmarks Preservation Commission to create a roof terrace at the attic level, install a new 3-story window system at the rear facade, and rebuild rear facade masonry in kind

120 Pacific Street , Cobble Hill Historic District : Certificate of Appropriateness application to the Landmarks Preservation Commission to replace six 1-over-1 double-hung windows on the front facade, replace five double-hung windows at the rear facade, and replace the existing terrace door with an aluminum-clad door

83 7th Avenue , Park Slope Historic District : Certificate of Appropriateness application to the Landmarks Preservation Commission for a newly recessed location for the storefront entrance

Committee Chairs:

David Briggs, Co-Chair, Landmarks

Alec Schierenbeck, Co-Chair, Land Use
