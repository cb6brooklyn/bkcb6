By Mike Racioppo, District Manager Monday, February 4, 2019

Maybe I was a bit too dismissive of the predictive power of groundhogs  as Chuck and Phil reached a consensus  and suddenly spring is in the air .

Starting off the week with some familiar reminders and one explosive tidbit.

1 - Gowanus Construction -  Update from DDC regarding the $26M High-Level Storm Sewers and Water Main Replacement in north Gowanus/ Boreum Hill (CB6 & CB2).

Before sailing away from the Gowanus Canal let me remind you that Wednesday is the Department of City Planning open house at PS 32 .

2 - Tomorrow, February 5th, is Lunar New Year .  NYC Public Schools are closed , alternate side parking is suspended but meters remain in effect.

Check out BKLYNER for some suggestions on how to celebrate.

3 - Tomorrow is the Green New Deal watch party.

4 - I know some  folks in Park Slope had heard a “mysterious boom” last night at around 11:30.  Authorities informed me that it was caused by fireworks.   

5 -  Legislative update from Assistant Speaker Felix Ortiz .

6 -   As mentioned in the second bullet point, Alternate Side is suspended tomorrow, February 5 th  while meters remain in effect.

-Subway service changes for tomorrow, February 5th

- Bus service changes for tomorrow, February 5th

As always, feel free to share this information anyway you see fit!

Best,
Mike Racioppo, District Manager
