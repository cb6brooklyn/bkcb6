By Mike Racioppo, District Manager Monday, July 13, 2020

I hope this email finds you well as we kick off the second week of Phase 3 after a jazz parade made an appearance in our district over the weekend. New York's Covid-19 statistics continue to show close to 1 percent infection rates and under ten deaths a day( no deaths in NYC Saturday ), although every death is still is a tragedy. The nation's statistics, on the other hand, especially in places that reopened early, such as Florida, set new records . So while, for those that get the reference, I sound like a broken record and to others just plain old repetitive, keep your distance and mask on.

I'll be brief before urging you to check out the items below. First Alternate Side Parking Is suspended until Sunday, July 19th. Also, please note that work on the BQE has been rescheduled to start today, July 13th , while work on the Union Street Bridge has been suspended until July 16th .

Keep scrolling for more information that may interest you. Whether you scroll or not, sit outside at a restaurant, or anything else we haven't been able to do, in case you haven't heard, keep your distance, with your face covered so you can enjoy the rest of the day and many more.

Best,
Mike Racioppo, District Manager
