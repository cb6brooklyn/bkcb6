I hope this email finds you well on this sunny and cold Thursday as we kick-off, the Twenty-Twenties !

By Mike Racioppo, District Manager Thursday, January 2, 2020

I hope this email finds you well on this sunny and cold Thursday as we kick-off, the Twenty-Twenties !

Just a few quick reminders as our community awakens from holiday hibernation.

Next week we have our first committee meeting and general board meeting of the twenties. On Monday, January 6 th our Permits and Licenses Committee will meet in the Cobble Hill Community Room at 250 Baltic Street to review a sidewalk café application and on Wednesday, January 8 th, our board will meet at P.S 58. Both meetings will start at 6:30 PM.

In between those 2 meetings, on the morning of Tuesday, January 7 th , Assemblymember Simon will host her first “ Java with Jo Anne ” of 2020 at Root Hill Café (4 th avenue and Carroll Street).

You can find out more about these, and other events/topics by continuing to scroll.

Of course, it’s always possible you’ve seen something I missed in our active community, so if there is a community-centric item you’d like me to include, please reach out.

As always, have a great evening, and feel free to share  this information .

P.S. Please forgive any typos or brain freezes in the text as I get these updates out in haste at the end of the day.

Best,
Mike Racioppo, District Manager
