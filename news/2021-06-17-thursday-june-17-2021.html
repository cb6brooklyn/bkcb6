I’ll be brief as tonight is likely to be a long one for CB6. Why is that likely?

By Mike Racioppo, District Manager Thursday, June 17, 2021

I’ll be brief as tonight is likely to be a long one for CB6. Why is that likely? Because, as mentioned many times, tonight at 6:30 PM, our Landmarks/ Land Use committee will hear from DCP and vote on the rezoning. After that, whatever the committee ends up saying will be passed on to the full board for a final resolution and vote at our June 23rd meeting. The process then moves on to the Borough President for his recommendation(s) on June 30TH.   Once again, here is the draft resolution that the committee is currently circulating. Also, another refresher, click here for all the previous presentations we’ve been given and statements we’ve made on the rezoning. 

On that note, I’ll suggest scrolling past my signature for some interesting items but either way, enjoy the rest of your Thursday.

Best,
Mike Racioppo, District Manager
