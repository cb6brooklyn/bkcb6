By Mike Racioppo, District Manager Thursday, December 26, 2019

Whether you celebrated Christmas, are preparing for the 5 th day of Chanukah, the start of Kwanzaa, or saying “Bah, Humbug,” I hope this email finds you well and that you’re able to put, at least some, of the following information to good use. For example, if you keep reading, you’ll find information on how to make a Christmas Tree become mulch or where to see fireworks on New Year's Eve.

This week is relatively dead, but our community continues to have a pulse. For that reason, it’s easy to miss something. So, if you’d like a community-centric happening in a future update,  please reach out .  

No matter what, or if you’re celebrating, have a great evening and feel free to share this information .

Best,
Mike Racioppo, District Manager
