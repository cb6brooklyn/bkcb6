By Mike Racioppo, District Manager Friday, February 7, 2020

This past week was a busy one for our community. It included GNCJ releasing their ( in line with what we’ve said ) priorities , GBD’s strategy finale , the CAG’s water quality petition , and the State of The City which included plans for solar power ,   Universal Literacy , housing , and small businesses .

The plans and priorities mentioned above are ideas at this point. Turning them into reality and implemented policies will require a great deal of action and work.

They are also ideas that are broadly in line with what CB-6 and our community at -large support. For that reason, we’ll have to be vigilant to ensure proper implementation.

Keep reading to find some community happenings, reminders, and more.

Have a great weekend and feel free to share this information however you'd like.

Best,
Mike Racioppo, District Manager
