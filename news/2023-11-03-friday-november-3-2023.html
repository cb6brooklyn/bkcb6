District Manager’s Notes

Friday, November 3, 2023

November's first weekend looks like it'll be our first fully rain-free one in quite some time. We even get an extra hour to make the most of it.

By Mike Racioppo, District Manager Friday, November 3, 2023

November's first weekend looks like it'll be our first fully rain-free one in quite some time. We even get an extra hour to make the most of it.

What you do with that extra hour, and any other, is obviously up to you, but there's a range of options in our ever-active community. Over the week ahead, that includes but is not limited to a CB6 Full Board meeting, the marathon, cheering, quilting, tasting, and voting.

As always, this is an advisory opinion, but you'll find more information about these and other items by reading the rest of this email. But no matter what you do, have a great weekend.

Best,
Mike Racioppo, District Manager
