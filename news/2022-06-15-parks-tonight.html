Parks Tonight

I hope this email finds you well on this fifteenth day of June. I have four items to highlight ; two reminders and two announcements.

By Mike Racioppo, District Manager Wednesday, June 15, 2022

I hope this email finds you well on this fifteenth day of June. I have four items to highlight ; two reminders and two announcements. 

The most immediate reminder is that tonight, Wednesday, June 15th, at 6:30 PM over Zoom, our Parks Committee will meet regarding whether to designate Upper Van Voorhees as a Dog Park officially. To register, go to bit.ly/June15ParksCB6.

﻿The second reminder is that tomorrow, Thursday, June 16th, from 6:00 PM to 8:00 PM, the Red Hook Coastal Resiliency Project will host a virtual meeting on their draft design. Register at tinyurl.com/RHCR60.

The first announcement is that we are co-sponsoring a Last Mile Warehouse Forum with Councilmember Alexa Aviles. The forum will take place next Thursday, June 23rd at Red Hook’s PS 15 and begin at 5 PM. It will include representatives from the Department of Transportation (DOT), City Planning, and the Economic Development Corporation (EDC) on the impact of Last Mile Warehouses on the Red Hook Community. RSVP for the forum at bit.ly/last-mileforum.

The other announcement is that the CB6 Landmarks/Land Use and CB6 Parks / Environmental Protection Committees will host a joint meeting a week from tomorrow, Thursday, June 23rd. The purpose of the meeting will be to receive presentations and updates from DCP and the Department of Environmental Protection (DEP). DCP's presentation will focus on the ongoing Gowanus Rezoning, and DEP will discuss the proposed design of the Owl's Head CSO Facility & Salt Lot. Register for this Zoom meeting at bit.ly/BKCB6June23GowanusUpdates

More information can be found by scrolling down this email. However, whether you scroll or not, stay safe and enjoy the rest of your day.

Best,
Mike Racioppo, District Manager
