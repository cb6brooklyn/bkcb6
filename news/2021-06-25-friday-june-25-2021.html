By Mike Racioppo, District Manager Friday, June 25, 2021

The busy week included an election, a full board meeting that featured a vote to approve the Gowanus Rezoning conditionally and last night's Land Use meeting. At last night's meeting e conditionally approved the Salt Lot ULURP; and, without conditions, approved the rezoning of 506 & 530 3rd Ave. We approved the text amendments for fitness centers & fresh food while disapproving the special hotel permit. 

If you want to see or relive the action, do so on Youtube. There's also coverage of our Gowanus vote in City Limits. Speaking of CB6 meetings and votes, we have a Permits & Licenses meeting this Monday, June 28th, over Zoom, at 6:30 PM. 

On that note, as the sun goes down earlier each day until December, I’ll wrap it up. But before I do, I suggest reading past my signature to see, at least some, of what our always active community is up to this weekend. 

But regardless of what you read, or don’t read, have a great weekend! 

Best,
Mike Racioppo, District Manager
