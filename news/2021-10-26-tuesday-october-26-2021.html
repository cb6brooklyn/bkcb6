Mike Racioppo Newsletter

Tuesday, October 26, 2021

I hope this email finds you well as we continue to deal with the storm’s impact that began last night and as we brace for more rain.

By Mike Racioppo, District Manager Tuesday, October 26, 2021

I hope this email finds you well as we continue to deal with the storm’s impact that began last night and as we brace for more rain.

Last night, before the heavy downpour, our Permits and Licenses committee met and approved two liquor license applicants. If you missed it or want to relive it, it’s available on YouTube. Those two items will now head to our full board for a vote on Wednesday, November 10th. After committee review, Thursday’s Landmarks/ Land Use agenda items will also be voted on at the full board.

I’ll stop here and suggest you scroll down for other items such as Halloween events in Cobble Hill, Carroll Park, Park Slope, and Red Hook.

But no matter what you decide, stay safe, and enjoy the rest of your Tuesday.

Best,
Mike Racioppo, District Manager
