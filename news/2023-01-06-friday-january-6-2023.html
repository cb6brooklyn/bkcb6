By Mike Racioppo, District Manager

With thirteen votes thus far, other governmental bodies have lived out the theory of eternal reoccurrence. As a community and governmental body, CB6 tries to be more linear. 

To that point, the first complete weekend and work-work of 2023 will see CB6 meet twice and welcome our new chair, Eric Mcclure. Both meetings will be on Zoom and begin at 6:30 PM. First. On Monday, January 9th, our Permits & Licenses Committee meets to vote on liquor licenses; register at bit.ly/cb6jan9. The second is our full board meeting on Wednesday, January 11th. Register at bit.ly/cb6jan11.

Of course, more than our meetings, and not just mulching, are happening across our community. I’ve listed many of those things below my signature and suggest you check them out. Please email me if you’d like me to include something in future newsletters, 

Enjoy your weekend. 

Best,
