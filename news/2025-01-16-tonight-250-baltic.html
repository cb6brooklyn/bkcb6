Tonight @ 250 Baltic

By Brooklyn Community Board 6 Thursday, January 16, 2025

Art, Library, Sponge, Flood

Who: 

CB6 Human Services & Environmental Sustainability Committee Meeting

Where:

Community Room at 250 Baltic Street

When: 

Thursday, January 16, 6:30 pm

Why: 

To go over the agenda items listed below :

Percent for Art Project Initiation for Red Hook Library : Overview of the Percent for Art program, the artwork selection process for this project, and opportunities for community participation.

City Sponge will present on local flooding and the upcoming Flood Solutions Fair on Saturday, January 25th, from 1 to 4 PM . Gowanus Canal Conservancy, FOFA, the Park Slope Civic Council, the Boreum Hill Association, and CB6 are co-sponsoring the meeting.

There will be a virtual option, but you are strongly encouraged to attend in person if you'd like to participate in the meeting. Email zoom@bkcb6.org with any questions regarding virtual option.
