By Mike Racioppo, District Manager Wednesday, May 6, 2020

I hope this email finds you well on and serves as a final reminder in advance of tonight's" Check-in." It will be structured just like our April "Check-in" . Before going any further, I’d like to mention a debt of gratitude to Councilman Lander and his staff, specifically Megan Flynn, for all their help in coordinating this. You can still register here and, for those that want to follow along, I’ll share a link to the video once it is available.

I've said the participant list was fluid. Now, along with Councilmember Brad Lander, we're expecting the New York State Attorney General Letitia James, NYC Comptroller Scott Stringer, Assemblymembers Carroll, Ortiz & Simon, and Senators Parker & Kavanagh. We will also hear from the Mayors Deputy Chief of Staff, Lincoln Restler, NYC Census Director Julie Menin, District 15 Superintendent Anita Skop, DOT, DOB Con- Ed, and NYP Brooklyn Methodist. I'll add that this list, even now, could still shift. As they say, the only constant is change.

I hope you join us tonight, but if not, I recommend checking out some of the items below and, either way, my main hope is for everyone reading this to stay safe.

Best,
Mike Racioppo, District Manager
