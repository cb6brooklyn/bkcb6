The snowless streak continues while civics never stops. 

To that point, the Gowanus CAG meets tonight; on Thursday, we've got a Landmarks meeting, and the MTA hosts a Brooklyn Bus town hall. The following weeks will include liquor licenses and our February full board meeting. All the meetings mentioned have virtual options, so no matter how much rain we get or if we end our snowless streak, you could attend while keeping quite dry. 

Other things are happening across our community, but you'll have to read the rest of this email to find them. The "other" includes ways to help migrants, job postings, a"Restaurant Week" that runs for a month, and more. 

Whether you read the rest or not, stay safe and enjoy the rest of your Tuesday. 

Best,
