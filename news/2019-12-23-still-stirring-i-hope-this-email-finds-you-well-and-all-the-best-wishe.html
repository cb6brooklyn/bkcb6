By Mike Racioppo, District Manager Monday, December 23, 2019

I hope this email finds you well and all the best wishes to everyone, whether celebrating Chanukah, preparing for Christmas or Kwanza, Festivus or nothing at all.

While this is a relatively quiet week, creatures are still stirring. Whether you’re one of those active creatures or not, you may want to mark your calendar or catch up on reading with GCC’s Lowland plans , GBD’s zoning strategies , or our letters to DCP .

Speaking of Gowanus, I’d like to thank everyone who has sent us comments regarding Public Place/Gowanus Green , and we’re still happy to accept them .

Keep reading below for a few items of potential interests and more visually appealing hyperlinks to things mentioned above. Thanks to our always active community, it’s easy to miss something. So, if you’d like a community-centric happening in a future update,  please reach out .

As always, have a great evening and feel free to share this information any way you’d like. 

Best,
Mike Racioppo, District Manager
