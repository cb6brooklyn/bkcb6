By Mike Racioppo, District Manager

Speaking of tomorrow’s board meeting, it starts at 6:30 PM, and you can register for it here. Please note that it will feature a presentation about the Covid-19 vaccines from doctors from our district's most prominent healthcare provider, New York-Presbyterian Brooklyn Methodist hospital. If you’d like to submit a comment or question in advance, please use this form. If you'd like to use the translation services, follow these steps and select the Spanish audio channel.

On the city services front, Alternate Side Parking regulations are suspended through Friday, and sanitation collections are resuming. However, delays are to be expected in pick ups as snow operations are still ongoing. Speaking of snow operations, you may sign up here to work part-time for DSNY clearing snow.

On that note, I look forward to virtually seeing many of you tomorrow. Lastly, check out the items below and enjoy the rest of your Tuesday. 

Best,
Mike Racioppo, District Manager
