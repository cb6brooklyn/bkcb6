Tomorrow Full Board Meeting , Thursday DEP

By Mike Racioppo, District Manager Tuesday, February 8, 2022

I hope this email finds you well on this partly cloudy board meeting eve. The sunset was twenty-two minutes after five today, and now I’ll shift to some civics.

First, as folks start to file their taxes this year, please be aware that New York City is offering free tax filing services. Families with an income of $72,000 or less and single filers with an income of $50,000 or less in 2021 are eligible. If you’re interested or want more information on this, go to:nyc.gov/TaxPrep.

Secondly, as referred to above, tomorrow, Wednesday, February 9th, is our February full board meeting. It will be held over Zoom and begin at 6:30 PM. To register to attend the meeting, go to bit.ly/Feb9CB6.

Third, on Thursday the 10th, at 7 pm, DEP will host a meeting on the Owls Head CSO. To register, go to https://bit.ly/feb10dep

Lastly, and this is a standing opinion of mine, if you keep scrolling down this email, you’ll find additional interesting items and happenings. That includes multiple meetings, job listings, and more. However, whether you read past here or not, stay safe and enjoy the rest of your day.

Best,
Mike Racioppo, District Manager
