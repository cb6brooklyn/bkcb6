Snow, School, Alternate Side & More

By Mike Racioppo, District Manager Monday, February 12, 2024

I'd guess you've heard about the anticipated snow that's expected to fall. No matter how much snow sticks or falls, some service changes have been announced for tomorrow, Tuesday, February 13th, such as schools being closed, alternate side parking suspended, and a travel advisory in place until 6 PM.

Moving to our more usual fare, the hearing for Council Member Aviles's shore power legislation (formerly intro 1050) has been postponed until Thursday, February 15th, at 10 AM. Borough President Reynoso's State of The Borough will be rescheduled. One more scheduling reminder: our February Full Board Meeting is next Wednesday, February 21st, at Van Alen and over Zoom. 

On that note, I suggest scrolling and reading the rest of this email, and I'll share more information if and when I have some. No matter what, stay safe and warm, and hopefully enjoy some snow.

Best,
Mike Racioppo, District Manager
