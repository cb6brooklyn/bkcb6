District Manager’s Notes

Wednesday, January 13, 2021

I offer a brief hello and reminder before "seeing" many of you later tonight for our first full board meeting of 2021. It will kick off at 6:30 PM via zoom.

By Mike Racioppo, District Manager Wednesday, January 13, 2021

I offer a brief hello and reminder before "seeing" many of you later tonight for our first full board meeting of 2021. It will kick off at 6:30 PM via zoom. If you're a board member, you will have received a panelist invite. If you're a board member and haven't received it, please check your spam, and if it's not there, email me, and I'll resend an invite to the email of your choice. 

Tonight’s agenda will see final votes on a liquor license, a landmark application, and a ferry over to Governors Island. So, if you want live-action civilized civics, can’t wait till it’s recapped in tomorrow’s newsletter or posted on YouTube, or have nothing better to do, click here to join us. 

With that, I’ll recommend reading on below, staying safe, seeing if you can get vaccinated, and maybe reading our Gowanus related writing. 

No matter what you decide to do, enjoy the rest of your day.

Best,
Mike Racioppo, District Manager
