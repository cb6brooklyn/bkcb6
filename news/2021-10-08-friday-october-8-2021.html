Friday, October 8, 2021

Our community has a lot going on this weekend, and the week ahead, so as usual, I’ll be brief and focus on two very important CB6 items and recommend reading further down this email.

By Mike Racioppo, District Manager

Our community has a lot going on this weekend, and the week ahead, so as usual, I’ll be brief and focus on two very important CB6 items and recommend reading further down this email. 

First, as the Gowanus rezoning continues to proceed along the ULURP process, I’ll again remind everyone that this coming Tuesday, October 12th, the City Council will hold a hearing on it. If you’d like to testify, you can register to do so here. If one wants to be reminded of our conditional approval, read it by clicking this link. You’ll also find Borough President Adams, very similar, conditional approval of the Gowanus rezoning in the same folder.

Secondly, on Wednesday, October 13th, we have our full board beginning at 6:30 PM.

Other useful information is below, like Alternate Side Parking, sanitation service, and resiliency meetings, so I’ll stop here and recommend you read on now. Whether you follow that recommendation or not, stay safe, and have a great weekend.

Best,
Mike Racioppo, District Manager
