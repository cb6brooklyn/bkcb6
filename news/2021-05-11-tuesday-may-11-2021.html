Mike Racioppo Newsletter

Tuesday, May 11, 2021

For the first time in months, the city’s COVID-19 positivity rate is below 2 percent! All other indicators are heading in the right direction, so, at the risk of being excessively repetitive, I’ll again remind you to get vaccinated ASAP.

By Mike Racioppo, District Manager Tuesday, May 11, 2021

For the first time in months, the city’s COVID-19 positivity rate is below 2 percent! All other indicators are heading in the right direction, so, at the risk of being excessively repetitive, I’ll again remind you to get vaccinated ASAP. This is one trend we should follow.

Tonight the CEC hosts a meeting on a school district rezoning, and, coincidentally, it’s also the last day to vote for CEC members. As alluded to above, don’t forget tomorrow night is our May full board meeting, and be aware that later this week, Thursday, Friday, and Saturday, Alternate Side Parking will be suspended.

I’ll recommend scrolling, and reading, below for more information on things happening across the community. But no matter what, stay safe, and enjoy the rest of your day.

Best,
Mike Racioppo, District Manager
