Tuesday, February 28, 2023

First, DOT is hosting an in-person BQE workshop at the Brooklyn Friends School in Brooklyn Heights tonight at 6 PM. If you can’t make it tonight, DOT will also present to our CB6 Transportation & Public Safety Committee on March 16th.

By Mike Racioppo, District Manager

First, DOT is hosting an in-person BQE workshop at the Brooklyn Friends School in Brooklyn Heights tonight at 6 PM. If you can’t make it tonight, DOT will also present to our CB6 Transportation & Public Safety Committee on March 16th. Speaking of CB6 meetings, don’t forget that our March Full Board meeting is next Wednesday, March 8th.

Secondly, if you want to comment on the Army Corps of Engineers Coastal Storm Plan, don’t forget that the deadline is March 7th. Before you submit comments, peruse sites such as Rebuild By Design’s website or watch the Gowanus Canal Conservancy offer their thoughts on YouTube.  

You'll be able to find out more by reading the rest of this email. No matter what you do, have a good night.

Best,
Mike Racioppo, District Manager
