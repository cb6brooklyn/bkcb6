Final Full Board Tonight

By Mike Racioppo, District Manager Wednesday, December 9, 2020

Good Afternoon

I hope this email finds you well on this, sort of snowy , 9th day of December.

Here's just a brief hello and reminder before "seeing" many of you later  tonight  for our  December board meeting . It will kick off at 6:30 PM via zoom.  If you're a  board member,  you will have received a panelist invite. If you're a board member and haven't received it, please check your spam, and if it's not there, email me, and I'll resend an invite to the email of your choice. Also, as noted we’ve got a busy agenda so please look it over prior to the meeting .

Thanks to everyone who has registered for today's meeting and we hope for it to be as civically engaging and civil as possible. If you are so energized and engaged that you’d like to apply for board membership please follow this link and note that that applications for the 2021-2023 term will be open until February 12th, 2021. 

Anyway, I hope you keep reading beyond my email signature, and I hope to see you later, but in any event, stay safe!

Michael Racioppo, District Manager
