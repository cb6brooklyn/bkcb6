District Manager’s Notes

Monday, January 8, 2018

We've just had Christmas and New Year’s eves and yes they are undeniably big events. However, we have now arrived at the biggest eve of them all; it is the day before the  January BCB6 General Board Meeting .

By Mike Racioppo, District Manager Monday, January 8, 2018

We've just had Christmas and New Year’s eves and yes they are undeniably big events. However, we have now arrived at the biggest eve of them all; it is the day before the  January BCB6 General Board Meeting . This awesome confab will take place tomorrow night, beginning at 6:30 PM, at  New York-Presbyterian Brooklyn Methodist Hospital  and will conclude with a motion to adjourn. 

Along with that important reminder I’m sending along a few bits of information that, I hope, can be put to good use.

1 - Next Wednesday, January 16 th  at my Alma Mater,  MS 51 , we are co-sponsoring, with  CEC15 ,  BKCB7  and other groups, a forum on the  Mayor's plan to improve diversity at Specialized High Schools.   

Check out the  flyer here.

 2 - Want to thank our very own Victoria Alexander for putting the  Red Hook Winter Coat giveaway on my radar.  Check it out.

3 - Alternate side parking rules, and meters,  will be in effect tomorrow Wednesday, January 9 th .

-Subway service changes for tomorrow, January 9th.

-  Bus Service Changes for tomorrow, January 9th.

Of course, as always, feel free to share this information with anyone, by any means you see fit!

Best,
Mike Racioppo, District Manager
