District Manager’s Notes

Monday, October 18, 2021

I hope this email finds you well after a weekend that saw the return of Open Studios and a Battle of Brooklyn reference on Saturday Night Live.

By Mike Racioppo, District Manager Monday, October 18, 2021

I hope this email finds you well after a weekend that saw the return of Open Studios and a Battle of Brooklyn reference on Saturday Night Live.

I’ll be brief but want to remind you that we have 2 CB6 meetings this week. On Wednesday, at our Parks & Environmental Protection Committee, we’ll hear from the Red Hook Coastal Resiliency Project. The following night, Thursday, October 21st, the NYC DOT will present plans for Columbia Street to our Transportation & Public Safety Committee.

In the interest of time, I’ll stop here, suggest you keep scrolling, but either way, stay safe and enjoy the rest of your day.

Best,
Mike Racioppo, District Manager
