By Mike Racioppo, District Manager Friday, March 20, 2020

The thing on most New Yorkers’ minds right now is Governor Cuomo putting New York State on, as he referred to it, “pause” with only “essential” businesses and services remaining open. In the most simplified sense, that means business's that provide healthcare and food(pick up/delivery), along with laundromat’s and plumbers, remaining active. You can click here to find out directly from the Governor’s office as information is frequently changing, and I don’t know when you’ll read this email. 

There is more information on schools, businesses, hospitals, and more below. 

Again thanks to all of you who have shared information with me to amplify here and on social media. Sharing has reinforced the only positive theme of the last few weeks- that we’re in this together.If you think someone you know may want to receive these updates (aside from those who received this email), please let them know you can sign up by texting BKCB6 to 22828 or using this link . 

Have as good of a weekend that could be, stay safe, and feel free to share this information however you’d like. 

Best,
Mike Racioppo, District Manager
