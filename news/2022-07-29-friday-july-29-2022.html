Mike Racioppo Newsletter

Friday, July 29, 2022

The hope is that you're doing well and that the humidity isn't dampening your spirits as we head into the final weekend of July. I won't hold you long from starting the weekend, but I have some recaps and reminders.

By Mike Racioppo, District Manager Friday, July 29, 2022

The hope is that you're doing well and that the humidity isn't dampening your spirits as we head into the final weekend of July. I won't hold you long from starting the weekend, but I have some recaps and reminders.

Last night our Landmarks/ Land Use Committee met. We had a sizeable agenda, but Committee Chair, David Briggs, ran the meeting efficiently while Angelica Ramdhari was indispensable hosting on Zoom. The committee approved all three Certificate of Appropriateness applications and saw presentations of waterfront plans for 420 Carroll Street and 300 Nevins Street. If the preceding prose makes you want to learn more about it, watch the meeting on our YouTube page or check out the presentations at bit.ly/728lmlumaterials.

Secondly, even as we approach August, a lot is going on. That includes surveys, jams, tastes, music, art, open streets, and more. The safest way to enjoy these things is to be vaccinated and boosted against covid and possibly monkeypox. So don't forget you can visit vax4nyc.nyc.gov or vax4nyc.nyc.gov/monkeypox and make an appointment. 

I'll stop here and suggest scrolling and finding out further information. But, no matter what you do, have a great weekend. 

Best,
Mike Racioppo, District Manager
