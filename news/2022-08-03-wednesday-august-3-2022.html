Hoping all is well in advance of the heat advisory that’ll be in effect tomorrow and Friday. If you need some assistance getting cool and staying that way, go to bit.ly/beattheheatnyc.

By Mike Racioppo, District Manager

Hoping all is well in advance of the heat advisory that’ll be in effect tomorrow and Friday. If you need some assistance getting cool and staying that way, go to bit.ly/beattheheatnyc. I’ll be brief today.

As our community had long worked for, there will be a supermarket at 120 5th Avenue, which was a Key Food for decades. When it opens, it will be a “Lidl” supermarket. I’ve not been to a Lidl, but our chair, Peter Fleming, spoke highly of it upon hearing the lease was signed. This is certainly good news in contrast to many supermarkets closing. 

I’ll stop here and recommend reading, or clicking, the items further down this email. You’ll find you can go back to the future, collect data, apply for an absentee ballot, and more. But, no matter what you do, enjoy the rest of your day. 

Best,
Mike Racioppo, District Manager
