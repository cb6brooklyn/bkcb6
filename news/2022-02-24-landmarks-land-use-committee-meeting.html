Agenda

41 Summit Street  - ULURP Application (C 200317 ZMK) to the Department of City Planning for a zoning map amendment to rezone 41 Summit Street (Block 352, Lot 60 and p/o Lot 1) from an M1-1 to R6B zoning district to facilitate the development of a Use Group 2 four-story plus basement residential building with a total floor area of 5000 sq. ft. (2.0 FAR) and four dwelling unit s.
