By Mike Racioppo, District Manager Wednesday, November 20, 2019

In yesterday’s update , I wrote that, while referring to Gowanus, when we "find out more we find out more, you’ll find out more." So, while we don’t yet have a completed DEIS, on Monday, December 2 nd at P.S 32 , the Department of City Planning (DCP) and Housing, Preservation and Development (HPD), will be presenting an update on the Public Place / Gowanus Green site to our Economic/Waterfront/ Community Development and Housing Committee . So, if Gowanus interests you, we hope you can attend.

Moving to reminders, tonight Wednesday, November 20th , 6:30 pm, our Environmental Protection Committee meets at 250 Baltic Street , and at 7 PM, the Red Hook Civic Association meets at  P.S 15 .

Tomorrow night, Thursday, November 21 st , 6:30 pm, our T ransportation and Public Safety Committee meets at the 78th Precinct . Also, on Thursday, November 21st, there will be a Red Hook East & West update meeting at the Miccio Center from 6 until 8:00 PM.

On Friday, November 22 nd , Councilmember Menchaca will be holding a Participatory Budgeting meeting at the Red Hook Justice Center from 1 until 3 pm and, looking ahead to the weekend , greenmarkets will be held are across the district/ at district adjacent locations such as  Grand Army Plaza , Bartell Pritchard Square , Carroll Gardens and the Old Stone House . Markets such as these could be especially helpful prior thanksgiving. 

As I say every day, we are lucky to have such an active community, and there is a chance I’ve missed something. If there is an event or something community-centric happening that you think I/others should know about, please let me know .

That’s all I’ve got for today, have a good evening and feel free to share this information however you’d like.

P.S Alternate side parking, like it was today, will be in effect tomorrow, November 21 st , don’t forget to take the Brooklyn Bus Survey , and a CB6 spot could be restaurant of the year!

Best,
Mike Racioppo, District Manager
