Check In Tuesday, Data, Maps & More

I hope this message finds you well as we head into what was once known as, and hopefully will be again the weekend.

By Mike Racioppo, District Manager Friday, April 3, 2020

I hope this message finds you well as we head into what was once known as, and hopefully will be again the weekend. 

I'll start by letting everyone know, as teased earlier in the week, that Tuesday night, starting at 6:30 PM we will be co-sponsoring a "Community Check-In " with Councilman Lander , Assemblymembers Carroll , Simon and Ortiz with panelists from agencies including DOE , DOT , Parks , FDNY , NYPD and NYC Census 2020 . The list is still in formation, but if you have a question for any of those listed, please send an email here with a subject identifying who your question is intended for. Before moving forward, I must offer a big thank you to Councilman Lander and his staff for doing a great job bringing me into the zoom world. 

It's mentioned below, along with lots of other resources. Still, please take special consideration of the Department of Health's new guidelines asking everyone to cover their noses and mouths when in public . Additionally, the Meal Hub program is being expanded to allow any child or adult to receive 3 meals a day for free. 

As these numbers continue to show, COVID-19 doesn't discriminate. So, please, unless you are needed on the front lines, STAY HOME. While home, if you can afford to support local businesses.﻿ If you are among those keeping society intact, THANK YOU .  

Feel free to share this information however you'd like, stay safe, and have as good of a weekend as possible. 

Best,
Mike Racioppo, District Manager
