As I have mentioned there are a bunch of FREE events this weekend across our district.If you haven't meticulously read every word, or clicked every link that I’ve sent you needn’t worry as I've listed them below:

Gowanus Grind, Saturday, June 8th Superfun Canoe Race, Saturday, June 8th Brooklyn Pride, Saturday, June 8th Tanker Time, Sunday June 9th

After the weekend, and the events events listed above we have our board meeting on Wednesday June 12th, 6:30 p.m at Pave Academy. We also just got confirmation that it will be live streamed here. IBZ Roundtable at the Bell House. If you are planning to attend the roundtable please fill out this survey in advance. Woodlands has some issues. Some coverage of the 76th precinct Community Council which is of course chaired by our very own Jerry Armer. Something to keep in mind as the temperature rises as we approach summer. Like most things, depending on how you look at it that could be a feature or a bug. If you think a certain street should be resurfaced, or pothole needs to be filled, click here Weekend Traffic Advisory Subway service changes for the weekend

Have a great weekend and, as always, feel free to share this information anyway/anywhere you’d like!
