By Mike Racioppo, District Manager Monday, December 9, 2019

Tomorrow, Tuesday, December 10th , at Brooklyn Borough Hall , there will be a “ Know Your Rights Forum ” Hosted by Borough President Adams ,and at the Green Building , there will be a panel discussion titled “ Net Zero for Neighborhoods .”

On Wednesday, December 11th , our final general board meeting of the year and decade will be held at the Cobble Hill Health Cente r . Later in the week, on Thursday, December 12th , the Carroll Gardens Association will host a census job workshop at their Columbia Street office .

Glancing ahead to next week, our Parks Committee will meet on Wednesday, December 18th , at  PS 58  to hear more about the Carroll Park redesign , on Thursday, December 19th ,   Assemblymember Carroll has an open house and on Friday, December 20th Assemblymember Simon has an open house . 

As I say every day, we’re lucky to have such an active community, so there is always a chance I’ve missed something. If there is an event or anything else community-centric happening that you think I/others should know about,  please reach out .

Have a great evening and, as always, feel free to share  this information  however you’d like.

PS- Alternate Side Parking is in effect tomorrow,  Tuesday, December 10th , and if you have the time, and because I can’t avoid including Gowanus related items in my emails, check out Gowanus by Design’s letter regarding the Gowanus Neighborhood Planning Study . 

Best,
Mike Racioppo, District Manager
