Marching, Meeting, More

By Mike Racioppo, District Manager Thursday, October 29, 2020

I’ll be brief, but I want to remind everyone that tonight, beginning at 6:30 PM, our Transportation & Public Safety Committee will meet. There are a few interesting items on the agenda, including an update from DOT on Open Streets, as well as a resolution from Resilient Red Hook. Also, and it’s happening rain or shine to mark the 8th anniversary of Sandy, a Climate Justice March will be held in place of the Barnacle Parade.

Last but not least, a big thank you to Superintendent Anita Skop for participating in last night’s Youth Human Services and Education Committee meeting. Also, Committee Co-Chair Jason Reichel’s zoom hosting skills have met with rave reviews, and if you want to confirm that, you can watch the meeting on YouTube.

Best,
Mike Racioppo, District Manager
