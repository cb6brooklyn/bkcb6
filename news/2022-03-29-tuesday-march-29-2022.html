Tuesday, March 29, 2022

By Mike Racioppo, District Manager Tuesday, March 29, 2022

I hope this email finds you well on this sunny and cold twenty-ninth day and the final Tuesday of March. I have a recap and a familiar suggestion.

Last night, our Permits & Licences (P&L)Committee met and reviewed three applications to the State Liquor Authority (SLA) for three new on-premises liquor licenses. All three (60 7th Ave, 226 7th Ave, and 354 Van Brunt Street) were approved unanimously. If that summary doesn’t suffice, you can watch a recording of the meeting on our YouTube page. Also, the next stop is a vote from our full board at the April 13th General Board Meeting. If you’d like to register for our April meeting, click here or go to bit.ly/Apr13BKCB6. 

I suggest you check out other items further down this email. If you’d like me to add something to future newsletters, let me know. No matter what you do, I hope you enjoy the rest of your day.

Best,
Mike Racioppo, District Manager
