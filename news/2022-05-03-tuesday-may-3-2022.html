District Manager’s Notes

Tuesday, May 3, 2022

By Mike Racioppo, District Manager Tuesday, May 3, 2022

I hope this email finds you well on this third day of May. Today, considering the gravity of last night’s now confirmed news leak, most of the focus is on the federal. I’ll be extra brief with one item.

For the first time since it was implemented in February, the city has raised its COVID alert level to medium(yellow) as cases surpassed a rate of 200 per 100,000 people in the five boroughs. However, no new COVID protocol will be implemented (or reimplemented) at this point, but that could change if rates rise again. The lack of change in protections but increased cases makes a solid argument for getting vaccinated and boosted asap. It makes even more sense to get vaccinated and boosted if you haven’t already. Make your appointment by clicking here or by going to vax4nyc.nyc.gov.

More information is listed beneath my contact information, including happenings like tomorrow’s Alternate Side Parking Suspension and 76th Precinct Community Council meeting. So I’d suggest you scroll down, but no matter what you think of the suggestion, stay safe and enjoy the rest of your day no matter what you think of the suggestion.

Best,
Mike Racioppo, District Manager
