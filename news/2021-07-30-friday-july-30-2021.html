By Mike Racioppo, District Manager Friday, July 30, 2021

As it becomes more obvious how important and necessary it is to get vaccinated against COVID-19 and more activities require it, it's also important to verify vaccination status. One way to do that is to hold onto your vaccination card; another is to download the New York State Excelsior Pass. Now the city has an app to verify your COVID-19 vaccination status and test results. You can download it for Apple and droid. If you’d prefer, as I do, or want to check out, the Excelsior Pass, click here.

Despite my long-winded nature, I do aspire for brevity. For that reason, I’ll stop here and suggest checking out the items below, many of which will be safer with vaccination, and I wish you all the best for the rest of July.

Best,
Mike Racioppo, District Manager
