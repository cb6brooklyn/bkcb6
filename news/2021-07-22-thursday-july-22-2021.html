By Mike Racioppo, District Manager Thursday, July 22, 2021

I’ll be very brief and remind everyone, if they haven’t already, please get vaccinated against COVID-19 asap. 70% of the city’s adults have received at least one shot of the vaccine, which is hardly poison, but still leaves millions unvaccinated and if you’re reading this as one of those millions, click here to change that. 

While not vaccine dependent, TONIGHT is our joint meeting with CB7 on last-mile warehouses, and it’ll be held over Zoom and begin at 6:00 PM. After that, there’s more going on, like tonight’s movies in Prospect Park, next Tuesday’s CB6 Landmarks/Land Use meeting, or even our September Board meeting on September 8th at the Van Alen Institute in Gowanus. 

On that note, I’ll wrap up and wish you all the best for the rest of Thursday.  

Best,
Mike Racioppo, District Manager
