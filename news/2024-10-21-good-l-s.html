By Mike Racioppo, District Manager Monday, October 21, 2024

First, congratulations to New York Liberty and their fans on winning the WNBA championship mere feet outside CB6 last night. If you’re interested in attending the official celebrations, go to liberty.wnba.com/2024champions. 

Second, regarding Land Use, tomorrow, Tuesday, October 22nd, the City Council will hear public testimony on the City of Yes. Like Borough President Reynoso, we already approved the City of Yes. If you'd like to testify, whether you approve or not, go to council.nyc.gov/land-use/. 

Third, for landmark lovers, this Wednesday, October 23rd, we have a CB6 committee meeting to review a landmarks application for Kane Street. It’ll start at 6:30 PM and be held in person at 250 Baltic Street. 

Last but certainly not least, we’re taking feedback on proposed streets for speed limit reduction under Sammy's Law to share with the DOT. To give this feedback, fill out our form at bit.ly/Sammylawbkcb6. 

Anyway, I hope your week is off to a good start. You’ll hear from me again before early voting starts on Saturday, but in the meantime, I recommend reading the rest of this email.

Best,
Mike Racioppo, District Manager
