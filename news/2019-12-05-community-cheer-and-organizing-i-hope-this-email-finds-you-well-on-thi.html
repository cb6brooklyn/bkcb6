Community Cheer & Organizing I hope this email finds you well on this, partly cloudy , Thursday. Today’s update

Today’s update will be, when compared to yesterday's , short and sweet/ focused on some fun events happening tonight and over the next few days. So let’s get to it.

By Mike Racioppo, District Manager Thursday, December 5, 2019

Today’s update will be, when compared to yesterday's , short and sweet/ focused on some fun events happening tonight and over the next few days. So let’s get to it.

Tonight, Thursday, December 5th , the evening will see businesses along Smith & Court Streets celebrating the holidays with extended house hours and free samples , Pig Beach hosts a tree lighting party in Gowanus, and the Park Slope Civic Council meets / at celebrates at the Old Stone House and in Red Hook, at PS 676 from 5 until 8 PM, there will be community organizing training.

The weekend will also feature more holiday cheer with celebrations such as festivities in Red Hook’s Coffey Park on Saturday , December 7th from 2 until 8 PM and at 5 PM sharp, in Carroll Gardens on the Court Street side of Carroll Park , they’ll be a holiday tree lighting and possible visit from a person famous for giving gifts in record time.

Again I’ll remind you that our final general board meeting of the decade will be next Wednesday, December 11th , 6:30 PM, at the Cobble Hill Health Center .

As I say every weekday, we’re lucky to have such an active community, and there is a chance I’ve missed something. If there is an event or anything else community-centric happening that you think I/others should know about,  please reach out .

Have a great evening and, as always, feel free to share  this information  however you’d like.

PS- Congrats to our very own Hasoni Pratts as she has been named National Director of Engagement for the Pete Buttigeg campaign.

Best,
Mike Racioppo, District Manager
