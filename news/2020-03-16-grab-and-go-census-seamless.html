Grab & Go,Census, Seamless

Until then, I'll continue to share information concerning the current emergency as it's received and remind everyone to complete their census questionnaire online!

By Mike Racioppo, District Manager Monday, March 16, 2020

Until then, I'll continue to share information concerning the current emergency as it's received and remind everyone to complete their census questionnaire online!

In the city, and across the tri-state area, casinos, gyms and movie theaters will be closed while restaurants and bars will be limited to pickup and delivery only. With that in mind, we have sent a letter to the NYC Department of Small Business Services asking for assistance to our local restaurants in the hope of getting fees waived to them by 3rd party delivery apps, such as Seamless, as we navigate this crisis. You can also call restaurants directly or use their websites to avoid such charges.

While schools are closed, be aware that lunch and breakfast are still available in all schools from 7:30 am until 1:30 pm. 

I've listed various other bits of information and items of interest below. As always, please feel free to share this information however you'd like and all the best to you and yours during these difficult times. 

Best,
Mike Racioppo, District Manager
