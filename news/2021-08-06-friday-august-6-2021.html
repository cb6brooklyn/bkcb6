District Manager’s Notes

Friday, August 6, 2021

By Mike Racioppo, District Manager Friday, August 6, 2021

Disregarding any risk of irritating you with repetition, I again remind everyone, especially those who want to participate in lots of stuff, to get vaccinated against Covid-19 ASAP.  Thankfully, the vaccination rate is ticking up, which will hopefully stop the infection rate from continuing to climb above 3%. Today it stands at 3.28%. I know nobody wants to go into a lockdown again. No one who lived through the last 18 months wants to channel Oliver Twist and ask, “may I have more time living through a pandemic? “. 

Anyway, there are lots of things that will be made safer if we’re all vaccinated that are listed below, like free bike helmets, my email signature, so I’ll let you get to it. Stay safe, and have a great weekend! 

Best,
Mike Racioppo, District Manager
