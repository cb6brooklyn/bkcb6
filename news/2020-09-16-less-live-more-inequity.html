Good  Evening 

I hope this email finds you well this Wednesday that reminds me that it’s still technically summer.

On our path to ”normalcy,” or more accurately, new normal, citywide public schools had their first day of remote learning for all students.  The DOE is still issuing new guidelines while reducing the amount of live (live via remote) instruction students are guaranteed. The change could lead to a situation where some students receive zero live instruction on four out of five school days each week and instead rely on assignments and pre-recorded lessons posted by their teachers. This will deepen inequities in a system that’s already inequitable. 

It will be sad if this becomes the legacy of someone who became mayor talking about the “tail of 2 cities”. Does he view inequality as a feature or a flaw? As an FYI, a school in a neighboring district is shut down due to COVID-19 infections before a single student has been in the building.

Moving on, I’d like to thank all the teachers that reached out after yesterday’s newsletter and to thank them again for their service to our city.

Lastly, I hope everyone stays safe, considers reading the items below, and enjoys the evening.

Best,
Mike Racioppo, District Manager
