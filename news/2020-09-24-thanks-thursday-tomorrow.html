By Mike Racioppo, District Manager Thursday, September 24, 2020

I’ll be brief today, but I want to once again thank the Warren Street Tenant Association for having me at their outdoor and distanced tenant meeting. Last night was an excellent candidate forum for the tenant association presidency in advance of their October 23 election, and I’m honored to be overseeing it. 

Speaking of October, our General Board meeting is October 14th, and it will be held on zoom beginning at 6:30 pm. I’m looking forward to it, and the general public may register for it here (board members will receive a unique link in the coming days, so no need to register if you are a board member).

While a tenant association and CB6 are significant, If you can’t wait another minute to do something civic, fill out the census, register to vote, help others do so, and then check out the items below my signature.

Best,
Mike Racioppo, District Manager
