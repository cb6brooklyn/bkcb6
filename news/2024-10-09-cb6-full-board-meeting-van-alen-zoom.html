CB6 Full Board Meeting @ Van Alen & Zoom

By Brooklyn Community Board 6 Wednesday, October 9, 2024

Tonight! Wednesday, October 9, 6:30 p.m.

AGENDA

I .ADOPTION OF SEPTEMBER 2024 MINUTES

II .CHAIRPERSON'S REPORT

III .DISTRICT MANAGER'S REPORT

IV .COMMITTEE REPORTS:

A . BUSINESS AFFAIRS & LICENSING

Motion to approve an application for a bank (Spring Bank) at 365 Van Brunt Street.

Motion to approve a liquor license at 517 Court Street.

Motion to approve a liquor license 2- 8 Prospect Place.

B. LANDMARKS, LAND USE & HOUSING

Motion to approve an application to the Landmarks Preservation Commission for 309 Carroll Street to allow for changes to the rear facade, yard deck, window replacement, and hardscape upgrades.

Motion to disapprove an application to the Board of Standards & Appeals application for 334 Douglass Street.

C. TRANSPORTATION, PARKS, PUBLIC SAFETY & PUBLIC INFRASTRUCTURE

Motion to support the initiative for Affordable Student Access to Citi Bike, advocating for the expansion of the Reduced Fare Bike Share program to include public high school and CUNY students aged 16 and above.

A motion to support the addition at least 3 public bathrooms to be sited in green spaces and buildings along the canal. This motion came at the request of the Gowanus Oversight Task Force.

V .NEW/OTHER BUSINESS

VI .Community Session

VII .ADJOURNMENT

All CB6 Meetings are open to the public.
