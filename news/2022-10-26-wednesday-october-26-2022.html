By Mike Racioppo, District Manager

The humid weather and increasingly early sunsets contrast in an annoying fashion. However, with a focus on the near future and the recent past, hopefully, the contrasts contained in the following paragraphs will be received better than the humidity. 

First, at 7 PM tonight, the New York City Department of Environmental Protection(DEP) is hosting a virtual meeting regarding the development of the Gowanus Canal "Owls Head" combined sewer overflow facility. To attend, register at bit.ly/depcso1026.  

Secondly, also tonight,  NYC  Schools Chancellor David Banks will hold a town hall meeting at M.S 88 from 6:30 until 8:30. The meeting will also be live-streamed; to register, go to learndoe.org/cec/.

Third, our Permits & Licenses(P&L)  committee met on Monday night. Rebecca Kobert chaired the meeting, had a quorum, and the committee approved all applications on the agenda. One of those approvals, Loft 251, was conditional. The next step for those applications will be a vote at our November 9th full board meeting, which will be a hybrid (virtual & in-person) meeting. The virtual part of the hybrid equation will be on Zoom @/bit.ly/CB6Nov922 and the in-person portion will take place at the Van Alen Institute. 

I’ll stop here and suggest scrolling further down this email. No matter what, enjoy the rest of your Wednesday.

Best,
Mike Racioppo, District Manager
