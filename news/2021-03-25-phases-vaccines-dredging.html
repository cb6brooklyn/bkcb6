I hope this email finds you well on a hazy but not lazy Thursday. 

As mentioned in previous newsletters, our Landmarks/ Land use Committee meets tonight at 630 PM. Just a few quick items before I wish you well and recommend reading on. 

﻿

There are dolphins in the water to our north, but in CB6’s most famous body of water, the Gowanus Canal, we remain in a holding pattern. While we wait, it may be of some interest to read about EPA& DEC’s letter intending to clarify their roles and ensuring that development on Public Place will be safe. Speaking of safe, this summer’s open streets program is once again accepting applications. 

On that note, as promised, I’ll recommend reading on below, getting a COVID-19 shot ASAP, and enjoying the rest of your Thursday. 

Best,
