Stay Home, Cover Your Face, Protect you, Protect Me

I hope this email finds you well on a rainy and windy Monday. I've always liked rain, but even more so now since it's helping people find a reason to stay inside.

By Mike Racioppo, District Manager Monday, April 13, 2020

I hope this email finds you well on a rainy and windy Monday. I've always liked rain, but even more so now since it's helping people find a reason to stay inside. I hope that if you do go outside, you're wearing a face-covering or mask much the way essential workers are now required to.

Encouraging info from our Governor and Mayor that the curve is flattening is a good reason for adhering to this requirement. However, these signs are no argument for relaxing restrictions, and they will likely remain in place, including schools being closed , until there is an indication that community spread is contained.

If you scroll below my email signature, you'll see more information on how inequality is impacting us during this crisis, along with a number of resources and items of interest.

Best,
Mike Racioppo, District Manager
