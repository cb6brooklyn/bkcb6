Tuesday, June 8, 2021

By Mike Racioppo, District Manager

As we strive to move past the Covid-19 pandemic, people are still dying from the virus, so we’re not past it yet. Reports indicate the B.1.617.2 (Delta) variant is a threat to reopening around the world. Being vaccinated is the best defense, so do so for free without even needing an appointment ASAP. 

Shifting to city civics, don’t forget early voting for the next city government (Mayor, Comptroller, City Council) begins this Saturday, June 12th, with the “election day” finale coming on Tuesday, June 22nd. If neither alternative suits your voting needs, you can request an absentee ballot until June 15th, and if you have questions about the Ranked Choice Voting (RCV) System check out tomorrow’s Southwest Brooklyn Tenant’s Union RCV workshop.

Aside from our meetings, there’s more happening that you can check out by scrolling down below. However, before you do that, I’d recommend checking out some coverage of the Monadnock ULURP that we approved back in December.

But no matter what you choose to do, stay safe, get vaccinated, and enjoy the rest of your day! 

Best,
Mike Racioppo, District Manager
