District Manager’s Notes

Landmarks/Land Use, Snow, Sanitation

By Mike Racioppo, District Manager Thursday, January 6, 2022

As we brace for some snow on this most troubling one-year anniversary, I hope this email finds you well.“ Some snow” could mean up to 5 inches of snow. Due to that forecast, we’ll be under a winter weather advisory starting at midnight. Alternate Side Parking is suspended, DSNY has issued a snow alert, “Code Blue” is in effect, but schools will remain open as of now. If anything changes with the services or policies mentioned, I’ll let you know ASAP.

With that, I’ll briefly touch on two points before recommending you check out more information below my email signature.

First, tonight at 6:30 pm over Zoom, our Landmarks/Land Use Committee will meet to review BSA applications. While on the topic of CB6 meetings, don’t forget that next Wednesday is our first full board meeting of 2022.

Secondly, click here to find free Covid testing locations and here to get vaccinated or boosted against the virus. On that note, I’ll stop and suggest you read further down this email. If you don’t, I’ll still hope for you to stay safe and enjoy the rest of the day.

Best,
Mike Racioppo, District Manager
