District Manager’s Notes

November 12 Daily Email 2

As always, I hope this email finds you well and that everyone was able to appreciate Veterans Day . I’d also hope that this wintry weather doesn’t affect any of your plans for the week.

By Mike Racioppo, District Manager Tuesday, November 12, 2019

As always, I hope this email finds you well and that everyone was able to appreciate Veterans Day . I’d also hope that this wintry weather doesn’t affect any of your plans for the week.

Tonight, Tues, Nov 12th, those plans could include, from 6 until 8 PM, the MTA Bus Redesign open house at the Park Slope Library . Plans could also include the 76th precinct’s “Build the Block” meeting , 7 PM at D’Amico’s , or an Integrated

Resilience roundtable at Pioneer Works from 7 until 9 PM.

Tomorrow, Wed, Nov 13th, plans could/should (I’m biased) include our November General Board Meeting , starting at 6:30 PM at M.S 51 .

While on Thurs, Nov 14th, the 78th Precinct hosts a Build the Block meeting , The Old Stone House will be the place for an ecological concert , and The Red Hook Community Justice Center has a grant information session for artists and organizers.

We are lucky to have such an active community, so there’s always a chance I miss something. If there is any community-centric event or information you’d like me to share,  please reach out .

That's all I've got for today, have a good evening, and feel free to share  this information  any way you’d like.

P.S, Alternate Side Parking is in effect tomorrow Wed, Nov 13th, and if you have the time you may want to read up on the cost of GPS , and how the market prevents climate action .

Best,
Mike Racioppo, District Manager
