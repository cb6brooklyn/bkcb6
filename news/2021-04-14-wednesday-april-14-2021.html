By Mike Racioppo, District Manager Wednesday, April 14, 2021

I hope this email finds you well in advance of tonight’s full board meeting, which kicks off via Zoom at 6:30 PM. If you're a board member, you will have received a panelist invite. If you're a board member and haven't received it, please check your spam, and if it's not there, email me, and I'll resolve it. The voting agenda will involve a landmark application, a BSA application, a request from a canoe club, and a liquor license. 

If, during the meeting, you’d like to or need to use the Spanish language translation services provided during the meeting, please click here and follow the directions. If you’re the representative of an elected official, please let me know if you’re attending the meeting on their behalf.

Looking beyond this evening, but still within our sphere of influence, don’t forget that our Transportation & Public Safety Committee meets tomorrow, Thurs, April 15th, EWCDH meets this coming Monday, April 19th Landmarks/Land Use next Thursday, April 22nd. You can register for those meetings here, and they’ll begin at 630 PM. 

I’m sure other topics will be discussed at the meeting, just like you’ll find other topics below my signature. I recommend checking out both, but most importantly, stay safe, get vaccinated, and enjoy the rest of the day!

Best,
Mike Racioppo, District Manager
