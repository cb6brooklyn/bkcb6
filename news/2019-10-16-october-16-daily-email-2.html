By Mike Racioppo, District Manager Wednesday, October 16, 2019

As we experience some rain , and I keep thinking its Tuesday, I hope you read this email from a waterproof location, and for those who are continuing to celebrate the season of Sukkot, my wish remains that you have a bountiful harvest and ‘Chag Sameach”.

While none of our committees will be meeting this week that doesn’t mean there aren’t a lot of other things going on. For example, tomorrow night, Thursday October 17th, at the Miccio Center beginning at 6 PM, the second of 2 school rezoning meetings will be held, and, at the same time, the Southwest Brooklyn Tenants Union is meeting in the Columbia Waterfront District . While we’re discussing our water adjacent neighborhoods, I suggest checking out Portside’s open house this coming Saturday. Also this Saturday, and Sunday, Gowanus Open Studios will be happening, and it includes the fine art of our very own David Briggs . 

Looking ahead, one week from today, October 23 rd , our Youth Human Services and Education Committee will meet at the Red Hook Library featuring a presentation from the NYC Census 2020 Complete Count. The following night, Thursday, October 24 th , with an agenda still in formation, our Landmarks/Land Use Committee will meet, 6:00 PM, at 250 Baltic Street. Also, four weeks from tonight, our next full board meeting will be held at M.S 51 on November 13th.

Before I reveal whether alternate side parking regulations are in effect, I’d recommend checking out what will be the most crucial item in this year’s election and why more can be done to fight poverty .

So the big reveal is that, just like today, alternate side parking will be in effect tomorrow, Thursday, October 17 th , while the trains and buses , as they do every day, will have some planned service changes.

That’s all I’ve got for today, but, as always, I hope you have a good evening and feel free to share this information any way you’d like. 

Best,
Mike Racioppo, District Manager
