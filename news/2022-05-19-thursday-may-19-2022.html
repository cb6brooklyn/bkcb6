Thursday, May 19, 2022

I hope this email finds you well on this formerly rainy Thursday, the 19th day of May. I have two items to highlight.

By Mike Racioppo, District Manager

I hope this email finds you well on this formerly rainy Thursday, the 19th day of May.   I have two items to highlight. 

First, if you’re interested in the proposed Interborough Express subway line, you’ll want to attend tonight’s MTA virtual town hall. You can register for the town hall and find out more by going to: new.mta.info/project/interborough-express. If the proposal comes to fruition, it will link neighborhoods underserved by the subway spanning Brooklyn and Queens. Regardless of participation in tonight’s town hall, you can share your thoughts on the proposal by going to https://bit.ly/interboromtafeedback.

Secondly, I’ll remind you that the Health Department’s Covid-19 alert system is now at high risk (Orange). So, if you haven’t already, get vaccinated and boosted by going to vax4nyc.nyc.gov.

If you read past my contact information you’ll find a lot is happening across our community this week and beyond. However, no matter what you do, stay safe, and enjoy the rest of your Thursday.

Best,
Mike Racioppo, District Manager
