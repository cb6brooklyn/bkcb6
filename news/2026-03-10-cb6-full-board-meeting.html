IV.COMMITTEE REPORTS:

A. LANDMARKS, LAND USE & HOUSING

152 Atlantic Ave - (Cobble Hill Historic District ) : motion to approve an application for a Certificate of Appropriateness for a rooftop addition, with the condition that the railing facing Atlantic Ave is set back an additional 2 feet.

175 3rd Street - Motion to conditionally approve an application to transfer development rights. The conditions being that the Powerhouse Arts promenade be opened to the public as a direct result of the acquisition, and that 30% of the units are affordable.

B. Business Affairs & Permits

Motion to approve a liquor license for 47 5th Avenue.

C. Transportation

Motion to approve , thanks to Sammy's Law and in line with CB6'S longstanding support for such measures, that NYC DOT institute a district-wide 20 MPH slow zone on all applicable streets with adjusted signal timing and camera enforcement, and to call for adoption of a 20 MPH citywide speed limit on all applicable streets.
