District Manager’s Notes

November 11 Daily Email 3

I hope this email finds you well as our office is closed today, Monday, November 11th for Veterans Day .

By Mike Racioppo, District Manager Monday, November 11, 2019

I hope this email finds you well as our office is closed today, Monday, November 11th for Veterans Day .

Events honoring our Military Veterans will take place across the city, including right here in our district at the Carroll Park War Memorial at 11:00 AM . On a more mundane note, please remember that, NYC Public Schools are closed and that Alternate Side Parking & DSNY pickup is suspended .

While tomorrow, Tuesday, November 12th, The Brooklyn Bus Redesign Open House will take place at the Park Slope Library from 6 until 8 pm ( B71 Rally at 5 pm ) and, beginning at 7 pm, the  76th Precinct hosts a “Build the Block”  meeting at Damico’s . Alternate Side Parking Regulations will be in effect, and DSNY pick up will return, and NYC public schools are back in session.

Lastly, don’t forget that our November General Board Meeting is on Wednesday, November 13, at M.S 51 and begins at 6:30 PM.

We are lucky to have such an active community, so there’s always a chance I miss something. If there is any community-centric event or information you’d like me to share,  please reach out .

﻿

For any Veterans reading this, thank you for your service.

As always, enjoy the rest of your day, and feel free to share  this information  any way you’d like.

Best,
Mike Racioppo, District Manager
