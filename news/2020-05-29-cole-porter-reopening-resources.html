By Mike Racioppo, District Manager Friday, May 29, 2020

I hope this email finds you as well as can be as we head into the final days of May and plan to start " reopening " on June 8 th . No matter what you're paying attention to, unless you're a billionaire , I'd find it hard to see a landscape that is any better than sobering to horrifying .

We have to get through it, and I am confident we will. I could do with fewer advertisements and articles suggesting people leave the city. I've don't know if I've ever been to Hackensack but, just like Bobby Short sang , if I ended up there, I'd take the next train back.

As is the case Monday through Friday, some items below my email signature could be put to good use. Whether you check them out or not, I hope you have a good weekend, stay safe, and keep covering your face.

Best,
Mike Racioppo, District Manager
