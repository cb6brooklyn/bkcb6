Friday, September 23, 2022

First, for those celebrating Rosh Hashanah, L'shanah Tovah! Happy 5783, and may you have a happy, healthy, and sweet new year.

By Mike Racioppo, District Manager

First, for those celebrating Rosh Hashanah, L'shanah Tovah! Happy 5783, and may you have a happy, healthy, and sweet new year. Even if you're not celebrating, alternate side parking will be suspended(ASP), and public schools(PS)will be closed Monday, September 26, and Tuesday, September 27.

However, on Wednesday, September 28, ASP will be enforced, and public schools will be open. Speaking of public schools being open on Wednesday, due to the Governor allowing an EO to lapse, we're back to meeting in person that night at PS. 32. The meeting will start at 6:30 PM with our full board reviewing two landmark items. If that doesn't interest you immediately, citywide and statewide elected officials may be joining us.

Before anything mentioned above occurs, a lot is going on across our community. I've listed lots of them, and you should check them out or at least click a few. A click could lead to a free movie, more robust protections against Covid-19, or something else entirely.

Whether you click or not, have a great weekend.

Best,
Mike Racioppo, District Manager
