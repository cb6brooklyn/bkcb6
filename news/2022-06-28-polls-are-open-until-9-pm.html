District Manager’s Notes

Polls are Open until 9 PM

Today is the Primary Election for Governor, Lieutenant Governor, State Assembly, Judges, & Party Positions. Polls are open until 9 p.m.

By Mike Racioppo, District Manager Tuesday, June 28, 2022

Today is the Primary Election for Governor, Lieutenant Governor, State Assembly, Judges, & Party Positions. Polls are open until 9 p.m.  To find your poll site, go to findmypollsite.vote.NYC or call 1(866)Vote-NYC.

Looking a bit past the election, tomorrow, Wednesday, June 29th, at 11 am, there will be a street co-naming ceremony for Salvatore “Buddy” Scotto at the corner of Court Street and First Place. Then, on Thursday, June 30th, from 6 until 8 pm, the Gowanus CAG will be holding an in-person town hall at PS 32 on the clean-up of the canal with EPA.

More information is below my email signature, so I suggest scrolling past here. However, enjoy the rest of your day whether you scroll or not.

Best,
Mike Racioppo, District Manager
