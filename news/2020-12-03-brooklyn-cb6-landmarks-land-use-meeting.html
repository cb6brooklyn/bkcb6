Brooklyn CB6 Landmarks/ Land Use Meeting

By Brooklyn Community Board 6 Thursday, December 3, 2020

Tonight at 6:00 p.m.

Meeting Location:

Register for meeting here. Committee members will receive email invites as panelists.

Agenda

473 9th Street ,  Park Slope Historic District : Application to Legalize a reduction in size for the 2nd and 3rd floor windows in a previously (LPC staff-level) approved alteration to the rear facade.

282 Garfield Place ,  Park Slope Historic District : Application to Amend a  Certificate of No Effect  (aka an LPC staff-level approval) to allow new, larger masonry openings at the 2nd and 3rd floors and the penthouse, and to relocate two stained-glass windows.

300 Huntington Street , Zoning Map Amendment ULURP, Block 477, Lot 8; Applicant proposes to rezone the property from   M2-1 to M2-3  to remove existing parking requirement pursuant to its plans to redevelop the site to construct a new headquarters with a construction yard, along with other offices and street level retail space.

Review of the  Department of City Planning's  proposed  Waterfront Text Amendment for Zoning for Coastal Flood Resiliency .

Chair

David Briggs, Landmarks & Land Use Co-Chair

Mark Shames, Land Use & Landmarks Co-Chair
