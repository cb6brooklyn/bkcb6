Permits, Pumpkins, Voting

By Mike Racioppo, District Manager Tuesday, October 27, 2020

Good afternoon,

I hope this email finds you well on this 27th day of October. Tonight’s our Permits & Licenses Committee meeting , Chaired by Rachel Freeman. The agenda will focus on reviewing one application for an on-premise liquor license. The applicant is  Terrace Restaurant and Bakery Corp. at 280 5th Avenue between Garfield Place & 1st ST), Bklyn, NY 12215. Also, starting at 6:30 PM tonight is the monthly Gowanus CAG meeting.

The second CB6 meeting of the week is tomorrow, Wednesday, October 28th , with our Youth Human Services and Education Committee welcoming School District 15 Superintendent and a great friend to Community Board 6, Anita Skop. The purpose will to discuss the current school year and answer questions folks may have. On Thursday, October 29th, we will cycle through our Transportation & Public Safety Committee meeting with DOT presentations and be introduced to the new Commanding Officer at the 76th Precinct.

Some more happenings and items of interest are listed below, including mask guidelines for the only day anyone tended to wear a mask before COVID, Halloween. Anyway, I hope you check out the rest of the newsletter, and I hope you stay safe and enjoy the rest of your Tuesday.
