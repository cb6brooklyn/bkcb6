Reminder- CB6 Landmarks/Land Use Committee Meeting

By Brooklyn Community Board 6 Tuesday, November 28, 2023

Thursday, November 30, 6:30 p.m @ Van Alen & Virtual

Who:

CB6 Landmarks/Land Use

What:

Committee Meeting

When:

Thursday, November 30th, 6:30 p.m.

Where:

Van Alen Institue @ 303 Bond Street- email zoom@bkcb6.org to attend virtually.

Why:

To review the following four items.

Landmarks :

467 Hicks Street (Cobble Hill Historic District )- Certificate of Appropriateness application to allow for a rear yard extension .

701 8th Avenue (Park Slope Historic District )- Certificate of Appropriateness application to allow for a  rooftop playground at Saint Saviour Catholic Academy .

Land Use :

341 10th Street - ULURP application to rezone to allow reduction of parking requirements, building two buildings for commercial, community, and residential uses. See more information @ bit.ly/341Tenthzap

41 Richards Street - ULURP to rezone for a 7-story mixed-use development allowing for light manufacturing, commercial office space, and ground-floor retail uses. See more information @ on.nyc.gov/3T21hJg

Committee Co-chairs:

David Briggs, Landmarks

Mark Shames, Land Use
