I'll highlight three items and make one suggestion. 

First, to all those who celebrate, happy Diwali! 

Second, our Permits & Licences committee meets tonight at 250 Baltic Street at 6:30 PM. The agenda consists of 6 applications for new on-premises liquor licenses. 

Third, voting in this year's general election starts this Saturday, October 29th. The election will determine who will be Governor, LT Governor, and represent us in the US. Senate & House, our state legislature, and include various ballot proposals. For more information on the candidates, go to nycvotes.org and/or watch the gubernatorial debate tomorrow night on NY1. You can do so even if you don't subscribe to NY1, as the debate will be available to be watched for free at NY1.com. 

In addition to the aforementioned CB6 committee meeting, Councilmember Restler is hosting a neighborhood assembly at Wyckoff Gardens tonight at 6 PM. There are DEP meetings and more later in the week. The "more" can be found by scrolling down this email.

Whether you scroll further or not, enjoy the rest of your gray day. 

Best,
