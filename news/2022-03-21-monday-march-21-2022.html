District Manager’s Notes

Monday, March 21, 2022

I hope this email finds you well as spring starts after a weekend of storms and the triumphant return of a great Saint Patrick’s Day parade.

By Mike Racioppo, District Manager Monday, March 21, 2022

I hope this email finds you well as spring starts after a weekend of storms and the triumphant return of a great Saint Patrick’s Day parade.

Looking to the week ahead, we’ve got our Landmarks/Land Use(LMLU) Committee on Thursday night over Zoom. With over 20 people already registered, the agenda consists of 2 landmarks items and the review of a BSA application for a logistics facility in Red Hook. You can register for the meeting by clicking here or going to the link: bit.ly/mar.24lmlucb6. 

The next CB6 meeting after LMLU will be our Permits & Licenses meeting one week from tonight on Monday, March 28th, over Zoom. It will begin at 6:30 PM, and the committee will review three liquor license applications.

Many things will happen between now and then, and I’ve listed much of it below my contact information. l. I suggest checking it out, and if you’d like me to include something, feel free to send it my way, but no matter what, you do enjoy the rest of today and spring.

Best,
Mike Racioppo, District Manager
