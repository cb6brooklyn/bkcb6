Eladia’s Kids

Child care center · Park Slope, Brooklyn

Eladia’s Kids is a child care center at 266 Flatbush Avenue in Park Slope.

In its own words: “For over 35 years Eladia’s Kids has provided excellent infant care and early childhood education to children ages 3 months to 5 years old.”
