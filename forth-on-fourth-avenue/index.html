Forth on Fourth Avenue

Park Slope Civic Council committee · Park Slope, Brooklyn

Forth on Fourth Avenue (FOFA) grew out of the Park Slope Civic Council’s 2010 public forum on the future of Fourth Avenue and became a full Civic Council committee in February 2012.

FOFA’s mission is to work with the diverse Fourth Avenue community in and next to Park Slope to promote well being, safety and vitality along Fourth Avenue, between Flatbush Avenue and the Prospect Expressway.

What Forth on Fourth Avenue does

FOFA is a hands on group focused on pedestrian, cyclist and motorist safety, transportation improvements, greening events and bringing color and art to 4th Avenue.
