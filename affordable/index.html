Affordable & income-restricted housing

Market rent is only part of the picture. Thousands of NYC apartments are income-restricted, with rents set well below market for households that qualify. See where your household fits, what those homes cost, and how to apply. You can also step into someone else's situation to see how eligibility changes.

Enter a household size and income, or step into one of the example situations. NYC affordable housing eligibility is based on income as a percent of Area Median Income (AMI).

Your options and how to enter
Affordable homes are awarded through lotteries and waitlists, not the open market. These are the main paths.
