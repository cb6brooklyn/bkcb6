What is proposed

On the deck over the railyard between 6th Avenue and Carlton Avenue, at its western end. It cannot open before B6, and it cannot change hands until work on the deck starts. The proposal puts community space in B5.

670 rentals, 184 of them income-restricted at an average of 80% of area median income, and no condominiums, from the example housing program in the July 2026 memorandum. The Block 1120 deck is due by December 31, 2032, and its buildings by December 31, 2037.
