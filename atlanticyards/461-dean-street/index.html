The building

461 Dean Street is parcel B2 of Atlantic Yards, on the arena block, bounded by Flatbush Avenue, Atlantic Avenue, 6th Avenue and Dean Street, in BKCB6: a 32-floor building of 363 apartments, 181 of them affordable, completed in 2016.

The first apartment building of the project. Ground was broken on December 18, 2012, and it opened in November 2016 as a modular tower; the housing lottery for its 181 affordable apartments opened in May 2016, announced at the CB6 full board meeting of May 11, 2016.
