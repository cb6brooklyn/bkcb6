What is proposed

Across Flatbush Avenue at 4th Avenue, the former P.C. Richard and Modell’s site, on solid ground, so it can be built without a deck over the tracks. The proposal allows a hotel of up to about 92,000 square feet and about 73,000 square feet of retail here. The income-restricted apartments at Site 5 must open before its condominiums or a hotel, and work on the Block 1120 deck must start before the condominiums or hotel open.

1,030 rentals, 258 of them income-restricted at an average of 60% of area median income, and 400 condominiums, from the example housing program in the July 2026 memorandum. Approved at 250 feet and 439,050 square feet; proposed at 799 and 570 feet. Site 5 and the Block 1120 buildings are due by December 31, 2037.
