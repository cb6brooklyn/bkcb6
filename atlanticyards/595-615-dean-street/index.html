The building

595 and 615 Dean Street is parcel B12 and B13 of Atlantic Yards, on the block between Pacific Street and Dean Street from Carlton Avenue to Vanderbilt Avenue, in BKCB8: a 23-floor building of 798 apartments, 240 of them affordable, completed in 2024.

Two towers on one tax lot. City records count the two together, so they share one profile.
