What is proposed

The plaza in front of Barclays Center at Flatbush Avenue and Atlantic Avenue, over the subway entrance. The approved plan has the project’s tallest tower here. The July 2026 housing program has no building on B1, and the new development team took over its development rights in October 2025.

Approved at 620 feet and 1,106,009 square feet, for commercial, office and hotel use.
