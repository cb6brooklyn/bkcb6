What is proposed

Partly on land below Atlantic Avenue, so it can go first along with Site 5, before the deck. It would hold the intergenerational community center, about 14,000 square feet. It must open before B5 and B7.

979 rentals, 245 of them income-restricted at an average of 60% of area median income, and no condominiums, from the example housing program in the July 2026 memorandum. Approved at 219 feet; proposed at 650 feet. Block 1120 buildings are due by December 31, 2037.
