What is proposed

The eastern end of the railyard, at Vanderbilt Avenue. The proposal puts community space in B10. No Block 1121 building can be leased for construction until Site 5 and every Block 1120 building has started.

819 rentals, 225 of them income-restricted at an average of 80% of area median income, and no condominiums, from the example housing program in the July 2026 memorandum. Block 1121 is due by December 31, 2040 with a funding agreement for its deck, and by December 31, 2042 without one.
