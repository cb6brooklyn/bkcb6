The building

662 Pacific Street is parcel B15 of Atlantic Yards, on the block between Pacific Street and Dean Street from 6th Avenue to Carlton Avenue, in BKCB8: a 26-floor building of 312 apartments, 94 of them affordable, completed in 2023.
