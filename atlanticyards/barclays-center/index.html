The arena

Barclays Center opened on September 21, 2012 with 18,000 seats, the home of the Brooklyn Nets, at Flatbush Avenue and Atlantic Avenue over the Atlantic Av-Barclays Ctr subway station and across Atlantic Avenue from the Long Island Rail Road’s Atlantic Terminal. Ground was broken on March 11, 2010. Joseph Tsai completed his purchase of the Nets and the arena in September 2019.

The arena’s tax lot is assigned to BKCB2, but 37.5% of it lies inside the BKCB6 line, the most of any part of the project. The plaza at its western tip is B1, approved for a tower that the July 2026 proposal does not include.
