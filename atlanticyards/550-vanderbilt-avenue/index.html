The building

550 Vanderbilt Avenue is parcel B11 of Atlantic Yards, on the block between Pacific Street and Dean Street from Carlton Avenue to Vanderbilt Avenue, in BKCB8: a 18-floor building of 278 condominiums, none of them income-restricted, completed in 2015.
