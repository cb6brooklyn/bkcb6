Smith Street Workshop, Carroll Street

French immersion preschool · Gowanus, Brooklyn

Smith Street Workshop holds the city child care licence at 388 Carroll Street in Gowanus, where its affiliated French immersion preschool, Smith Street Maternelle, runs classes.

In its own words: “The Maternelle began in 2016 as a small playgroup organized in a tiny space with the owner’s then 2-year-old daughter and her friends. Since then, the program has grown organically into a larger French immersion preschool and kindergarten program grounded in the fundamentals of progressive education.”
