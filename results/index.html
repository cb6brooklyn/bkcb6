Unofficial results from the NYC Board of Elections election night reporting. Election night counts do not include all absentee, affidavit and early mail ballots, and change until the Board certifies the results. Community board figures add up every election district in that community district; an election district that crosses a community district line is split by the share of its area on each side (an election district 80% in CB6 counts 80% of its votes toward CB6).
