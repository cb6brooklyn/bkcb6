Gowanus Canal Conservancy

🤝 Community based organization · Brooklyn Community Board 6

Mission

Gowanus Canal Conservancy advocates and cares for ecologically sustainable parks and public spaces in the Gowanus lowlands while empowering a community of stewards.

What it does

The Conservancy is the environmental steward of the Gowanus Canal and the watershed that drains into it. It works with the EPA, the city Department of Environmental Protection, Riverkeeper and a string of universities on water quality, and it runs the green infrastructure and street tree work that decides whether the rain that falls on Gowanus ends up in the canal.

It runs the Lowlands Nursery, which grows native urban-adapted plants and sells them to residents, institutions and landscape designers, with the proceeds going back into education and stewardship. It runs the Gowanus Green Team, paid high school apprentices doing conservation work. And it runs the Gowanus Tree Network, training neighbors as Citizen Pruners to look after the street trees on their own blocks.

In a district where a Superfund canal, a rezoning and a combined sewer system all meet, this is the organization holding the environmental thread.

The building

The Conservancy works out of the Old American Can Factory, a three storey 1890 complex at Third Street and Third Avenue that now houses artists, makers and non-profits. The city files the lot under 361 3 Avenue, so a search for 248 Third Street lands on that record. It is zoned M1-4/R7X inside the Gowanus special district, which is the mixed use zoning the 2021 rezoning brought in.
