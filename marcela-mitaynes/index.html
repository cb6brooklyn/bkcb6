Marcela Mitaynes

Assembly Member · State Assembly District 51

Assembly Member Marcela Mitaynes represents State Assembly District 51. It sits in Brooklyn.

The district overlaps community boards Brooklyn 6, Brooklyn 7, Brooklyn 10 and Brooklyn 12.

It is policed by the 66th, 72nd and 76th Precincts.

It overlaps council districts 38 and 39 and Senate districts 17 and 26.

Members of the State Assembly serve two year terms.

Committees and legislation

Marcela Mitaynes chairs the Subcommittee on Regulated Mortgage Lenders.

Marcela Mitaynes also sits on the Committee on Banks, Committee on Children and Families, Committee on Education, Committee on Judiciary, Committee on Transportation, Asian Pacific American Task Force, Black, Puerto Rican, Hispanic & Asian Legislative Caucus, Legislative Women's Caucus, Task Force on New Americans, Puerto Rican/Hispanic Task Force, Task Force on Women's Issues.

Recent bills sponsored include A03575, establishes an indirect source review for certain warehouse operations; A03578, establishes a SNAP and cash assistance fraud victims compensation fund; A03715, repeals section 3 of article 16 relating to the taxation of moneys, credits, securities and other intangible personal property in the state that is not employed in carrying on any business therein; A03868, changes the designation of the public holiday of Columbus day to Indigenous People's Day.
