Chai Tots Preschool, Park Slope

Preschool · Park Slope, Brooklyn

Chai Tots Preschool, Park Slope is a preschool at 70 Prospect Park West in Park Slope.

In its own words: “Our hope at Chai Tots is to encourage individuality, creativity, exploration, and a love for learning, as experienced through the five senses.”
