NYC DOT designated truck routes, by community district, with the changes effective October 4, 2026

← Transportation

Every designated truck route in New York City: through routes, local routes and limited local routes, all 32,939 segments across the five boroughs. Turn districts on and off below, search an address, or open a whole borough. Every community district is labeled and colored on the map. The City Council, State Senate, State Assembly and Congressional districts that overlap the selected districts are listed as toggles; tap one to draw it. Tap any segment for the street, route type, posted restrictions and the section of the NYC Traffic Rules that designates it.

Network changes take effect October 4, 2026. NYC DOT has finalized the truck route network redesign. The additions, removals and one designation change are drawn on the map (green, red and orange) and listed below. A new printed truck map is due in 2027. DOT map and list of changes (PDF)
