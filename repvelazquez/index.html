Representative · New York’s 7th Congressional District

In brief

Representative Nydia Velázquez represents New York’s 7th Congressional District in the United States House of Representatives. It sits in Queens and Brooklyn.

The district overlaps community boards Brooklyn 1, Queens 5, Queens 2, Brooklyn 4, Brooklyn 5, Queens 1, Brooklyn 2, Queens 9, Brooklyn 3 and Queens 10.

It is policed by the 75th, 79th, 83rd, 84th, 88th, 90th, 94th, 102nd, 104th, 106th, 108th and 114th Precincts.

It overlaps council districts 22, 26, 30, 32, 33, 34, 35, 36 and 37, Assembly districts 23, 28, 30, 34, 36, 37, 38, 50, 52, 53, 54, 56 and 57 and Senate districts 12, 15, 18, 19, 25, 26 and 59.

Members of the House serve two year terms, with no term limit.
