Red Hook Initiative

🤝 Community based organization · Brooklyn Community Board 6

Mission

Creating change from within. RHI nurtures young people and the Red Hook community to be inspired, resilient and healthy, and to envision themselves as agents of change.

What it does

RHI works with more than 6,500 Red Hook residents a year, and the phrase it leads with, creating change from within, is the actual method rather than a slogan. Its youth development, community building and public health work is staffed and led substantially by people from the neighborhood it serves.

It runs school age child care, youth programs, workforce development and community organizing out of 767 Hicks Street, and it became a hub for the whole peninsula after Sandy, when it was one of the places that functioned while institutions above it did not.

Red Hook is a place the city has repeatedly promised things and repeatedly not delivered. RHI is one of the constants.
