Sources and method

How this was put together

Today’s lots come from PLUTO. Each lot is placed on the City Planning 1961 zoning district map and on today’s zoning map, and checked against the outline of every adopted zoning map amendment. Where a lot was rezoned more than once, the zoning between changes comes from the Department of City Planning’s zoning change records for 2001 on, and from the project description for earlier amendments. Dates are the amendment’s effective date, or the date the City marked the project complete when no effective date is recorded.

  1. Department of City Planning, 1961 zoning districts ("zoning districts on December 15, 1961")
  2. Department of City Planning, 1961 commercial overlay districts
  3. Department of City Planning, zoning districts today (NYZD)
  4. Department of City Planning, commercial overlay districts today (NYCO)
  5. Department of City Planning, Zoning Map Amendments (NYZMA). The City describes it as covering changes since January 1, 2002; it also holds outlines for some earlier amendments, which are used here, and its earlier coverage may not be complete.
  6. Department of City Planning, zoning change polygons (old and new district for each change, 2001 on)
  7. Department of City Planning, PLUTO tax lots, version 26v2 (lot area, homes, year built)
  8. Department of City Planning, Zoning Application Portal project data (projects since the late 1970s)
  9. Department of City Planning, Zoning Application Portal lots (BBL) for each project
  10. Department of City Planning, Zoning Application Portal project records (actions for each application)
  11. Department of City Planning, Housing Database (jobs filed or completed since January 1, 2010)
  12. Department of City Planning, Zoning Handbook, Chapter 1: p. 8 (Zoning Resolution took effect December 15, 1961), p. 11 (letter suffixes and contextual districts; commercial overlays)
  13. Zoning Resolution, Article XII, Chapter 3, Special Mixed Use District, §123-10 ("an M1 District is paired with a Residence District")
  14. 2025 New York City Charter Revision Commission, Final Report, p. 38 (ULURP created by the 1975 Charter Review Commission)
  15. NYC Parks, Parks Properties (park names and outlines, April 21, 2026 file)
  16. Landmarks Preservation Commission, Historic Districts (boundaries, LP numbers and designation dates)
