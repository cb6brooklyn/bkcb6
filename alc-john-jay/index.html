Alternate Learning Center, John Jay Campus

NYC Public Schools Alternate Learning Center, District 79 · Park Slope, Brooklyn

The Alternate Learning Center at the John Jay Campus, 237 Seventh Avenue in Park Slope, is one of the NYC Public Schools Alternate Learning Centers run by District 79, where students serving a superintendent’s suspension keep up their schoolwork.
