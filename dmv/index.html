NYS Department of Motor Vehicles

State agency · driver licenses, IDs and vehicle registrations

The New York State Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV) issues secure identity documents, delivers motor vehicle and driver related services, and administers the state’s motor vehicle laws to promote safety and protect consumers.

The office nearest Brooklyn Community Board 6 is Brooklyn Atlantic Center, at 625 Atlantic Avenue, 2nd Floor. The DMV lists its hours as Monday to Friday, 7:30 AM to 5:00 PM. Brooklyn’s other state office is Coney Island, at 2875 West 8th Street.

What the DMV does

The DMV handles driver licenses and learner permits, REAL ID and Enhanced licenses, non-driver IDs, vehicle registrations, plates and titles.

Traffic tickets issued in New York City go to the DMV’s Traffic Violations Bureau. The Brooklyn North bureau is in the same building, at 625 Atlantic Avenue, 2nd Floor; the Brooklyn South bureau is at 2875 West 8th Street.

The DMV call center is 800-698-2931, Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 4 PM.
