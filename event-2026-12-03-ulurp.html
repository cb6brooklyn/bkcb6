What is being proposed

Ailanthus Hoyt LLC is asking the City to rezone 424 Hoyt Street (Block 468, Lot 25), a 45,900 square foot lot between 4th Street and 5th Street that is currently used for parking. According to the application filed with the Department of City Planning, the rezoning would allow a 17-story, 175-foot mixed-use building of about 330,000 square feet: about 400 apartments, including 100 income-restricted homes, about 46,000 square feet for a school, and about 14,000 square feet of retail.

There are two actions. The zoning map amendment (270102ZMK) changes the site from M1-4 to M1-4/R8X within the Special Gowanus Mixed Use District. The zoning text amendment (N270103ZRK) maps Mandatory Inclusionary Housing on the site, which makes income-restricted housing a requirement of any residential development there.

The ULURP timeline for this application

ULURP, the Uniform Land Use Review Procedure, is the City's public review for rezonings. Every step has a fixed deadline, and the clock starts when the Department of City Planning certifies the application as complete.

Sept 10, 2026
Application filed with the Department of City Planning.
Oct 30, 2026
Pre-certification notice sent to CB6 and the Borough President. The application can be certified no sooner than November 30, 2026.
Dec 3, 2026
CB6 Landmarks, Land Use & Housing Committee. The applicant presents and the public can comment. The committee makes a recommendation to the full board.
60 days
Community Board 6. Holds a public hearing and votes at a full board meeting. The recommendation is advisory and goes to the Borough President.
30 days
Brooklyn Borough President. Issues an advisory recommendation.
60 days
City Planning Commission. Holds a public hearing and votes to approve, approve with modifications, or disapprove. This is the first binding vote.
50 days
City Council. The Land Use Committee holds a hearing and the full Council votes to approve, modify or disapprove. The local Council Member's position carries great weight.
5 days
Mayor. May veto the Council's decision. The Council can override a veto with a two-thirds vote within 10 days.

If the application is certified on November 30, 2026, the earliest date allowed, the latest each step could finish is: CB6 by January 29, 2027; Borough President by February 28, 2027; City Planning Commission by April 29, 2027; City Council by June 18, 2027; Mayor by June 23, 2027. What the zoning means: use groups

Zoning sorts every kind of activity into ten use groups, numbered I through X. Each zoning district allows some use groups and not others. That is how zoning decides whether a lot can hold apartments, a school, a store or a factory.

M1-4 (today) is a light manufacturing district. It allows businesses, offices, storage and production, but no new housing, and community facilities such as schools are limited. R8X is a high-density residential district. M1-4/R8X (proposed) pairs the two: anything allowed in either district is allowed on the site, so housing and a school can be built alongside commercial and light industrial uses. Industrial uses in a paired district must still meet M1 performance standards for noise, odor and other impacts.
