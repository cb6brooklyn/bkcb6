What is being proposed

Ailanthus Hoyt LLC is asking the City to rezone 424 Hoyt Street (Block 468, Lot 25), a 45,900 square foot lot between 4th Street and 5th Street that is currently used for parking. According to the application filed with the Department of City Planning, the rezoning would allow a 17-story, 175-foot mixed-use building of about 330,000 square feet: about 400 apartments, including 100 income-restricted homes, about 46,000 square feet for a school, and about 14,000 square feet of retail.

There are two actions. The zoning map amendment (270102ZMK) changes the site from M1-4 to M1-4/R8X within the Special Gowanus Mixed Use District. The zoning text amendment (N270103ZRK) maps Mandatory Inclusionary Housing on the site, which makes income-restricted housing a requirement of any residential development there.

What the zoning means: use groups

Zoning sorts every kind of activity into ten use groups, numbered I through X. Each zoning district allows some use groups and not others. That is how zoning decides whether a lot can hold apartments, a school, a store or a factory.

M1-4 (today) is a light manufacturing district. It allows businesses, offices, storage and production, but no new housing, and community facilities such as schools are limited. R8X is a high-density residential district. M1-4/R8X (proposed) pairs the two: anything allowed in either district is allowed on the site, so housing and a school can be built alongside commercial and light industrial uses. Industrial uses in a paired district must still meet M1 performance standards for noise, odor and other impacts.
