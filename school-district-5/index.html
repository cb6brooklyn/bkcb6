Community School District 5

NYC Public Schools · Manhattan

Community School District 5 is one of the 32 community school districts of NYC Public Schools, for the public elementary and middle schools in its area. It reaches into MNCB10 (53%), MNCB9 (25%), MNCB11 (22%).
