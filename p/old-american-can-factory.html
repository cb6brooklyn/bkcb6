The Old American Can Factory

🏭 Individual landmark · Gowanus, Brooklyn

What it is

A six building complex on the Gowanus Canal that made cans, and later cardboard boxes, from 1890. It is an individual New York City landmark, and one of the clearest surviving pieces of the industrial Gowanus that the neighbourhood is named for.

Today it is a working arts and light manufacturing building. Studios, small manufacturers, designers, performance space and non profits share it, including the Gowanus Canal Conservancy. That mix is not incidental: the lot is zoned M1-4/R7X inside the Gowanus special district, the paired manufacturing and residential designation the 2021 rezoning used along the canal.

The numbers tell the rest. The complex is built to a floor area ratio of 1.74 on a lot that allows 5.0 residential and 2.0 commercial. It is nowhere near as built out as it is allowed to be, which is exactly the calculation that decides the future of buildings like this one.
