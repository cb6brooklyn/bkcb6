Mark Levine

Comptroller of the City of New York

Mark Levine is the Comptroller, elected citywide.

Independent financial watchdog. Audits city agencies, manages the pension funds and reviews contracts.

First elected in 2025 and sworn in January 2026. Citywide and borough offices carry four year terms and are limited to two consecutive terms.

Next on the ballot in November 2029; Comptroller Levine is in a first full term and may run again. For the election in front of us, see where you vote in November 2026 or what is coming up on the calendar.
