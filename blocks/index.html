How to read this.
The stretch
Streets and cross streets come from the Department of Transportation street centerline. When you pick From and To, the tool walks the centerline between those two intersections and highlights it in orange. Everything within the reach you chose of that line is counted.
Reach
A Brooklyn block is roughly 60 to 80 meters deep from the street to the middle of the block. "Both sides of the block" catches the buildings fronting the street on both sides. "A block either way" reaches into the neighboring blocks too.
Live records
311 reports (last 12 months), NYPD crime complaints (this year to date), and lots with zoning are pulled live from NYC Open Data each time you pick a stretch. Everything else is from this site's own data files, refreshed nightly.
Placement
Every record is placed by its own coordinates. Nothing here is summed from precinct or district totals.
