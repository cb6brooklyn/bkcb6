Fifth Avenue Committee

🤝 Community based organization · Brooklyn Community Board 6

Mission

To advance economic, social and racial justice in New York City through integrated, community-centered affordable housing, grassroots organizing, policy advocacy, and transformative education, training, and services that build the power to shape our community's future.

What it does

FAC has been at this since 1978, and it is one of the few organizations in the district that does development, organizing and services at the same time rather than picking one. It reaches more than 6,500 low and moderate income New Yorkers a year.

On housing it builds and manages affordable and supportive housing, with a pipeline running to as many as 1,900 units, and it has done the unglamorous work too: gut renovations of small rental buildings, LEED Gold mixed-income developments, and a public library, a public park and five Pre-K classrooms folded into its projects.

On the ground it runs tenant counseling and organizing, foreclosure prevention and homebuyer counseling, adult education including GED prep, English classes and digital literacy for over a thousand adults a year, and workforce development through its affiliate Brooklyn Workforce Innovations. South Brooklyn Against Displacement is its organizing arm.

The lot at 621 DeGraw is owned by FAC Center Local Development Corporation, which tells you something: they own the building they organize from.
