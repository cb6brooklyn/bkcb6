Neighborhood Preservation Companies

Free housing help, close to home, in every borough

Neighborhood Preservation Companies are nonprofits contracted by New York State to provide housing services in a specific service area. They are not city agencies and they are not landlords. Most offer some combination of tenant rights counselling, help with repairs and code complaints, foreclosure and eviction prevention, help applying for affordable housing, and assistance for landlords of small buildings.

Services are generally free. Each company serves a defined area, so the one nearest you is usually the one to call.
