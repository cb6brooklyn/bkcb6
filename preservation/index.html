Neighborhood Preservation Companies

Free housing help, close to home, in every borough

Neighborhood Preservation Companies are nonprofits contracted by New York State to provide housing services in a specific service area, sometimes called a catchment area. Most offer some combination of managing affordable housing, tenant rights counseling, help with repairs and code complaints, foreclosure and eviction prevention, help applying for affordable housing, and assistance for landlords of small buildings.

Services are generally free. Each company serves a (sort of) defined area. If you are reading this in CB6, the one closest to you is probably either the Fifth Avenue Committee or the Carroll Gardens Association.
