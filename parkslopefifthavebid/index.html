Fifth Avenue from Dean Street to 18th Street. The boundary, every business listing and every property inside it.

Boundary: the city's file of business improvement districts (NYC Department of Small Business Services), Nov 16, 2026. The district spans Community Boards 6 and 7. Businesses: the bkcb6.app Directory, which draws on NYC Health Department restaurant inspections, NYC Department of Consumer and Worker Protection licenses, New York State food store, liquor and salon licenses, the federal health provider and bank branch registers, and OpenStreetMap (read Nov 16, 2026), and the BID's own business directory (parkslopefifthavenuebid.com, read Aug 27, 2026).
