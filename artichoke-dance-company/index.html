Artichoke Dance Company

Eco-arts dance company · Gowanus, Brooklyn

Artichoke Dance Company calls itself a national leader in eco-arts performance and climate action, creating innovative performances and programs addressing climate change.

Artichoke makes site specific and site adaptive works as well as works for the stage, centering environmental justice and resilience.
