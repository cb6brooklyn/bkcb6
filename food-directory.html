Sources: the New York State Department of Agriculture and Markets retail food store license file, downloaded August 2026, and the Department of Health and Mental Hygiene farmers market list published on NYC Open Data. Community district is assigned by point in polygon against Department of City Planning district boundaries. A store is counted as a supermarket when it carries a supermarket banner or reports at least 10,000 square feet; pharmacies, dollar stores, delis, bakeries, breweries and wholesalers are excluded. Square footage is self reported to the state. License records lag closings and openings, and market days and hours change through the season, so confirm before you travel. Mike Racioppo, Brooklyn Community Board 6.
