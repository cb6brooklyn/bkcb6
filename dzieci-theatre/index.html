Dzieci Theatre

Experimental theatre ensemble · Park Slope, Brooklyn

Dzieci Theatre is an experimental ensemble founded in 1997, directed by founding director Matt Mitler with co-founder Yvonne Brecht. It works in the tradition of Jerzy Grotowski and describes every show as a quest for deeper meaning.

What Dzieci Theatre does

Dzieci brings its work into hospitals and institutions serving people in need, working alongside health care professionals. Its Fools Mass, a comedy of medieval fools holding a mass, has played around New York City since 1998, including at the Cathedral of St. John the Divine.
