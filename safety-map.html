NYC Public Safety Map

Crime complaints, arrests, parking tickets and camera violations for every district in the city, 2020 to 2026. Pick a dataset, a year and a district layer; tap any district for its trend and profile. Every record is placed by its own location, never summed from precinct totals.

Pick a district

Tap any district on the map, or a row in the ranking below, to see its year-by-year trend, all four datasets, and the link to its full profile.
Sources: NYPD complaint and arrest data, DOF parking violations (camera codes split out per the DOF code table), each record placed by its own coordinates or exact street address into the district it falls in; details and remainders on every district’s profile page. Fiscal-source parking data is shown by calendar year. Built by Mike Racioppo, District Manager, Brooklyn Community Board 6.
