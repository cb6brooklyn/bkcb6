2025 · June primary and November general

The mayoral race, the citywide offices, and six ballot questions. Two of the six failed and the three housing measures passed overwhelmingly. In 73 of CB6’s 75 election districts, voters approved them.

2026 · The general election and the June primary

The general election is Tuesday, November 3. Early voting runs October 24 to November 1. Below that, the June 23 primary: Comptroller, NY-10, AD 52 and Council District 3, by election district and by community board across the city. CB6 cast 24,053 ballots.
