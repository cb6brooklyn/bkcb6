CHiPS

Soup kitchen, food pantry and shelter · Park Slope, Brooklyn

CHiPS (Community Help in Park Slope) is a community meal provider, food pantry and shelter for women with young children, in a late 1800s building on 4th Avenue between Park Slope and Gowanus.

CHiPS was founded in 1971 by members of St. Francis Xavier Church. The mission is to ameliorate food insecurity and homelessness in New York City.

What CHiPS does

CHiPS serves breakfast and lunch six days a week, runs on site and mobile pantries, and provides transitional housing for prenatal and postpartum women and their infants at the Frances Residence.

CHiPS relies on volunteers and donations, and rescues food from businesses that would otherwise be thrown away.
