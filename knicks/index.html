Security Screening

Everyone is searched before entering. Security will check you and turn away anything on the prohibited list, so leave those items at home.

  • No bags of any kind
  • Plastic water bottles are OK — no glass or metal bottles

Can’t Bring (you’ll be turned away)

  • Bats / batons
  • Bicycles or scooters
  • Chairs
  • Coolers
  • Drones
  • Pets
  • Strollers
  • Umbrellas
  • Weapons
  • Glass or metal bottles
