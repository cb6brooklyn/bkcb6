What this page counts

Bergen Street between 4th Avenue and 5th Avenue, about 784 feet long (calculated from the city centerline), one block of the Dean and Bergen bike boulevard corridor.

  • "Within 100 feet." Crashes, 311 requests and police complaints are counted if their mapped point is within 100 feet of the block's centerline, which takes in the 4th Avenue and 5th Avenue intersections. Each count links to the NYC Open Data query that returns it, drawn with that same outline.
  • Dates. From January 1, 2020: crashes through June 11, 2026, 311 through September 25, 2026.
  • Distances are straight lines from the block's centerline (calculated). A sidewalk walk is longer.

Where the block sits

The block is in BKCB6, Council 39, Assembly 52, Senate 25 and the 78th Precinct (calculated from the district boundary files on bkcb6.app). At its 4th Avenue end it meets other districts.

  • Community boards. The block is in BKCB6. BKCB2 is about 21 ft away at the nearest point; no point on the block is more than 517 ft from it. BKCB8 is about 569 ft away at the nearest point; no point on the block is more than 1,352 ft from it.
  • City Council districts. The block is in Council 39. Council 33 is about 22 ft away at the nearest point; no point on the block is more than 806 ft from it. Council 35 is about 347 ft away at the nearest point; no point on the block is more than 786 ft from it.
  • State Assembly districts. The block is in Assembly 52. Assembly 44 is about 347 ft away at the nearest point; no point on the block is more than 784 ft from it. Assembly 57 is about 774 ft away at the nearest point; no point on the block is more than 815 ft from it.
  • State Senate districts. The block is in Senate 25. Senate 26 is about 24 ft away at the nearest point; no point on the block is more than 265 ft from it. Senate 20 is about 505 ft away at the nearest point; no point on the block is more than 522 ft from it.
  • Congressional districts. The block is in NY-10. NY-8 is about 1,200 ft away at the nearest point; no point on the block is more than 1,347 ft from it. NY-7 is about 1,534 ft away at the nearest point; no point on the block is more than 1,962 ft from it.
  • Police precincts. The block is in 78th Precinct. 84th Precinct is about 21 ft away at the nearest point; no point on the block is more than 805 ft from it. 88th Precinct is about 963 ft away at the nearest point; no point on the block is more than 1,119 ft from it.
  • Along the whole corridor the boulevard crosses 4 community boards and 6 Council districts; DOT's notice goes to each affected board and elected official (how the plan is reviewed).

Truck route

The block is a designated local truck route, and so is Bergen Street between 3rd and 4th Avenues (calculated from NYC DOT truck route data, on the bkcb6.app truck routes map).

  • What a local route is. "The Local Truck Route Network is designated for trucks with an origin and destination within a borough" (NYC DOT, Truck Routing).
  • October 4, 2026. None of the 103 changes is on Bergen Street (NYC DOT).
  • Not addressed. The boulevard announcement does not say whether the truck route would change (Mayor's Office).

Open questions for this block

  • The design here. The city has not published a block-by-block design (Mayor's Office).
  • Trucks and deliveries. The block stays a local truck route after October 4; the announcement does not say how deliveries and the boulevard would share the street.
  • Parking and curb use. Not addressed in the public documents above; illegal parking is the most common 311 request here (217 since 2020).
  • After the B65. When the reroute takes effect, and which service would be closest, is not in the MTA draft (MTA).
