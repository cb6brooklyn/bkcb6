History

The City acquired this land between 1940 and 1945 for the Brooklyn-Battery Tunnel, now the Hugh L. Carey Tunnel. Robert Moses had wanted a bridge; after President Roosevelt had the Army refuse permits for it, a tunnel was built instead, begun in 1940 and finished in 1950. A tunnel ventilation shaft sits in a corner of the playground.

In 2024 Parks turned an underused asphalt lot here into Red Hook's first BMX and skate park, with a playground, climbing boulders and open space for parkour.

Named for Harold L. Ickes (1874-1952), Secretary of the Interior in Franklin Roosevelt's New Deal cabinet, who oversaw the public works program that built parks, highways, public buildings and the Hoover Dam.
