St. Mary's Park

Playground · Carroll Gardens · 0.71 acres

What it is

Under the F and G subway's Culver Viaduct, serving Carroll Gardens, Gowanus and Red Hook. South of Nelson Street a children's playground stays shaded even on hot days; the north lot has basketball courts and a skate park for older kids and young adults.

History

Named for the nearby St. Mary Star of the Sea Roman Catholic Church, it has been a city playground since the late 1960s.

Parks' page reports a $19.6 million renovation under the Anchor Parks program, opened with a ribbon cutting that also unveiled the newly renamed Gil Scott-Heron Amphitheater.
