Cross Island Parkway

🌳 Parkway · Queens

What it is

A parkway. This is a road corridor, and the Parks Department holds the right of way along with the planted land beside it, so the acreage covers the whole corridor rather than a park you walk into. Some stretches carry a greenway or a waterfront promenade; others are roadway and its verges.
