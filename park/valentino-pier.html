History

Named for Louis J. Valentino, Jr. (1958-1996), a Parks lifeguard and firefighter who grew up and studied in Red Hook, Bay Ridge and Brooklyn Heights. He joined the Fire Department in 1984, served with Engine 281 and Ladder 147, joined Rescue Company 2 in 1993 and was cited twice for bravery. He died on February 5, 1996, searching for injured firefighters at a three-alarm fire in Flatlands.

The pier recalls the shipping industry that once lined Buttermilk Channel and the Upper Harbor, when Red Hook was one of the nation's busiest ports.

The City's Economic Development Corporation built the pier in 1996, and it became a city park in 1999. From it you can see the Statue of Liberty, Governors Island, the Manhattan skyline, Staten Island and the harbor.
