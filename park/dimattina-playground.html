History

The land was once part of the Luquer family farm. Jean L'Escuyer came to New Netherland from France in 1658 and married Rachel Dircks, daughter of one of Brooklyn's earliest settlers; the farm stayed in the family until it was divided and sold.

The City acquired the two parcels that make up the park in three phases between 1941 and 1947. A 1967 local law named the larger part for Vincent J. DiMattina; the smaller part was left without a name.

Parks added a dog run to the larger part in 2001 and improved the play areas. In 2015 the DiMattina Dog Run was rebuilt and enlarged with separate areas for large and small dogs.

Vincent J. DiMattina (1915-1966) started on the Brooklyn waterfront at 16, served as a Navy Seabee deep-sea diver in the South Pacific in World War II, became a lawyer in 1954, was New York State Commander of the Veterans of Foreign Wars in the 1960s, and was active in civic and church life in Carroll Gardens.
