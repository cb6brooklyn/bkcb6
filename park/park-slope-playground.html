History

The City acquired the land in 1959, and the playground opened in 1964 as the P.S. 282 Playground. Parks renamed it Park Slope Playground in 1985.

The Park Slope Playground Committee formed in 1988 and pushed for rebuilding this and other playgrounds. Council Member Ken Fisher and Borough President Howard Golden raised $927,000, and the reconstruction was built in 1994 and 1995: new play equipment, swings, tennis and basketball courts, a spray shower, safety surfacing, benches, drinking fountains and game tables.

An eagle weathervane sits on the restroom building among London plane trees.
