History

Named for Mother Frances Xavier Cabrini (1850-1917), founder of the Missionary Sisters of the Sacred Heart and the first American citizen made a saint. Sent to the United States in 1889 to help Italian immigrants, she taught at parishes in Manhattan, the Bronx and at what is now Sacred Hearts and St. Stephen's Church in Brooklyn.

On this site in 1892 she opened her first school in the Northeast, St. Charles School, in a house at President and Van Brunt Streets bought by Bishop Charles E. McDonnell of Brooklyn.

A local law named the site Mother Cabrini Park in 1992. Parks acquired the 0.287-acre lot in 1993 and the 0.18-acre lot next door in 2000, extending the park to the corner of President and Van Brunt Streets.

It has benches, a spray shower, play equipment, and stone sculptures of an eagle and a turkey on the gateposts.
