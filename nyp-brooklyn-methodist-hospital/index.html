NewYork-Presbyterian Brooklyn Methodist Hospital

Hospital · Park Slope, Brooklyn

NewYork-Presbyterian Brooklyn Methodist Hospital at 506 6th Street in Park Slope is open 24 hours a day, seven days a week, with an emergency department. Its doctors are Weill Cornell Medicine physicians, and its care runs across specialties from cancer and heart care to pediatrics, women’s health and orthopedics.

The hospital lists heart care among the Top 50 Cardiovascular Hospitals in the nation, based on U.S. News & World Report’s 2024-2025 cardiology, heart and vascular surgery rankings, along with the Meyer Cancer Center, an on-site infusion therapy center and community care programs.

What the hospital does

Its services: Advanced Surgery, Advanced Otolaryngology, Pulmonology, Diabetes & Endocrinology, Emergency Medicine, Interventional Radiology, Radiology, General Surgery, Bariatric Surgery, Vascular Surgery, Internal Medicine, Thoracic Surgery, Cardiothoracic Surgery, Geriatrics, Pregnancy & Maternity Care, Asthma & Other Lung Diseases, Dentistry, Cancer Care, Digestive Diseases, Heart, Neurology & Neurosurgery, Ophthalmology, Organ Transplantation, Orthopedics, Pediatrics, Psychiatry and Mental Health, Primary Care, Rehabilitation, Sleep Disorders, Spine Care, Women’s Health and Urology.

The Center for Community Health, steps from the hospital at 515 6th Street, is NYP’s outpatient building: same-day surgery, diagnostics and specialty care, from cancer treatment to women’s imaging, without an overnight stay in the hospital.

The cafeteria, in the lobby of the Carrington Pavilion, serves breakfast 7:00 to 10:30 AM and lunch 11:15 AM to 2:30 PM, and reopens 3:15 to 6:30 PM with beverages, fruit and snacks. Guest trays can be sent up to a patient’s room by calling 718-780-3401.
