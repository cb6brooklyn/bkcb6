The Use of Land Hub

Everything in one place

The use of land (more commonly land use, but I’m hoping you notice) is about who gets what, when, and how. What can be built, where, at what scale, and for whom is settled years before anything goes up, in applications most people never see.

So I put everything I’ve built for reading that record in one place. I’ll be adding to it, but let me know what you think. Also, if anyone is a non-iPhone user who would be interested in demoing the app so that it can be in the Google Play Store, let me know.
