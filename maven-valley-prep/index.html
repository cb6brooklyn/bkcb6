Maven Valley Prep

Child care center · South Slope, Brooklyn

Maven Valley Prep is a child care center at 348 13th Street in South Slope.

In its own words: “At Maven Valley Prep daycare in Park Slope, we constantly strive for the finest care possible by providing the safest and most loving environment for your child. Our goal is for kids to feel safe and secure while preparing them for successful elementary school and early childhood experience.”
