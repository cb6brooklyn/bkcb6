The Comprehensive Plan for Brooklyn in CB6

The plan’s maps of Brooklyn, cut to the district and laid on a street map, and everything it says about CB6

← The whole plan
Borough President Antonio Reynoso’s Comprehensive Plan for Brooklyn maps the whole borough. Here 111 of its 113 maps are cut to BKCB6, Park Slope, Carroll Gardens, Cobble Hill, Red Hook, Gowanus, and the Columbia Street Waterfront District, and placed on a street map so you can search an address, zoom to a block and see the street names under it. The other two are not drawn to a scale a street map can match and open on their page. Below the map is every passage of the plan that names the district or its neighborhoods.

    What the plan says about CB6

    Every passage of the plan that names Park Slope, Carroll Gardens, Cobble Hill, Red Hook, Gowanus, the Columbia Street Waterfront District, the Brooklyn Marine Terminal or Community District 6, in page order. Each links to its place in the plan; passages from the existing conditions maps open that map above.
    Sources Brooklyn Borough President Antonio Reynoso, The Comprehensive Plan for Brooklyn, 2025. The maps are the plan’s own, rendered from the PDF at high resolution around CB6 and positioned on the street map from the borough outline each map draws; each map’s data source is printed in its key.
