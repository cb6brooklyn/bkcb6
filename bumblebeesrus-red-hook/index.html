BumbleBeesRus, Red Hook

Child care center and preschool · Red Hook, Brooklyn

BumbleBeesRus, Red Hook is a child care center and preschool at 76 Lorraine Street in Red Hook.

In its own words: “We offer educational programs for children ages 6 weeks to 5 years, plus afterschool and summer programs for ages 6–10. Our experienced staff provide nurturing care and encouragement, while our on-site playground offers opportunities for outdoor play.”

The program

Licensed by the NYC Health Department as a infant and toddler program for ages birth to 2, with room for 45 children (permit DC21878).

Hours, per the program: Monday – Friday 8 AM – 6 PM; 3-K and Pre-K SDY 9:00 AM – 3:20 PM.
