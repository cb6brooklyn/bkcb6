Barnacle Parade 2026

14th Annual Barnacle Day Parade · Red Hook, Brooklyn · Thursday, October 29, 2026

When and where

Steps off
4:00 PM, Coffey Park
Gather in Coffey Park, on the Richards Street side at Pioneer Street.
Along the way
Key Lime Steve’s
Dance party, key lime pies and more down by Steve’s Authentic Key Lime Pies, 185 Van Dyke Street, at the Van Dyke and Ferris corner of the route.
Ends
Pioneer St & Van Brunt St
Block party til 9:00 PM at San Pedro Inn, 320 Van Brunt Street, with food, music and raffle prizes.

The route

Tap the map to see it full size

From Coffey Park the parade goes up Richards Street to Verona Street, along Verona to Columbia Street, down Columbia to Lorraine Street, along Lorraine to Otsego Street and on to Wolcott Street, down Wolcott to Van Brunt Street, along Van Brunt to Van Dyke Street, down Van Dyke to Ferris Street, along Ferris to Sullivan Street, along Sullivan to Conover Street, along Conover to Pioneer Street, and down Pioneer to the block party at Pioneer and Van Brunt.
