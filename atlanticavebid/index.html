Directory

Every business, property and new building in the proposed district. Search by name or address, pick a category, or tap a listing to find it on the map.

Boundary: the city's file of business improvement districts (NYC Department of Small Business Services), Nov 16, 2026. The district spans Community Boards 2 and 6. Businesses: the bkcb6.app Directory, which draws on NYC Health Department restaurant inspections, NYC Department of Consumer and Worker Protection licenses, New York State food store, liquor and salon licenses, the federal health provider and bank branch registers, and OpenStreetMap (read Nov 16, 2026). Properties: every tax lot inside the boundary, from the Department of City Planning's PLUTO file, with its storefronts, occupied or vacant, from the city's storefront registry.
