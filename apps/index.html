Brooklyn Community Board 6

BKCB6

Brooklyn Community Board 6 covers Park Slope, Carroll Gardens, Cobble Hill, Red Hook, Gowanus and the Columbia Street Waterfront District. This app puts the board and the district in one place.

Meetings and the board

  • Every upcoming Full Board and committee meeting, with the details and an add-to-calendar button
  • All 48 board members, the Chair and the District Manager
  • Every set of CB6 minutes from 2010 to today, searchable without a connection

Land use and zoning

  • Every tax lot in the district, searchable by address, owner, zoning or BBL
  • Zoning, land use and historic district maps, with landmark permit applications
  • The Gowanus rezoning and every lot in the rezoning area
  • The Brooklyn Marine Terminal redevelopment
  • A zoning code decoder

Your address and your vote

  • Look up any CB6 address for its lot, zoning, districts and where it votes
  • Your Election Day and early voting sites for November 3, the offices on the ballot and the ballot questions, with key dates

The district

  • 311 complaints, housing and buildings, NYCHA, Vision Zero and transportation
  • Parks, schools, libraries and childcare
  • Liquor licenses, street work permits, business improvement districts and community organizations
  • Public safety: police sectors, fire battalions and sanitation sections
  • Healthcare and social services
  • Weather: alerts, the forecast, air quality, places to cool off, the surface heat map, heat vulnerability and coastal and rainfall flood zones

All 59 community boards

CB6 & Beyond

CB6 & Beyond starts with Brooklyn Community Board 6 and covers all 59 community boards in New York City.

Where you vote

  • Your Election Day poll site and early voting site for every election district in the five boroughs
  • What is on your ballot for the November 3 general election, with key dates

Any address

  • Type any address in the five boroughs to see its community board, who represents it and where it votes

Every community board

  • All 59 boards by borough, with a map, officers, office address, the neighborhoods they cover, median rent, 2025 election results and their elected officials

Across the city

  • Rent by community board, all 59 ranked
  • 2025 general election results for mayor and the ballot questions, by board
  • How New York City and State government are organized: the City Charter, org charts, the City Council, borough presidents and district attorneys, community board leaders, business improvement districts and the State Constitution
  • City and state budgets

Brooklyn Community Board 6

  • Meetings, minutes, the board, land use and zoning, and every CB6 tool

The borough of Brooklyn

BK CIVICS

Brooklyn civic information in one place.

Any Brooklyn address

Look up any Brooklyn address and get its community board, City Council, Assembly, State Senate and Congressional districts, zoning and land use, plus what is nearby.

The borough map

Every layer: land use, zoning, historic districts, parks, schools, childcare, libraries, school districts, NYPD sectors, fire battalions, subway, bus, Citi Bike, speed limits, rents, culture and the Gowanus rezoning. Search by address, pick a street and cross street, drop a pin, or use your location.

The calendar

What is coming up across Brooklyn: all 18 community board meetings, City Council hearings, public comment deadlines, the Borough President, alternate side parking suspensions and culture events.

The directory

Every elected official who represents Brooklyn and every New York City and New York State agency, each linking to a profile.

Elections

Where to vote, results by community board and council district, and how each election district voted.

And the rest

Housing, 311 complaints, rezonings, ULURP applications, landmarks, public safety, transportation, parks, libraries and local organizations.

Trivia, puzzles and games

NYC Civics Quiz

Trivia, puzzles and games on how New York City works, from the people who run a community board. Every question, clue and puzzle comes from the quizzes and games on bkcb6.app. Nothing here needs a connection.

Trivia boards

  • Four boards on city and state government: NYC Government, City Council, Charter and Mayor, Government Agencies, and New York State Government
  • Five borough history boards: Brooklyn (one clue per community district), Manhattan, the Bronx, Queens and Staten Island
  • Pick a clue, choose one of four answers or type your own, and climb the ranks from Community Associate to Mayor

How the city works

  • NYC Government Org Chart: ten questions on who reports to whom at City Hall
  • Land Use Game: fill in the blanks on ULURP, the BSA and SEQR, put the ULURP stages in order and match the five variance findings
  • Which Review Track: put the steps of ULURP and the fast tracks in order
  • Brooklyn Map Puzzle: find all 18 community districts on the map

Crosswords

  • Ten crosswords on city government, state government, land use and Brooklyn history, with an on-screen keyboard, check and reveal

Every answer has a source

  • Each answer links to its source and to the matching page on bkcb6.app

High scores

  • Enter your initials, arcade style, and keep a top ten for every game on your phone. No account, no sign-in and nothing leaves the device.

Questions

Which iPhone do I need?

BKCB6, CB6 & Beyond and NYC Civics Quiz need iOS 17 or later. BK CIVICS needs iOS 15 or later.

Which one should I get?

If you live or work in Park Slope, Carroll Gardens, Cobble Hill, Red Hook, Gowanus or the Columbia Street Waterfront District, BKCB6. For the rest of the city, CB6 & Beyond. For the whole borough of Brooklyn, BK CIVICS. To test yourself on how the city works, NYC Civics Quiz.

Not on an iPhone?

Everything in the apps is on the web at bkcb6.app. No download required.
