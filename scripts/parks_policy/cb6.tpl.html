Tree canopy and pruning

How much of CB6 is shaded by trees, block by block, how every street tree is doing, and when each street was last pruned
Source: NYC Tree Canopy Change 2010 to 2017 (by9k-vhck), the 2017 LiDAR survey New Yorkers for Parks also uses; Forestry Tree Points hn5i-inap; Forestry Work Orders bdjm-n7q4 (block pruning back to 1995). Blocks are tax blocks; each block's share includes the ground between it and the next block, split down the middle. Street trees are those the Parks Department cares for along sidewalks; canopy counts every tree, including in parks and backyards. Canopy has not been surveyed citywide since 2017.
