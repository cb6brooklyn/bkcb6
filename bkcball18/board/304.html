About

Brooklyn Community District 4 encompasses Bushwick. Covering 2 square miles and home to more than 110,000 residents, it is a compact, dense, transit-connected district built around residential blocks, industrial spaces, elevated train corridors, and commercial streets.

Its built environment includes residential blocks, industrial spaces, elevated train corridors, and commercial streets, with many older industrial buildings adapted to new uses.
