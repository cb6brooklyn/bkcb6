About

Brooklyn Community District 1 encompasses Greenpoint and Williamsburg. Covering 4.7 square miles and home to more than 200,000 residents, it sits on the borough's northern edge, where the East River, Newtown Creek, and the Queens line shape the district.

Greenpoint and Williamsburg give the district a mix of waterfront industry, older rowhouse blocks, large apartment development, nightlife, manufacturing, public housing, and ongoing redevelopment along the waterfront and near transit.
