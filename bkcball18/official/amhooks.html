About

Assembly Member Larinda C. Hooks represents State Assembly District 35. It sits in Queens.

The district overlaps community boards Queens 3, Queens 4 and Queens 6.

It is policed by the 110th, 112th and 115th Precincts.

It overlaps council districts 21, 22 and 24 and Senate districts 11, 13 and 15.

Members of the State Assembly serve two year terms.
