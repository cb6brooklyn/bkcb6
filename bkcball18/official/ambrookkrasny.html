About

Assembly Member Alec Brook-Krasny represents State Assembly District 46. It sits in Brooklyn.

The district overlaps community boards Brooklyn 10, Brooklyn 11 and Brooklyn 13.

It is policed by the 60th, 62nd and 68th Precincts.

It overlaps council districts 47, 48 and 50 and Senate districts 17, 23 and 26.

Members of the State Assembly serve two year terms.
