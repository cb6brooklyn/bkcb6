About

Assembly Member Charles D. Fall represents State Assembly District 61. It reaches into Manhattan and Staten Island.

The district overlaps community boards Manhattan 1 and Staten Island 1.

It is policed by the 1st, 120th and 121st Precincts.

It overlaps council districts 1, 49 and 50 and Senate districts 23, 24, 26 and 27.

Members of the State Assembly serve two year terms.
