About

Council Member Farah Louis represents City Council District 45. It sits in Brooklyn.

The district overlaps community boards Brooklyn 14, Brooklyn 17 and Brooklyn 18.

It is policed by the 63rd, 67th and 70th Precincts.

It overlaps Assembly districts 41, 42, 58 and 59 and Senate district 21.

Took office June 4, 2019. Members of the City Council serve four year terms and are limited to two consecutive terms.

Next on the ballot in November 2029, when the seat will be open: Council Member Louis is term limited and cannot run again. For the election in front of us, see where you vote in November 2026 or what is coming up on the calendar.
