About

Assembly Member Nikki Lucas represents State Assembly District 60. It sits in Brooklyn.

The district overlaps community boards Brooklyn 5 and Brooklyn 18.

It is policed by the 69th and 75th Precincts.

It overlaps council districts 42 and 46 and Senate district 19.

Members of the State Assembly serve two year terms.
