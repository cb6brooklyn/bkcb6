About

Assembly Member Stacey Pheffer Amato represents State Assembly District 23. It sits in Queens.

The district overlaps community boards Queens 10 and Queens 14.

It is policed by the 100th, 101st and 106th Precincts.

It overlaps council districts 31 and 32 and Senate districts 10, 15 and 19.

Members of the State Assembly serve two year terms.
