About

Representative Gregory Meeks represents New York’s 5th Congressional District in the United States House of Representatives. It sits in Queens.

The district overlaps community boards Queens 12, Queens 13, Queens 14, Queens 10, Queens 9 and Queens 8.

It is policed by the 100th, 101st, 102nd, 103rd, 105th, 106th, 107th, 113th and 116th Precincts.

It overlaps council districts 23, 24, 27, 28, 29, 31 and 32, Assembly districts 23, 24, 28, 29, 31, 32, 33 and 38 and Senate districts 10, 11, 14, 15, 16 and 19.

Members of the House serve two year terms, with no term limit.
