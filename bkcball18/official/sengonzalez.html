About

State Senator Kristen Gonzalez represents State Senate District 59. It reaches into Brooklyn, Manhattan and Queens.

The district overlaps community boards Brooklyn 1, Manhattan 6, Queens 1 and Queens 2.

It is policed by the 13th, 17th, 94th, 108th and 114th Precincts.

It overlaps council districts 2, 4, 22, 26 and 33 and Assembly districts 36, 37, 50 and 74.

Members of the State Senate serve two year terms.
