About

Assembly Member Grace Lee represents State Assembly District 65. It sits in Manhattan.

The district overlaps community boards Manhattan 1, Manhattan 2 and Manhattan 3.

It is policed by the 1st, 5th, 7th and 9th Precincts.

It overlaps council districts 1 and 2 and Senate district 27.

Members of the State Assembly serve two year terms.
