About

Council Member Harvey Epstein represents City Council District 2. It sits in Manhattan.

The district overlaps community boards Manhattan 2, Manhattan 3, Manhattan 5 and Manhattan 6.

It is policed by the 6th, 7th, 9th and 13th Precincts.

It overlaps Assembly districts 65, 66 and 74 and Senate districts 27, 28 and 59.

Took office December 4, 2025. Members of the City Council serve four year terms and are limited to two consecutive terms.

Next on the ballot in November 2029; Council Member Epstein is in a first full term and may run again. For the election in front of us, see where you vote in November 2026 or what is coming up on the calendar.
