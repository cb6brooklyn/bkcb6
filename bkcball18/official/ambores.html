About

Assembly Member Alex Bores represents State Assembly District 73. It sits in Manhattan.

The district overlaps community boards Manhattan 5, Manhattan 6 and Manhattan 8.

It is policed by the 14th, 17th, 18th and 19th Precincts.

It overlaps council districts 4 and 5 and Senate districts 28 and 59.

Members of the State Assembly serve two year terms.
