About

Assembly Member Jessica González-Rojas represents State Assembly District 34. It sits in Queens.

The district overlaps community boards Queens 1 and Queens 3.

It is policed by the 114th and 115th Precincts.

It overlaps council districts 21, 22 and 25 and Senate districts 11, 12 and 13.

Members of the State Assembly serve two year terms.
