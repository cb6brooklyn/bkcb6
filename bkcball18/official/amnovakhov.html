About

Assembly Member Michael Novakhov represents State Assembly District 45. It sits in Brooklyn.

The district overlaps community boards Brooklyn 12, Brooklyn 13, Brooklyn 14 and Brooklyn 15.

It is policed by the 60th, 61st, 66th and 70th Precincts.

It overlaps council districts 44, 47 and 48 and Senate districts 22 and 23.

Members of the State Assembly serve two year terms.
