About

Representative Dan Goldman represents New York’s 10th Congressional District in the United States House of Representatives. It sits in Brooklyn and Manhattan.

The district overlaps community boards Brooklyn 7, Brooklyn 6, Manhattan 3, Manhattan 1, Manhattan 2, Brooklyn 12, Brooklyn 2, Brooklyn 10, Brooklyn 8 and Manhattan 4.

It is policed by the 1st, 5th, 6th, 7th, 9th, 13th, 66th, 68th, 72nd, 76th, 77th, 78th and 84th Precincts.

It overlaps council districts 1, 2, 3, 33, 35, 38, 39, 40, 43, 44 and 47, Assembly districts 44, 48, 49, 51, 52, 61, 64, 65, 66 and 74 and Senate districts 17, 20, 22, 25, 26, 27, 28 and 47.

Members of the House serve two year terms, with no term limit.
