About

Assembly Member David I. Weprin represents State Assembly District 24. It sits in Queens.

The district overlaps community boards Queens 8, Queens 9, Queens 10, Queens 11 and Queens 13.

It is policed by the 102nd, 105th, 106th, 107th and 111th Precincts.

It overlaps council districts 23, 24, 28 and 29 and Senate districts 11, 14, 15 and 16.

Members of the State Assembly serve two year terms.
