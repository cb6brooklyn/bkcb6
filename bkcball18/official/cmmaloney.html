About

Council Member Virginia Maloney represents City Council District 4. It sits in Manhattan.

The district overlaps community boards Manhattan 5, Manhattan 6 and Manhattan 8.

It is policed by the 13th, 14th, 17th, 18th and 19th Precincts.

It overlaps Assembly districts 73, 74 and 75 and Senate districts 28 and 59.

First elected in 2025 and sworn in January 2026. Members of the City Council serve four year terms and are limited to two consecutive terms.

Next on the ballot in November 2029; Council Member Maloney is in a first full term and may run again. For the election in front of us, see where you vote in November 2026 or what is coming up on the calendar.
