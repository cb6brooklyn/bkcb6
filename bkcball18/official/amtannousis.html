About

Assembly Member Michael Tannousis represents State Assembly District 64. It reaches into Brooklyn and Staten Island.

The district overlaps community boards Brooklyn 10, Staten Island 2 and Staten Island 3.

It is policed by the 68th and 122nd Precincts.

It overlaps council districts 47, 50 and 51 and Senate districts 23, 24 and 26.

Members of the State Assembly serve two year terms.
