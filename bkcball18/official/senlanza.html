About

State Senator Andrew J. Lanza represents State Senate District 24. It sits in Staten Island.

The district overlaps community boards Staten Island 1, Staten Island 2 and Staten Island 3.

It is policed by the 120th, 121st, 122nd and 123rd Precincts.

It overlaps council districts 49, 50 and 51 and Assembly districts 61, 62, 63 and 64.

Members of the State Senate serve two year terms.
