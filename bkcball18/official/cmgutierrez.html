About

Council Member Jennifer Gutiérrez represents City Council District 34. It reaches into Brooklyn and Queens.

The district overlaps community boards Brooklyn 1, Brooklyn 4 and Queens 5.

It is policed by the 83rd, 90th, 94th and 104th Precincts.

It overlaps Assembly districts 37, 38, 50 and 53 and Senate districts 12, 18 and 59.

First elected in 2021 and sworn in January 2022. Members of the City Council serve four year terms and are limited to two consecutive terms.

Next on the ballot in November 2029, when the seat will be open: Council Member Gutiérrez is term limited and cannot run again. For the election in front of us, see where you vote in November 2026 or what is coming up on the calendar.
