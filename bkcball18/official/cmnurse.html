About

Council Member Sandy Nurse represents City Council District 37. It sits in Brooklyn.

The district overlaps community boards Brooklyn 4, Brooklyn 5 and Brooklyn 16.

It is policed by the 73rd, 75th and 83rd Precincts.

It overlaps Assembly districts 53, 54 and 55 and Senate districts 18, 19 and 25.

First elected in 2021 and sworn in January 2022. Members of the City Council serve four year terms and are limited to two consecutive terms.

Next on the ballot in November 2029, when the seat will be open: Council Member Nurse is term limited and cannot run again. For the election in front of us, see where you vote in November 2026 or what is coming up on the calendar.
