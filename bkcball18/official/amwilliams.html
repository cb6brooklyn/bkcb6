About

Assembly Member Jaime R. Williams represents State Assembly District 59. It sits in Brooklyn.

The district overlaps community board Brooklyn 18.

It is policed by the 61st, 63rd and 69th Precincts.

It overlaps council districts 42 and 46 and Senate districts 19, 21 and 22.

Members of the State Assembly serve two year terms.
