About

Representative Nicole Malliotakis represents New York’s 11th Congressional District in the United States House of Representatives. It sits in Staten Island and Brooklyn.

The district overlaps community boards Staten Island 3, Staten Island 2, Staten Island 1, Brooklyn 10 and Brooklyn 11.

It is policed by the 62nd, 68th, 120th, 121st, 122nd and 123rd Precincts.

It overlaps council districts 38, 43, 47, 49, 50 and 51, Assembly districts 46, 47, 49, 61, 62, 63 and 64 and Senate districts 17, 23, 24 and 26.

Members of the House serve two year terms, with no term limit.
