About

Assembly Member Maritza Davila represents State Assembly District 53. It sits in Brooklyn.

The district overlaps community boards Brooklyn 1 and Brooklyn 4.

It is policed by the 83rd, 90th and 94th Precincts.

It overlaps council districts 34 and 37 and Senate district 18.

Members of the State Assembly serve two year terms.
