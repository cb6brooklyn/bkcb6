About

Assembly Member Steven Raga represents State Assembly District 30. It sits in Queens.

The district overlaps community boards Queens 1, Queens 2, Queens 3, Queens 4 and Queens 5.

It is policed by the 104th, 108th, 110th, 114th and 115th Precincts.

It overlaps council districts 25, 26 and 30 and Senate districts 12 and 13.

Members of the State Assembly serve two year terms.
