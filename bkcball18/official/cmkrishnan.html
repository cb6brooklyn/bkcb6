About

Council Member Shekar Krishnan represents City Council District 25. It sits in Queens.

The district overlaps community boards Queens 1, Queens 3 and Queens 4.

It is policed by the 110th, 114th and 115th Precincts.

It overlaps Assembly districts 30, 34 and 39 and Senate districts 12 and 13.

First elected in 2021 and sworn in January 2022. Members of the City Council serve four year terms and are limited to two consecutive terms.

Next on the ballot in November 2029, when the seat will be open: Council Member Krishnan is term limited and cannot run again. For the election in front of us, see where you vote in November 2026 or what is coming up on the calendar.
