About

Assembly Member Claire Valdez represents State Assembly District 37. It sits in Queens.

The district overlaps community boards Queens 2 and Queens 5.

It is policed by the 104th and 108th Precincts.

It overlaps council districts 26, 30 and 34 and Senate districts 12 and 59.

Members of the State Assembly serve two year terms.
