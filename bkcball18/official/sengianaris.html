About

State Senator Michael Gianaris represents State Senate District 12. It sits in Queens.

The district overlaps community boards Queens 1, Queens 2, Queens 4 and Queens 5.

It is policed by the 104th, 108th, 110th and 114th Precincts.

It overlaps council districts 22, 25, 26 and 30 and Assembly districts 30, 34, 37, 38 and 39.

Members of the State Senate serve two year terms.
