About

Assembly Member Emily Gallagher represents State Assembly District 50. It sits in Brooklyn.

The district overlaps community board Brooklyn 1.

It is policed by the 90th and 94th Precincts.

It overlaps council districts 33 and 34 and Senate districts 18 and 59.

Members of the State Assembly serve two year terms.
