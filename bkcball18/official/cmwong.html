About

Council Member Phil Wong represents City Council District 30. It sits in Queens.

The district overlaps community boards Queens 4, Queens 5 and Queens 6.

It is policed by the 104th, 110th and 112th Precincts.

It overlaps Assembly districts 28, 30, 37, 38 and 39 and Senate districts 12 and 15.

First elected in 2025 and sworn in January 2026. Members of the City Council serve four year terms and are limited to two consecutive terms.

Next on the ballot in November 2029; Council Member Wong is in a first full term and may run again. For the election in front of us, see where you vote in November 2026 or what is coming up on the calendar.
