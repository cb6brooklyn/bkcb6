About

State Senator Roxanne J. Persaud represents State Senate District 19. It reaches into Brooklyn and Queens.

The district overlaps community boards Brooklyn 5, Brooklyn 16, Brooklyn 17, Brooklyn 18 and Queens 10.

It is policed by the 63rd, 67th, 69th, 73rd, 75th and 106th Precincts.

It overlaps council districts 32, 37, 41, 42 and 46 and Assembly districts 23, 54, 55, 58, 59 and 60.

Members of the State Senate serve two year terms.
