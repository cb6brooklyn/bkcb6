About

Representative Jerry Nadler represents New York’s 12th Congressional District in the United States House of Representatives. It sits in Manhattan.

The district overlaps community boards Manhattan 8, Manhattan 7, Manhattan 4, Manhattan 5, Manhattan 6 and Manhattan 11.

It is policed by the 10th, 13th, 14th, 17th, 18th, 19th, 20th, 22nd, 23rd, 24th and 114th Precincts.

It overlaps council districts 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 and 8, Assembly districts 67, 68, 69, 73, 74, 75 and 76 and Senate districts 28, 29, 30, 47 and 59.

Members of the House serve two year terms, with no term limit.
