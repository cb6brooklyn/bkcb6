About

Council Member Susan Zhuang represents City Council District 43. It sits in Brooklyn.

The district overlaps community boards Brooklyn 7, Brooklyn 10, Brooklyn 11 and Brooklyn 12.

It is policed by the 62nd, 66th, 68th and 72nd Precincts.

It overlaps Assembly districts 47 and 49 and Senate district 17.

First elected in 2023 and sworn in January 2024. Members of the City Council serve four year terms and are limited to two consecutive terms.

Next on the ballot in November 2029; Council Member Zhuang is in a first full term and may run again. For the election in front of us, see where you vote in November 2026 or what is coming up on the calendar.
