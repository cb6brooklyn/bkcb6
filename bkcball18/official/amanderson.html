About

Assembly Member Khaleel M. Anderson represents State Assembly District 31. It sits in Queens.

The district overlaps community boards Queens 10, Queens 13 and Queens 14.

It is policed by the 100th, 101st, 106th, 113th and 116th Precincts.

It overlaps council districts 28 and 31 and Senate district 10.

Members of the State Assembly serve two year terms.
