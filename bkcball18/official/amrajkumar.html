About

Assembly Member Jenifer Rajkumar represents State Assembly District 38. It sits in Queens.

The district overlaps community boards Queens 5 and Queens 9.

It is policed by the 102nd and 104th Precincts.

It overlaps council districts 29, 30, 32 and 34 and Senate districts 12, 15 and 18.

Members of the State Assembly serve two year terms.
