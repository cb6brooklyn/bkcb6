About

Assembly Member Kalman Yeger represents State Assembly District 41. It sits in Brooklyn.

The district overlaps community boards Brooklyn 14, Brooklyn 15, Brooklyn 17 and Brooklyn 18.

It is policed by the 61st, 63rd, 67th and 70th Precincts.

It overlaps council districts 45, 46 and 48 and Senate districts 21, 22 and 23.

Members of the State Assembly serve two year terms.
