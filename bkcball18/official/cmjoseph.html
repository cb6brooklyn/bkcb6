About

Council Member Rita Joseph represents City Council District 40. It sits in Brooklyn.

The district overlaps community boards Brooklyn 9, Brooklyn 12, Brooklyn 14 and Brooklyn 17.

It is policed by the 66th, 67th, 70th and 71st Precincts.

It overlaps Assembly districts 42, 43, 44, 48 and 58 and Senate districts 20, 21 and 22.

First elected in 2021 and sworn in January 2022. Members of the City Council serve four year terms and are limited to two consecutive terms.

Next on the ballot in November 2029, when the seat will be open: Council Member Joseph is term limited and cannot run again. For the election in front of us, see where you vote in November 2026 or what is coming up on the calendar.
