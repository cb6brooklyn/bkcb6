About

Assembly Member Tony Simone represents State Assembly District 75. It sits in Manhattan.

The district overlaps community boards Manhattan 4 and Manhattan 5.

It is policed by the 10th, 13th, 14th, 18th and 22nd Precincts.

It overlaps council districts 3, 4 and 6 and Senate districts 28 and 47.

Members of the State Assembly serve two year terms.
