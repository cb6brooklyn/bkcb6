About

Assembly Member Simcha Eichenstein represents State Assembly District 48. It sits in Brooklyn.

The district overlaps community boards Brooklyn 12 and Brooklyn 14.

It is policed by the 66th and 70th Precincts.

It overlaps council districts 39, 40 and 44 and Senate districts 21 and 22.

Members of the State Assembly serve two year terms.
