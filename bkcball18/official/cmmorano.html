About

Council Member Frank Morano represents City Council District 51. It sits in Staten Island.

The district overlaps community boards Staten Island 2 and Staten Island 3.

It is policed by the 121st, 122nd and 123rd Precincts.

It overlaps Assembly districts 62, 63 and 64 and Senate district 24.

Took office May 14, 2025. Members of the City Council serve four year terms and are limited to two consecutive terms.

Next on the ballot in November 2029; Council Member Morano is in a first full term and may run again. For the election in front of us, see where you vote in November 2026 or what is coming up on the calendar.
