About

Assembly Member Catalina Cruz represents State Assembly District 39. It sits in Queens.

The district overlaps community boards Queens 3, Queens 4, Queens 5 and Queens 6.

It is policed by the 104th, 110th, 112th and 115th Precincts.

It overlaps council districts 21, 25 and 30 and Senate districts 12, 13 and 15.

Members of the State Assembly serve two year terms.
