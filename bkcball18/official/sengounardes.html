About

State Senator Andrew Gounardes represents State Senate District 26. It sits in Brooklyn and takes in Governors Island.

The district overlaps community boards Brooklyn 2, Brooklyn 6, Brooklyn 7 and Brooklyn 10. Governors Island is filed under Manhattan Community Board 1.

It is policed by the 1st, 68th, 72nd, 76th, 78th, 84th and 88th Precincts.

It overlaps council districts 1, 33, 38, 39, 47 and 50 and Assembly districts 46, 49, 51, 52, 61 and 64.

First elected in 2018 and sworn in January 2019. Members of the State Senate serve two year terms.
