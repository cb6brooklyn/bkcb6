About

Assembly Member William Colton represents State Assembly District 47. It sits in Brooklyn.

The district overlaps community boards Brooklyn 11 and Brooklyn 13.

It is policed by the 60th and 62nd Precincts.

It overlaps council districts 38, 43, 44, 47 and 50 and Senate district 17.

Members of the State Assembly serve two year terms.
