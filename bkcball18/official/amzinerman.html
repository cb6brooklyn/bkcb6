About

Assembly Member Stefani Zinerman represents State Assembly District 56. It sits in Brooklyn.

The district overlaps community boards Brooklyn 3 and Brooklyn 8.

It is policed by the 77th, 79th and 81st Precincts.

It overlaps council districts 36 and 41 and Senate districts 20 and 25.

Members of the State Assembly serve two year terms.
