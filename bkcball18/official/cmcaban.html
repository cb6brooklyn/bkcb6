About

Council Member Tiffany Cabán represents City Council District 22. It sits in Queens.

The district overlaps community boards Queens 1 and Queens 3.

It is policed by the 114th and 115th Precincts.

It overlaps Assembly districts 34, 35, 36 and 85 and Senate districts 11, 12, 13 and 59.

Took office December 1, 2021. Members of the City Council serve four year terms and are limited to two consecutive terms.

Next on the ballot in November 2029, when the seat will be open: Council Member Cabán is term limited and cannot run again. For the election in front of us, see where you vote in November 2026 or what is coming up on the calendar.
