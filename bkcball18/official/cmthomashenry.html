About

Council Member Shanel Thomas-Henry represents City Council District 21. It sits in Queens.

The district overlaps community boards Queens 3 and Queens 4.

It is policed by the 110th and 115th Precincts.

It overlaps Assembly districts 27, 34, 35 and 39 and Senate districts 11, 13 and 16.

First elected in 2025 and sworn in January 2026. Members of the City Council serve four year terms and are limited to two consecutive terms.

Next on the ballot in November 2029; Council Member Thomas-Henry is in a first full term and may run again. For the election in front of us, see where you vote in November 2026 or what is coming up on the calendar.
