About

Council Member James Gennaro represents City Council District 24. It sits in Queens.

The district overlaps community boards Queens 6 and Queens 8.

It is policed by the 107th, 110th and 112th Precincts.

It overlaps Assembly districts 24, 25, 27 and 35 and Senate districts 11, 14 and 16.

First elected in 2001 and sworn in January 2002. Members of the City Council serve four year terms and are limited to two consecutive terms.

Next on the ballot in November 2029, when the seat will be open: Council Member Gennaro is term limited and cannot run again. For the election in front of us, see where you vote in November 2026 or what is coming up on the calendar.
