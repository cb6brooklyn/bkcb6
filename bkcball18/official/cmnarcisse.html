About

Council Member Mercedes Narcisse represents City Council District 46. It sits in Brooklyn.

The district overlaps community boards Brooklyn 15 and Brooklyn 18.

It is policed by the 61st, 63rd and 69th Precincts.

It overlaps Assembly districts 41, 58 and 59 and Senate districts 19, 21 and 22.

First elected in 2021 and sworn in January 2022. Members of the City Council serve four year terms and are limited to two consecutive terms.

Next on the ballot in November 2029, when the seat will be open: Council Member Narcisse is term limited and cannot run again. For the election in front of us, see where you vote in November 2026 or what is coming up on the calendar.
