About

Assembly Member Sam Pirozzolo represents State Assembly District 63. It sits in Staten Island.

The district overlaps community boards Staten Island 1 and Staten Island 2.

It is policed by the 120th, 121st and 122nd Precincts.

It overlaps council districts 49, 50 and 51 and Senate districts 23 and 24.

Members of the State Assembly serve two year terms.
