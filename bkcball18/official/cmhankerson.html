About

Council Member Ty Hankerson represents City Council District 28. It sits in Queens.

The district overlaps community boards Queens 10 and Queens 12.

It is policed by the 103rd, 106th and 113th Precincts.

It overlaps Assembly districts 23, 24, 31 and 32 and Senate districts 10, 14 and 15.

First elected in 2025 and sworn in January 2026. Members of the City Council serve four year terms and are limited to two consecutive terms.

Next on the ballot in November 2029; Council Member Hankerson is in a first full term and may run again. For the election in front of us, see where you vote in November 2026 or what is coming up on the calendar.
