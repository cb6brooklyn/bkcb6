About

State Senator Joseph P. Addabbo Jr. represents State Senate District 15. It sits in Queens.

The district overlaps community boards Queens 5, Queens 6, Queens 9 and Queens 10.

It is policed by the 102nd, 104th, 106th and 112th Precincts.

It overlaps council districts 28, 29, 30 and 32 and Assembly districts 23, 24, 28, 38 and 39.

Members of the State Senate serve two year terms.
