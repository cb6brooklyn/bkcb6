How to read this

Parking tickets carry no coordinates, so the city publishes them by precinct only. These figures are for the whole precinct and cannot be split by community district or patrol sector. Camera violations such as school zone speed and bus lane are issued without a precinct and are not counted here. Ticket counts come from the Fiscal Year 2026 file. Dollar figures come from the Open Parking and Camera Violations file and cover every year on record.

Counts are reported NYPD complaints, arrests, and shooting incidents from NYC Open Data, each placed by its own coordinates inside the precinct boundary. A complaint is a report, not a conviction. Counts are raw and not adjusted for population. Built by Mike Racioppo, District Manager, Brooklyn Community Board 6. All 78 precincts →
