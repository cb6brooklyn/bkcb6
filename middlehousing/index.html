Middle Housing Gone Missing: Mediations of Neighborhood Change in Brooklyn Community District 6, 2003-2024

Rebecca J. Kobert · Pratt Institute, M.S. Urban and Community Planning · May 2025

Rebecca Kobert wrote Middle Housing Gone Missing in collaboration with CB6, where she was the Land Use Coordinator. It studies how zoning and preservation in Brooklyn Community District 6 shaped housing supply from 2003 to 2024, and how the district loses homes when rowhouses with several apartments are consolidated into fewer, larger ones.
