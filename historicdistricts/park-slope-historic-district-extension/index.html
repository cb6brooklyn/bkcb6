Park Slope Historic District Extension

The district boundary, every business listing and every property inside it.

Boundary: the Landmarks Preservation Commission's map of the Park Slope Historic District Extension (LP-02443), designated April 17, 2012. Businesses: the bkcb6.app business directory, which draws on New York State food store and liquor license records and OpenStreetMap. Properties: every tax lot inside the boundary, from the Department of City Planning's PLUTO file.
