NEW YORK CITY LANDMARKS PRESERVATION COMMISSION

Historic districts in Brooklyn Community Board 6

The map, the numbers and every district.

Brooklyn Community Board 6 has 3 historic districts. The first was Cobble Hill, designated December 30, 1969. Park Slope followed on July 17, 1973, then Carroll Gardens on September 25, 1973.

Two of the three were later extended. The Cobble Hill Historic District Extension was designated June 7, 1988. Park Slope was extended twice: the Park Slope Historic District Extension on April 17, 2012 and Extension II on April 12, 2016. Carroll Gardens has no extension.

That makes six designations in all. Together they hold 3,847 properties and 14,764 homes, and the Landmarks Preservation Commission has 16,973 permit records on them, from 1960 to 2026.
