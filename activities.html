Where this data comes from

Locations are drawn from four NYC Parks datasets on NYC Open Data: Athletic Facilities, Pools, SkateParks, and Dog Runs. Each record is placed at the center of its mapped footprint and matched to the community district it sits in.

Lap Swimming covers Olympic, Intermediate, and Large pools. Wading and mini pools are listed separately. Pools are marked indoor or outdoor, which matters for anything outside the summer season.

This covers property run by NYC Parks. School gyms, private clubs, and YMCA facilities are not in these datasets. Parks data does not carry closure status, so a location listed here may be shut for construction or seasonal maintenance. Check with NYC Parks before you go.
