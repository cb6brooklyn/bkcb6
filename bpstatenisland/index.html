Vito Fossella

Borough President of Staten Island

In brief

Vito Fossella is the Borough President of Staten Island, elected by every voter in the borough.

The office appoints the members of all 3 community boards in Staten Island together with the council members, reviews every land use application that comes through the borough, and allocates borough capital funding.

First elected in 2021 and sworn in January 2022. Citywide and borough offices carry four year terms.
