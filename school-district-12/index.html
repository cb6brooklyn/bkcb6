Community School District 12

NYC Public Schools · Bronx

Community School District 12 is one of the 32 community school districts of NYC Public Schools, for the public elementary and middle schools in its area. It reaches into BXCB9 (38%), BXCB3 (30%), BXCB6 (25%), BXCB2 (8%).
