bkcb6.app › Brooklyn › Hardware Stores

Every hardware store in Brooklyn, mapped and listed

Tap a pin or a row for the address. Each address was checked against the City Planning address file and assigned to its community district by location.

Addresses confirmed against the NYC Department of City Planning Property Address Directory via NYC Planning Labs GeoSearch. Community district assigned by point-in-polygon against DCP community district boundaries. Logos are each store's own mark, or the mark of the hardware co-op or brand in its name, taken from that organization's website; stores without a published mark show an initial. Missing a store or see an error? Mike@bkcb6.org.
