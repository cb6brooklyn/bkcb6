Thomas P. DiNapoli

Comptroller of the State of New York

Thomas P. DiNapoli is the State Comptroller, elected statewide.

The state’s chief fiscal officer. Audits state and local governments, and is the sole trustee of the state pension fund.

Appointed Comptroller by the Legislature in February 2007 and first elected in 2010. Statewide offices carry four year terms, with no term limit.

Next on the ballot in November 2026, after winning the June 2026 Democratic primary. Find your poll site and sample ballot there, or see what is coming up on the calendar.
