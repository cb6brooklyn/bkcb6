What every part of the city is used for, and how it is zoned
Two different questions. Land use is what is on the ground today. Zoning is what the rules allow. They are often not the same, and where they differ is usually where the argument is. Pick a category and the map shades every area by how much of its lot area that category covers. Darker means more. No area is entirely one thing, so the number matters more than the colour.
