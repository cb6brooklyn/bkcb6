Land Use & Zoning Map

What every part of the city is used for, and how it is zoned
Two different questions. Land use is what is on the ground today. Zoning is what the rules allow. They are often not the same, and where they differ is usually where the argument is. The map draws every zoning district and every lot in its own colour, straight from the city records. Outlines show community boards, council districts or boroughs on top. Tap any outline to highlight it and it appears in the comparison below. Highlight as many as you want, from any level. Search an address or long press the map to drop a pin, and use the link button to share exactly what you are looking at.
