The posted speed limit on every mapped street segment in New York City, 141,440 segments across the five boroughs, from NYC DOT's Vision Zero speed limit data. Since November 7, 2014 the citywide default has been 25 mph unless a street is signed otherwise; DOT marks each segment as signed or unsigned. Turn districts on and off below, search an address, or enter a street and cross streets. Every community district is labeled and colored on the map. The City Council, State Senate, State Assembly and Congressional districts that overlap the selected districts are listed as toggles; tap one to draw it. Tap any segment for the street, its speed limit, whether it is signed, and the district.

DOT advises that speed limits are subject to temporary or permanent change and that posted signage governs. Segments shown as "No value" carry a 0 in DOT's data; DOT's data dictionary does not define that value.
Data: NYC DOT VZV_Speed Limits via NYC Open Data (rows updated September 1, 2026, downloaded September 11, 2026), 141,444 rows, 141,440 with geometry, assigned to community districts by segment midpoint. District boundaries: NYC DCP. Built by Brooklyn Community Board 6 for bkcb6.app.
