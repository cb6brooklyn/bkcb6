Eric Gonzalez

District Attorney of Kings County (Brooklyn)

Eric Gonzalez is the District Attorney of Brooklyn, elected by every voter in Brooklyn.

Prosecutes crimes committed in Brooklyn. A state office under the County Law, filled by the voters of the county.

Became acting District Attorney in 2016, was first elected in 2017, and was re-elected in 2021 and 2025. District attorneys serve four year terms, with no term limit.
