Zellnor Myrie

State Senator · State Senate District 20

In brief

State Senator Zellnor Myrie represents State Senate District 20. It sits in Brooklyn.

The district overlaps community boards Brooklyn 6, Brooklyn 7, Brooklyn 8, Brooklyn 9 and Brooklyn 17.

It is policed by the 67th, 71st, 72nd, 77th and 78th Precincts.

It overlaps council districts 35, 36, 39, 40 and 41 and Assembly districts 43, 44, 52, 55, 56, 57 and 58.

First elected in 2018 and sworn in January 2019. Members of the State Senate serve two year terms.
