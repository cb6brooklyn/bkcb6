What Cobble Hill Association does

The CHA organizes stewardship days in Cobble Hill Park and Van Voorhees Playground, summer concerts, and neighborhood history writing by residents.

The CHA follows land use and waterfront issues including the Brooklyn Marine Terminal redevelopment.
