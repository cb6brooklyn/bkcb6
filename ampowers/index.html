Keith Powers

Assembly Member · State Assembly District 74

In brief

Assembly Member Keith Powers represents State Assembly District 74. It sits in Manhattan.

The district overlaps community boards Manhattan 3, Manhattan 5 and Manhattan 6.

It is policed by the 7th, 9th, 13th and 17th Precincts.

It overlaps council districts 2 and 4 and Senate districts 27, 28 and 59.

Members of the State Assembly serve two year terms.
