Privacy Policy

CB6 & Beyond — Brooklyn Community Board 6

Data Collection

CB6 & Beyond does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information from its users. The app does not use analytics, advertising trackers, or third-party tracking tools.

Information You Enter

When you use features such as the address search or land use lookup, the address you enter is sent to the NYC Geoclient API, an official New York City government service, in order to return geographic information about that address. No user identifier is attached to these requests, and the data is not retained by the app.

Map Data

Interactive maps are rendered using OpenStreetMap and CARTO basemap tiles, which may log standard server request information (such as tile coordinates and approximate location). These are governed by their own privacy policies. No personal data is sent.

External Links

The app contains links to official government sites including nyc.gov, NYC Open Data, NY State Senate, and the New York City Council. Once you follow an external link, you are subject to that site's privacy practices.

Children

This app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact Mike Racioppo, District Manager, Brooklyn Community Board 6, at Mike@bkcb6.org.
