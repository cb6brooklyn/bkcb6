Privacy Policy

BKCB6 and CBNYC for Android

This policy covers the Android apps BKCB6 (com.mikeracioppo.BKCB6App) and CBNYC (com.mikeracioppo.CB6andBeyond), published on Google Play by Michael Racioppo. Both are independent apps. Neither is an official app of any community board, the City of New York or any government agency.

Summary

The apps have no accounts, no sign-in and no advertising. They do not ask for your name, email address or phone number, and nothing is sold. Some information leaves your phone so that the apps can work, as described below.

Page visits

Some screens use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. Cloudflare Web Analytics uses no cookies, does not follow you across other sites or apps and does not build a profile of you. It reports totals only, such as how many times a screen was opened and the general type of device.

Addresses you type

When you search for an address, a street or a place, what you type is sent to New York City's address services (NYC Planning Labs GeoSearch and the NYC Geoclient API) so they can return the location and its districts. No account or user identifier is attached to these requests, and the apps do not keep a record of what you searched.

Location (optional)

The apps ask Android for permission to use your location only when you tap a feature that needs it, such as Find My Community Board, a "use my location" button or a map's locate button. You can say no, and you can turn the permission off at any time in your phone's Settings. Everything else in the apps works without it.

Find My Community Board works out your community board on your phone; your location is not sent anywhere. When you ask for the block you are standing on, your latitude and longitude are sent to NYC Planning Labs GeoSearch so it can return the nearest address. Your location is used for that one request. The apps do not store it, and it is not sent to the developer.

What the apps load from the internet

Much of each app is stored on your phone. Pages, maps and data that are not stored on the phone are loaded from bkcb6.app, and public data is loaded from government sources including NYC Open Data and the NYC Department of City Planning. Maps are drawn with Apple Maps and with CARTO and OpenStreetMap map tiles, and fonts and map software are loaded from Google Fonts and public code libraries (unpkg and cdnjs). Like any website, these services receive your phone's IP address and the page or map area being requested, and they handle that under their own privacy policies.

Stored on your phone

The apps keep their pages and a few settings on your phone so that they open quickly and work without a connection. Uninstalling an app removes them.

Sharing

The developer does not receive, sell or share personal information about you. The only information that leaves your phone is what is described above, and it goes directly to the services named.

Deleting data

The apps have no accounts and the developer holds no personal data about you, so there is nothing for the developer to delete. To clear what is stored on your phone, uninstall the app or clear its storage in your phone's Settings.

External links

The apps link to official sites including nyc.gov, NYC Open Data, the New York City Council and the New York State Senate and Assembly. Once you follow a link you are subject to that site's privacy practices.

Children

The apps are suitable for all ages and do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.
