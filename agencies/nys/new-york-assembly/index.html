New York Assembly

New York State · Statewide Elected Officials

In brief

New York Assembly is a New York State agency, listed by ny.gov under Statewide Elected Officials.

What it does

The New York State Assembly is the lower house of the State Legislature. It is composed of 150 members, each representing a district with an average population of 128,652.
