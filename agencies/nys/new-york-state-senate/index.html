New York State Senate

New York State · Statewide Elected Officials

In brief

New York State Senate is a New York State agency, listed by ny.gov under Statewide Elected Officials.

What it does

The New York State Senate is the upper house in the State Legislature. It currently has 63 members each elected to two-year terms without term limits.
