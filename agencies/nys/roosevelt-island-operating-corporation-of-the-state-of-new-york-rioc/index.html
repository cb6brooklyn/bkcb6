Roosevelt Island Operating Corporation of the State of New York (RIOC)

New York State · Recreation and Environment

Roosevelt Island Operating Corporation of the State of New York (RIOC) is a New York State agency, listed by ny.gov under Recreation and Environment.

What it does

RIOC is a public-benefit corporation responsible for developing Roosevelt Island, a small strip of land in the East River, part of the borough of Manhattan.
