About

Council Member Carl Wilson represents City Council District 3. It sits in Manhattan.

The district overlaps community boards Manhattan 2, Manhattan 4 and Manhattan 5.

It is policed by the 6th, 10th, 13th, 14th and 18th Precincts.

It overlaps Assembly districts 66, 67 and 75 and Senate districts 27, 28 and 47.

Elected in a special election to fill the seat after Erik Bottcher moved to the State Senate. Members of the City Council serve four year terms and are limited to two consecutive terms.

Next on the ballot in November 2029; Council Member Wilson is serving the remainder of a term and may run again. For the election in front of us, see where you vote in November 2026 or what is coming up on the calendar.
