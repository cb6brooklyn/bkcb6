About

Council Member Shahana Hanif represents City Council District 39. It sits in Brooklyn.

The district overlaps community boards Brooklyn 6, Brooklyn 7 and Brooklyn 12.

It is policed by the 66th, 72nd, 76th and 78th Precincts.

It overlaps Assembly districts 44, 51 and 52 and Senate districts 17, 20, 21, 22 and 26.

First elected in 2021 and sworn in January 2022. Members of the City Council serve four year terms and are limited to two consecutive terms.

Next on the ballot in November 2029, when the seat will be open: Council Member Hanif is term limited and cannot run again. For the election in front of us, see where you vote in November 2026 or what is coming up on the calendar.
