About

Council Member Lincoln Restler represents City Council District 33. It sits in Brooklyn.

The district overlaps community boards Brooklyn 1, Brooklyn 2 and Brooklyn 3.

It is policed by the 79th, 84th, 88th, 90th and 94th Precincts.

It overlaps Assembly districts 50, 52 and 57 and Senate districts 18, 25, 26 and 59.

First elected in 2021 and sworn in January 2022. Members of the City Council serve four year terms and are limited to two consecutive terms.

Next on the ballot in November 2029, when the seat will be open: Council Member Restler is term limited and cannot run again. For the election in front of us, see where you vote in November 2026 or what is coming up on the calendar.
