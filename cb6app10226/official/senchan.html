About

State Senator Stephen T. Chan represents State Senate District 17. It sits in Brooklyn.

The district overlaps community boards Brooklyn 7, Brooklyn 10, Brooklyn 11 and Brooklyn 12.

It is policed by the 62nd, 66th, 68th and 72nd Precincts.

It overlaps council districts 38, 43, 44 and 47 and Assembly districts 47, 49 and 51.

Members of the State Senate serve two year terms.
