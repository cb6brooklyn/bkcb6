About

Assembly Member Jo Anne Simon represents State Assembly District 52. It sits in Brooklyn.

The district overlaps community boards Brooklyn 2 and Brooklyn 6.

It is policed by the 76th, 78th, 84th and 88th Precincts.

It overlaps council districts 33, 35 and 39 and Senate districts 20, 25 and 26.

First elected in 2014 and sworn in January 2015. Members of the State Assembly serve two year terms.
