About

Assembly Member Phara Souffrant Forrest represents State Assembly District 57. It sits in Brooklyn.

The district overlaps community boards Brooklyn 2, Brooklyn 3, Brooklyn 8 and Brooklyn 9.

It is policed by the 71st, 77th, 79th and 88th Precincts.

It overlaps council districts 33, 35 and 36 and Senate districts 20, 25 and 26.

Members of the State Assembly serve two year terms.
