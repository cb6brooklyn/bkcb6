About

Council Member Chris Banks represents City Council District 42. It sits in Brooklyn.

The district overlaps community boards Brooklyn 5, Brooklyn 16, Brooklyn 17 and Brooklyn 18.

It is policed by the 63rd, 67th, 69th, 73rd and 75th Precincts.

It overlaps Assembly districts 54, 58, 59 and 60 and Senate district 19.

First elected in 2023 and sworn in January 2024. Members of the City Council serve four year terms and are limited to two consecutive terms.

Next on the ballot in November 2029; Council Member Banks is in a first full term and may run again. For the election in front of us, see where you vote in November 2026 or what is coming up on the calendar.
