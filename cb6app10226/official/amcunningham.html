About

Assembly Member Brian Cunningham represents State Assembly District 43. It sits in Brooklyn.

The district overlaps community boards Brooklyn 8, Brooklyn 9 and Brooklyn 17.

It is policed by the 67th, 71st and 77th Precincts.

It overlaps council districts 35, 36, 40 and 41 and Senate district 20.

Members of the State Assembly serve two year terms.
