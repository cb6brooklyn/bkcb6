About

Council Member Kayla Santosuosso represents City Council District 47. It sits in Brooklyn.

The district overlaps community boards Brooklyn 10, Brooklyn 11 and Brooklyn 13.

It is policed by the 60th, 62nd and 68th Precincts.

It overlaps Assembly districts 45, 46, 47 and 64 and Senate districts 17, 23 and 26.

First elected in 2025 and sworn in January 2026. Members of the City Council serve four year terms and are limited to two consecutive terms.

Next on the ballot in November 2029; Council Member Santosuosso is in a first full term and may run again. For the election in front of us, see where you vote in November 2026 or what is coming up on the calendar.
