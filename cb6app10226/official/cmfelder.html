About

Council Member Simcha Felder represents City Council District 44. It sits in Brooklyn.

The district overlaps community boards Brooklyn 11, Brooklyn 12, Brooklyn 14 and Brooklyn 15.

It is policed by the 61st, 62nd, 66th and 70th Precincts.

It overlaps Assembly districts 45, 47 and 48 and Senate districts 17 and 22.

First elected in 2001 and sworn in January 2002. Members of the City Council serve four year terms and are limited to two consecutive terms.

Next on the ballot in November 2029; Council Member Felder is in a first full term and may run again. For the election in front of us, see where you vote in November 2026 or what is coming up on the calendar.
