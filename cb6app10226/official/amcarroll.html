About

Assembly Member Robert C. Carroll represents State Assembly District 44. It sits in Brooklyn.

The district overlaps community boards Brooklyn 6, Brooklyn 7, Brooklyn 8, Brooklyn 12 and Brooklyn 14.

It is policed by the 66th, 70th, 72nd, 77th and 78th Precincts.

It overlaps council districts 35, 39 and 40 and Senate districts 17, 20, 21, 22, 25 and 26.

First elected in 2016 and sworn in January 2017. Members of the State Assembly serve two year terms.
