Election District 58, Assembly District 52

Election District 58, Assembly District 52 is in Brooklyn Community Board 2 (99% of its area) and Brooklyn Community Board 6 (1% of its area). Its voters are represented by Jo Anne Simon in the Assembly, Andrew Gounardes in the State Senate, Dan Goldman in Congress and Lincoln Restler on the City Council. On Election Day they vote at PS 38, Enter on Dean Street Gate to Exit.
