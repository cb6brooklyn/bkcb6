Election District 15, Assembly District 69

Election District 15, Assembly District 69 is in Manhattan Community Board 7 (98% of its area) and Manhattan Community Board 9 (2% of its area). Its voters are represented by Micah C. Lasher in the Assembly, Cordell Cleare in the State Senate, Adriano Espaillat in Congress and Shaun Abreu on the City Council. On Election Day they vote at UTACPT, 125 West 109 Street.
