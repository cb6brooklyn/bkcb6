Election District 35, Assembly District 62

Election District 35, Assembly District 62 is in Staten Island Community Board 3. Its voters are represented by Michael Reilly in the Assembly, Andrew J. Lanza in the State Senate, Nicole Malliotakis in Congress and Frank Morano on the City Council. On Election Day they vote at The Tides at Charleston, Double Doors 15 Tides Lane.
