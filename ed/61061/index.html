Election District 61, Assembly District 61

Election District 61, Assembly District 61 is in Manhattan Community Board 1. Its voters are represented by Charles D. Fall in the Assembly, Brian Kavanagh in the State Senate, Dan Goldman in Congress and Christopher Marte on the City Council. On Election Day they vote at PS 276, Exit 3 - Second Pl. (Bet. Battery Pl & Little West St).
