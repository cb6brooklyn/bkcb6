Election District 3, Assembly District 74

Election District 3, Assembly District 74 is in Manhattan Community Board 6. Its voters are represented by Keith Powers in the Assembly, Kristen Gonzalez in the State Senate, Jerry Nadler in Congress and Virginia Maloney on the City Council. On Election Day they vote at The River School/PS 281, 425 East 35 Street.
