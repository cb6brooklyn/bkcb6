Election District 7, Assembly District 42

Election District 7, Assembly District 42 is in Brooklyn Community Board 14. Its voters are represented by Rodneyse Bichotte Hermelyn in the Assembly, Kevin S. Parker in the State Senate, Yvette Clarke in Congress and Rita Joseph on the City Council. On Election Day they vote at PS 249, Enter on Caton Avenue through gate.
