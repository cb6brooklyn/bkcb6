Election District 61, Assembly District 64

Election District 61, Assembly District 64 is in Staten Island Community Board 1. Its voters are represented by Michael Tannousis in the Assembly, Jessica Scarcella-Spanton in the State Senate, Nicole Malliotakis in Congress and Kamillah Hanks on the City Council. On Election Day they vote at Saint Christophers Church, 136 Midland Avenue.
