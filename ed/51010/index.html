Election District 10, Assembly District 51

Election District 10, Assembly District 51 is in Brooklyn Community Board 7. Its voters are represented by Marcela Mitaynes in the Assembly, Stephen T. Chan in the State Senate, Dan Goldman in Congress and Alexa Avilés on the City Council. On Election Day they vote at Our Lady of Perpetual Help, 552 59th Street.
