Election District 13, Assembly District 31

Election District 13, Assembly District 31 is in Queens Community Board 14. Its voters are represented by Khaleel M. Anderson in the Assembly, James Sanders Jr. in the State Senate, Gregory Meeks in Congress and Selvena Brooks-Powers on the City Council. On Election Day they vote at RISE Rockaway, 58-03 Rockaway Beach Boulevard.
