Election District 22, Assembly District 38

Election District 22, Assembly District 38 is in Queens Community Board 9. Its voters are represented by Jenifer Rajkumar in the Assembly, Joseph P. Addabbo Jr. in the State Senate, Gregory Meeks in Congress and Lynn Schulman on the City Council. On Election Day they vote at PS 254-Rosa Parks School, 84-40 101 Street.
