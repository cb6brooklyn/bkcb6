Election District 34, Assembly District 52

Election District 34, Assembly District 52 is in Brooklyn Community Board 6. Its voters are represented by Jo Anne Simon in the Assembly, Andrew Gounardes in the State Senate, Dan Goldman in Congress and Shahana Hanif on the City Council. On Election Day they vote at PS 29, 425 Henry Street.
