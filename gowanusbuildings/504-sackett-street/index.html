The building

504 Sackett Street is a new building in the Gowanus rezoning area, filed with the Department of Buildings on November 30, 2021: 173 apartments, 45 of them affordable, 232,797 square feet of construction. It is opened with a certificate of occupancy on February 26, 2025.
