The building

323 Bond Street (Westmark) is a new building in the Gowanus rezoning area, filed with the Department of Buildings on February 10, 2022: 179 apartments, 153 of them affordable, 179,976 square feet of construction. It is opened with a certificate of occupancy on July 1, 2026.
