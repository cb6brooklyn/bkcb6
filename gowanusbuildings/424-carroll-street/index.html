The building

424 Carroll Street (420 Carroll) is a new building in the Gowanus rezoning area, filed with the Department of Buildings on February 15, 2022: 360 apartments, 90 of them affordable, 386,521 square feet of construction. It is opened with a certificate of occupancy on November 26, 2024.
