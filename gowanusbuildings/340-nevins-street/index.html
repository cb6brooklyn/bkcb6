The building

340 Nevins Street (Nevins Landing South (Gowanus Wharf)) is a new building in the Gowanus rezoning area, filed with the Department of Buildings on November 24, 2021: 320 apartments, 80 of them affordable, 322,258 square feet of construction. It is completed under a DOB letter of completion.
