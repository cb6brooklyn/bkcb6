Smith Street Stage

Theater company · Carroll Gardens, Brooklyn

Smith Street Stage is an actor led company that explores the works of Shakespeare for their examination of character, human conflict and power. The company seeks to tell classic stories in new ways through affordable, exciting and consequential theater.

Smith Street Stage produces Shakespeare in Carroll Park, free Shakespeare in the park for audiences of all ages.
