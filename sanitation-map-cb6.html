CB6 Collection Schedule Map

DSNY collection days for every block in Brooklyn Community Board 6. The city divides the district into sanitation sections, and every section carries its own set of days for refuse, recycling, organics, and bulk. Click any area on the map to see its full schedule. Set out material after 6 PM the night before the collection day.

Source: NYC Department of Sanitation, DSNY Sections and Frequencies, via NYC Open Data. Section boundaries follow block faces, so a single street can be split between two schedules. Holiday suspensions and weather delays are not reflected here. Confirm at nyc.gov/dsny or by calling 311.
