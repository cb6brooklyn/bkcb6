New York Society for the Prevention of Cruelty to Children

Legal Services & Justice · Manhattan

In brief

New York Society for the Prevention of Cruelty to Children is a community-based organization in Manhattan: legal services and justice.

In its own words, to the IRSFounded in 1874. The New York society for the prevention of cruelty to children (nyspcc) is the first child protective agency in the country. The nyspcc responds to the complex needs of abused and neglected children, and those involved in their care, by providing best practice counseling, and educational services.
