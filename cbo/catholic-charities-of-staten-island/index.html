Catholic Charities of Staten Island

Human & Social Services · Staten Island

In brief

Catholic Charities of Staten Island is a community-based organization in Staten Island: human and social services.

In its own words, to the IRSCatholic charities of staten island (formerly known as mission of the immaculate virgin for the protection of homeless and destitute children) is a not-for-profit agency of the archdiocese of New York. Founded in 1871 by father john c. Drumgoole, it has always responded with services for those in need.
