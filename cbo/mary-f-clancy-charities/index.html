Mary F. Clancy Charities

Philanthropy & Foundations · Bronx

In brief

Mary F. Clancy Charities is a philanthropy and foundations organization in Bronx that the New York City Council has funded.

In its own words, to the IRSThe organization provides grants to other tax-exempt organizations that battle against family, women, and child abuse. The organization also provides scholarships to inner city catholic schools for underprivileged children to be able to attend those schools.
