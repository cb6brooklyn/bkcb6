Asian American Growth and Development Center

Human & Social Services · Queens

In brief

Asian American Growth and Development Center is a community-based organization in Queens: human and social services.

In its own words, to the IRSThe company was initiated by henan immigrants currently living in the US. Members include henan natives, the second and third generations from both sides of the taiwan strait, and henanese from hong kong. The purpose of the association is to actively help new and old immigrants to quickly integrate into the mainstream society of the US, to strengthen mutual understanding between various groups of overseas chinese in the US and to provide communication, recreation, career services and development opportunities for community residents and newcomers.
