Center for Alternative Sentencing and Employment Services

Employment & Workforce · Brooklyn

In brief

Center for Alternative Sentencing and Employment Services is a community-based organization in Brooklyn: employment and workforce.

In its own words, to the IRSCases' mission is to increase public safety through innovative services that reduce crime and incarceration, improve behavioral health, promote recovery and rehabilitation, and create opportunities for success in the community. We believe in a New York city in which a person's previous criminal involvement or behavioral health needs do not limit the opportunity to engage in the support and treatment services they need to succeed.
