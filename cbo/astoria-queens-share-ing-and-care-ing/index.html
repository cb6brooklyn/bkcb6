Astoria/Queens Share-ing and Care-ing

Health & Mental Health · Queens

In brief

Astoria/Queens Share-ing and Care-ing is a health and mental health organization in Queens that the New York City Council has funded.

In its own words, to the IRSOur mission is to improve the lives of those living with breast, ovarian, colon and other forms of cancer through the provision of direct and indirect services, as well as counseling support.(continued on schedule o).we strive to reduce fear and eliminate cultural barrier through educational outreach in order to promote early detection and treatment as well as to improve access to life-saving services such as mammograms especially in uninsured mono-lingual immigrant communities.
