Fulton Market Association

Community & Economic Development · Brooklyn

In brief

Fulton Market Association is a community-based organization in Brooklyn: community and economic development.

In its own words, to the IRSFulton market association operates the fulton stall market as a farmers public market for local farm and food products connecting family farmers and small-scale food producers in New York and adjacent states with lower manhattan residents and workers to enable local farmers and producers to sustain their farm and food enterprises through direct marketing and meet consumers need for high-quality fresh and affordable food. We seek to provide consumers with enhanced access to fresh, locally grown, high quality seasonal produce and locally processed small-batch food products in a multi-vendor public market that also educates the community about local farmers and food producers, the diversity, quality and nutritional value of local food products, and the economic and environmental importance of local agriculture & strengthening our regional food system.
