Hear Your Song

Health & Mental Health · Manhattan

In brief

Hear Your Song is a health and mental health organization in Manhattan that the New York City Council has funded.

In its own words, to the IRSHear Your Song empowers children and teens living with serious illnesses and complex health needs to make their voices heard through collaborative songwriting. At the heart of our work is a "kid-driven," totally individualized process that puts every child and teen in our community - youth who live with physical and mental health diagnoses that so often deprive them of power and choice - in complete creative control. Hear Your Song supports youth songwriters through partnerships with children's hospitals and nursing homes, summer camps, schools, and diagnosis-specific organizations.
