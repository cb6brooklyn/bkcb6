The TechLINC Project

Youth Development · Bronx

In brief

The TechLINC Project is a community-based organization in Bronx: youth development.

In its own words, to the IRSOur purpose is to provide computer technology and solutions, through accessibility and availability, for at-risk and economically disadvantaged persons (students, people with disabilities, and the elderly). Our mission is to assist in eliminating the technological information digital divide in minority-based and at-risk communities by providing capacity building strategies for at-risk youth in metropolitan areas through support services, training, technical assistance, and program monitoring in relationship to technology advancement.
