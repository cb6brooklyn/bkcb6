Theater Breaking Through Barriers

Health & Mental Health · Manhattan

In brief

Theater Breaking Through Barriers is a health and mental health organization in Manhattan that the New York City Council has funded.

In its own words, to the IRSTheater Breaking Through Barriers (TBTB) is NYCs only Off-Broadway theater organization dedicated to advancing artists and developing audiences of people with disabilities, and altering the misperceptions surrounding disability by proving, once and for all, that disability does not affect the quality or integrity of our art or artists. We believe that through artistic excellence and the development of role models, we can claim at last for the full inclusion of people with disabilities in our society.
