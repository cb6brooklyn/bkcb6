Federation of Italian American Organizations of Brooklyn

Religious Organizations · Brooklyn

In brief

Federation of Italian American Organizations of Brooklyn is a religious organizations organization in Brooklyn that the New York City Council has funded.

In its own words, to the IRSThe federation, a not-for-profit charitable community service organization, has been a leader in bridging the rich diversity of our multi-cultural communities by delivering free programs and services to help improve the quality of life for all children, youth, families and seniors. "il cintro" is the first italian-american multicultural community center in New York. We are located in southern brooklyn, home to italian-americans since the early 1900s. Il cintro serves as a resource for the italian- american population and hopes to preserve the heritage and culture of this vibrant community. One major theme of il cintro is to acknowledge and disseminate a positive image and legacy of italian-american men and women. While working towards this goal, we look forward to strengthening the ties among the generations of italian-americans in the community and working with all individuals who share this common bond.
