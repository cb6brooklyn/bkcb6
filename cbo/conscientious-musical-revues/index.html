Conscientious Musical Revues

Arts & Culture · Staten Island

In brief

Conscientious Musical Revues is a community-based organization in Staten Island: arts and culture.

In its own words, to the IRSMay 31, 2023 was the 33RD anniversary of Conscientious Musical Revues increasing quality arts access to low income, African American, and Latino communities. Our Motto is Always a Gooood Time! CMR ethnically reflects community served because our Board has 1 Latino and 4 African American members; staff and consultants are African American and Latino. Our website is conscientiousrevues.org. Conscientious Musical Revues served 1025 seniors and adults with 30 hours/wk. of arts, pro concert series, and technology services. CMR's BizBeyond project servied 400 NYC entrepreneurs with FREE 1 on 1 coaching, newsletters, and technology workshops that helped them thrive and grow.
