In brief

United Senior Citizens Center of Sunset Park is a community-based organization in Brooklyn: human and social services.

In its own words, to the IRSUnited senior citizens of sunset park, inc. Is a non-profit organization supported throug funding by the New York city department for the aging. Its mission is to serve the needs of the seniors population with love, dedication and commitment. The senior center provides a comfortable and friendly environment where our seniors can feel accepted and are treated with dignity and respect. United senior centeer provide the following services: 1. Social services (bi-lingual) 2. Nutritional services - breakfast and lunch 3. Educational and recreational: bingo, billiards, dominoes, mah-jong, poker card, ping-pong, crafts, recreational trips, dance lessons, lectures on different subjects, movies, tai chi, yoga, zumba, exercise, gym, cable television, karaoke, wii, monthly party, etc. 4. C.e.r.t. Program 5. Health services ESL & computer classes for hispanic and chinese seniors
