Trans Formative Schools

Education · Manhattan

In brief

Trans Formative Schools is an education organization in Manhattan that the New York City Council has funded.

In its own words, to the IRS(a) To advocate for systemic societal change through education in order to help trans youth thrive because they feel loved, safe and seen; (b) To build resilient learning environments and form a community of educators, students and families-touched-by-transness; (c) To provide student support services and afterschool programming focused on academic support and enrichment, social-emotional learning, community building, student-led activism projects, affinity and allyship groups and identity exploration; (d) to support the eventual founding and flourishing of schools that will center transness and social justice; (e)t o conduct any and all lawful activities that may be useful in accomplishing the foregoing activities as the Board of Directors may determine from time to time in their sole and absolute discretion, except as restricted in the Bylaws of the Corporation
