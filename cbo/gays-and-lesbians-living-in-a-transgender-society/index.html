Gays and Lesbians Living in a Transgender Society

Civil Rights & Advocacy · Queens

In brief

Gays and Lesbians Living in a Transgender Society is a civil rights and advocacy organization in Queens that the New York City Council has funded.

In its own words, to the IRSG.L.I.T.S is a direct services organization that is dedicated to fighting systemic discrimination against marginalized communities, in New York City and beyond. We provide services and support to the community. We focus on housing justice, community empowerment, long-term success, harm reduction, and our fundamental human rights.
