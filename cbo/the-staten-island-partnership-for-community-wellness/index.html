The Staten Island Partnership for Community Wellness

Health & Mental Health · Staten Island

In brief

The Staten Island Partnership for Community Wellness is a community-based organization in Staten Island: health and mental health.

In its own words, to the IRSStaten island partnership for community wellness (sipcw) seeks to create a healthier staten island by bringing together partner organizations and community members to address pressing wellness needs and give all staten islanders a chance to live their best lives. The focus of our efforts is in communities and populations with the greatest health inequities such as bipoc and LGBTQ+ identifying. For more than two decades, sipcw has addressed critical issues such as chronic disease prevention, mental health, and substance use. Through its ability to bring diverse partners to the table, sipcw has enabled data-driven decitions and identified evidence-based strategies to collectively approach complex health issues on staten island. Instead of working alone to address complex health issues, sipcw empowers organizations and individuals to use their resources together to fill serious gaps in service and treatment.
