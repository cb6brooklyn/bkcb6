Emma's Place - Grief and Loss Center For Children and Families

Human & Social Services · Staten Island

In brief

Emma's Place - Grief and Loss Center For Children and Families is a community-based organization in Staten Island: human and social services.

In its own words, to the IRSEmma's Place-Staten Island Grief and Loss Center For Children and Families Inc. Provides, free of charge, bereavement counseling for children ages 4-17 who have suffered the death of a Mother, Father, Primary Caregiver, or Sibling. Emma's Place also hosts Grief Conferences and provides grief-based training to schools and other organizations who interact with children.
