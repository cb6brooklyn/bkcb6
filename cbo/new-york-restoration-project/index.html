New York Restoration Project

Environment & Animals · Manhattan

In brief

New York Restoration Project is an environment and animals organization in Manhattan that the New York City Council has funded.

In its own words, to the IRSNature is a fundamental right of every new yorker. New york restoration project ("nyrp") partners with residents, community-based organizations, civic institutions, public agencies, and the private sector to create, maintain, and program public open spaces. These partnerships deepen social ties and sustain underserved neighborhoods. We work daily to ensure that these meaningful partnerships make New York city a safer, healthier, and happier city. In the short year, we remain steadfast that nature is a fundamental right of every new yorker. Nyrp continues to work collaboratively with residents in communities across the five boroughs to renovate gardens, restore parks, plant trees, promote urban agriculture, and build partnerships that transform the city's landscape.
