Ryan/Chelsea-Clinton Community Health Center

Health & Mental Health · Manhattan

In brief

Ryan/Chelsea-Clinton Community Health Center is a community-based organization in Manhattan: health and mental health.

In its own words, to the IRSThe ryan chelsea-clinton community health center, inc., is guided by the founding principle that "health care is a right, not a privilege." the center is dedicated to providing high quality, affordable, comprehensive, linguistically appropriate and culturally competent health care services to medically underserved populations on the west side of manhattan. All patients are treated equally with dignity, respect, courtesy, confidentiality, and concern for safety.
