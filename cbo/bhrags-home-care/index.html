BHRAGS Home Care

Health & Mental Health · Brooklyn

In brief

BHRAGS Home Care is a community-based organization in Brooklyn: health and mental health.

In its own words, to the IRSWas founded in 1981 by a group of individuals with a shared passion for helping the needy and unfortunate. Amongst these individuals are people with years of experience working with but not limited to home health care, nycs department of homeless services helping the homeless; as well as an involvement with other charitable activities. Our staff has deep relationships with diverse communities and is committed to providing support and relief to those who need it most. Mission and vision, our services allow for individuals and families to maintain their dignity while affording them the opportunity to regain their independence in a safe and healthy environment. Our services include home care, nursing care, case management and appropriate referrals to mltcs, mco's chha, medical and health services
