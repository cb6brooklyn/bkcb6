Human & Social Services · Brooklyn

In brief

Riseboro Community Partnership is a human and social services organization in Brooklyn that the New York City Council has funded.

In its own words, to the IRSContinually to seek out and implement new and effective strategies to improve the area in which we operate and provide the necessary resources to local residents so they may improve their present living conditions and work towards long-term self sufficiency.
