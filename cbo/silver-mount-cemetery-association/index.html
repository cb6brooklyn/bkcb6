Silver Mount Cemetery Association

Environment & Animals · Staten Island

In brief

Silver Mount Cemetery Association is a community-based organization in Staten Island: environment and animals.

In its own words, to the IRSWe are comprised of an all volunteer group whose mission is to provide a peaceful resting place for our loved ones in a park like landscape setting. Our goal is to establish a self -sustaining community asset that transcends the traditional concept of a cemetery. We want to empower the community, ensuring its lasting positive impact through historical and cultural engagement. With the support of our elected officials, Veterans Associations neighborhood civic groups and dedicated Silver Mount Volunteers, we would like to preserve Silver Mount Cemeterys beauty and Serenity for future generations to come, all while maintaining financial stability.
