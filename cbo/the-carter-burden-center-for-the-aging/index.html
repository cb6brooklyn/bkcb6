The Carter Burden Center for the Aging

Human & Social Services · Manhattan

In brief

The Carter Burden Center for the Aging is a community-based organization in Manhattan: human and social services.

In its own words, to the IRSCarter burden network (cbn) promotes the well-being of older adults 60 and older through a continuum of services, advocacy, arts and culture, health and wellness, and volunteer programs, all oriented to individual, family and community needs. We are dedicated to supporting the efforts of older people to live safely and with dignity.
