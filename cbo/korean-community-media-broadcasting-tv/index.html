Korean Community Media Broadcasting TV

Youth Development · Queens

In brief

Korean Community Media Broadcasting TV is a youth development organization in Queens that the New York City Council has funded.

In its own words, to the IRSThe purpose for which the corporation is formed is the following spescific purposes: 1) to serve as a korean online resource center for korean american families, especially with limited-english proficiency; 2) to help korean american families to overcome the information in equality caused by the language barrier; 3) to provide any varieties and categories of internet contents which can help korean american families stay updated and informed about the community; and 4)to promote the common goods and general welfare of korean american families
