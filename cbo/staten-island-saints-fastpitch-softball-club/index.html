Staten Island Saints Fastpitch Softball Club

Sports & Recreation · Staten Island

In brief

Staten Island Saints Fastpitch Softball Club is a community-based organization in Staten Island: sports and recreation.

In its own words, to the IRSThe purpose of the Corporation is to provide a fast pitch softball program for girls on Staten Island. The Corporation would run clinics, tournaments, and practices for the girl's team and would enter leagues.The Corporation provides pitching, hitting, and fielding clinics that are run by coaches from college and high school levels. The Corporation also provides all of the equipment for the girls' team such as masks for the catchers, bats, balls, batting cages for hitting clinics, pitching trainers, batting gloves, and uniforms. The Corporation hopes to achieve expert training that will expose the girls to high school and college scouts at tournaments and travel leagues as well as getting them ready for playing in high school, college, and in advance tournament play for softball. In addition, the Corporation will teach the girls the fundamentals of the game as well as teach them good sportsmanship and teamwork.
