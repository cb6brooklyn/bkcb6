Waterwell Productions

Arts & Culture · Manhattan

In brief

Waterwell Productions is an arts and culture organization in Manhattan that the New York City Council has funded.

In its own words, to the IRSWaterwell is a nonprofit production company dedicated to telling stories that reflect the real complexity of pressing civic questions and by doing so, advancing change toward a more just and humane society. Built on our foundation of community engagement, education, and live performance, we create theatrical productions and live events that are timely, creatively daring, and emotionally potent to cultivate connections across real and perceived divides. Our projects manifest in more that one medium but everything we do is infused with our love of the immediacy of live performance.
