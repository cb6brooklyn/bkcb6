Staten Island Tuna Club

Arts & Culture · Staten Island

In brief

Staten Island Tuna Club is a community-based organization in Staten Island: arts and culture.

In its own words, to the IRSTo educate the members of the group and community, how to fish, and understand the guidelines set forth to preserve the fisheries; to advance, promote and enjoy the sport of fishing in the state of New York and waters adjacent thereto; to better protect fish and other water life of this state, and to prevent the violation of the laws thereof relative to fish and fishing, to help secure, through legislation, advanced and better laws that from time to time will better aid in the propagation and conservation of our fish, to promote the interests of rod and reel angling and to establish a social and good fellowship among members of this club.
