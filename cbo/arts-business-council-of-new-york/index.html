Arts & Business Council of New York

Arts & Culture · Manhattan

In brief

Arts & Business Council of New York is a community-based organization in Manhattan: arts and culture.

In its own words, to the IRSThe Arts & Business Council of New York stimulates partnerships between the arts and business that strengthen both sectors in the communities they serve. This is accomplished through programs that promote voluntarism, build arts management capacity, gather and disseminate relevant information, and advocate closer ties between business and the arts.
