Museum of Contemporary African Diasporian Arts

Arts & Culture · Brooklyn

In brief

Museum of Contemporary African Diasporian Arts is an arts and culture organization in Brooklyn that the New York City Council has funded.

In its own words, to the IRSThe museum of contemporary african diasporan arts (mocada) celebrates the cultural breadth of africa and its diaspora through a diverse range of exhibitions, public art, interactive learning, media, performances, food experiences, music, and community-based interventions.
