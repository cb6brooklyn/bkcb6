OpenPlans

Public & Civic Benefit · Manhattan

In brief

OpenPlans is a community-based organization in Manhattan: public and civic benefit.

In its own words, to the IRSOpen plans' mission is to transform how people experience New York city's streets. Open plans uses grassroots advocacy and policy change to inspire structural reforms and cultural shifts. We promote a people-first street culture that prioritizes community, safety, joy, mobility and empowerment. What we do: build practical solutions, from policy to physical changes, that solve real issues in communities;empower neighborhoods to vision and create change on their streets;promote a people-first approach to cities that dismantles car culture;create a culture of mobility justice and abundance of options, to ensure every city resident has what they need to thrive;celebrate public spaces and their stewards;advance legislation that builds a people-centered city;partner with allied organizations on city and state legislative efforts.
