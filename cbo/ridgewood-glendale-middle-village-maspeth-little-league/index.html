Ridgewood Glendale Middle Village Maspeth Little League

Sports & Recreation · Queens

In brief

Ridgewood Glendale Middle Village Maspeth Little League is a community-based organization in Queens: sports and recreation.

In its own words, to the IRSWe at rgmvm little league are more than just a baseball league. We are the oldest little league in queens, NY with this being our 72nd year. We offer not just baseball games for kids from 3 years old to 19, but also a safe enironment in which children and parents can develop and grow through coaching, clinics, game sportsmanship and friendship. At our facilities, we allow our mgrs. And coaches to use our fields and batting cages to help their teams grow. For years we had and continue to have a great relationship with the community and our sponsors, as well as with the New York mets that do so much for US by donating equipment, tickets to games and clinics for everyone. All our volunteer coaches teach all the necessary skills needed to become better ballplayers and we have so many 2nd and 3rd generations of parents that have always been part of our organization. Our volunteer board members and coaches are the driving force behind what makes US what we are and to be successful all these
