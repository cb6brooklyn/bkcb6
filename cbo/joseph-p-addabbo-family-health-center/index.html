Joseph P. Addabbo Family Health Center

Health & Mental Health · Queens

In brief

Joseph P. Addabbo Family Health Center is a health and mental health organization in Queens that the New York City Council has funded.

In its own words, to the IRSThe organization operates freestanding diagnostic and treatment centers, licensed under article 28 of the nys health law, located in the boroughs of queens, brooklyn, and New York. The center provides a broad range of health services to a largely medically underserved population.
