In brief

Volunteer Firemans Association of the City of New York is a community-based organization in Queens: public and civic benefit.

In its own words, to the IRSThe VFACNY was established to advocate for the rights and interests of volunteer firefighters in New York City and to assist them with recruitment training equipment and other resources Providing such support ensures that the communities served by the associations member departments are protected by fire crews that are appropriately trained equipped and staffed
