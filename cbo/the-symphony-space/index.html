The Symphony Space

Arts & Culture · Manhattan

In brief

The Symphony Space is a community-based organization in Manhattan: arts and culture.

In its own words, to the IRSSymphony space is a multi-disciplinary performing arts center where adventurous programming, presented in a uniquely warm and welcoming environment, forges indelible relationships between artists and audiences. Our fundamental mission is to connect art, ideas, and community through our programs and our commitment to literacy and education through the arts. On our New York city stages, through our national broadcasts, and in the classrooms and communities we serve throughout the country, we foster access to the arts through all disciplines. Our embrace of the arts is wide, and the experiences of our audiences are deep and enriched.
