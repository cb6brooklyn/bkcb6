Invisible Hands Deliver

Public Safety & Emergency Services · Manhattan

In brief

Invisible Hands Deliver is a community-based organization in Manhattan: public safety and emergency services.

In its own words, to the IRSInvisible hands deliver inc. (invisible hands) is a nonprofit corporation organized and operated exclusively for chraitable purposes, and at this time is specially dedicated to relieving the plight of elderly and other at-risk persons, resulting from the covid-19 pandemic, many of whom are homebound, by lending a helping hand during and in the wake of covid-19. Invisible hands partners with food pantries, mutual aid groups, and religious institutions to address food insecurity by delivering fre food to those who cannot afford it themselves.
