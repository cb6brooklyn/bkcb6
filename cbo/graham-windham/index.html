Graham Windham

Human & Social Services · Brooklyn

In brief

Graham Windham is a human and social services organization in Brooklyn that the New York City Council has funded.

In its own words, to the IRSGraham provides life-changing tools and resources for children, young adults, and families who face some of the most difficult obstacles caused by poverty, racial injustice, and lack of access to educational opportunities, living wage employment, quality healthcare, and affordable housing.we collaborate with communities and strong partners to create and implement innovative strategies so everyone can lead healthy, joyful, and successful lives.
