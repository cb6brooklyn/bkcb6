Homes for the Homeless

Human & Social Services · Manhattan

In brief

Homes for the Homeless is a community-based organization in Manhattan: human and social services.

In its own words, to the IRSThe mission of homes for the homeless is to help families experiencing homelessness address the issue that creates instability in their lives. Hfh operates shelters for families experiencing homelessness in NYC. Our family residences are more than just a place to sleep. Families can make the most of their stay through accessing an array of onsite programs. Many of these programs are also available to people living in the local community. Our goal is to have a positive impact so families can thrive long after they leave our shelters.
