Catholic Migration Services

Housing & Shelter · Brooklyn

In brief

Catholic Migration Services is a housing and shelter organization in Brooklyn that the New York City Council has funded.

In its own words, to the IRSCatholic migration services (cms) is a non profit organization providing quality legal services to immigrants primarily in brooklyn and queens, regardless of race, ethnicity, and religion. Catholic migration services is focused on "welcoming the stranger in our midst," by being committed to empowering underserved immigrant communities regardless of religion or ethnicity. We provide high quality legal services, education, and advocacy to immigrants to advance equality and social justice in a changing and diverse population.
