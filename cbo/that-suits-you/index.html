In brief

That Suits You is a community-based organization in Brooklyn: employment and workforce.

In its own words, to the IRSThat Suits You Inc is organized exclusively for charitable and educational purposes under Section 501c3 of the Internal Revenue Code Our primary exempt purpose is to empower underserved youth and adults by providing workforce development career readiness mentorship educational programming leadership development and professional image services We help individuals overcome barriers to employment and self-sufficiency through life skills training financial literacy etiquette interview preparation mentoring and access to professional attire Through school-based initiatives community partnerships and reentry support for justice-impacted individuals we promote economic mobility personal growth and community engagement Our programs equip participants with the confidence skills and resources needed to achieve long-term educational employment and leadership success while strengthening families and communities
