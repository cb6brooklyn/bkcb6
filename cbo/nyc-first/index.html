NYC First

Youth Development · Manhattan

In brief

NYC First is a community-based organization in Manhattan: youth development.

In its own words, to the IRSSee Schedule O.NYC FIRST's mission is to inspire and empower K-12 students from diverse communities across New York City each year to engage with STEM and robotics in meaningful and joyful ways. It creates learning experiences that encourage young people to think critically and creatively and to persevere. Its programs set young people on pathways to success academically and professionally.
