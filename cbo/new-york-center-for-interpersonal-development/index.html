New York Center for Interpersonal Development

Legal Services & Justice · Staten Island

In brief

New York Center for Interpersonal Development is a community-based organization in Staten Island: legal services and justice.

In its own words, to the IRSThe Organization promotes the improvement of human relationships and the strenghtening of communities as fundamental to achieving a civil society The Organization provides youth, community, and professional development programs, and dispute resolution services that educate and inform about constructive problem solving, effective communication, violence prevention, and intercultural awareness The Organization helps individuals, families and organizations discover solutions that are mutually agreeable and enduring.
