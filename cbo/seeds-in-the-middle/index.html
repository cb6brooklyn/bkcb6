Seeds in the Middle

Youth Development · Brooklyn

In brief

Seeds in the Middle is a community-based organization in Brooklyn: youth development.

In its own words, to the IRS(1) organize and promote partnerships between farms, schools, cultural, educational, and non-profit organizations for the purpose of empowering and enriching youth and community activists; (2) to promote and organize gardening, nutrition and cooking education and awareness among youth and community activists; (3) to promote and organize physical fitness activities and health and wellness education for youth and community activists; (4) to assist schools with activities related to professional development, advocacy, education and cultural awareness; (5) to promote and organize music and art education; (6) to build community and partnerships between racial, socioeconomic, religious, gender and age divides by means of the foregoing, and (7) related and incidental activities.
