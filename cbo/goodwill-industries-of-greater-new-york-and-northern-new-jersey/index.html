Goodwill Industries of Greater New York and Northern New Jersey

Employment & Workforce · Brooklyn

In brief

Goodwill Industries of Greater New York and Northern New Jersey is an employment and workforce organization in Brooklyn that the New York City Council has funded.

In its own words, to the IRSGoodwill nynj empowers people with disabilities and other barriers to employment to gain independence through the power of work. Goodwill nynj provided services to more than 11,087 adults in fiscal year 2025 through all programs.
