# App Store Connect — Reviewer Notes

## What to paste into "App Review Information → Notes"

CB6 & Beyond is a free civic-information app built for Brooklyn Community Board 6 (BKCB6), the official City of New York advisory body covering Park Slope, Gowanus, Carroll Gardens, Red Hook, Cobble Hill, and the Columbia Street Waterfront District. It also provides reference information for every Community Board across all five NYC boroughs.

This is an information-only app. No login is required. The app does not collect, transmit, or store any user data — please see the privacy policy at https://bkcb6.app/privacy.html for full disclosure.

The app provides residents with:
- Interactive maps of land use, zoning, landmarks, public safety, and transportation
- Lookup by address for the relevant Community Board and district information
- Calendars and information for all 59 NYC Community Boards
- A civic glossary, plain-language guides, and civic literacy games
- Native iOS home-screen access for ongoing civic engagement

Demo instructions:
1. Open the app — you will see the CB6 hero banner and a grid of all five boroughs plus Citywide
2. Tap the CB6 banner to access the Brooklyn Community Board 6 dashboard
3. Tap any borough tile (Brooklyn, Manhattan, Queens, The Bronx, Staten Island, or Citywide) to see the Community Boards in that area
4. Tap the Citywide tile to search any NYC address (try 250 Baltic Street, Brooklyn) and find the relevant Community Board
5. Tap Glossary, Guides, or Games to access civic literacy resources

Mike Racioppo, the listed developer, is the District Manager of Brooklyn Community Board 6 (an NYC mayoral appointment) and a Commissioner on the New York City Charter Revision Commission. This app is published in his individual capacity as a civic technology project and is not an official publication of the City of New York.

Contact for review questions: Mike@bkcb6.org

## What to paste into "Description"

CB6 & Beyond is the civic dashboard for Brooklyn Community Board 6 — and every Community Board across NYC.

Brooklyn Community Board 6 represents Park Slope, Gowanus, Carroll Gardens, Red Hook, Cobble Hill, and the Columbia Street Waterfront District — roughly 120,000 residents in central-western Brooklyn. CB6 advises city government on land use, liquor licenses, transportation, parks, public safety, and the city budget.

This app puts the data behind those decisions in your pocket — and extends the same tools to every Community Board across all five boroughs.

Features
• Interactive maps: land use, zoning, landmarks, transportation, public safety
• Address search: find your Community Board, City Council district, NYPD precinct, FDNY battalion, and DSNY district
• Land use by lot: thousands of CB6 parcels with zoning, ownership, and year built
• Budget: NYC and NY State budget process explained, with timelines
• Government org charts: NYC and NY State
• Rent rankings: Brooklyn and citywide rent maps by Community Board
• Elections and legislative districts
• CB6 calendar, 311 complaints, liquor licenses
• Gowanus rezoning: every lot with zone, land use, and NYCHA designation
• Beyond CB6: information for all 59 NYC Community Boards
• Civic glossary, plain-language guides, and civic literacy games

Privacy: This app does not collect, transmit, or store any user data. See privacy policy at bkcb6.app/privacy.html.

Built for Brooklyn Community Board 6 by Mike Racioppo, District Manager, BKCB6.

## What to set for "App Privacy" → Data Types

Select: **Data Not Collected**

If asked about address inputs:
- The address you enter is sent to the NYC Geoclient API (a public NYC government service) to return geographic information.
- No user identifier is attached.
- The address is not retained by the app.
- This is "user-generated content sent to a third-party service for the sole purpose of providing the requested information; not collected by the app developer."
