Jo Anne Simon

Assembly Member · State Assembly District 52

Assembly Member Jo Anne Simon represents State Assembly District 52. It sits in Brooklyn.

The district overlaps community boards Brooklyn 2 and Brooklyn 6.

It is policed by the 76th, 78th, 84th and 88th Precincts.

It overlaps council districts 33, 35 and 39 and Senate districts 20, 25 and 26.

First elected in 2014 and sworn in January 2015. Members of the State Assembly serve two year terms.

Committees and legislation

Jo Anne Simon chairs the Committee on Mental Health.

Jo Anne Simon also sits on the Committee on Higher Education, Committee on Judiciary, Committee on Transportation, Committee on Ways and Means, Legislative Women's Caucus, Task Force on Women's Issues.

Recent bills sponsored include A01106, requires instruction on civic education for grades 5 through 12; A01111, establishes a reverse mortgage loan program for seniors and requires counseling; A01141, establishes the "health care information management act"; A01146, establishes a student loan borrower bill of rights.
