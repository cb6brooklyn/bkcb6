New York Immigration Coalition

Immigrant and refugee rights coalition · statewide, based in Midtown Manhattan

The New York Immigration Coalition (NYIC) is an umbrella policy and advocacy organization that represents more than 200 immigrant and refugee rights groups throughout New York: grassroots and nonprofit community organizations, religious and academic institutions, labor unions, and legal and socioeconomic justice organizations. It was founded in 1987.

A state-wide, member-led coalition, NYIC works to transform the lives of all New Yorkers by strengthening and building its members’ power, and using their collective voice to advocate for opportunity and justice.

What NYIC does

Immigrant rights. NYIC works at the city, state and federal levels to expand access to counsel and status and to end cooperation between local police and federal immigration enforcement. Its campaigns include New York for All, which would prohibit state and local officers from enforcing federal immigration law and bar ICE and CBP from non-public areas of state and local property without a judicial warrant; the Access to Representation Act, with the Vera Institute and I-ARC, to guarantee a lawyer for immigrants at risk of deportation in New York; Dignity Not Detention; and ICE Out of NYC.

Civic participation. NYIC runs the New Citizen Voter Registration Project, the largest voter registration project in New York State, voter education on rights inside and outside the voting booth, and Our City, Our Vote, its campaign to expand municipal voting rights for immigrants living in New York City.

Education, health and language. NYIC works to improve education for immigrant families and students, to advance immigrants’ access to quality health care (Coverage4All), and on Language Access, Legal Service Funding, Welcoming New York and the state Office for New Americans.

Know Your Rights. NYIC publishes resources on your rights as a New Yorker and how to access help, including a guide to immigration legal services in Brooklyn.
