New Dawn Charter High School

Public charter high school · Carroll Gardens, Brooklyn

New Dawn Charter High School is a public charter high school at 242 Hoyt Street in Carroll Gardens.

In its own words: “New Dawn Charter Schools will provide over-aged and under-credited students 16 - 21 years of age, including those who are English Language Learners and those with special needs, the opportunity to return to school and obtain a high school diploma through a rigorous NYSED standards-based education program.”
