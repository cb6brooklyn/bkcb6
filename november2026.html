Your districts

What is on your ballot

Every voter in the state votes for these statewide offices. The rest depends on where you live, and your districts are above.

Every date that matters

Oct 24
Voter registration deadline. Your application has to be received by a board of elections by this date to vote in the general election.
Oct 24
Last day to apply online or by mail for an early mail or absentee ballot.
Oct 24 – Nov 1
Early voting. Nine days. Your early voting site is usually not your Election Day site, so check both above. You can also drop off a completed mail ballot at any early voting site in your county during this window.
Nov 2
Last day to apply in person at your county board of elections for an early mail or absentee ballot.
Nov 3
Election Day. Polls open 6 AM to 9 PM. If you are on line at 9 PM you get to vote.
Nov 3
Last day to postmark a mail ballot. You can also drop it at any poll site by 9 PM, or at your borough board of elections office by 9 PM.
Nov 10
Last day a postmarked mail ballot can arrive at your county board of elections and still be counted.

The nine days of early voting

Hours are set by the Board of Elections and posted closer to the election. Any early voting site in your county works, but the one assigned to you above is the one the Board expects you at.

Two things worth knowing. Poll sites move. The Board of Elections mails a notice before every election, and the assignment shown here is the one the Board has on file right now, so check back in late October before you go. And the certified list of candidates and any ballot proposals is published by the Board in the fall, after independent nominating petitions and any court challenges are resolved.

If something is wrong

Call 1-866-VOTE-NYC (1-866-868-3692). The Brooklyn office of the Board of Elections is at 345 Adams Street, 4th Floor, Brooklyn, NY 11201, 1-718-797-8800. You can check your registration and request a mail ballot at vote.nyc. If you are told you cannot vote, ask for an affidavit ballot. They have to give you one.
