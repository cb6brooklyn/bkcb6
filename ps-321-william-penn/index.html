PS 321 William Penn

Public elementary school · Park Slope, Brooklyn

PS 321 William Penn is a public elementary school at 180 7th Avenue in Park Slope.

In its own words: “PS 321 is a vibrant and diverse neighborhood school. In our heterogeneously grouped classrooms, children of many different ethnic groups learn to become part of a classroom and school community that fosters not only tolerance, but also true respect for all people. We help children learn to think creatively and analytically.”
