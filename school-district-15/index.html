Community School District 15

NYC Public Schools · Brooklyn

Community School District 15 is one of the 32 community school districts of NYC Public Schools, for the public elementary and middle schools in its area. It reaches into BKCB7 (44%), BKCB6 (39%), BKCB12 (10%), BKCB2 (6%).
